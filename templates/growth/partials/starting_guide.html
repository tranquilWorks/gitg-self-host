How it works

Answer a short assessment, choose a useful practice, try its actions in real life, then review what happened. You can add a direction and your available time to help choose what fits now.

Starting estimate

Your answers suggest areas to practise. They are a starting point, not a test you pass or fail.

Completion credit

Finishing the required actions and submitting a final review records a completed practice.

Coverage

This is how much of the practice map your completed work covers. It is not a measure of mastery.

Remaining priority

Suggestions begin with your assessment and completed work. The fit and capacity you choose to share can help narrow them down.
