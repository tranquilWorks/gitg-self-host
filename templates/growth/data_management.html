Optional assessment research

Choose whether your answers may be used for research

Normal assessment storage is private account data. This separate opt-in controls whether one completed assessment may also enter a manual, local calibration export. Nothing is uploaded automatically, and participation does not change your assessment, profile, recommendations, completion credit, or scores.

The contribution contains item-level answers, N/A choices, answered clarifiers, timing, response-quality summaries, a random participant token, and coarse day intervals between included retakes. It excludes your account identity, exact dates, share codes, free text, Personal OS data, practice history, and score outputs. It is still sensitive pseudonymous data and must not be treated as anonymous.
