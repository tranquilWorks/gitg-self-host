# Grounded Growth — content-quality audit

## Verdict

Coverage complete. Catalog-wide professional depth and self-contained instructional acceptance not established; specific content defects found.

This is a source-content review, not publication or integration acceptance. The full corpus was checked structurally; substantive findings come from a targeted spot review. No percentage of editorially accepted competencies is asserted.

## Structural result

Combining the exact base commit with the uploaded completion files yields 383 competencies across 27 domains, 1,151 action instructions and 4,441 observation checks. All 1,151 normalized action-instruction strings are distinct. Exact-text uniqueness does not establish semantic originality, suitable difficulty, conceptual coverage or teaching quality.

## Specific findings

### QA-01 — 21.03

Default references supplied facts and supplied answer keys but supplies category names and asks the learner to invent three fictional events; no answer keys are included in the entry.

**Required revision:** Supply three complete fictional event briefs, fixed assessment criteria and matching check keys; retain optional user-chosen work as an alternate.

### QA-02 — 10.01

The delayed unfamiliar-application instruction prints the mean, median and range for the new dataset in the same sentence as the task.

**Required revision:** Separate teaching material, unseen test prompts and answer keys; do not treat exposed numerical answers as unaided application. Distinguish the still-open explanation task from already-supplied calculations.

### QA-03 — 12.05

The canonical scope is progressive training of major movement patterns; the default is two sessions of three familiar supported chair rises and explicitly does not demonstrate strength gain. This is a bounded introductory option, not a sufficiently developed pathway for the full competency across different starting abilities.

**Required revision:** Retain the accessible entry path and safety boundaries; provide a capability-fit pathway, relevant technique and recovery checks, and a meaningful supervised or appropriate established-plan progression route without prescribing one universal regimen.

### QA-04 — 12.08

A card-touching sequence addresses elementary coordination while explicitly leaving athletic power, speed and agility untested.

**Required revision:** Treat it as an introductory or adapted branch, explain its transfer limits, and supply appropriate separate routes for the other canonical components.

### QA-05 — 10.02

The scope_note contains internal protocol compatibility details about measurement IDs, ratios and frozen completion rules instead of explaining the learner-facing scope.

**Required revision:** Move implementation compatibility notes into authoring/governance metadata and keep learner instructions about the skill, evidence and next steps.

## What should be retained

Uses concrete numerical datasets, distinct denominators, sampling concerns, and bounded conclusions rather than only a generic reflection prompt. This is evidence of meaningful task specificity, not a full editorial sign-off.

## Required next review passes

1. **Scope and level:** review all 383 against the canonical definition and evidence-of-progress intent. Identify what is explained, practised and deferred; build appropriate pathways where a single introductory task is insufficient. Do not grandfather earlier batches solely because they were marked authored.

2. **Instructional and professional editing:** provide all promised materials; separate worked examples from unassisted checks; remove engineering copy from learner instructions; ensure adaptations preserve the underlying purpose and real work is not replaced by merely writing a plan.

3. **Cold-start acceptance:** walk through each entry without conversational context. Check that the materials, steps, scope, meaningful observations, failure cases and next action can be followed without inventing the intervention. Record pass or required revision per competency. Actual learner and qualified subject review remain separately identified, not fabricated.

Automated compilation and later CI are necessary implementation checks but cannot substitute for these content judgments. Adequate depth is task-dependent; this review does not recommend padding every entry to the same length or requiring every learner to follow the same path.
