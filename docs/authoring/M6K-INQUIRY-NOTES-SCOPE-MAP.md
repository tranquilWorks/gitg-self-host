# Inquiry to reusable knowledge: three substantive runtime rewrites

This three-ID cohort is tightly related but not interchangeable. `10.03` turns an
observation into a question and retains uncertainty. `10.04` locates and audits evidence
for a decision. `10.05` compresses source-linked knowledge and proves the retrieval route
through later reuse. Combining them permits one coherent authoring batch while preserving
separate learner artifacts, prompts, checks, evidence and limitations for every ID.

Selection increases substantive compiler/runtime coverage from **96 to 99 of 383**;
**284 remain rewrite-pending**. It does not establish formal A/B/C, independent learner,
qualified or owner acceptance. Exact pre-projection packages are retained in each
competency's quality folder.

## 10.03 — Curiosity and question formation

Starting ability: inspect or listen to a short fictional evidence packet and record a
question using paper, audio or an accessible note tool. Someone already experienced in
inquiry may use a low-stakes technical or civic question only after declaring authority,
evidence and stopping boundaries.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and practice | Observable evidence and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confusion, observation and contradiction | `inquiry-boundary`, `board-packet`, action 1 | Observation, expectation and contradiction occupy separate fields; no invented explanation |
| Precise question formation | `question-ladder`, `classify-questions` | Descriptive, causal and evaluative questions name observable objects/criteria |
| Worth investigating | Evidence-match ranking in action 2 | Relevance, answerability, consequence, access and time fit support selection |
| Inquiry | Supplied overlap counts or an authorized live observation | The packet answers a descriptive count but supplies no causal, motive or reader evidence |
| Backlog and refinement | `backlog-template`, `resolve-question`, action 3 | Status, evidence, uncertainty, next check and revisit trigger remain visible |
| Progression and transfer | `pathway-options`, `next-inquiry` | A different harmless context tests the method; question volume is not progress |

The complete default includes twelve synthetic notices, five explicitly listed overlap
pairs, four zones and stated missing evidence. The prompt key checks the count and rejects
causal overreach. Curiosity about people never authorizes investigation of private lives.

## 10.04 — Research and information gathering

Starting ability: read or listen to three short source cards and use a table. The default
requires no live web access. A live route must replace fictional sources with inspectable
current public records and preserve the same provenance and claim audit.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and practice | Observable evidence and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search broadly and strategically | `research-plan` decomposes five information needs and includes an alternate perspective | A first result is not automatically enough; stopping depends on material claim coverage |
| Primary and expert sources | `research-model`, `source-role` | Authority is judged for the exact claim; community experience remains useful but cannot issue policy |
| Compare perspectives and conflicts | P1/P2/P3 cards and `conflict-resolution` | Publication/effective dates, scope and issuing authority resolve only supported clauses |
| Document provenance | `claim-matrix` | Exact claim, issuer, title, dates, locator, relevant passage, limitation and disposition |
| Traceable summary | `answer-template` and action 3 | Each material claim reopens to a source; unsupported and unknown rows remain visible |
| Progression | `live-and-advanced`, `next-research` | More ambiguous sources or methods increase difficulty without increasing consequences |

The correct fictional result changes the item limit from one to two on 1 April while
retaining seven days; eligibility and exceptions remain unknown. It is not advice about
an actual lending service or evidence of domain expertise.

## 10.05 — Knowledge organization and notes

Starting ability: use one paper, text, audio or supported note and a three-entry index.
The supplied arithmetic is deliberately small so the practice can examine the knowledge
system. Someone fluent in it should use a genuinely unfamiliar but authorized source and
new problem rather than add tool complexity.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and practice | Observable evidence and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capture | `averages-source`, action 1 | Source identity/revision and relevant definitions remain traceable |
| Compress | `note-template`, `worked-note`, `note-quality` | Own-words distinction and correct example; copied volume is not rewarded |
| Connect | Note and index require where/why the idea matters | An unexplained tag is not a connection |
| Retrieve | `index-template`, action 2 | Actual cue, outcome, support, barrier, repair and retest are recorded |
| Revise | Revision log and delayed application | One change responds to an observed error, omission or poor route |
| Reuse and progression | New 2-3-4-5-16 problem and `next-note` | Mean 6, median 4 and missing spread are checked; one reuse is not long-term retention |

The medium can change without changing the purpose. Finding the note is not unaided
recall, and same-session application is useful practice but not delayed retrieval.

## Source and review boundaries

The adopted ACRL information-literacy framework supports inquiry, strategic search,
contextual authority, ethical attribution and organizing/synthesizing information; it is
not a prescriptive product checklist or intervention validation. Cornell's learning center
supports purpose-driven selection among note-taking strategies; its guidance does not
validate this atomic-note design or impose handwriting over necessary access supports.
No source wording, proprietary module or external exercise is reproduced.

The competency minimum standard prefers multiple independent evidence types. These
runtime actions retain the existing three action identities and prospective structured
artifact criteria, but a self-guided fictional result is not independent learner evidence,
longitudinal transfer or mastery. All formal human dimensions remain separate and pending.
