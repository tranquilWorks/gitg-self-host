# 19.02 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Parenting and child development

**Scope (unchanged):** Understand safety, attachment, development, discipline, education, modeling, autonomy, and changing needs.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Care supports both security and growing competence rather than control or neglect.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Safety, attachment and development | Authorized role, care plan, warmth and current ability | Safe preparation card and specific observations | No recruitment of unrelated child, diagnosis or attachment score |
| Discipline, education and modeling | Accessible choice, demonstrate one item, stay available | Child attempts, adult help and retained story time | No shame, physical punishment or affection withdrawal |
| Autonomy and changing needs | Tiredness, picture cue and smaller task | Two occasions with different supports compared | More help can be appropriate; no fixed age benchmark |
| Security and growing competence | Both days two child placements; conditions differ | Child feedback and next support adjustment | Cannot isolate cause or generalize independence from two trials |

Canonical classification: applicability role_conditional; normative status role_conditional; formation modes: interdependence, stewardship, receptivity.

These roles are optional. Single life, no parenting/caregiving role, fictional preparation and no attempt are legitimate. Live activity requires an appropriate willing context and actual task authority; a worksheet creates neither.

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: qualified_assessment, observer_feedback, artifact, longitudinal_review. This is preserved metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.

Canonical professional boundary: Medical, reproductive, child-safety, guardianship, and elder-care decisions require qualified local guidance.

No runtime selection, action, evidence field, score, completion or participant record changes. Supplied fiction cannot count as actual observer evidence.
