# 18.06 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Relationship stages and changing needs

**Scope (unchanged):** Understand formation, commitment, consolidation, strain, repair, renewal, and possible ending.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Adapts expectations and practices as the relationship changes.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Formation, commitment and consolidation | Organizing terms, not a required timeline | Before/now rows for five relationship areas | No stage diagnosis or universal sequence |
| Strain, repair, renewal and ending | Capacity, conduct and fit are separate questions | Short contact trial plus separately unresolved dinner breach | Two meetings do not establish complete repair or require staying |
| Changing expectations and practices | Work/care limits, preserved contact and lost long outing | Dated trial and review; conditional outing remains proposed | No unnamed replacement caregiver or permanent sacrifice |
| Adapts to actual change | Both feedback accounts and actual meetings | Keep/revise/stop with unperformed actions visible | One useful trial does not settle longer-term needs |
