# Corrective answers

1. A stage term cannot set another person's needs or rank maturity. Ask what affection currently means to each person and what is welcome and feasible. The label does not substitute for their accounts or create an entitlement to contact.
2. The holiday shows a pleasant event, not necessarily accountability or changed conduct around the promise. Seek acknowledgment of the specific effect, any appropriate practical repair and behavior at a relevant later opportunity. Do not demand forgiveness as the evidence.
3. The limited trial may have served its agreed purpose. Its consent and feasibility do not extend automatically. Record the useful short-term outcome, the refusal of indefinite continuation and the need to negotiate, stop or reconsider fit without coercion.
