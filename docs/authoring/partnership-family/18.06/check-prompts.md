# Fresh checks

Answer before opening [corrective answers](check-answers.md).

1. A partner says “We've reached consolidation, so wanting more affection is immature.” What is wrong with that inference, and what question would help?
2. After an unacknowledged broken promise, a pleasant holiday goes well. Does that establish repair? Specify what evidence is missing.
3. A two-week contact trial is useful, but one person declines to continue it indefinitely. Has the trial failed, and what can be concluded?
