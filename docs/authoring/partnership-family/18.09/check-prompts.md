# Fresh checks

Use the facts only; then [check answers](check-answers.md).

1. An adult uses an established communication device to clearly agree to one specified activity and decline another. Does the device itself make the choice invalid or authorize both activities?
2. A person says “I'm not sure” while their partner insists they already promised. What should happen immediately?
3. Someone withdraws consent after an activity begins. The other person says stopping now would be unfair. Which obligation governs the next action?
