# 18.09 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Sexual ethics and consent

**Scope (unchanged):** Ground sexual behavior in free consent, honesty, health, mutual dignity, commitment expectations, and consequences.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Consent is informed, ongoing, reversible, and never inferred from pressure or status.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free informed consent and mutual dignity | Specific activity, material information, privacy and consequences | Four adult case decisions with immediate responses | No legal capacity assessment, touch or sexual assignment |
| Ongoing reversible choice | Handholding only, withdrawal and sleeping cases | Stop/no-start; no justification required | Past agreement/status/nonresistance not permission |
| Pressure, honesty and commitment expectations | Threat/gift pressure; prevention and promise questions | No technical yes after coercion; qualified-question list | AAC not incapacity; no clinical or legal conclusion |
| Ethical responsive conduct | Ask/no/stop no-contact rehearsal | B stops; D does not begin; A pursuit and C pressure remain wrong | Fiction is reasoning practice, not actual sexual conduct |
