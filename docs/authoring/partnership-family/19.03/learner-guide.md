# 19.03 — Caregiving for elders, illness, and disability

Prepare one bounded handoff in an existing authorized care arrangement, complete an ordinary task if suitable, and review usefulness and burden. Allow twenty minutes to plan and fifteen to review around the actual task. Caregiving is role-conditional, not a required identity. Without an appropriate role, use the fiction; do not arrange care for an uninvolved person to obtain evidence.

## Support the person, not only the schedule

Practical support may involve transport, an accessible reminder or an agreed errand. Emotional support asks what concern or reassurance is welcome. Financial support needs authority and limits around expenses; being a caregiver does not grant control over money. Advocacy helps the person raise their own questions and preferences, rather than substituting your voice whenever you think you know best.

Age, illness or disability does not itself establish incapacity. Ask how the person wants to make and communicate decisions, using established supports and relevant legal arrangements. Someone may want help with transport while retaining control over appointment questions and private information. Different tasks require different permissions: booking a ride, spending an agreed amount, receiving an arrival update and speaking during a clinical visit are not a single bundle.

A care plan should identify who does what, the recipient's preferences, access requirements, resources, backup and a completion signal. A handoff is not complete because an offer was sent. The receiver must accept the relevant responsibility, have what is needed and know how to report completion or a problem. Unclaimed work remains unclaimed. Do not make essential care disappear from the plan when a caregiver reaches a limit.

Sustainability includes time, rest, cost and emotional burden. An honest capacity limit is useful information for planning. It does not justify an unsafe abandonment, and devotion does not make unlimited labor feasible. Use appropriate services or authorized backup when needs exceed the arrangement. Clinical deterioration, urgent risk and unclear authority belong with appropriate professional or emergency support, not with an improvised learning task.

## Worked example

A relative agrees to arrange an accessible ride to an existing appointment. The appointment is attended, but no clinical findings are shared. The transport task can be completed while the health question remains private or unresolved. If the relative also starts answering every question against the person's preference, the timely ride does not cancel that loss of agency. Usefulness and respect must be reviewed separately.

## Supplied fictional task

Maren, an adult receiving established support, has a booked appointment at 10:30 on 12 October. Maren wants to ask two private questions personally and wants helper Eli to arrange transport only. Maren permits Eli to receive arrival updates and use an agreed transport budget of 40 units. Eli has no authority to read clinical records, change medication, manage finances generally or speak for Maren at the visit. Maren uses an established communication aid and wants it available.

The regular authorized provider offers accessible pickup at 09:40, a 25-minute journey and a 10-unit fare each way. The check-in target is 10:15. Maren wants the return arranged through the same provider after the visit, not a presumed fixed clinical duration. Eli has forty minutes available for planning and handoff, not an entire day. If pickup has not arrived by 09:45, the agreed route is to call the provider and designated care coordinator for a safe authorized alternative; no alternative has yet been confirmed. Do not invent a safe vehicle or manual transfer technique.

## Make the plan operational

1. Confirm the task, actual authority and Maren's preferences. Separate permission to arrange transport, spend up to 40, receive logistics updates and accompany or speak at the visit. Only the first three are supplied. Output: a private logistics card with no clinical details copied into the learning record.
2. Calculate arrival from 09:40 plus 25 minutes and compare it with the 10:15 target. Calculate the expected round-trip fare and remaining budget. Include communication-aid availability without assuming Eli should operate it for Maren. Output: a feasible ordinary plan with time and cost margins, not a clinical promise.
3. Write the missed-pickup trigger and contact route. A call requests an alternative; it does not prove one exists. Identify who must accept any replacement handoff and how Maren's preference will be heard. Output: backup status, including anything unconfirmed, with no unsafe substitute invented.
4. Explain the emotional and advocacy part modestly: ask whether Maren wants help preparing a question list or reassurance about logistics, accept a no, and preserve Maren's voice. Set Eli's capacity limit so other needed tasks receive a named accepted owner. Output: welcome support and bounded responsibilities, not a takeover.
5. Save the card before opening the [later packet](later-packet.md). Trace actual pickup, arrival, return and expense. Check whether Maren's choices were respected and whether any work remains unowned. Output: completed logistics, remaining questions and a sustainability revision.

## Adapt and transfer

For support, use a one-page card with three headings: person’s preference, accepted task and problem contact. A familiar accessible format is preferable to a complex dashboard. For a harder fictional case, let the provider miss the trigger and require an actual safe handoff or an honest statement that transport remains unresolved; do not make a late promise into a completed ride.

For an authorized real attempt, choose an ordinary task already allowed by the care arrangement. Confirm preferences, permissions and backup before acting. Ask afterward whether the help was useful or controlling, and record only minimal task-level evidence. Do not change medication, feeding, transfers, clinical decisions or financial control through this exercise. If the person's needs exceed the arrangement, obtain suitable qualified assistance and preserve continuity rather than hiding the gap.

## Scope and evidence boundary

Medical, reproductive, child-safety, guardianship, and elder-care decisions require qualified local guidance.

This is role-conditional learning. Fiction is preparation, not actual participant evidence. Keep private identifying, intimate, medical and family details out of app records.
