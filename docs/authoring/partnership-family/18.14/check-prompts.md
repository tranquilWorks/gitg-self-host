# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. One partner says “maybe someday,” but clarifies they are unsure whether they ever want children. Is it accurate to record a shared plan to parent later?
2. A person agrees to research public consultation costs. Does that grant access to their partner's medical records or authority to select treatment?
3. A consultation is attended, but the packet gives no clinician findings. Can a learner infer fertility or a guaranteed future pregnancy from attendance?
