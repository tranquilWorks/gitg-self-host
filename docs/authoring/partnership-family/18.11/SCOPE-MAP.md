# 18.11 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Commitment, fidelity, and trust

**Scope (unchanged):** Define exclusivity, loyalty, privacy, transparency, temptation, and repair before ambiguity becomes betrayal.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Behavior matches the promises and boundaries the relationship actually established.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exclusivity, loyalty and actual promises | Explicit/assumed/needs-discussion columns | Invitation considered before booking | Attraction is not conduct; no covert test |
| Privacy and transparency | Material truth without passwords or message archive | Bounded disclosure and account-access limit | Privacy cannot hide a material breach; fear not automatic authority |
| Temptation and repair | Choose before contrary action; prior breach cannot be relabeled | Declined invitation consistent with existing agreement | Future change cannot erase past conduct |
| Trustworthy follow-through | Notification done, expansion unaccepted, review pending | One actual opportunity versus longitudinal reliability | No restored-trust claim when no prior breach supplied |
