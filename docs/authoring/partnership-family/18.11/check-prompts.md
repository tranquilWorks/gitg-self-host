# Fresh checks

Attempt independently before [answers](check-answers.md).

1. Someone breaks an exclusivity promise, then says the relationship is now nonexclusive so there was no betrayal. What distinction is missing?
2. A person feels attracted to someone else but declines an opportunity that would breach their agreement. Is attraction alone evidence of broken fidelity?
3. A partner demands continuous location access as the only acceptable proof of repair. Is that automatically justified by a previous breach?
