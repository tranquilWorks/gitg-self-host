# 19.01 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Family systems and intergenerational patterns

**Scope (unchanged):** Recognize roles, alliances, inherited beliefs, trauma, loyalty, estrangement, and repetition across generations.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Preserves what is good and interrupts a harmful pattern with proportionate boundaries or repair.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
