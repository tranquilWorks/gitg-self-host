# 18.13 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Relationship diversity and chosen form

**Scope (unchanged):** Understand that people may live in different lawful and consensual relationship structures, orientations, or single lives while still owing honesty and dignity.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can state personal commitments without dehumanizing people whose consensual arrangements differ.
