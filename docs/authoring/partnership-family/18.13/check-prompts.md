# Fresh checks

Write a reasoned answer, then [compare](check-answers.md).

1. A person's orientation differs from yours. What can you infer about whether their relationship is exclusive?
2. Someone pressures a reluctant partner to accept a new structure, calling refusal intolerant. Does respect for diversity require agreement?
3. Can a learner honestly choose only an exclusive partnership for themselves while respecting a friend who remains single or chooses another consensual arrangement?
