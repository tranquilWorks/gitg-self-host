# Individual author editorial review

Reviewed 24 September 2026 for this source-only publication. This is author editorial review, not independent review. Each of the twelve guides, twelve separate later packets, 36 fresh prompts/corrective answers and canonical maps was reviewed for individual scope, depth, usability, distinctiveness, source limits and missing human evidence. Shared headings and boundary wording do not replace distinct teaching and tasks.

Word counts describe saved text; they are not a quality threshold or proof of learning. Literal repetition checks and source regressions support integrity, not pedagogy. Formal M6K A/B/C, independent cold-start, actual learner, qualified and owner content acceptance remain pending. No participant response, professional endorsement or independent receipt is fabricated.

## Retained quality decisions and limits

Review retained all twelve partial or disconfirming outcomes. In particular, a contact trial does not repair a dinner breach; a successful meal retains an overrun; restitution does not prove prevention; withdrawing a threat does not instantly restore free consent; a booking does not answer a health question; a proposed rule is not accepted; an ending does not settle the lease; a fictional reader is not an actual friend; reproductive wishes remain distinct; a family-route recurrence remains visible; reduced prompts under changed conditions do not establish independence; and attended transport does not establish a medical result.

No material author-level scope or case defect was found in this review. That is a bounded editorial judgment, not a full-domain pass. Every map preserves the exact role-conditional classification, domain-specific preferred evidence types, minimum measurement rule and professional boundary. These are retained metadata, not new runtime scoring instructions. Voluntary adult, no-contact, no-attempt, authorized-care and privacy boundaries are substantive parts of the tasks.

All 105 earlier additional companions and their 707 source tests remain protected. This batch adds no legacy companion. The first full run found one overbroad new test assertion: it required the word “authorized” for family-pattern observation as well as live parenting/caregiving. The new test was corrected to require controlled own action and optional family contact for 19.01, while retaining explicit care authority checks for 19.02–19.03. No protected earlier test or teaching text was changed to satisfy a misplaced keyword. Automated results are recorded separately in [verification](verification.json); they do not close qualified review, actual transfer or formal acceptance.
