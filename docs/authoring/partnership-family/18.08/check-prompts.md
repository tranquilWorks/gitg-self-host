# Fresh checks

Try first, then [read answers](check-answers.md).

1. Someone apologizes clearly, but the same agreed notification fails at the next comparable event. What can and cannot be credited?
2. A person accepts a friendly gesture but says they are not ready to forgive. Does accepting the gesture commit them to reconciliation?
3. One partner threatens retaliation if the other will not attend a repair meeting. Should the learner improve the meeting script and insist both take responsibility?
