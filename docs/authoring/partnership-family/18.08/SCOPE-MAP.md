# 18.08 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Partnership conflict and repair

**Scope (unchanged):** Manage recurring disagreements, escalation, bids for connection, apology, forgiveness, and behavioral change.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** A rupture receives concrete repair or a clear recognition that repair is not occurring.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recurring disagreements and escalation | Specific station event, accusation/defense and pause | Rupture account, return time and no forced equal blame | Abuse is not a mutual communication exercise |
| Bids for connection, apology and forgiveness | Tea accepted while rupture remains; distinct choices | Accountability and accepted restitution separated | Kindness does not purchase forgiveness or reconciliation |
| Repair and behavioral change | Next comparable shared-trip opportunity | Notification sent but confirmation missing | Less harm does not prove agreement followed |
| Recognize absent repair | Recurrence preserved and changed reliance considered | Narrow conclusion and proportionate next decision | One apology cannot reset history or restore all trust |
