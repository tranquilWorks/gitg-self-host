# 18.12 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Separation, divorce, estrangement, and endings

**Scope (unchanged):** End or restructure relationships with honesty, safety, grief, legal responsibility, and protection of dependents.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Avoids unnecessary cruelty, ambiguity, retaliation, and denial of real obligations.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Separation, divorce, estrangement and ending | Emotional decision versus jurisdictional processes | Clear statement and nine-area responsibility map | No forced direct contact or compulsory reconciliation |
| Honesty, safety and grief | Suitable medium, private support and no false reunion promise | Email route and support/information action | No retaliatory exposure, monitoring or safety guarantee |
| Legal responsibility and dependents | Lease/table unresolved; care earlier than logistics meeting | Accepted care owner and completed visit | No unilateral asset deprivation, care withdrawal or order changes |
| Avoid cruelty, ambiguity and denied obligations | Inquiry/acknowledgment/resolution distinctions | Future care and property remain open | No completed legal ending or grief resolution inferred |
