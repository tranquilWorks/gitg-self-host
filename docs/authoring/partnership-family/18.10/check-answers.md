# Corrective answers

1. A boundary does not require a diagnosis to be valid. Accept the refusal without bargaining or demanding disclosure. Medical support may be appropriate if the person wants help with symptoms, but it is not the price of saying no.
2. Pregnancy prevention and infection prevention are distinct. Ask what protection and testing considerations are relevant, using current qualified health guidance. Do not infer coverage, prescribe a method or promise absence of risk from the fact that contraception is used.
3. No. Booking establishes an appointment, not attendance or a clinical result. A proposed preference is not an agreed or tried practice. Record each actual status and wait for relevant evidence, preserving privacy and optional participation.
