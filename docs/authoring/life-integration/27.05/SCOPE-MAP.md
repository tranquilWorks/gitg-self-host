# 27.05 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Personal operating reviews

**Scope (unchanged):** Review commitments, health, relationships, money, learning, work, service, and direction at sustainable intervals.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Reviews produce decisions, scheduled actions, and removed obligations.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commitments, health, relationships, money, learning, work, service and direction | Eight complete dispositions with explicit unknown | Bounded scan and chosen decisions | No comprehensive health/financial assessment |
| Decisions, scheduled actions and removed obligations | Late form, welcome call and genuinely solo project | Receipts, actual call and legitimate removal | Deleting a reminder does not satisfy reliance |
| Sustainable intervals | Two reviews and one subsequent information action | 27-minute burden and still-open cost | Unreviewed is not satisfactory |

Preferred evidence types: artifact, observer_feedback, longitudinal_review, outcome_indicator. This is preserved metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.
