# 27.05 — Personal operating reviews

## Let the review end in fewer loose ends

A review is useful when it changes what happens next. It is easy to spend the whole period rearranging a task list and leave every commitment untouched. The purpose is a modest decision cycle: notice a real loose end, choose a proportionate action, schedule something that needs remembering and release one genuinely optional demand.

Use the complete fictional review file. Allow two fifteen-minute reviews one week apart in a live route; for the supplied case, allow twenty minutes to inspect both records and ten for checks. [NASA's technical-assessment discussion, S02](../SOURCES.md#S02) distinguishes reported status from analysis that informs corrective decisions. The application here is an analogy to personal coordination, not a NASA-approved life assessment.

Worked example: a person marks an overdue form “urgent” at three consecutive reviews but never opens its normal submission route. A five-minute submission with receipt changes the situation; a brighter label does not. Conversely, deleting the form would remove a reminder, not fulfill the obligation.

## Review one: October 6, 18:00–18:15

Ari uses eight headings and only the supplied ordinary records. The file contains no clinical findings, account credentials or other people's private data.

| Heading | Disposition | Available fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commitments | Needs action | An ordinary club form was due October 5; standard late submission is still accepted |
| Health | Stable for coordination only | Existing care appointments remain scheduled; no clinical status inferred |
| Relationships | Needs action | A welcome invitation awaits a reply by October 9 |
| Money | Uncertain | October transport cost is not yet confirmed; no purchase needed now |
| Learning | Needs action | An optional solo reading challenge no longer fits |
| Work | Stable | Next agreed deadline is already in the work calendar |
| Service | Not applicable now | No current service promise or dependent task is supplied |
| Direction | Stable | Keep existing care and ordinary work reliable this week |

The club form takes five minutes and has a standard receipt screen. The invitation requires a two-minute response and proposes a welcome ten-minute call October 10 at 19:00. Ari may accept or decline; do not infer a meeting from the incoming invitation. The reading challenge is solo, free and carries no promise to another person. Removing it does not require inventing a cancellation agreement.

Within the first fifteen minutes, use three to scan, five to submit the form, two to reply, two to schedule any accepted call with a reminder, one to remove the optional challenge and two to close the review. If a task takes longer, preserve the limit by scheduling it honestly; do not fill a late form with guesses to meet the timer. The uncertain transport cost belongs in an information request, not an invented satisfactory result.

## Close the loop next week

1. Give all eight headings a disposition using only available facts. State what “stable” means here: no new coordination issue identified, not a comprehensive health or financial clearance. Select the overdue form, the invitation and the optional challenge as the concrete decisions.
2. Save the first review and inspect the [later packet](later-packet.md). Distinguish submitted, acknowledged, scheduled, performed and removed. Check whether anyone actually relied on the released item.
3. At the second review, compare those decisions with outcomes and the review's actual burden. Keep an unresolved cost question open and choose no more than one next information action. Simplify the format if the administration crowds out the activity it was meant to support.

## Sustainable does not mean every heading is always green

A voice checklist, accessible reminder or supported conversation is suitable. During a constrained period, scan urgent safety and binding duties first; label omitted areas unreviewed rather than stable. For a harder case, the supposedly solo challenge includes a promised reading-group summary. Pause only after an appropriate notice or handoff; deleting its reminder is not release from reliance.

At a later real weekly review, use minimal private information, one performed action and one subsequent check. Qualified questions stay with the relevant professional or process. The useful evidence is an actual decision and follow-through with a manageable review burden, not eight optimistic labels or a newly elaborate personal dashboard.

## Use the result at its proper scale

Capacity, access and context govern actual attempts. Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Human dignity is never scored.
