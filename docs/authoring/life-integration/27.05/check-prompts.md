# 27.05 — Fresh checks

Attempt these before opening [answers](check-answers.md).

1. Two reviews take 15 and 12 minutes. A ten-minute call occurs between them. What is review time, and is the call included?

2. A care heading is marked stable because appointments are scheduled. Does that establish good health?

3. Deleting a task that someone else relied on makes a list shorter. What is still needed?
