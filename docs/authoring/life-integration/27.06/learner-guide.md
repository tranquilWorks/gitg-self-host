# 27.06 — Goals as experiments

## Test the pursuit without putting your identity on trial

A goal can become difficult to revise when it stands for who you are supposed to be. An experiment asks a smaller question: under these conditions, does a particular way of pursuing it produce something useful at a tolerable cost? The original hypothesis needs to survive beside the result so that adaptation does not become retrospective self-congratulation.

Allow twenty minutes to design the supplied trial, fifteen to inspect its later record and ten for checks. A live version would include three fifteen-minute sessions over two weeks. [IHI's Model for Improvement, S03](../SOURCES.md#S03) describes small tests, observation and adaptation. This original educational case applies that limited idea outside clinical work; it is not a controlled study or a treatment experiment.

Worked example: a learner tries early-morning writing and discovers the period regularly collides with necessary transport. Moving the session tests a method; it need not mean the learner lacks seriousness. Pretending all three planned mornings occurred would prevent useful learning about the constraint.

## Complete explanation trial E2

The fictional pursuit is learning to explain an unfamiliar simple filing system. The original hypothesis is: “Three fifteen-minute evening sessions will fit my present week and let a willing reader use the labels correctly.” The learning question is whether the explanation makes the classification rule usable, not whether the reader thinks the author is talented.

**Complete source card.** A community table uses three temporary trays. BLUE is for a card that needs a reply. GREEN is for a card needing no reply but containing information worth keeping for this event. GREY is for a duplicate with no new information. Apply the rules in this order: exact duplicate first, then reply needed, then keep information. The tray names have no meaning outside this invented system. No real records or private messages are used.

**Practice cards.** A asks “Can you confirm the room?” B says “Doors open at 18:00; no reply needed.” C is an exact second copy of B. Correct classifications are A BLUE, B GREEN and C GREY. The lesson must explain the precedence rather than simply supply these answers.

The trial runs November 1–14. Sessions are planned for days 2, 6 and 10, with a maximum of fifteen minutes each. A willing adult reader has agreed to one separate five-minute written check on day 12, with a right to decline. Continue at the same level only if all three sessions fit and the reader correctly applies all three rules without instructional prompting. Adapt if access/scheduling or a rule remains unclear. Stop or defer if repeated conflict with essential duties makes the trial unsuitable. These are local decision conditions, not learning thresholds validated for everyone.

## Preserve the comparison

1. Copy the hypothesis, dates, assumptions, process measure and learning question. Define the effort limit separately from the reader check: 45 session minutes plus at most five for the check. Name what this tiny trial cannot answer, including long-term interest or public communication skill.
2. Draft a short explanation from the complete source card. Save it and the decision conditions before opening the [later packet](later-packet.md). Record actual sessions and any alteration; do not count planned time as exposure to the material.
3. Apply the prewritten continue/adapt/stop conditions to the observations. Keep confusion and missing sessions visible. Choose a bounded next design, not an identity verdict. A solo rehearsal remains preparation if the reader declines.

## Change one feature without hiding why

For support, use speech, accessible text or a reader; distinguish an access aid from a hint that supplies the classification. A harder case adds a duplicate that itself asks a question. The supplied precedence still makes it GREY; explain why. Do not introduce a fourth rule midway and score old responses against it silently.

Later transfer can test one other safe pursuit with its own observable output and effort cap. Major career, relationship, financial or medical choices require more evidence and appropriate support than a fortnight's trial. Curiosity, mixed results and stopping all remain legitimate. The result is a reasoned next experiment, not a certificate of talent or a duty to continue.

## Use the result at its proper scale

Capacity, access and context govern actual attempts. Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Human dignity is never scored.
