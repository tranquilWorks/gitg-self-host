# 27.12 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Annual reckoning and recommitment

**Scope (unchanged):** Review who was becoming, what was served, where harm occurred, what changed, and what the next season requires.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Produces an honest annual account and a limited set of renewed commitments.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who becoming, served, harm, change and received | Eight dated records under five reflective headings | Honest account with four unknown months | No total-character judgment or coerced confession |
| What the next season requires | Reduced evening capacity and optional newsletter release | One repair offer, one release, one renewed commitment | Real obligations and safe contact remain protected |
| Limited recommitment | January first action and February review distinction | Performed shelf check and unresolved repair | No annual outcome inferred from one action |

Preferred evidence types: artifact, observer_feedback, longitudinal_review, outcome_indicator. This is preserved metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.
