# 27.13 — Applicability, role choice, and the right to leave domains unchosen

## Choose roles without ranking lives

Some responsibilities arise from being human in relationship with others; particular life roles are often optional. Marriage, parenthood, leadership, entrepreneurship and a particular religious practice are not a universal checklist. A person may be exploring, may consciously decline or may find a role irrelevant to the present decision without becoming deficient. At the same time, an existing dependent, an agreement or a legal duty cannot be made to disappear by changing a label.

Allow twenty minutes for the supplied fictional choice and five for a later planning check. The normative distinction comes from the canonical competency, not from a legal opinion or a personality test. Edinburgh's reflection sequence [S06](../SOURCES.md#S06) supports examining a decision and naming a next action; it does not validate which life path someone ought to choose. No disclosure of intimate reasons, health information, family conflict or religious belief is required.

## Five role cards and one real decision

Avery's supplied cards read:

| Optional role | Current disposition | Existing responsibility relevant here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marriage | Consciously unchosen at present | No spouse or marriage agreement is supplied. |
| Parenthood | Not relevant to this planning decision | No child or dependent is supplied. |
| Formal leadership | Exploring | Avery has promised one ten-minute team briefing, regardless of future title. |
| Entrepreneurship | Consciously declined | Avery still owes ordinary agreed work as an employee. |
| A particular religious practice | Not chosen | Other people's freedom and dignity remain relevant; no belief declaration is required. |

“Unchosen” can be stable or revisable; “exploring” is not a promise to accept. These are Avery's supplied dispositions, not defaults for the learner. If a learner already holds a role, record “held” and identify its actual responsibilities rather than automatically using Avery's card.

The immediate planning choice is whether to enroll in a voluntary start-a-business workshop. It costs two free Saturday mornings; nobody has paid for Avery, and there is no commitment or dependency on attendance. Avery does not want to start a business. The goods Avery does endorse are useful work, generosity and responsible contribution. Employment, craft and freely chosen community activity can express these goods without entrepreneurship. This equivalence concerns the underlying goods, not every practical function or legal consequence of a business.

A worked private statement is: “I am declining entrepreneurship at present. I will keep my agreed work and the ten-minute briefing. I do not need the workshop to prove initiative.” It is deliberately brief. A person may choose not to give this statement to anyone. If someone pressures Avery, the safe response can be a private planning change; confrontation is not required.

## Make the choice visible in a plan

1. Read each card and separate the optional role from duties already arising from actual circumstances. Mark held, exploring, consciously unchosen or not relevant; include unknown when facts are insufficient. Do not infer a child's needs, legal duties or freedom to withdraw from silence in a real case.
2. For the workshop choice, name the good underneath the role and two possible expressions that do not require it. Check whether declining is an endorsed preference, a current constraint, uncertainty or pressure. Those distinctions can matter without making any answer a moral score. Choose one feasible next planning action.
3. Save the role statement and proposed calendar change before the [later packet](later-packet.md). Compare the actual decision with the statement. Keep the briefing and work obligations visible; declining a title does not cancel an existing promise. Leave a review point only if Avery wants one, rather than periodically testing whether Avery has adopted the socially expected path.

## Applicability is not an escape clause or a deficit label

For support, work with the supplied cards without personal disclosure, use a trusted interpreter or write only “not chosen.” Without enough facts, leave a role undecided. For a harder case, someone wants to decline parenthood while already responsible for a dependent: separate future role preference from current care duties and seek appropriate local guidance where needed. This guide supplies no legal determination.

Later, apply the same distinction to a different optional role such as committee chair. An honest choice may be yes, no, exploration or no decision. A good outcome is a plan consistent with endorsed commitments and actual obligations, not convergence on Avery's life. Calling a role “chosen” does not by itself establish freedom from coercion.

## Use the result at its proper scale

Capacity, access and context govern actual attempts. Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Human dignity is never scored.
