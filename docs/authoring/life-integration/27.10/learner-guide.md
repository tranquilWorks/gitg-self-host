# 27.10 — Mentorship and generativity

## Help the learner need your explanation less

Mentorship combines receiving correction with making capability available to someone else. The evidence is what the learner can do with suitable access, not how generous the teacher feels or how closely the learner repeats the teacher's words. Two contacts can reveal useful learning and limits; they cannot establish a lifetime of generativity.

Allow fifteen minutes for source learning and preparation, two fifteen-minute learner contacts over two weeks in a live route, and ten minutes for review. The default supplies fictional contacts. [CDC's communication guidance, S04](../SOURCES.md#S04) emphasizes audience, main message and action. Studying that source is not receiving personal mentorship. The notice-checking method and responses below are original and are not a CDC assessment.

Worked example: an experienced organizer suggests checking preparation as well as date and place. The novice teacher initially overlooked that part. Keeping the correction with attribution, then allowing a learner to try independently, preserves knowledge better than performing all checks on the learner's behalf.

## Complete notice set and teaching materials

The agreed skill is extracting three practical facts from a small event notice: date/time, location and required preparation. A suitable next step follows from those facts. There is no hidden answer based on typography or cultural knowledge.

**Baseline notice A:** “Paper folding workshop: November 6, 14:00, Room L. Bring one sheet of scrap paper.” Fictional adult learner Rae freely agrees to practice. Rae initially identifies November 6 at 14:00 and Room L but says nothing is needed. That is a missed preparation fact, not a judgment about intelligence.

**Demonstration notice B:** “Map-reading discussion: November 9, 16:00, Room M. Read the supplied one-paragraph route description first.” The entire route description is: “The entrance is south of the hall. The reading room is east of the hall. Use the hall corridor; the west storage room is private.” There is no actual trip. Demonstrate circling or speaking each of the three facts and then naming the preparation action; explain why location alone would not prepare the attendee.

**First try notice C:** “Drawing exchange: November 11, 15:00, Room N. Bring one existing drawing or a verbal description; no new work required.” Rae may use text-to-speech or another normal access aid. The teacher may ask whether help is wanted but must record a content hint if one is given.

**Reminder to leave:** “When is it? Where is it? What must I prepare? If a fact is missing, ask the organizer rather than guess.” Rae controls whether to keep or change this reminder. No public name, recording or gratitude is requested. Both agree that a second check can occur within two weeks and either may decline.

## Receive, demonstrate, then observe without taking over

1. Record the source lesson about clear action and the preparation fact missed at baseline. Label source study accurately; the worked adviser example is not an actual adviser event. Choose help that addresses the observed gap rather than giving a long generic lecture.
2. Demonstrate B, then let Rae try C through the chosen mode. Preserve an answer before offering correction. Save this sequence before the [later packet](later-packet.md), which supplies the actual fictional responses and fresh follow-up notice.
3. At follow-up distinguish independent use with ordinary access aids, success after an instructional hint, unresolved difficulty and no contact. Ask what helped or intruded only if welcome. Update the reminder together and name something the teacher learned from Rae.

## Capability belongs to the learner

For support, use AAC, interpretation, accessible text or a harmless familiar skill you can actually teach. Without a willing learner, rehearse the reminder alone and label transfer untested. Teaching regulated tasks or working with minors/vulnerable adults requires the setting's proper qualifications, consent and safeguarding; this source case supplies none.

For a harder notice, one required fact is absent. Asking the responsible organizer is competent handling of uncertainty, not failure to memorize. At later actual contacts, use new materials and a suitable delay; avoid making the learner depend on your approval to apply the method. A preserved reminder and corrected teacher assumption can support future opportunity, but only real sustained follow-through could substantiate wider generativity.

## Use the result at its proper scale

Capacity, access and context govern actual attempts. Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Human dignity is never scored.
