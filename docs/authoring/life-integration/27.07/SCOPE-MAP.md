# 27.07 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Transition preparedness

**Scope (unchanged):** Anticipate changes in work, location, partnership, parenthood, health, aging, dependency, and loss.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Creates practical and emotional preparation for at least one foreseeable transition.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Work, location, partnership, parenthood, health, aging, dependency and loss | Multiple transition contexts with one complete schedule case | Known change and practical/emotional dependencies | No presumed life pathway or clinical timeline |
| Practical and emotional preparation | Transport verification, whole-day rehearsal and goodbye | Confirmed main route, unresolved fallback and materials | No invented future meetings |
| Foreseeable transition readiness | Before/after check dates and delay conflict | Readiness distinct from lived first day | No transition competence from planning alone |

Read the [learner guide](learner-guide.md), preserve the initial attempt before the [later packet](later-packet.md), and answer [fresh checks](check-prompts.md) before [corrective answers](check-answers.md). See [sources](../SOURCES.md) and [individual review](../QUALITY-REVIEW.md).

Canonical classification: applicability cross_context_core; normative status cross_tradition_core_or_broadly_recurrent; formation modes: agency, receptivity, interdependence, stewardship.

The canonical record has no professional_boundary field; its absence is preserved. Adaptations, fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Actual transfer requires suitable access, current conditions, willing participation and appropriate authority. No private participant records are required.

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: artifact, observer_feedback, longitudinal_review, outcome_indicator. This is preserved metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.

No runtime selection, action, evidence field, score, completion or participant record changes. Formal M6K A/B/C, independent cold-start, actual learner, qualified and owner content acceptance remain pending. Author review is not an acceptance receipt.
