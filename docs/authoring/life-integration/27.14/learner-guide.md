# 27.14 — Multiple pathways to flourishing

## Preserve the purpose while changing the route

A standard practice may assume mobility, speech, a particular schedule, family form, cultural custom or faith. Those assumptions can obscure the capability the practice was intended to develop. An equivalent pathway preserves a defensible underlying purpose and an accepted way to demonstrate it while changing unnecessary demands. It need not resemble the standard route. Nor does merely calling an alternative equivalent make it meet every qualification, safety rule, consent requirement or genuine outcome.

Use this fictional community planning case in twenty minutes, then inspect the supplied later response. No diagnosis or private conscience statement is required. W3C's accessible-events guidance [S05](../SOURCES.md#S05) supports accessible information and participation modes. The three purpose checks below are original teaching criteria; they are not WCAG conformance, legal accommodation approval or a validated capability measure.

## A complete agenda with more than one participation mode

The standard suggestion says, “Attend the evening meeting and speak aloud.” The actual bounded purpose is to understand one shared issue, account for another affected perspective and contribute a relevant question through an accepted route. Live spoken dialogue is a different additional capability and is not required for these three checks.

**Agenda item:** A fictional shared reading room has one quiet corner and one activity table. The proposal is to move the activity table beside the quiet corner every Wednesday from 17:00 to 18:00 for a craft session. No vote or decision has yet occurred.

**Complete supplied notes:** Existing quiet-corner users need low noise to read or process text. Craft participants need a usable table and conversation to explain their work. A coordinator says the far wall also has space for the activity table, but its lighting has not been checked. The existing clear access route must remain clear. The coordinator accepts relevant written questions received in the message book by Tuesday at 16:00, will acknowledge receipt and will include accepted questions in the Wednesday agenda. Acknowledgment does not promise a particular answer or decision.

Fictional participant Ellis cannot use the evening spoken route in this case and chooses accessible text. The reason may remain private. An authorized supporter may transcribe Ellis's chosen words without replacing Ellis's viewpoint. A remote route is not assumed available: it requires confirmation. A written route is explicitly accepted here, so it can meet the contribution check when the question actually reaches that route.

A worked draft states: “The proposal would place conversational activity beside the quiet area on Wednesdays. Craft users need a table, while quiet-corner users need lower noise. Could the far-wall lighting be checked before choosing the table location, while preserving the access route?” This identifies the issue, includes another perspective and asks something relevant. The unsent draft satisfies preparation only; the contribution check remains open until the authorized channel receives it.

## Define equivalence before judging performance

1. Separate the purpose from attendance and speech. Write three checks: accurately identify the proposed change; fairly account for an affected perspective beyond one's own; submit a relevant question through the accepted route. Do not add eloquence, stamina, a diagnosis or agreement with the proposal as hidden conditions.
2. Choose the accessible text route, use the complete notes and make an original question or adapt the worked draft. Record who selected the substance if a supporter transcribes it. Check the Tuesday deadline and accepted channel. Save the draft and expected evidence before reading the [later packet](later-packet.md).
3. Compare actual receipt and agenda inclusion with the three checks. If the channel rejects a suitable contribution, record a barrier and seek a workable authorized alternative if welcome; do not reinterpret exclusion as incapability. If the question itself misstates the proposal, correct that substantive error. Route access and content quality are related but separate issues.

## Alternatives keep both dignity and honest limits

For support, use AAC, an accessible summary containing all supplied facts, interpretation or transcription under the participant's control. Without a confirmed receiving route, label the work preparation and leave contribution untested. For a harder case, a qualification specifically requires a safe live demonstration: negotiate a legitimate adapted demonstration with the responsible body; a worksheet cannot self-authorize equivalence.

At a later real opportunity, test another mode that fits the person's culture, faith, vocation, disability, family circumstances and actual setting. Do not assume every person in a group wants the same adaptation. This one case shows how the bounded purpose can be met; it does not measure all flourishing, establish live dialogue skill or prove the eventual room decision fair.
