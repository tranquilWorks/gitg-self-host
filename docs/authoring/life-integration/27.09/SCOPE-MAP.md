# 27.09 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Coherence without rigidity

**Scope (unchanged):** Integrate principles into a stable identity while remaining capable of revision, surprise, and growth.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can change a method or belief without losing the larger moral direction of life.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coherence and flexibility | Principle, old method and both prior observations | Four-part comparison with original view preserved | No convenience-based excuse |
| Evidence, context and legitimate surprise | Agreed ordinary window and earlier decision exception | One timely answer and one late substantive answer | Acknowledgement is not fulfillment |
| Stable good and revisable belief | Access-based challenge to reliability and pending repair | Provisional method with unmeasured interruption benefit | No fabricated successful retest |

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: artifact, observer_feedback, longitudinal_review, outcome_indicator. This is preserved metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.
