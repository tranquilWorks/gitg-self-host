A complete audit does not mean assigning seven positive diagnoses.

## Check A

1. **Confirmation:** only favorable information is inspected. Read the current access notice and state the access finding that would disqualify the preferred venue.
2. **Availability:** vividness substitutes for an appropriate comparison. Check which features of the earlier event differ and inspect the current session's capacity and booking information. The old story may remain relevant to a specified risk; it does not automatically describe this event.
3. **Anchoring:** the headline continues to control an estimate despite a task decomposition. Sum the five non-overlapping 5-minute steps: 25 minutes. Compare that result with the headline and identify whether any steps actually overlap. Do not claim the estimate was never exposed to the anchor.
4. **Sunk cost:** irrecoverable effort drives further commitment without a future benefit or obligation. Compare remaining workload and suitability, keeping the 80 past minutes in the history record, not as a future cost.
5. **Overconfidence:** certainty exceeds the supplied coverage. Identify plausible disruption conditions, obtain proportionate information, and state a contingency rather than inventing a precise probability or insisting on certainty.
6. **Tribal reasoning:** the evidence standard changes with group identity. Apply the same access-confirmation requirement after swapping irrelevant labels. Preserve any genuine relevant differences if additional evidence later supplies them.
7. **Self-serving bias:** equivalent constraints receive different judgments according to whose conduct is evaluated. Reverse the roles while holding those constraints fixed and state a consistent standard or a relevant difference that would justify different treatment.

Other labels may overlap, but an acceptable answer must explain the described mechanism and perform or specify a matching check. A name without a procedural change is incomplete.

A familiar organizer's documented reliability can matter. Past work can produce a reusable asset or create a real future obligation. The packet removes those differences to isolate the exercise; real decisions must inspect them rather than mechanically erase them.

Outside this closed packet, actual current access, a changed schedule, personal capacity, or a real commitment could matter. Name such a fact as a future verification need, not an invented problem that overwrites the supplied fictional stipulations.

**Repair:** If you selected Y solely to show that you “overcame bias,” return to the prespecified criteria. If you selected X without calculations or a countercheck, add the missing procedure rather than treating the correct answer as sufficient evidence of how it was reached.

## Evidence boundary

A fictional choice supplies a rehearsal artifact. The canonical real-choice indicator remains untested until an actual appropriate decision is recorded and its procedure is changed accordingly. A before-and-after choice alone is not causal evidence that this checklist reduced bias. Independent learner, specialist where needed, and formal owner acceptance are still separate.
