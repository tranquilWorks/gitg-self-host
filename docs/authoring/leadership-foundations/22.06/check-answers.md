# Corrective answers

1. A specific communication gap or unclear instruction to investigate. Ask about the reasoning; do not infer laziness, low intelligence or a fixed learning style.
2. Make the changed room prominent, preserve the other correct facts and remove obsolete instructions. Copying the demonstration's surface wording is not transfer.
3. Independent generation on that case with the stated aid. Do not call it unaided, durable mastery, recipient success or evidence from an actual learner.
