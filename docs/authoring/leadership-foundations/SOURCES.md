# Inspected sources and bounded uses

Inspected on 24 September 2026. Nine resources support the narrow uses below. The teaching designs, organizations, governing cards, figures and responses are original supplied fiction, not source case studies or actual participant evidence.

## S01

[Civil Service Leadership Statement](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/civil-service-leadership-statement/civil-service-leadership-statement) — UK Civil Service; updated 25 August 2016.

Inspected: Statement characteristics: modeled conduct, clear direction, candid communication, objectives and authority. Used for 22.01, 22.02, 22.05: Relate expectations to visible leadership conduct and a usable direction.

Limit: Historical statement of institutional expectations, not a validated leadership scale. Linked videos, wallet cards, training and self-assessments were not inspected.

## S02

[OECD Public Integrity Handbook: Leadership](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-public-integrity-handbook_ac8ed8e8-en/full-report/leadership_1aa35fa2.html) — OECD; 20 May 2020.

Inspected: Chapter 6 abstract, 6.1 and opening 6.2: support, selection, incentives and accountability for integrity leadership. Used for 22.03, 22.09: Examine organizational support and systems around use of authority.

Limit: Selected chapter passages only; no full handbook, underlying studies, country framework or validated causal effect. Administrative public-sector context is not a universal employment or governance rule.

## S03

[Government Functional Standard GovS 002: Project delivery (HTML)](https://projectdelivery.gov.uk/library-products/government-functional-standard-govs-002-project-delivery/) — Government Project Delivery; version 2.1.

Inspected: 4.1 governance limits and autonomy, 4.3 decisions, 4.4.1 role definition, 7.12 stakeholder engagement and 7.13 communications. Used for 22.02, 22.04, 22.05, 22.10: Use explicit decision rights, timely options, relevant expertise and updated stakeholder understanding.

Limit: Selected sections only, not the full standard, Teal Book, investment rules or linked analyses. The original low-stakes exercises do not establish government-standard conformance or optimal decisions.

## S04

Limit: Brief agency framing only; general benefits are not adopted as measured outcomes or validation of the exercise. No coach certification, career guarantee or actual learner evidence.

## S09

[Identifying and managing conflicts of interest in a charity](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/identifying-and-managing-conflicts-of-interest-in-a-charity-cc29/conflicts-of-interest-a-guide-for-charity-trustees) — Charity Commission for England and Wales; updated 22 April 2026.
