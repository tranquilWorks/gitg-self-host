# 22.10 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Organizational politics and stakeholder navigation

**Scope (unchanged):** Understand interests, coalitions, status, informal authority, and legitimate negotiation without corruption or naïveté.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Achieves a defensible objective while preserving trust and principles.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Interests, status and informal influence | Formal scheduler/room roles and respected member | Evidence map without invented coalition | Relevant volunteered constraints only |
| Legitimate negotiation and coalitions | Two consultation records, accepted setup and clearance | Transparent benefits, burdens, timing and consistent terms | No private favor or incompatible promise |
| Defensible objective, trust and principles | Two-date authorized trial and retained disagreement | Ten-minute exact clearance; review date and temporary status | Approval is not consensus, trust or observed access benefit |
