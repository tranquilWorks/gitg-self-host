**Canonical competency:** Direction, vision, and priorities

**Scope (unchanged):** Define a worthwhile future, explain why it matters, and translate it into limited priorities.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** People understand both destination and immediate focus.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
