# 22.07 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Accountability and standards

**Scope (unchanged):** Set clear expectations, observe performance, recognize success, confront shortfalls, and follow through.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Standards are consistent, humane, and connected to purpose.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Expectations and observation | Prior agreed criteria, two current-looking inputs and actual draft | Met criteria and superseded time separated | No motive or character finding |
