# 22.12 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Succession and institutional stewardship

**Scope (unchanged):** Develop replacements, distribute knowledge, document governance, and reduce dependence on one person.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** The group remains capable and principled after leadership changes.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Develop replacements and distribute knowledge | Formal appointment, willing backup, shared G4 and commitments | Usable pack, current decisions, rationale and permitted access | Documentation cannot substitute for appointment |
| Document governance and reduce dependence | Ordinary agenda, two-current-approval rule and review route | Assisted E1, fresh E2 deferred properly, valid new format | Former leader intervention stays visible |
| Institutional capability and principles | Accepted upkeep, role notice and remaining channel dependency | No purchase; notice access transfer pending | One small transition is not institution-wide resilience |

Canonical classification: applicability role_conditional; normative status role_conditional; formation modes: agency, stewardship, interdependence.

The canonical record has no professional_boundary field. Practical safeguards do not add canonical metadata. Leadership is role-conditional and not compulsory. Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Actual work requires authority, willing participation, privacy and appropriate qualified support; do not manufacture dependence, conflict or emergencies.

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: observer_feedback, artifact, outcome_indicator, longitudinal_review. This is preserved metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.

No runtime selection, action, evidence field, score, completion or participant record changes. Supplied fiction is not actual observer evidence. Formal M6K A/B/C, independent cold-start, actual learner, qualified and owner content acceptance remain pending; author review is not an acceptance receipt.
