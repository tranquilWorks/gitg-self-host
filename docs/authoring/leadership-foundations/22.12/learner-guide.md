# 22.12 — Succession and institutional stewardship

Prepare a successor to run a limited planning decision using shared knowledge, legitimate authority and an exception route. Allow twenty minutes for the transition pack and twenty to review its two tabletop attempts. The goal is a group that can act without hidden dependence on one person. A folder full of documents is useful only if successors can understand, access and legitimately use what it contains.

## Identify what the institution would lose

Start with decisions, relationships, records and exceptions that would stall if the current leader were absent. Some dependencies are technical, such as access to an editable agenda. Others are social or constitutional: people may still ask the former leader for approval, or the role may require a formal appointment. A password copied into a handover document is neither a safe access transfer nor a legitimate appointment.

Prioritize critical knowledge rather than copying everything. Include the purpose of the role, current commitments, the governing principles and why a rule exists. A successor who knows only the former leader's habits cannot adapt when the case changes. Explain which practices are preferences and which preserve safety, fairness, authority or continuity. Retain relevant rationale without exposing private personnel information or unnecessary historical disputes.

Develop more than a nominal replacement. Use the legitimate selection process, ask whether the person wants the role and identify the preparation they need. A willing backup reduces one kind of dependence, but the backup also needs access and appropriate authority. Do not assume that attendance at a walkthrough establishes readiness. Give a bounded ordinary decision and a harmless exception to reveal what the pack leaves implicit.

Distribute knowledge through usable resources and relationships. A named role contact is often more durable than “ask me.” Shared editable records, version status, review triggers and accepted maintenance ownership make the transition easier to sustain. Actual account access should follow organizational procedures with individual authorization, not shared credentials or copied secrets. Authority to read a record does not necessarily include authority to approve the action it describes.

During the successor attempt, observe without hidden coaching. Let a different valid method stand if it respects the governing principles. If you intervene, record it and why; the result is then assisted. Stop for a real boundary violation rather than preserving a clean-looking assessment. Independence is not demonstrated by letting someone make an unauthorized consequential decision for the sake of the exercise.

Use the exception to check governance. Who may change a commitment, resolve a conflict or approve spending? Which decisions must wait when an authority is absent? A successor may show sound stewardship by deferring a decision and using the legitimate route. Completing every agenda item is not always the right outcome. Keep open issues visible so an orderly meeting does not disguise unresolved authority.

Close the transition by confirming the role owner, backup, record maintainer and review process. Let the group know where decisions now sit and how to challenge an error. Review which dependency remains: perhaps the former leader still owns a notice account or a second approver is unavailable. One successful low-stakes transition supports a narrow capability claim, not assurance that the institution can survive any departure or crisis.

## Worked example

A departing chair hands over last month's agenda. The successor copies a spending approval from it into today's minutes, assuming it is precedent. The real rule requires two current approvals. A better pack explains that the old minute records a past authorization, not a standing permission, and supplies the route for a new request. Sound succession preserves principles while allowing a new chair to choose a different agenda style.

## Complete fictional transition pack

The Paper Survey Circle's purpose is to compare public maps and decide small preparation tasks. The council formally appoints **Neri** as facilitator for **December 2026**, with willing backup **Ames**. Outgoing facilitator **Ro** provides the pack. Neri and Ames have accepted their roles. This is a fictional appointment, not one you should make in a real organization.

Governing card **G4**, current as of **26 November**, states: a facilitator may order agenda items, keep the meeting within **30 minutes**, and record voluntarily accepted task owners and dates. The facilitator may not change the group's purpose, spend funds or approve public notices alone. Any spending needs two named current council approvals before a commitment. This protects shared resources; a prior purchase is not standing permission. Questions or challenges to a decision go to the council's shared review role, **Review Desk**. Review Desk is a role label, not a private address.

The shared editable paper record **CIRCLE-RECORD** contains G4, the agenda and commitment log. Neri and Ames both have access. Agenda minima are: current commitments **5 minutes**, compare two layouts **10**, agree one next task **10**, recap/open issues **5**. The total is thirty. The order may vary except that comparison must precede choosing its follow-up task, and recap is last.

Current commitments are: Jo checks the public legend text by **29 November**; Lee prepares two layout cards by **30 November**. Both have accepted. The comparison cards say **A: date at top, location below** and **B: location at top, date below**. Neither is selected. The normal decision is to choose one card for a readability revision and obtain an owner/date, not authorize publication. Neri may use a table instead of Ro's paragraph minutes.

The supplied exception **E1** asks for **12 fictional units** of printing. Only council member Tavi has approved; council member Uma has not. The correct current status is pending second approval. If Uma is unavailable, defer the spending commitment and ask Review Desk for the next authorized route. Neri may still conduct the comparison. No actual purchase should occur.

Known access dependency: the group's real-world analogue would use an individually authorized notice account, but in this fiction only Ro currently controls the notice channel. Neri cannot yet send an official notice. The proposed maintenance rule is a G4/access/commitments review at each role transition and on a rule change; an owner must accept it. No passwords or private contact information belong in your output.

## Transfer, observe and repair

1. Map authority, knowledge, relationships and access dependencies. Identify purpose, rules, rationale, commitments and limits. Output: a transition pack that distinguishes appointment from documentation.
2. Plan a walkthrough of the ordinary agenda and E1. Let Neri choose a valid format without taking back the role. Output: explicit decision rights and a usable exception route.
3. Save before the [later packet](later-packet.md). Classify the first attempt and the fresh E2 attempt by actual help and authority. Output: repaired guidance, remaining dependency and a bounded successor result.
4. Confirm owner, backup, maintenance acceptance, review and challenge route. Output: a transition status the group could use, with unresolved notice access retained rather than declared solved.

## Support and further challenge

Readable cards, a checklist, an interpreter or extra preparation time can remain part of competent facilitation. A harder case removes both current and backup facilitators: identify the legitimate appointment route and defer decisions that lack authority. For actual use follow access, records, governance and professional requirements. Do not treat retaining the former leader as an informal shadow approver as completed succession.

## Scope and evidence boundary

The canonical record has no professional_boundary field. These practical safeguards do not add canonical metadata. Applicability and normative status both remain role_conditional. Taking a leadership role is not compulsory. Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Use actual authority and willing participation; keep protected records outside app evidence. Human dignity is never scored.
