# 09.08 — Answers and corrective guidance

## Check A — Key

A measurable failure is notebook unavailable at the chosen work start in the recorded context. Label clarity and work location are distinct proposed contributors. The intervention changes both label and location pattern. Four subsequent available desk opportunities show availability in those observed conditions; they do not isolate the label's effect. The baseline already contained successful desk days.

A discriminating plan could observe naturally occurring desk and away opportunities with context recorded, or use a harmless spare notebook in a reversible rehearsal. A planned comparison would vary label while keeping a comparable location, task, and opportunity definition, and separately examine the location effect. It must not remove a needed aid or engineer a disruptive loss.

In the separate follow-up, availability matches location across the supplied combinations and does not differ by label within either location. That pattern favors investigating the two-location handoff rather than crediting the label. With only two opportunities per cell and no broader sampling or assignment details, it does not establish a universal causal law or prove labels ineffective elsewhere.

**Repair:** Preserve the successful change but narrow the attribution: “Availability improved after changing the routine; the available comparison cannot attribute that improvement to the label alone.” Then specify a safe, genuinely discriminating next observation.

After: incorrectly routed 0/20 = 0%; correctly completed 8/20 = 40%; held 12/20 = 60%.

Avoiding wrong routes is a real improvement in that metric. Correct completion nevertheless falls from 70% to 40% in the supplied groups. Appropriate containment is not completed service. Ask why required information is missing, whether the rule requires it appropriately, and how it can be obtained without unsafe guessing or hidden burden.

The later group reaches 20/20 correctly completed with no wrong routes or holds under its reported conditions. It supplies a further verification result for the combined corrected rule and feed. It does not prove a permanent zero-error rate, eliminate all future failure modes, or establish a unique cause independently of those conditions.

**Repair:** Report all three outcome categories with their denominators. State the improved mechanism, remaining bottleneck, and fresh verification result separately. Do not enlarge the success numerator by counting appropriate holds as delivered records.
