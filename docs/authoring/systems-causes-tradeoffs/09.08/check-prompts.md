# 09.08 — Fresh checks

These fictional records require no experiment on a real person or their belongings. Save your answers before opening the keys.

## Check A — A convenient fix and a confounded comparison

A person's notebook is available on two recorded desk-work days and unavailable on two recorded away-work days. They add a bright return label and also stop working away from the desk. On the next four desk-work days it is available. They conclude, “The label fixed the cause.” No other observations are supplied.

Define the failure and at least two explanations. Explain which variables changed together, what the before/after comparison establishes, and what it does not. Propose a harmless observation or controlled comparison that could distinguish the label explanation from the work-location explanation. Do not deliberately make an essential item unavailable to create a failure.

For a separate fictional follow-up, the record contains two opportunities for each combination: no label/desk 2 available of 2; label/desk 2 of 2; no label/away 0 of 2; label/away 0 of 2. State the narrow pattern this supports and why it does not prove that labels never help anyone.

## Check B — Zero errors can hide incomplete service

Before a change, 20 fictional records include 14 correctly completed, 6 incorrectly routed, and 0 held. After a change, another 20 include 8 correctly completed, 0 incorrectly routed, and 12 held for missing information. No records are missing, and the held records have not been completed.

Calculate the incorrect-routing rate, completed-correctly rate, and held rate in both groups. Evaluate the announcement “We fixed the process: zero wrong routes.” Distinguish what improved from what remains unsatisfactory and name an upstream question.

A later verification group under a corrected feed and corrected rule has 20 correctly completed, 0 wrongly routed, and 0 held. What additional evidence does that supply? What future reliability or unique-cause claim would still exceed these three fictional summaries?
