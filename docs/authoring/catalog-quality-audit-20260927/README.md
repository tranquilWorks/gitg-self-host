The 310 follow-ups are **not 310 equally severe instructional failures**. In the editorial notes, 45 concern learner copy only and 21 concern presentation/progression or unperformed acceptance evidence. The other 244 have substantive follow-up questions of varying severity, including missing materials, scope or pathway coverage, answer separation and evidence interpretation. These categories summarize note content; they are not a validated severity scale. Repeated calculations may be useful consolidation. They become an assessment problem when presented as unseen independent transfer.

## Findings to act on first

- **10.03:** five overlap pairs involve eight of twelve notices, leaving four nonoverlapping notices; the packet says seven.
- **10.11:** the two-lens key identifies society as uninvestigated despite selecting social access, and omits science from the remaining-field list.
- **10.09, 19.10, 21.04, 21.11, 21.13 and 21.18:** prior answers or packet ordering undermine the proposed fresh attempt. The exact circumstances differ and are recorded individually.
- **Promised materials:** earlier fictional alternatives and specific later cases omit facts needed to execute the task. Examples include 13.04's repair guide, 17.14's inventory and 19.12's headcount/preferences/prices.
- **Learner presentation:** move internal schema, review and compatibility material into author records, while keeping useful plain-language access, consent and task limits.

[Findings and repair order](FINDINGS.md) preserve exact source anchors and the original five-correction regression review. The original 21.03 briefs and 10.01 delayed prompt are materially repaired; 12.05 and 12.08 now have broader pathways. Other defects remain, including compatibility language elsewhere in 10.02. Neither repairs nor passing tests establish professional acceptance.
