# 14.04 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Cybersecurity and account resilience

**Scope (unchanged):** Use strong authentication, updates, backups, encryption, recovery plans, and safe device practices.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can recover access or data and demonstrates reduced exposure to common compromise paths.



| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strong authentication | Factor categories, phishing boundary, Postbox AND/OR routes | Method choice and dependency trace under phone loss | No secret disclosure or claim every MFA method is phishing-resistant |
| Updates and safe device use | Trusted laptop status; unexpected prompt/session case | Official independent incident route and retained working access | No penetration testing, unauthorized account or forced lockout |
| Backups and encryption | Sample backup and unknown key location; device-loss variation | Separate data availability from usable decryption and access | Successful login is not full-device restoration or compromise repair |
| Recovery plans | Vault, phone, sessions, paper code and consumed-code branch | Complete prerequisite graph; current unused method status | Circular dependencies and consumed codes cannot count as available |
| Demonstrated reduced exposure | Original versus changed route; real supported improvement | Prepared/tested/paper-available statuses and remaining gaps | No guarantee against attack or fabricated actual disaster recovery |

Use the [learner guide](learner-guide.md), save the initial output before the [later packet](later-packet.md), then attempt [fresh checks](check-prompts.md) before the [corrective key](check-answers.md). The default has a complete fictional route; practical adaptations, harder cases and later transfer are in each guide. See [source/claim dispositions](../SOURCES.md).

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: performance_task, artifact, behavioral_adherence, longitudinal_review. This inherited scoring-oriented statement is preserved as curriculum metadata, not a new source-exercise scoring rule. Subjective experience and pain reports remain legitimate; the statement is not a criterion for believing or treating a person's report. Human dignity is never scored.

This additional source companion does not change compiler selection, canonical runtime packages, stable IDs, actions, observations, completion, scoring or participant records. Authoring and author editorial review are separate from independent cold-start, actual learner, qualified and owner acceptance, all of which remain pending. A produced paper/file artifact demonstrates only the work actually attempted; supplied later outcomes remain simulation. This map is not a formal M6K A/B/C receipt.
