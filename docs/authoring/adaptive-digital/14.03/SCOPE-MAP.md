# 14.03 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Digital identity, privacy, and data rights

**Scope (unchanged):** Understand data collection, tracking, identity linkage, consent, platform policies, and the persistence of online records.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can audit major accounts and change settings, sharing, or services to match stated privacy priorities.



| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Collection and tracking | Supplied/observed/inferred vocabulary; P1/P4 | Five-item data-flow ledger with policy references | No specific inference system assumed from a general concept |
| Identity linkage | Recovery email, contact list, nickname and old caption | Separate provider identity from public nickname/location | A pseudonym or hidden field does not establish anonymity |
| Consent, policies and data rights | Purpose limits, complete fictional P1–P6 and scoped request | Chosen changes, tradeoffs, request scope and actual status | Real rights depend on law; no fictional deadline generalized |
| Persistence and recipients | Old caption, downloaded drawing, linked-app harder case | Distinct hosted result, provider confirmation and unknown copies | Revocation/removal is not universal erasure |
| Audit and actual changes | Saved settings/preview records, later real account route | Verify changed controls against original priority | Fictional observation is not a live account audit |
