## Retrieval limitations

NICE NG193 recommendations and its full guideline PDF returned HTTP403, as did the NINDS pain page, NHS Scotland referred-pain pathway and CISA Secure Our World. The CISA ransomware guide and an initially guessed NCSC password-manager path failed to return readable content; an initially guessed FTC strong-password URL was inaccessible. The attempted NCI referred-pain dictionary URL also returned no usable content. Those pages are not claimed inspected or used as primary support. MedlinePlus, NIAMS/NHS and the accessible NCSC/FTC/NIST pages supply the stated limits instead. A search result marked the CISA campaign archived, so no current-program status was inferred. The FTC privacy URL redirected to the current collection-and-use article. No paywall or access control was bypassed.
