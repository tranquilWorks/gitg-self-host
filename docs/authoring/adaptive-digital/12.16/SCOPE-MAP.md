# 12.16 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Pain literacy and safe response

**Scope (unchanged):** Distinguish soreness, acute injury, chronic pain, referred symptoms, red flags, and fear-driven avoidance.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Uses appropriate load modification and professional evaluation rather than reckless persistence or total withdrawal.

Health and exercise content must be adapted to the person and does not replace licensed medical care.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Soreness and acute injury | A/B comparison and observation-first note | Separate a possible explanation from sudden functional loss | No diagnosis from timing or a pain number |
| Chronic pain and fear-driven avoidance | Kit plan and activity concern; harder same-number comparison | Check plan applicability and ask an activity/review question | Neither dismiss pain nor force movement or indefinite withdrawal |
| Referred symptoms | Concept explanation and third fresh check | Record location without declaring the tissue/source | No speculative diagnosis of an unseen cause |
| Red flags and changed route | C2 emergency combination; explicit non-exhaustive boundary | Emergency next action, with contact still unperformed | Not a screening/triage certification; real emergencies bypass exercise |
| Load modification and professional evaluation | Proportionate response rehearsal and later contact records | Preserve request, acknowledgement, attendance and advice separately | No prescription, medication change or calendar-only return |

Use the [learner guide](learner-guide.md), save the initial output before the [later packet](later-packet.md), then attempt [fresh checks](check-prompts.md) before the [corrective key](check-answers.md). The default has a complete fictional route; practical adaptations, harder cases and later transfer are in each guide. See [source/claim dispositions](../SOURCES.md).

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: objective_indicator, behavioral_adherence, qualified_assessment, longitudinal_review. This inherited scoring-oriented statement is preserved as curriculum metadata, not a new source-exercise scoring rule. Subjective experience and pain reports remain legitimate; the statement is not a criterion for believing or treating a person's report. Human dignity is never scored.

Authoring and author editorial review are separate from independent cold-start, actual learner, qualified and owner acceptance, all of which remain pending.
