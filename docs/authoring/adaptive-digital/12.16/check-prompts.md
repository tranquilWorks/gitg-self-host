# Fresh checks — pain literacy

1. A person says a new sharp symptom must be harmless because it is only “3 out of 10.” They have not checked onset, functional change or associated symptoms. Write a better first response without diagnosing the problem or offering a movement test.
2. A person has a familiar chronic back-pain plan. Today they report back pain and new difficulty controlling their bladder. A routine clinician message has been drafted, not sent. What should happen next, and what is the honest current action status?
3. A learner's note says, “I requested an appointment, so the injury was assessed; the pain is felt at the shoulder, so its cause is definitely in the shoulder.” Correct both conclusions and name the facts a useful handoff would retain.
