# 12.15 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Adaptive movement and fitness

**Scope (unchanged):** Modify physical activity for disability, chronic illness, pregnancy, aging, injury, environment, and changing capacity.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Maintains safe, meaningful movement using qualified guidance and individualized progression.

Health and exercise content must be adapted to the person and does not replace licensed medical care.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Disability and chronic illness | Purpose/task/load/conditions distinction; Morgan support card | Current-plan adaptation card with required aids and help | No capacity judgment from diagnosis, no automatic increases |
| Pregnancy and postpartum | Specific changed-context card and care questions | Activity/load/change request with unresolved decision marked | No inherited case dose or universal clearance |
| Aging and injury | Transfer support card; excluded-movement card | Confirmed assistance map and specific substitute question | Age alone is not ability; excluded movement stays unavailable |
| Environment and changing capacity | Arrival, indoor setting, helper absence, new-symptom records | Feasible schedule; actual versus deferred bouts and later response | Weather, recovery and delayed effects can change fit |
| Qualified guidance and progression | Existing stipulated plan, harder shortened window, later real route | Preserve no-increase rule; resolve specific constraint before reuse | Paper planning is not movement, tolerance or qualified acceptance |

Use the [learner guide](learner-guide.md), save the initial output before the [later packet](later-packet.md), then attempt [fresh checks](check-prompts.md) before the [corrective key](check-answers.md). The default has a complete fictional route; practical adaptations, harder cases and later transfer are in each guide. See [source/claim dispositions](../SOURCES.md).

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: objective_indicator, behavioral_adherence, qualified_assessment, longitudinal_review. This inherited scoring-oriented statement is preserved as curriculum metadata, not a new source-exercise scoring rule. Subjective experience and pain reports remain legitimate; the statement is not a criterion for believing or treating a person's report. Human dignity is never scored.

This additional source companion does not change compiler selection, canonical runtime packages, stable IDs, actions, observations, completion, scoring or participant records. Authoring and author editorial review are separate from independent cold-start, actual learner, qualified and owner acceptance, all of which remain pending. A produced paper/file artifact demonstrates only the work actually attempted; supplied later outcomes remain simulation. This map is not a formal M6K A/B/C receipt.
