# Fresh checks — adaptive movement

Use the guide's concepts, without copying Morgan's schedule. Explain each decision before opening [the corrective key](check-answers.md).

1. A fictional established plan has two four-minute bouts with three minutes rest, five minutes setup and two minutes closing. Confirmed arrival is 10:00 and pickup is 10:17. Does the complete session fit? What must be resolved without silently changing the plan?
2. An adult uses their usual movement aid. A new instructor offers the same activity without the aid to “prove independence”; the person's plan requires it. The adult wants to participate. Write a response and a workable next action that preserves the activity's purpose.
3. Two sessions were planned. One was completed within the person's plan; the second was cancelled because its required support was unavailable. The first session's next-day response is missing. A report claims two successful sessions and readiness to increase duration. Correct the report and identify the next evidence needed.
