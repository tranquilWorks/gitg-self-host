# Corrective key

1. Demand is 50 minutes; shortfall is five. After the twenty-minute urgent case, 25 minutes remain, allowing two complete ordinary reviews with five minutes left and one ten-minute review pending. A five-minute arithmetic shortfall does not mean half a case can be declared reviewed.
2. Provide a reachable alternative that does not require the unavailable session, proportionate ownership checks, keyboard-accessible controls and a staffed receiving process. Test the actual banned-user route. A written deadline alone repairs neither access nor capacity.
3. Accountability and abuse prevention are plausible aims, but elimination is an unsupported empirical claim. Compare proportionate anti-abuse controls, pseudonymous participation, restricted case-specific evidence and justified retention limits. Actual law and safety requirements need contextual review; indefinite surveillance should not be assumed necessary by definition.
