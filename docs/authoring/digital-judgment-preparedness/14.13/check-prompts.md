# Fresh checks

1. One moderator has 45 minutes. Three ordinary reviews take ten minutes each and one urgent case takes twenty. What is the shortfall, and how many full ordinary reviews fit after the urgent case?
2. A policy promises appeal within a day, but banned users can access the form only after signing in. What concrete repairs and verification are needed?
3. A platform claims public legal names will eliminate abuse and proposes retaining every private message forever. Separate the intended benefit, evidence need and privacy alternative.
