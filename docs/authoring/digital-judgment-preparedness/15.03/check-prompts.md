# Fresh checks

1. A diversion sign points to a stairway, but you require level access. A second corridor is unverified. Which route is confirmed suitable, and what is a proportionate next step?
2. A stranger looks around at signs. A trolley blocks the passage. Which observation justifies a functional response, and what should the response address?
3. One visitor records no relevant change on a second visit. Another did not make the second visit. Are these the same evidence? What if staff suggested an alternative but nobody tested or confirmed its suitability?
