# Corrective key

1. Neither alternative is yet confirmed suitable: the stairs fail the requirement and the other route is unknown. Ask staff for a verified suitable path or reachable service alternative. Do not enter a closed corridor or assume a generic diversion meets every access need.
2. The blocked passage supports asking staff for a clear appropriate route or reporting the obstruction. Looking at signs does not establish harmful intent. Address movement/access without monitoring or confronting the stranger.
3. No. The first is an actual observation of no relevant change; the second is an absent observation. A suggested route without suitability/result evidence remains unverified. Keep observation, advice, attempt and successful access as separate statuses.
