# 15.03 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Situational awareness

**Scope (unchanged):** Observe environment, exits, people, hazards, anomalies, and changes without living in paranoia.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Notices relevant risk early while remaining socially and emotionally functional.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Environment, exits, people and hazards | Entrance/help/exits, queue, unknown exit suitability | One bounded orientation and fact/interpretation split | No profiling, surveillance or emergency-exit certification |
| Anomalies and changes | East closure, stepped west diversion and unknown garden route | Same-landmark comparison and staff question | Absence of a visit not observation of no change |
| Notice relevant risk while functioning normally | Level-route requirement, desk pickup and return to reading | Action/result table and proportionate stop/help thresholds | Ordinary route success not all-exit suitability; distress ends/reduces exercise |

Use the [learner guide](learner-guide.md), preserve the initial work before the [later packet](later-packet.md), and attempt [fresh checks](check-prompts.md) before the [corrective key](check-answers.md). Each guide includes its own supported, harder and real-transfer route. See [source dispositions](../SOURCES.md).

Canonical classification: applicability `context_contingent`; normative status `universally_relevant_as_literacy_but_conditionally_practiced`; formation modes: agency, interdependence, stewardship.

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: certification, performance_task, artifact, observer_feedback. This is preserved curriculum metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule or a test of a person's credibility. Human dignity is never scored.

Canonical professional boundary (unchanged): Hands-on safety, medical, aquatic, survival, and defensive skills require qualified instruction and compliance with local law.
