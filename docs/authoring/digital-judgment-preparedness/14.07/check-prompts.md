# Fresh checks

1. Source: “Applications close Friday; places are limited.” Draft: “Everyone who applies Friday gets a place.” Classify the deadline and guarantee separately, then repair the sentence.
2. A generated citation resolves to a real report, but the cited page discusses another town. What has been verified, and what remains unsupported?
3. An assisted draft takes two minutes plus nine minutes checking/correction; manual drafting and review take seven. It also includes a private attendee list in its prompt. Decide whether to use this workflow and whether a disclosure note cures the input problem.
