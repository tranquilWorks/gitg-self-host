# Corrective key

1. No. Suitable permission for the use and correct credit are separate requirements. A voluntary donation does not alter license terms. Obtain a separate applicable grant or use a suitable replacement; do not equate payment with ownership.
2. Restore the required copyright and permission notice with the substantial copied component; preserve the applicable license text and disclaimers as required. An author's name alone is insufficient. Review that component's actual version and any other included licenses rather than applying a generic image-credit rule.
3. An actual grant covering the new distribution/reuse is needed unless another valid legal basis applies. The stated single-use agreement does not supply it. Hold the new use or replace the asset while clarification is pending. Do not claim a broader grant because the invoice was paid.
