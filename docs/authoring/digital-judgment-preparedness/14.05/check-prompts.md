# Fresh checks

1. A caller knows the last four digits of an account and supplies a second number for “independent security.” What does that knowledge establish, and what route would you use?
2. A genuine renewal is visible in an existing portal, while a text demands a gift-card payment. Decide separately about the renewal and payment instruction.
3. A person sent 120, reported it and received a case number. An unsolicited recovery agent promises a guaranteed refund for 25. What amount is confirmed recovered, and what should happen next?
