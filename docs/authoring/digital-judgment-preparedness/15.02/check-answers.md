# Corrective key

1. Information retrieval under the stated constraint succeeded. Network availability, reaching the person and agreed assistance were not tested by that retrieval. Keep those as separate statuses rather than calling the whole communication plan proved.
2. No. Receipt of a question is not an agreed care plan, and assignment is not helper consent. Obtain the relevant provider arrangement and a willing, suitable support route. Do not guess battery duration or improvise a physical transfer to make the plan appear complete.
3. No. Hazard-specific instructions must be reconciled with the actual immediate danger, building fire plan and emergency guidance. A shelter instruction for one weather scenario does not authorize remaining in fire danger. Follow the actual safe response and seek emergency help as needed; never create either hazard for rehearsal.
