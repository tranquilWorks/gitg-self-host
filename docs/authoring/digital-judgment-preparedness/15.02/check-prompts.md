# Fresh checks

1. A paper contact card is retrieved during an outage rehearsal, but no call is made. What succeeded and what remains untested?
2. A provider acknowledges a request about powered medical equipment; a neighbor has been named as backup without consent. May the care and assistance gaps be closed?
3. A household's severe-weather plan says shelter indoors. A separate fire alarm occurs. Should the shelter instruction automatically govern? What sources and constraints should determine the response?
