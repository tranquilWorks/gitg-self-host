# 14.10 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Online communication and community conduct

**Scope (unchanged):** Apply consent, privacy, context, permanence, moderation, and de-escalation in online spaces.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Participates without harassment, pile-ons, deceptive identity use, or careless disclosure.
