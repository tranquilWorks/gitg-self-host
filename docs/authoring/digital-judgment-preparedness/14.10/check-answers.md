# Corrective key

1. Cropping does not create consent or guarantee anonymity. Ask a neutral public version question, seek specific permission where appropriate, or rely on a public source. If you chose the screenshot because it was relevant, separate relevance from publication authority.
2. For example: “The version 4 manual you linked supports your setting. I used the version 3 manual and overstated its scope. My earlier statement should have specified version 3.” Preserve the source and correct the exact error rather than saying vaguely that “mistakes were made.”
3. The problems are evading disengagement through a deceptive account and coordinating repeated pressure. A permitted pseudonym is different from manufacturing identities to bypass boundaries. Stop the repeated contact; a refusal alone is not a reason to punish the user.
