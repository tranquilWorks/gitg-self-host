# 15.01 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** First aid, CPR, and emergency response

**Scope (unchanged):** Learn bleeding control, CPR/AED, choking, shock, burns, poisoning, and emergency communication.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Completes recognized training or demonstrates procedures accurately.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bleeding control, CPR and AED | Topic concepts, immediate help, B/C equipment-based training scope | Topic-by-topic training selection and receipt audit | Paper work not hands-on competence; actual emergency help never delayed |
| Choking, shock, burns and poisoning | Distinct concepts and professional topic sources; C scenario instruction | Missing/assessed knowledge and practical-skill statuses | No forceful healthy-person drill; age/context limits; no improvised treatment |
| Emergency communication | Location, entrance, condition, callback placeholder and delegation | Two actual communication rehearsals if performed; hazard branch | No emergency test call; asked-to-fetch not AED used |
| Recognized training or accurate procedure demonstration | Online A versus supervised B/C; fictional receipt | Only adult CPR/AED satisfactory observed topics in receipt | Actual learner/provider assessment pending; enrollment and certificate title not broad proof |

Canonical classification: applicability `context_contingent`; normative status `universally_relevant_as_literacy_but_conditionally_practiced`; formation modes: agency, interdependence, stewardship.

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: certification, performance_task, artifact, observer_feedback. This is preserved curriculum metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule or a test of a person's credibility. Human dignity is never scored.

Canonical professional boundary (unchanged): Hands-on safety, medical, aquatic, survival, and defensive skills require qualified instruction and compliance with local law.
