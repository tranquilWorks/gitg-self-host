# Corrective key

1. The sample is concentrated in one source group, with dependent reposts. A broader and repeated sample, other routes used and evidence about selection would be needed to assess narrowed exposure and its causes. Eight cards are not eight independent perspectives.
2. Two observed sessions, 14 minutes, mean seven minutes, one met endpoint. Dividing by three incorrectly gives an absent opportunity a performed duration. If a stop time were missing instead, preserve duration as unknown.
3. Only the setting change is established. The full purpose and deliberate stop failed. Add a checked-source endpoint and a direct route for this question, then observe another ordinary attempt. Avoid claims about compulsion, model retraining or universal benefit from this small case.
