# 23.06 — Insurance, estate, and dependent protection

Make a continuity map for a fictional adult who is unavailable for one month. Use two twenty-minute sessions and one bounded official-source inquiry. Your output is a map of locations, roles and unanswered questions, not a will, power of attorney, coverage recommendation or legal determination.

## A helpful contact may still lack authority

Continuity requires more than a folder of documents. The right person must be able to locate relevant information, understand the next step and have the authority that step requires. Being trusted, related, named as an emergency contact or designated as a beneficiary does not automatically authorize every action. A person may be able to notify a provider of a concern without being entitled to withdraw funds or make a dependent's decisions.

The CFPB's [financial-help explanation, S12](../SOURCES.md#S12) distinguishes arrangements such as a trusted contact from authority to act on money. That official source answers one narrow role question in this exercise: a trusted contact is not automatically a bill-paying agent. Any financial power needs its actual scope, effective conditions and local validity checked. This guide does not draft or execute a power or resolve disputed capacity.

A beneficiary designation concerns a benefit under its governing terms; it does not by itself grant spending authority during the policyholder's life. A will concerns an estate after death and should not be treated as a general living financial authority. Insurance is likewise purpose-specific: naming a life policy does not establish coverage for a month of absence, incapacity or lost income. Ask what event is covered, what exclusions and conditions apply, how claims work and who may obtain information.

The NAIC's [beneficiary guidance, S13](../SOURCES.md#S13) supports keeping insurer and policy-location information findable and reviewing beneficiary information when circumstances change. It does not verify the fictional policy's coverage or make an estate plan legally effective. The task is to identify a legitimate next inquiry, not to infer protection from a document title.

## Supplied continuity card C7

Adult **Mara** is unexpectedly unavailable for one month. The cause is unspecified; do not diagnose incapacity or assume death. Mara rents a home, maintains a policy labeled “life insurance,” and coordinates a dependent's scheduled support. Friend **Jo** is willing to help and is listed as a trusted contact only. No financial agency, dependent-care appointment or legal authority for Jo is supplied.

| Map heading | Supplied information | Gap to keep visible |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ongoing obligations | Rent due day 5; policy premium due day 12; dependent support each Tuesday | Payment arrangements and substitute support responsibility are unverified |
| Coverage and claims contact | Insurer contact label INS-SERVICE appears on the neutral contact card | Coverage for absence is not established; claims and exclusions need official clarification |
| Beneficiary questions | A beneficiary form exists in the secure policy folder | Current designation, update date and legal effect have not been checked |
| Document locations | Lease and billing instructions in secure HOME folder; policy in secure POLICY folder; estate file locator in secure LEGAL folder | Only locator labels are shared; contents, credentials and access permissions are not copied |
| Authorized decision roles | Jo may be contacted; legal adviser contact LEGAL-DESK is recorded | No bill-paying or dependent-care authority for Jo is established |

The separate dependent-support schedule identifies service label **CARE-SERVICE**, Tuesday time **15:00**, and a failure consequence: planned assistance may be missed if nobody coordinates with the authorized provider. Its current public contact route is absent from the first map. The rent consequence is an unpaid obligation; the policy consequence could involve coverage continuity under terms that must be checked. Do not announce eviction, cancellation or legal consequences that the supplied facts do not establish.

Mara knows the secure folder locations. Jo has only the neutral locator card and public contact labels, with no login credentials or access to contents. The exercise includes no account numbers, beneficiary names, signatures or private dependent details. A real locator should also be protected appropriately; knowing where sensitive material lives does not authorize entry or unrestricted sharing.

## Worked handoff that stops at the right boundary

An unsafe instruction would be: “Jo is trusted, so use Mara's password and pay everything.” That confuses willingness, access and authority. A bounded rehearsal instead has Jo identify the rent due date, locate the provider's public contact instructions, explain the concern without unnecessary personal detail and ask what authorized process applies. Jo may not impersonate Mara, use credentials or move money merely because a bill is urgent.

The most consequential unresolved question in C7 is how essential payments and dependent support can be authorized during the absence. Use S12 to establish the limited trusted-contact role, then draft an **unsent question** to the appropriate provider or qualified local professional: “What appointment or document would establish this role, when would it take effect, what actions would it permit, and how would you verify or change it?” A source-based question is concrete progress; it is not an appointment or a determination that a particular document is valid.

## Build, rehearse and correct the map

1. Fill all five headings. For each obligation, record timing, consequence, needed record type, secure locator, who knows the locator and who is actually authorized. Use “unknown” when the supplied card does not establish a role.
2. Separate policy coverage, beneficiary designation, estate documents, financial authority and dependent-care authority. Name one unresolved question in each area without assuming one document answers them all.
3. Record the inspected official source and its narrow answer about trusted contacts. Draft the bounded authority inquiry and a separate insurer question about coverage and exclusions for the relevant event. Do not send personal records or sign documents.
4. Rehearse with a fictional Jo: find the next contact, state the permitted inquiry and identify the action that must wait. Try to locate CARE-SERVICE without inventing an address. Save this first handoff before opening the [later packet](later-packet.md).
5. Correct the missing locator or role description shown by the handoff. Assign a review trigger such as a move, changed dependent need, relationship change or policy renewal, and a concrete next information request. Keep drafted, sent, answered and legally effective as distinct statuses.

For support, use large-print location cards or an independent interpreter without sharing secrets. A harder variant makes the willing helper unavailable; identify both an information backup and a legally valid appointment question rather than merely adding another name. Optional actual transfer is an appropriate provider inquiry or professional appointment for a real gap, using secure channels and genuine authorization. No beneficiary change, signature or property transfer is needed to complete learning. Suspected exploitation, coercion or disputed capacity needs qualified independent support. A usable map can reveal that protection is still incomplete.

## Scope and evidence boundary

Canonical professional boundary: Financial education only; tax, investment, insurance, estate, and benefits decisions may require licensed or jurisdiction-specific advice. Applicability remains context_sensitive; normative status remains cross_tradition_core_or_broadly_recurrent. Use fictional or privately redacted figures. Do not upload account identifiers, credentials or private financial records. Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Human dignity is never scored.
