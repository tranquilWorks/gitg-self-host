# 22.16 — Governance, checks, and accountable authority

Design a small decision system that can correct a leader's error while the leader is absent or disagrees. Allow twenty minutes to create the decision and appeal record, then ten to inspect the later test. The aim is a usable allocation of authority rather than confidence in one person's character.

## Give each decision a legitimate home

Decision rights state who may decide what, within which limits. Oversight checks whether those limits are respected. Appeal lets an affected person challenge a decision through a route that can actually change it. Succession keeps these functions available when a role-holder leaves or cannot act. Transparency makes the basis of a decision understandable while protecting information that should remain private. Independent review requires enough separation, competence and authority to resist the original decision-maker's preference.

These elements interact. An appeal address is ineffective if the leader can veto every correction. An independent reviewer without the current rulebook may be free of the conflict but unable to decide. A substitute with access but no appointment does not acquire authority by being helpful. Design the record and the appointment process together, so correction does not depend on a leader voluntarily admitting error.

The Charity Commission's [decision-making guidance, S04](../SOURCES.md#S04) emphasizes powers, governing documents, reasoned records and accountable delegation. It applies to charity trustees in England and Wales. The fictional R2 association rule below is an original low-stakes example, not a legal constitution or claim of compliance with charity law.

## Complete fictional rule R2

The Lantern Association allocates one weekly meeting room. Its members have validly adopted R2 for this exercise. Eligible requests must be for a member activity, fit the room's supplied approved capacity and arrive by noon Monday. Among eligible requests, the one with the earlier recorded submission wins. All supplied requests meet capacity; do not assess real building safety.

Coordinator **Jae** applies R2 and publishes a redacted reason code. Jae may not invent new eligibility restrictions. Clerk **Oli** maintains the request log and rule version. Uninvolved reviewer **Nia**, appointed by the members, may uphold, correct or return a decision for a stated missing fact. An applicant may appeal through Oli within two working days of notification under this fictional process. This is a service rule, not a legal limitation period. Jae cannot veto an appeal correction.

The members also appointed **Ruth** as Nia's substitute for absence or conflict and **Paz** as acting coordinator during Jae's absence. Each has accepted and has access to the current redacted decision pack. Reviewers must disclose relationships to applicants and recuse where R2 requires it. If both review appointees are conflicted or absent, Oli records the block and calls the members' authorized appointment meeting; the coordinator cannot appoint their own appeal judge. The room award stays unresolved until a valid decision can be made.

Rule changes need a members' vote and a stated future effective date. They cannot be silently backdated to make an earlier rejection valid. The clerk reports unresolved appeals and rule deviations at the monthly oversight meeting. Members review the system after two cycles for timeliness, correction and burden. These authorities are supplied facts, not appointments made by the learner.

## The contested allocation

Request A, submitted **Monday 09:00**, is from a new member's reading group. Request B, submitted **Monday 10:00**, is from a long-standing member's craft group. Both meet all R2 criteria. Jae rejects A because “new members must wait three months,” a restriction absent from R2, and awards B. A's applicant asks Oli for review the next working day. There is no misconduct finding or allegation about Jae's motive.

The necessary file contains R2's version and effective date, both submission times, eligibility checks, Jae's stated reason, the appeal request and the reviewer's authority/conflict declaration. Member tenure is included only because it explains the disputed reason; unrelated personal information is unnecessary. Redacted public notice can state the rule and corrected allocation without exposing the full appeal correspondence.

## Worked correction that does not require confession

An unrelated club mistakenly applies a 17:00 deadline when its current rule says 18:00. A reviewer can identify the current rule, compare the timestamp and correct the eligibility judgment even if the chair believes 17:00 would be better. The chair may propose a future rule change through the proper process. Personal agreement with the correction is not a precondition for applying the existing rule.

For Lantern, reason from the same distinction. The first question is whether A was eligible under R2, not whether a waiting period sounds sensible. The second is which eligible request came first. A defensible review addresses both applicants' reliance and timely communication without pretending the earlier award never happened. The record should show the original decision and correction, not erase the error.

## Write and test the governance pack

1. Create a rights table for coordinator, clerk, reviewer, substitutes and members. Include their limits, reporting obligation and source of appointment. Give every exceptional branch an authorized next step or an explicit unresolved status.
2. Produce the appeal file from the supplied facts. State the question, applicable rule, relevant evidence, missing facts if any and conflict check. Do not add character judgments or assume that a newcomer deserves priority for unrelated reasons.
3. Draft the review outcome and a redacted explanation to both applicants. Name who updates the allocation record and who confirms notification. A drafted notice is not a receipt.
4. Test absence, conflict and leader disagreement. Can an authorized substitute obtain the same current pack? Can the leader stop a correction? If all reviewers are unavailable, can the system admit the block without inventing authority?
5. Save your pack before the [later packet](later-packet.md). Compare the actual supplied path with your intended one and identify what remains untested.

For support, draw only the ordinary decision and single appeal first, then add the supplied substitutes. For a harder attempt, make both Nia and Ruth conflicted and specify the member appointment route with an unresolved allocation. Optional actual transfer is an authorized review of a group's public or blank governance procedure. Do not rewrite its constitution, appoint yourself or expose a complainant. A rule can be legible yet inaccessible in practice, so actual review would also need willing users, usable channels and evidence across time. One corrected room decision does not establish institutional accountability.

## Scope and evidence boundary

The canonical record has no professional_boundary field. Practical safeguards do not add canonical metadata. Applicability and normative status remain role_conditional. A leadership role is not compulsory. Use actual authority and willing participation; keep protected records outside app evidence. Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Human dignity is never scored.
