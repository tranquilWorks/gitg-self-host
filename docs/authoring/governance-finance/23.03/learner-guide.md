# 23.03 — Emergency reserves and liquidity

Design a reserve for a particular disruption and test access before calling it useful. Use two twenty-minute sessions, then a brief funding review. A target is a plan, a funded balance is money present, and a tested withdrawal is evidence of access through a particular route. Keep those three claims separate.

## Start with the event and its timing

Emergency money serves a job: making an important payment possible when an unexpected disruption occurs. The needed amount depends on consequences, timing, dependable resources and competing commitments. A fixed number of months can hide these differences. Predictable annual bills also deserve planning, but they are not made unexpected merely by arriving infrequently.

The CFPB's [emergency-fund guide, S07](../SOURCES.md#S07) frames a reserve around unplanned expenses, situation-specific needs, accessibility and replenishment. Its general guidance does not establish an amount suitable for you. Debt obligations, care needs, unstable income, eligibility rules and access barriers may materially change an actual plan. If there is no affordable surplus, record that constraint and consider suitable assistance rather than shifting money away from essentials to make the exercise look successful.

## Supplied disruption choices

The fictional household has **200 of immediately accessible cash** beyond already funded current essentials. It could contribute **50 monthly** only while the stated capacity remains available. The case excludes interest, fees and other movements from the contribution arithmetic. It offers two separate scenarios, not two simultaneous events:

| Event | Need and deadline | Consequence without money | Gap after 200 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essential repair | 600 due within two days of an unexpected failure | Ordinary necessary travel is interrupted | 400 |
| Temporary income gap | 900 needed across the next month | Several essential payments lack cover | 700 |

For the teaching choice, the household ranks the repair as the first reserve purpose because the short deadline and travel dependency leave little adjustment time. That ranking is supplied preference, not a claim that repair is more likely or always more serious. The larger income-gap exposure remains on the map. A known annual subscription of 120, due next year, belongs in a predictable-expense plan and is not included in either emergency target.

## Educator-provided sample disclosure D4

This is complete fictional access material for the exercise, not a real bank offer or insurance certification. Read it as a disclosure with missing terms, not as marketing.

| D4 field | Supplied term |
| --- | --- |
| Storage type | Separate cash savings pot linked to a spending account |
| Standard withdrawal | Request through secure account access; arrives in spending account after two business days |
| Weekend and holiday timing | Processing starts next business day; holidays not specified |
| Immediate cash access | None from this pot |
| Fees and minimums | No monthly fee, no withdrawal fee, no minimum balance in this sample |
| Protection | Label says “protected”; institution, eligible deposit type and scheme conditions are not supplied |
| Access instructions | Locate the provider's withdrawal page, check destination and displayed arrival date, stop before confirmation |

The existing 200 is held separately and immediately accessible. Future contributions are proposed for D4, but no transfer has occurred. The disclosure's two-business-day timing is not the same as two calendar days. If a repair payment is needed on Sunday after a Friday failure, D4 cannot be assumed to deliver money in time. The phrase “protected” supplies no verified insurance basis.

The CFPB's [payment-app explanation, S08](../SOURCES.md#S08) describes why protection can differ across providers and services. In the United States, verify the institution, deposit type and applicable conditions rather than inferring insurance from an app logo or account label. No current provider has been checked in this exercise. Insurance against specified failure is also different from immediate access during an outage.

## Worked funding calculation and its limits

The repair gap is **600 − 200 = 400**. At 50 per contribution it needs **8 monthly contributions**. The income-gap target needs **900 − 200 = 700**, or **14 contributions**. These are counts under unchanged assumptions, not protection already in place or a promise about when a disruption will happen. An event tomorrow arrives before even the first contribution.

The repair purpose permits use for the supplied unexpected essential repair; it does not automatically fund unrelated shopping. After use, review essentials and capacity before replenishing. A funding plan should include a fallback while incomplete: contact an appropriate assistance or hardship service, ask the responsible provider about a feasible arrangement and assess other dependable support. No assistance eligibility, credit approval or payment extension is assumed. Avoid a risky investment or an unverified app balance as a shortcut to guaranteed emergency cash.

## Build the reserve and access record

1. Complete both event rows with deadlines, consequences, dependable resources and gaps. Explain the first-purpose choice while retaining the other exposure and predictable annual cost separately.
2. Inspect every D4 field. Record the timing mismatch, explicit zero fees and minimums, and unverified protection. Write the specific official information you would need before selecting real storage. A fictional disclosure review is not provider verification.
3. Rehearse only the navigation sequence in the supplied instructions. State where you would read the arrival date and where you would stop. Do not log into another person's account, share credentials, disable security or move funds to prove completion.
4. Write a contribution rule with a capacity check before each proposed 50. Specify use, replenishment, review and an insufficient-funds fallback. Separate planned contributions from money already present.
5. Save the record before the [later packet](later-packet.md). Recompute progress from actual supplied contributions rather than elapsed calendar months. Reassess access when the due day changes.

For support, use a six-field access card and a calculator. For a harder variant, make the affordable contribution irregular and identify the earliest unmet need without inventing a payday. Optional actual transfer is to inspect a real official disclosure privately and locate its access instructions without a transaction. Use qualified benefits or banking help where rules or accessibility matter. A reserve can be modest and useful without proving financial security, and inability to fund one is not a moral failure.
