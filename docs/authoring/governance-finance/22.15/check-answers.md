# Corrective answers

1. As three different evidence classes: direct observation, attributed secondhand account and explicitly labeled inference. Include uncertainty and lawful access; do not upgrade suspicion into a finding.
2. The inspected OSHA retaliation process is not anonymous and is distinct from its safety-hazard route. Coverage, jurisdiction, disclosure handling and the applicable deadline need verification. Neither filing nor an internal complaint guarantees protection.
3. An internal response target is not a legal deadline and does not establish tolling. Record the missed response, use an authorized alternative and obtain timely qualified guidance about outside routes and deadlines; do not disclose restricted records through an ordinary portal.
