# 22.13 — Anti-corruption and conflicts of interest

Build a procurement decision that remains reviewable when a personal relationship and a gift offer enter the process. Allow twenty minutes for the case and ten for its later record. The intended product is a conflict disposition with usable controls, not a declaration that someone is corrupt.

## Notice the interest before deciding the outcome

A conflict exists when another interest can pull a person away from their assigned duty. A relationship can create a conflict even when the person expects to act fairly. Disclosure makes the interest visible; it does not by itself decide whether participation is permissible. Recusal removes a person from a particular decision, while an independent review checks whether the replacement process actually applied the agreed criteria.

Distinguish mechanisms. Favoritism gives a preferred person an advantage unrelated to the task. Self-dealing uses entrusted authority for one's own benefit. A personal reward tied to an award raises a bribery concern, but recognizing that concern is not a legal verdict. Procurement abuse can include hiding a bid, changing specifications to favor one supplier or splitting a purchase to evade a control. An undisclosed interest can compromise review without any completed payment. Repeatedly calling small deviations harmless can normalize a process that nobody can later defend.

The Charity Commission's [conflict guidance, S01](../SOURCES.md#S01) supports identifying, managing and recording conflicts. It governs charity trustees in England and Wales. The procurement card below is original fiction; it does not import that jurisdiction's authorization or quorum rules into another organization.

## Supplied procurement file P6

The fictional River Workshop needs **200 identical instruction cards by Friday**. No purchase has occurred. Its preapproved P6 rule says: select the lowest total price among bids meeting quantity, legibility and Friday delivery; retain all bids; disclose relevant relationships and gifts; exclude conflicted people from scoring and approval; keep request, approval and delivery verification with different people. A personal gift connected to selection must be declined and recorded for the designated review steward. Only the council can authorize an exception before commitment. Urgency creates no automatic exception.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Birch | 180 | Meets both | Thursday |
| Alder | 170 | Meets both | Friday |
| Cedar | 160 | Meets both | Monday after the event |

Requester **Lee** normally also recommends a supplier. Lee's sibling owns Birch; this relationship was omitted from the first request. Birch's message offers Lee a **20-unit personal voucher if Birch receives the order**. The message is supplied fiction, already in the authorized procurement file. Do not seek further messages or arrange a test payment. **Sam**, who normally approves other purchases, says, “It is a small job; family businesses deserve a chance. Mark the gift as a discount.” The voucher is personal, not a price reduction to the workshop.

Independent reviewer **Noor** has accepted the council's appointment to score this purchase and has declared no relevant interest. Approver **Pat** and delivery verifier **Eli** have separately accepted their roles, with no declared conflicts. Council steward **Rae** handles potential rule breaches. These appointments are facts of the case, not authority granted by your worksheet. P6 provides a pending status if an eligible approver is unavailable.

## Worked distinction: a cheap bid can still be ineligible

An unrelated example has a 90-unit quote arriving after a required date and a 95-unit quote arriving on time. The late quote fails a stated threshold; the five-unit saving does not change that threshold. Record the disqualification reason before ranking eligible prices. Similarly, a relationship does not automatically make a supplier's work poor, but the interested requester cannot cure the conflict by promising to be impartial. Supplier eligibility and decision-maker eligibility are separate questions.

For P6, remove Lee from recommendation and approval, preserve the original omission and corrected disclosure, and record the gift refusal. Do not silently edit the history to look clean. Route the offer and Sam's proposed misclassification to Rae without asserting criminal intent. Noor can then compare eligible bids using the existing card. Cedar's date matters even though its price is lowest. Pat's independent approval is a separate step from Noor's recommendation; Eli later verifies delivered quantity and legibility before any authorized payment process.

## Produce an inspectable control record

1. Make a five-column register: observed fact, possible mechanism, affected duty, uncertainty and assigned response. Include the omitted family interest, contingent voucher, proposed relabeling and late bid. Mark motives and legal conclusions unknown.
2. Write the disclosure and recusal disposition, naming which tasks Lee may still perform. Lee may clarify the requested quantity through the shared record but may not privately influence scoring. Name Noor, Pat, Eli and Rae with their distinct authority.
3. Apply P6 to all three bids. Show each eligibility decision, then the lowest eligible price. Draft a recommendation with the gift excluded from organizational cost. Keep approval and purchase as pending until their own evidence exists.
4. Answer Sam in two sentences: identify the rule and explain the next authorized route. Avoid accusing Sam publicly or accepting an undocumented exception. If genuine urgency remains, put the options and consequences before the council.
5. Define the audit trace: original bids, disclosure, recusal, criteria, recommendation, approval and delivery check. Restrict access appropriately. Save your first record before opening the [later packet](later-packet.md).

The audit is useful because another authorized person can reconstruct the choice, not because the file is large. Collect only what the decision and responsible review require. A minor omission may warrant correction; suspected serious misconduct requires the applicable qualified process. Enforcement must follow actual authority and fair procedure. A learner does not impose penalties or conduct an investigation.

## Adjust the difficulty without manufacturing misconduct

For support, use one row per supplied fact and highlight eligibility before price. A helper may explain the difference between a personal voucher and a supplier discount; record that help. For a harder pass, make Pat unavailable and ask whether the case can proceed under P6. The answer needs an authorized replacement or a pending decision, not Lee's return to approval.

For optional actual transfer, review a low-stakes policy or blank procurement template you are entitled to see. Identify one missing separation of duties and propose a question to its owner. Do not create a conflict, solicit a gift, upload vendor records or contact a suspected wrongdoer. Evidence could be an authorized, redacted disposition and a later control check. One well-written plan does not establish that corruption was prevented.

## Scope and evidence boundary

The canonical record has no professional_boundary field. Practical safeguards do not add canonical metadata. Applicability and normative status remain role_conditional. A leadership role is not compulsory. Use actual authority and willing participation; keep protected records outside app evidence. Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Human dignity is never scored.
