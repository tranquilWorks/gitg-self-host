# 16.04 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** One-to-one and group conversation

**Scope (unchanged):** Initiate, sustain, balance, transition, include, and close conversations in different settings.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Conversations feel reciprocal rather than interrogative, dominating, or absent.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Initiate and sustain | Context opener, linked follow-up and bounded self-contribution | Pair sequence and response to correction | No question quota or guaranteed liking |
| Balance and include | Group topic connection, optional entry and text contribution | Invitation/participation/consideration distinctions | Quietness is not a defect or compulsory turn |
| Transition and close | Task deadline and honest pair ending | Actual transition and ending notes | No empty future commitment |
| Different settings and reciprocity | Separate pair and group opportunities | Two attempt statuses and optional actual feedback | One-to-one rehearsal cannot establish group performance |

Canonical classification: applicability `cross_context_core`; normative status `cross_tradition_core_or_broadly_recurrent`; formation modes: agency, interdependence.
