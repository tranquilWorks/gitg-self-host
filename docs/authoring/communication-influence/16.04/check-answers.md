# 16.04 — Corrective answers

1. Share a relevant, bounded experience or view and leave room for response. The partner is asking for reciprocity; increasing question volume does not address it. Avoid replacing the interview with a monologue.
2. With the group's agreement, pause to read or otherwise include the text contribution, credit its author and allow their preferred response format. Inclusion requires usable access and consideration, not a compulsory spoken turn or automatic adoption of the idea.
3. State the constraint respectfully, offer time for the immediate point if feasible and close at the stated time: “I need to leave in two minutes; what's the part you'd most like to finish before I go?” Do not use an abrupt disappearance or a false promise to conceal the limit. The storyteller can also prefer to stop now.
