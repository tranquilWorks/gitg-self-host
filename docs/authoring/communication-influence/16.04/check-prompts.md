# 16.04 — Fresh checks

1. You ask six questions in a row and your partner says, “What about you?” What should your next contribution do, and why is another question an incomplete response?
2. A group member uses text and is being missed while spoken turns continue. Offer a process adjustment without requiring them to speak aloud.
3. You must leave in two minutes while someone is midway through a long story. Give an honest closing route that does not promise a future meeting you do not intend.
