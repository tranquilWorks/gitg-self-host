# 16.05 — Fresh checks

1. You infer a friend dislikes long visits. They say, “I enjoy them, but the last bus is early tonight.” What changes in your representation, and what stays unknown?
2. A person confirms your summary of their preference but rejects your proposed solution. Has empathic accuracy necessarily failed? Explain.
3. Someone declines to explain why they prefer a written exchange. What practical response can respect that preference without inventing an explanation?
