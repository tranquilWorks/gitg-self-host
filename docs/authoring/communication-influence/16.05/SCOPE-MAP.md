# 16.05 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Perspective taking and empathic accuracy

**Scope (unchanged):** Imagine another viewpoint while checking assumptions against what the person actually reports.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can represent another perspective fairly without pretending to know their inner state.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Imagine another viewpoint | Multiple reasonable accounts and own constraint separation | Observed/inferred/reportable table | Imagination does not establish inner knowledge |
| Check against actual reports | Open practical question and contradiction handling | Original hypotheses and revised two-sentence account | Supplied fiction is not actual interpersonal accuracy |
| Fair representation without assumed inner state | Museum preference and parcel uncertainty | Supported/contradicted/unknown claims | Reports can change; private reasons need not be disclosed |
| Preserve disagreement | Own deadline and independent option | Decision separate from perspective confirmation | Understanding does not require agreement or condoning harm |
