# 16.09 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Public speaking and presentation

**Scope (unchanged):** Structure, rehearse, deliver, adapt, and respond to an audience with clarity and composure.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can deliver a coherent talk and improve it using observed audience response.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Structure and rehearse | Purpose, opening/sequence/example/close and two actual passes | Outline, timings and edits | Mental walkthrough is not actual rehearsal |
| Deliver with clarity and composure | Accessible delivery, pauses and honest unknown answer | Actual delivery/question status | No eye-contact, memorization or extroversion requirement |
| Adapt and respond to audience | Uncoached process explanation and task-specific redelivery | Misunderstanding, revision and retest | Politeness/applause/nerves do not establish comprehension |
| Observed improvement | Tray correction with log step still missing | Specific supported gain and residual gap | Supplied replies cannot replace actual audience evidence |
