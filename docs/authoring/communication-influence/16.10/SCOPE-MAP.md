# 16.10 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Storytelling and explanation

**Scope (unchanged):** Use sequence, character, tension, example, analogy, and resolution to make meaning memorable.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** A listener can retell the essential message accurately.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sequence, character and tension | Reasonable helper goal and five causal beats | Beat map and original story | No ridicule or invented real event |
| Example, analogy and resolution | Conflicting signs and maps analogy with boundary | Two-minute explanation and explicit limit | Example/analogy does not prove universal prevalence |
| Memorable meaning | Uncoached why/what-changed retelling | Causal omission or distortion record | Decorative recall is not essential-message recall |
| Accurate essential retell | Targeted transition repair and second account | Revised passage and residual generalization limit | Self-retelling weaker; actual listener/long-term recall pending |
