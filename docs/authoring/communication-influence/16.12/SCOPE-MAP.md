# 16.12 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Conflict and de-escalation

**Scope (unchanged):** Recognize escalation, regulate self, clarify issues, own contributions, reduce threat, and define next steps.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** A conflict becomes safer and more solvable, or is ended with clear boundaries.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recognize escalation and regulate self | Own cue, pause/return and safe ending conditions | Prepared opening and actual attempt status | No compelled emotional performance |
| Clarify issues and own contributions | Message evidence separates checking, booking and confirming | Accounts and proportionate acknowledgment | No motive claim, global blame or forced equal responsibility |
| Reduce threat and define next steps | Authority-bounded room remedy and future acknowledgment | Owner/time/confirmation plan | Abuse/threats routed beyond peer communication drill |
| Safer/solvable or clear ending | Immediate 25-minute remedy; future confirmation overdue | Separate safety, agreement and follow-through statuses | Calmness/apology do not prove repaired process |

Use the [learner guide](learner-guide.md), preserve your first attempt before the [later packet](later-packet.md), then answer [fresh checks](check-prompts.md) before the [corrective key](check-answers.md). See [inspected sources](../SOURCES.md) and [individual review](../QUALITY-REVIEW.md).

Canonical classification: applicability `cross_context_core`; normative status `cross_tradition_core_or_broadly_recurrent`; formation modes: agency, interdependence.
