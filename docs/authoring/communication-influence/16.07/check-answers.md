# 16.07 — Corrective answers

1. Ask for a specific feature and the task it affected: “Which part made it hard to use, and what were you trying to find?” You need not accept a judgment of your ability. If the person continues with insults rather than useful information, end or bound the review.
2. Acknowledge that the deadline was hard to locate, place it in a clear labeled line near the required action and explain that all capitals throughout would not distinguish the deadline. Retest finding the deadline rather than treating the reviewer's preferred formatting as the only valid repair.
3. You can show the concrete edit and its intended mechanism. Improved reader performance remains untested; neither feeling less defensive nor receiving the suggestion proves the repair worked. Record the retest as pending and retain the before/after copy.
