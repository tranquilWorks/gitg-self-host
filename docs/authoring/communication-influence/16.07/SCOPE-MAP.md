# 16.07 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Feedback and developmental conversation

**Scope (unchanged):** Give specific, timely, actionable feedback and receive criticism without collapse or automatic defense.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Both parties leave knowing what should continue, change, and be reviewed.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Specific timely actionable feedback | Agreed artifact feature, observable phrase and practical effect | Continue/change comment and author-intent question | No personality or surprise performance appraisal |
| Receive criticism without collapse or automatic defense | Restatement and example request before disposition | Received comment and accept/adapt/decline reason | Receptivity is not forced agreement or abuse tolerance |
| Developmental change | Actual invitation revision and original-task retry | Before/after artifact and result/pending status | Proposal quality and observation validity are separate |
| Continue, change and review | Named retained feature, changed ambiguity and review point | Shared next-step record | Supplied replies are not interpersonal evidence |

Use the [learner guide](learner-guide.md), preserve your first attempt before the [later packet](later-packet.md), then answer [fresh checks](check-prompts.md) before the [corrective key](check-answers.md). See [inspected sources](../SOURCES.md) and [individual review](../QUALITY-REVIEW.md).

Canonical classification: applicability `cross_context_core`; normative status `cross_tradition_core_or_broadly_recurrent`; formation modes: agency, interdependence.

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: performance_task, observer_feedback, artifact, longitudinal_review. This is preserved curriculum metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.

This source companion changes no runtime selection, package, action, observation, completion, score or participant record.
