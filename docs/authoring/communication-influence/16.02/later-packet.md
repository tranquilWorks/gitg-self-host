# 16.02 — Later packet: a clear counteroffer

Open after drafting and preserving your first message. All replies below are supplied fiction, not observer evidence.

Ari replies Thursday at 16:00: “I understand you need it by Friday at 17:00 and won't lend again without a return plan. I can bring it Friday at 18:00, or you can collect Thursday at 18:30. I can't leave it outside.”

Write a reply that distinguishes comprehension from the available arrangement. Friday at 18:00 misses the need by one hour. Thursday at 18:30 fits your stated collection window, but requires an agreed location and acknowledgment; it is not permission to enter or take anything unannounced. A workable answer confirms that option and asks where to meet. Preserve the future lending boundary without adding a punishment for the delay.

Then change one fact: you can no longer collect Thursday. Do not retain the old agreement as feasible. Ask whether a different timely return is possible or use a real alternative for Friday's activity. The reason for the original delay remains unknown; a counteroffer supplies no evidence of carelessness or malice.

Record: message understood in the fictional reply; Thursday option proposed/confirmed only as the dialogue supports; actual return unobserved. A clear exchange does not establish completed return or successful real-world communication.
