# 16.06 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Audience and context adaptation

**Scope (unchanged):** Adjust language, detail, medium, timing, emotion, culture, role, and urgency.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Communicates the same core idea effectively to materially different audiences.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Language, detail and medium | Two task-specific messages from one fact card | Visitor and setup drafts | No demographic assumptions or image-only essential facts |
| Timing, emotion, culture, role and urgency | Explicit eight-dimension audit | Choice of channel/send time and adaptation rationale | Routine change is not invented emergency; known norms checked |
| Same core idea across audiences | Room/start/route and role-specific arrival distinction | Fact comparison and fresh time-label case | Material uncertainty cannot be removed for simplicity |
| Effective communication | Uncoached arrival and setup tasks | Mismatch, corrected copy and retest status | Sending, access, understanding and action remain separate |
