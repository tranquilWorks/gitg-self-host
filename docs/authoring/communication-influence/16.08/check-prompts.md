# 16.08 — Fresh checks

1. A popular speaker repeats a claim three times and displays many positive reactions. What does that establish about the evidence for the claim?
2. A trial has a 90-token budget, 65-token materials and 30-token required cleanup. The pitch says it is fully funded. Repair the factual claim and the decision request.
3. “If you care about newcomers, you'll endorse my proposal tonight.” Identify the pressure and rewrite the invitation to decide without erasing the underlying value.
