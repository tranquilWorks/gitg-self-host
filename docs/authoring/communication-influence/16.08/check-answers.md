# 16.08 — Corrective answers

1. It establishes repetition and visible reactions, not independent corroboration or correctness. Credibility and popularity can affect persuasion; inspect the evidence and reasoning that actually bear on the claim. Reactions might also come from an unrepresentative audience.
2. Required costs total 95 tokens, so there is a 5-token shortfall. State it, then request discussion of a legitimate funding or scope change, postponement or refusal. Do not call it funded by hiding required cleanup or assuming an unoffered contribution.
3. It ties moral belonging to one proposal and uses an unexplained immediate deadline. For example: “The goal is to make participation easier for newcomers. Here is the proposal, its costs and alternatives; please consider whether it serves that goal, and declining or suggesting another approach is welcome.” A genuine deadline can be stated with its reason.
