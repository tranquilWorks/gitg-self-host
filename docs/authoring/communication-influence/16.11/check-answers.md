# 16.11 — Corrective answers

1. Only 40 shared minutes remain from 09:20 to 10:00, so the package is 5 minutes short. The room staying open later does not change B's departure. Seek a genuinely revised option or decline; do not describe the arithmetic as a fair compromise that meets both minima.
2. It is a possible alternative, not a verified available one. Check access, timing, costs and authority before relying on it. If it cannot be verified, retain that uncertainty and consider another feasible route rather than bluffing about guaranteed access.
3. The refusal names the unmet interest or constraint and uses an actual alternative without imposing punishment. Clear understanding and no agreement can coexist. A threat instead tries to make refusal costly through an imposed adverse consequence; neither comprehension nor affection obligates acceptance.
