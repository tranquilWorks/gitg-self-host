# 16.11 — Fresh checks

1. A room is available 09:00–10:30. A needs the first 20 minutes quiet; B must leave at 10:00 and needs 45 shared minutes. Is quiet-then-shared feasible? Show the gap.

3. Both sides clearly understand a proposed agreement, but one declines because it is worse than their workable alternative. What distinguishes a principled refusal from a failed communication or a threat?
