# 16.11 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Negotiation

**Scope (unchanged):** Identify interests, alternatives, leverage, constraints, fairness, trades, and walk-away conditions.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Reaches a better agreement or principled refusal than reactive bargaining would produce.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Interests, alternatives and constraints | Separate role cards and feasible home alternatives | Preparation sheet and checked hypotheses | No inferred interests or imaginary BATNA |
| Leverage, fairness and trades | Fair reference, prohibited guilt and two packages | Gains/concessions and interruption responsibility | No exploitation of dependency or worsening the other alternative |
| Walk-away conditions | Original minima and changed departure | 75-minute feasible interval; later 30-minute shortfall | Voluntary refusal is a legitimate outcome |
| Better agreement or principled refusal | Mutual restatement, change mechanism and separate follow-through | Agreement/refusal and later review status | Rehearsal is not actual consent or performance |

Use the [learner guide](learner-guide.md), preserve your first attempt before the [later packet](later-packet.md), then answer [fresh checks](check-prompts.md) before the [corrective key](check-answers.md). See [inspected sources](../SOURCES.md) and [individual review](../QUALITY-REVIEW.md).

Canonical classification: applicability `cross_context_core`; normative status `cross_tradition_core_or_broadly_recurrent`; formation modes: agency, interdependence.

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: performance_task, observer_feedback, artifact, longitudinal_review. This is preserved curriculum metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.

This source companion changes no runtime selection, package, action, observation, completion, score or participant record. Author editorial/source review is not independent cold-start, actual learner, qualified or owner acceptance; those remain pending. Supplied outcomes are fictional, never actual partner/observer evidence. This map is not a formal M6K A/B/C receipt.
