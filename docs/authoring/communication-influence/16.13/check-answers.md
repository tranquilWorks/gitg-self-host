# 16.13 — Corrective answers

1. Take the reported impact seriously, acknowledge it, stop the line and make a proportionate repair. Majority laughter cannot cancel the person's experience or establish consent. Do not insist on “only joking,” request a vote or repeat the comment to prove its harmlessness.
2. Return to the shared activity, respond to another person's contribution or offer ordinary practical warmth. Whether people were amused, distracted or uncomfortable remains unknown unless clarified voluntarily. Repetition, explanation and fishing for reassurance would add pressure.
3. Read the timing and practical needs, choose a welcome greeting or permission-based helpful action, allow a neutral response and review its actual effect. For example, ask whether space for someone's materials would help, act if welcome and notice whether they can join the task. Record actual action separately from a plan. Quick wit and extroversion are not requirements.
