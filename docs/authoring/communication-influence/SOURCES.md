# Inspected source registry

Checked 24 September 2026. Fifteen primary, author-authored or first-party guidance entries support bounded teaching claims; the S02 publisher corrigendum is additionally inspected. An abstract-only inspection is labeled explicitly. This is an author source review, not a systematic review, specialist approval or evidence that these exercises have been validated.

The canonical curriculum and inherited current/recovery domain-16 drafts provide the scope and exercise intent. Their exact hashes, together with the frozen 16.03 protocol hash, are in [cohort.json](cohort.json). All worked examples, fictional facts, later outcomes, fresh checks, keys and task designs here are original companion material. Sources are summarized selectively; no article, image, assessment scale or external exercise is reproduced.

## S01

**[The 4 Components of Nonviolent Communication — Rosenberg framework teaching sheet](https://www.nonviolentcommunication.com/bookshare/4_components_of_NVC_4_rev_6.20.19.pdf)** — publisher-hosted teaching aid. Used by 16.02.

Inspected: One-page distinctions for observation, feeling, need and request.

Supported use: Names the four planning distinctions used in 16.02.

Limits: Not an outcome study or a universal communication norm. Reasoning/boundary work and all cases are original additions; broad claims about universal needs are not adopted.

## S02

**[Barrett et al. (2019), Emotional Expressions Reconsidered](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6640856/)** — research review by its authors. Used by 16.03.

Inspected: Abstract, scope discussion and context/variability account.

Supported use: Facial movement does not supply a single invariant emotion label.

Limits: Facial research is not a validated decoder for voice, gesture or every culture. No images, action-unit training or deception detection reproduced.

[Publisher correction](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1529100619889954): Publisher corrigendum text inspected: Figure 5 image attribution, action-unit activation labels, improved images and acknowledgment. No affected figure or action-unit claim is used here.

## S03

**[Correction to Huang et al. (2017), It does not hurt to ask (2025)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40111841/)** — journal correction and original abstract reproduced by publisher through PubMed. Used by 16.04.

Inspected: Correction abstract and included original abstract.

Supported use: Audit reports minor reporting corrections and unchanged conclusions; original work concerns question asking and liking in studied conversations.

Limits: Not a retraction. No universal question quota or group-conversation benefit claimed. The independent reanalysis S04 is a distinct methodological dispute, not the same event. Full audit data were not reanalyzed.

## S04

**[Kluger and Malloy (2019), Question asking as a dyadic behavior](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31714108/)** — authors primary reanalysis abstract. Used by 16.04.

Inspected: Abstract describing social-relations reanalysis of Study 3.

Supported use: Reanalysis challenges equating second-date offers with liking and question asking with listening.

Limits: Only abstract inspected; PMC full-text route challenged access. Does not establish an opposite universal rule or reanalyze all original studies. Rehearsal uses no numerical question target.

## S12

**[Harvard HMS/HSDM/HSPH Ombuds — Seven Elements of Effective Negotiations, Jerome Slavik (2008)](https://hms.harvard.edu/sites/default/files/Departments/Ombuds%20Office/files/SEVEN%20ELEMENTS%20OF%20EFFECTIVE%20NEGOTIATIONS.pdf)** — first-party institutional teaching handout adapting Getting to Yes. ## S13

## S15

## Retrieval limits and editorial decisions

The direct CNVC page failed retrieval; S01 uses the successfully inspected publisher-hosted teaching sheet. NCA ethical-credo pages/PDF attempts failed and are not counted as inspected support. The Epley paper PDF failed; only its author-hosted abstract is used. PMC access challenged the question-asking reanalysis, Dahlstrom narrative paper and humor paper; S04 and S10 use inspected primary abstracts, while S15 was read at its publisher. The separate Huang authors' 2019 response page did not return readable content and is not claimed as inspected. Original-study findings are available in S03's reproduced abstract, alongside the correction; S04 is reported as a separate reanalysis, not as a settled reversal of the entire study.

The initial PubMed route for the Barrett corrigendum yielded no useful text; its publisher text was subsequently inspected. It concerns Figure 5 attribution/labels/images and acknowledgment, none of which are reproduced. No inaccessible article is used to justify a numerical benefit, universal norm or scientific guarantee.

The negotiation handout's alternative-worsening tactic is deliberately excluded, W3C's learning-style phrasing is not a learning-style theory here, MIT's eye-contact convention is not a performance requirement, and the dated Acas manager/legal context is not general legal guidance. Source authority does not make every instruction in a source suitable for this curriculum.
