# 07.09 — Fresh checks

Attempt these before opening [answers](check-answers.md).

1. A listener agrees to hear a message. Have they also agreed to the requested change?

2. The chat was muted and the speaker did not know a change was posted. Must the speaker deny feeling left out after learning this?

3. A four-line message is saved but never sent or rehearsed. Is difficult emotional disclosure complete?
