# 07.09 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Healthy emotional expression

**Scope (unchanged):** Communicate feelings and needs appropriately rather than leaking them through withdrawal, hostility, performance, or control.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can disclose a difficult emotional truth to a trustworthy person with clarity and proportion.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Feelings and needs communicated proportionately | Full changed-plan facts and dramatic/factual drafts | Observation/feeling/value/request message | No motive assertion or emotional coercion |
| Trustworthy person and accessible channel | Specific consent to private written time | Delivered message with correction invited | Listening consent is not request acceptance |
| Impact and next relational action | Declined direct-note promise and agreed channel alternative | Eight-minute exchange and actual settings choice | Future effectiveness and repair unknown |
