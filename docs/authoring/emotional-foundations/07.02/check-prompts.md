# 07.02 — Fresh checks

Attempt these before opening [answers](check-answers.md).

1. A familiar ache stays the same before and after a message. Can the worksheet classify it as anger-caused?

2. A cue appears at 09:05:06 and the response starts at 09:05:09, before drafting at 09:05:30. Was the response early relative to drafting?

3. Jaw tension appears once and is absent next time. Must the learner invent it to complete the scan?
