# 07.02 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Body-based emotional awareness

**Scope (unchanged):** Notice breathing, tension, temperature, energy, posture, pain, and activation before emotion becomes behavior.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Identifies early physiological signals and uses them to intervene sooner.
