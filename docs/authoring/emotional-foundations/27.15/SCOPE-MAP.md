# 27.15 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Receiving as well as achieving

**Scope (unchanged):** Integrate grace, dependence, inheritance, help, contemplation, celebration, and care into a model otherwise oriented toward action.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** A life review names what was received and how to respond, not only what was accomplished.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grace, dependence, inheritance, help, contemplation, celebration and care | Three received goods, mixed history and optional interpretations | Contribution/receipt/response distinctions | No required belief, disclosure or gratitude |
| Receiving alongside achievement | Bounded helper and courtyard opportunities | Actual receipt separately from plans | Remembered inheritance is not a new event |
| Proportionate response | Declined condition and timely borrowed-card return | Genuine obligation retained without equal repayment | No future stewardship or spiritual result invented |
