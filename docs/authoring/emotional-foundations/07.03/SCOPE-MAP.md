# 07.03 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** The pause between feeling and action

**Scope (unchanged):** Use breath, grounding, movement, delay, prayer, writing, or consultation before consequential reactions.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Prevents a regrettable response without denying the underlying feeling or issue.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Breath, grounding, movement, delay, prayer, writing or consultation | Choice of accessible brief action with optional spiritual route | Cue/action/return card and two complete rehearsals | No required breath focus or unsafe delay |
| Feeling retained while reaction interrupted | Unsent blame and genuine room ambiguity | Accurate factual response | No denial of feeling or claim of relationship repair |
| Return and actual prevention | Mixed later outcome with missed promise | Five-minute return miss retained | Rehearsal cannot become actual prevented behavior |
