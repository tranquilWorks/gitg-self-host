# 07.10 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Self-compassion with standards

**Scope (unchanged):** Respond to suffering and failure with care while preserving accountability, effort, and consequences.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** The internal response supports repair and renewed action rather than indulgence or punishment.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Care with accountability, effort and consequences | Punishing, indulgent and accountable-care lines | Specific missed commitment and revised standard | No care deprivation or denial of impact |
| Repair and feasible renewed action | Complete sorting material and twenty-minute window | Consented shorter summary and actual receipt | Reduced output must be agreed, not relabeled equivalent |
| Follow-through rather than self-attack | Eighteen-minute attempt with lost joint-practice time | Outcome and disappointment retained | No isolated cause or clinical effect established |
