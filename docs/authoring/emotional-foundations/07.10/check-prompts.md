# 07.10 — Fresh checks

Attempt these before opening [answers](check-answers.md).

1. A person owes a repair. Must ordinary sleep or food be withheld until the other person forgives them?

2. A new eighteen-minute attempt succeeds after rest, notice and a smaller task. Does this prove the kind sentence alone caused success?

3. A partner accepts a shorter practice after a missed commitment. May the review erase the lost fifteen minutes?
