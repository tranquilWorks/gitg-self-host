## Retrieval, originality and care boundaries

The WHO publication route for Doing What Matters timed out; its IRIS PDF returned 403. Neither is credited as read or used to reproduce a grounding protocol. The search results and PAHO listing were not treated as a full guide. Linked CCI modules and NHS videos were not inspected. Page dates refer to those pages, not to validation of these original materials. No source worksheet, diagram, participant story or private record was copied.

The gradual-approach case is deliberately narrower than the NHS page: an ordinary familiar group's welcome question period, repeated small step, access preserved, voluntary stopping and no requirement to stay until fear halves. The canonical source exercise's local ceiling is not a clinically validated threshold. Threat, coercion, medical concern and clinical fears exclude the self-guided live route regardless of a rating. Qualified review remains pending; these constraints do not make this a treatment.

Domain 07's exact professional boundary is retained in each guide and scope map. Severe/persistent symptoms, impaired functioning, safety concerns or high-stakes harm require appropriate qualified or urgent support as applicable; no local service availability, diagnosis or treatment selection is inferred. Body cues have possible non-emotional causes. Breath manipulation, forced disclosure, prescribed-support removal, imposed gratitude, coerced repair, surveillance and mandatory escalation are excluded from these original cases.

The thirteen outputs are substantively distinct: a receiving account, differentiated emotion record, early-cue timeline, pause-and-return check, repeated prediction comparison, anger request, loss/continuity map, specific responsibility record, comparison-to-learning action, emotional disclosure, accountable-care restart, hope with a refused outcome and a setback experiment with confounding conditions. Literal sentence checks supplement individual review, not replace it.

Formal M6K A/B/C, independent cold-start, actual learner, qualified and owner content acceptance remain pending. No diagnosis, efficacy, universal suitability, moral worth, runtime integration, score, completion or deployment claim follows from source publication.
