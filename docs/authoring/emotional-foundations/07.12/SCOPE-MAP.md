# 07.12 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Resilience and post-setback adaptation

**Scope (unchanged):** Separate facts, responsibility, lessons, support, recovery, and the next experiment after failure or disruption.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** A setback produces a concrete adjustment and renewed attempt.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Facts, responsibility, lessons, support and recovery | Six headings and complete interrupted-attempt timeline | Narrow responsibility and provisional scope lesson | No forced lesson from trauma or injustice |
| One planned adjustment and renewed attempt | Six-item material, authorized destinations and table-only goal | Actual eighteen-minute restart | Uninterrupted time is an additional changed condition |
| Compare adverse and unresolved effects | Crowded tray, retained key and unchanged shelf | Bounded result with next decision pending | No isolated cause or global resilience proof |
