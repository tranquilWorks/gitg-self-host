# 07.04 — Fresh checks

Attempt these before opening [answers](check-answers.md).

1. A person rates a situation 2/10, but it involves a threatening person. Is the rating permission to proceed?

2. The first attempt has no critical remark; the second has one and both peak at 4/10. Must the second be hidden as a failed exercise?

3. A learner uses an ordinary speech device or written card. Must that support be removed before the attempt counts?
