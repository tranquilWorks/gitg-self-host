# 07.04 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Anxiety and courageous exposure

**Scope (unchanged):** Approach worthwhile feared situations gradually while testing predictions and building tolerance.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Completes repeated exposures and compares feared outcomes with what actually occurred.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Worthwhile gradual approach | Complete notices, three-step ladder and bounded prediction | Chosen repeated step with support and stop rule | Local four-point ceiling is not clinical clearance |
| Repeated observations and tolerance | Two five-minute occasions one week apart | No remark followed by partial feared remark | No promised distress reduction or mandatory escalation |
| Limits of self-guided work | Access preserved and actual threat overrides rating | Repeat/adapt/defer/help decision | No unsupervised clinical exposure protocol |
