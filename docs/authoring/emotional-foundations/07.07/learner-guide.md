# 07.07 — Shame, guilt, and remorse

## Replace a verdict about the whole self with accountable detail

Guilt can concern a specific harmful choice; remorse can include recognizing its impact and wanting to act differently. Global shame can turn “I missed an agreement” into “I am incapable of change.” These experiences may overlap. Removing the global verdict does not remove responsibility, and accepting responsibility does not require endless self-punishment.

Use two fifteen-minute private passes, a bounded repair or prevention action and ten minutes to review. CCI's self-compassion overview [S05](../SOURCES.md#S05) supports care rather than habitual self-criticism. The repair structure here is original curriculum-based work; no workbook module is reproduced or credited as read. This is not a finding of legal culpability, therapy or permission to contact someone who wants no contact.

## One omission and a limited repair window

Fictional Ari agreed to send four ordinary room labels to teammate Bea by Monday 16:00. The exact labels were “Welcome,” “Materials,” “Finished work” and “Return here.” Ari had the text by 14:00, delayed the task to browse unrelated material and forgot to send it. Bea recreated the four labels in twelve minutes before the 16:30 setup. An unrelated printer delay also affected setup, but there is no evidence Ari caused it.

Ari's first response is, “I ruin everything; people should never rely on me.” The accountable event is narrower: Ari missed the 16:00 send without notice, creating twelve minutes of replacement work. The printer delay belongs in context rather than being assigned wholly to Ari or used to erase the missed send. Bea has said a short message is welcome but does not want repeated apologies.

Three separate decisions are available. Repair: offer to prepare the next four labels if Bea wants that help. Consequence: accept that Bea already completed the work and cannot get those twelve minutes back; Ari cannot claim a late duplicate undoes the cost. Prevention: set a 15:30 send reminder and check receipt by 15:50 for the next agreed 16:00 task. A shared reminder requires agreement; a private reminder is within Ari's control.

A worked acknowledgment is: “I missed the agreed send and left you twelve minutes of replacement work. I’m sorry. Would preparing the next labels help, or would you prefer no further action from me on this?” It asks rather than presumes what repair is wanted. Bea need not reassure Ari, forgive, accept an offer or become responsible for Ari's self-esteem.

## Repair where welcome and renew a real standard

1. Make five headings: fact, my choice, impact, context without excuse, and what is not established about my worth. Keep the four supplied labels so no new task material must be invented. Translate the global verdict into a specific omission while retaining its consequence.
2. Separate repair, consequence and prevention before the [later packet](later-packet.md). Check welcome and authority. If direct contact is unsuitable in a real case, use an appropriate adviser or accountable process instead of sending a confession to relieve your own distress.
3. Review the recipient's actual response, any performed action and the next similar commitment. A declined repair offer remains declined. Prevention can occur without earning absolution; a later kept agreement is one instance of changed conduct, not proof that harm has vanished.

## Care remains available while consequences remain

For support, dictate the private record or use the fictional case alone. No trauma history, unlawful conduct or identifying details are required. For a harder case, responsibility is disputed: distinguish what you know from a conclusion requiring qualified legal, clinical, safeguarding or spiritual counsel. Do not use this exercise to bypass formal accountability.

In future transfer, retain an ordinary feasible standard and inspect actual follow-through. Renewed conduct matters more than escalating self-attack. Severe or persistent distress needs qualified help; a fictional label task cannot establish recovery from shame or moral character.

## Use the result at its proper scale

Not therapy or diagnosis; severe or persistent symptoms require qualified mental-health or medical care.

Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Subjective ratings, source exercises and completion do not diagnose, establish mastery or score human dignity. Actual situations require appropriate access, consent, authority and support.
