# 07.08 — Envy, jealousy, and comparison

## Find the desired good without taking ownership of another person

Envy may focus on a good someone else has; jealousy may include fear about a valued relationship. Admiration, insecurity, a real shortage and a possessive urge can be mixed together. None proves another person's intention. A useful comparison review asks what you want, what is actually scarce, what you can learn and which urge should not become behavior.

Allow two ten-minute analyses, one fifteen-minute response and a brief later review. NHS guidance on examining thoughts [S01](../SOURCES.md#S01) supports checking evidence and alternative interpretations. It does not diagnose envy, validate personality judgments or authorize monitoring. The default is fictional; no private information about a peer is needed.

## Recognition, opportunity and a separate relationship fear

At an ordinary community showcase, fictional peer Nia receives the one featured display slot. Organizer rules say this month's feature is limited to one display, while next month's open table has four places available by request. No selection criteria or guarantee of acceptance are supplied. Rowan admires Nia's clear step-by-step diagram and feels disappointed at not being selected. Rowan's reaction is described as 6/10.

Rowan's six headings begin: event—Nia received this month's feature; admired quality—clear explanation; threatened need—recognition for useful work; scarce fact—one feature this month; relationship fear—“my collaborator will only value Nia now”; controlling urge—discourage the collaborator from speaking to Nia. There is no evidence about the collaborator's valuation or any promise of exclusive contact. Mark that fear unverified. Attention and learning opportunities are not shown to be limited to the one feature.

The complete original diagram Nia allows others to study says: “To file a room card, put future dates in NEXT, today's date in TODAY, and dates already passed in PAST. If the date is missing, ask the organizer before filing.” Rowan may learn the explanation method without copying authorship. No private drafts or messages may be accessed.

A worked fact check is the published four-place open-table rule; it establishes another possible route, not a reserved place. A clean skill goal is to make the date rule clearer with an example and ask for a place through the normal route. A boundary could concern Rowan's own use of a comparison feed. It cannot prohibit another person's friendships. Appreciation of an existing collaborator, if honest, can coexist with wanting recognition.

## Turn the comparison into a chosen response

1. Fill the six headings with a fact, a hypothesis or “not present.” Distinguish admiration of Nia's diagram from wanting Nia's whole life. Name which desired good is scarce now and which may have other routes. Do not interrogate a peer to establish your emotional story.
2. Use the supplied rule to draft one original practice example: for a fictional current date of April 12, April 13 belongs in NEXT, April 12 in TODAY and April 10 in PAST. A missing date needs clarification. Choose a learning action, fair request, self-controlled boundary or genuine appreciation that matches the concern. Save the plan before the [later packet](later-packet.md).
3. Review what changed through the actual action and what remains disappointing. A submitted application does not establish a place, and learning a filing example does not entitle Rowan to admiration. Any relationship request leaves the other person free to decline or modify it.

For support, use this invented showcase or an anonymized public example. With no willing interaction, keep learning private and mark relationship transfer untested. For a harder case, all four places are filled: preserve actual scarcity and choose another proportionate route without concluding there is no future possibility. Where jealousy involves coercion, stalking, abuse risk or severe distress, use safety and qualified support rather than a comparison exercise. No amount of feeling authorizes surveillance or possession.
