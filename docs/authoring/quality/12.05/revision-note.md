# 12.05 prepared runtime repair

The former source-only draft reduced a broad strength competency to a generic
low-stakes exercise and did not supply a useful path for someone beyond the chair
rise default. The repair now maps five major patterns and provides three complete
routes: an accessible familiar entry, an unchanged established-plan observation,
or instruction-first preparation. It supplies fixed technique and recovery
criteria, a two-session record, a separate progression case/key and concrete
questions for qualified instruction.

The author review disposition is **ready for independent cold-start and qualified
review**: the package is complete and its safety and claim boundaries are explicit.
It is not formal quality acceptance. Two sessions cannot establish strength gain,
medical clearance, broad pattern competence or causal effectiveness. Actual
learner, independent, qualified and owner review remain pending.
