Connect seven disciplinary lenses without pretending depth

Learning guide — unscored

Supplied fictional waiting-area records

W0: six seats occupy a 4-by-6-meter room; four receive afternoon shade; three sound readings are 70, 72 and 71 dB at the same marked point, with no comfort threshold supplied. H1: a 2024 memo says the stop moved indoors after summer heat complaints; it does not explain who was consulted. D1: the route display uses 8-millimeter characters and refreshes each minute; no legibility standard is supplied. E1: the improvement budget is $2,400; either eight $300 high-contrast signs or four $600 shaded seats fit before maintenance, but mixed options are allowed. U1: in a voluntary fictional test at the intended distance, two of two readers could not identify the route number; the sample cannot represent all users. A1: one designer describes the current gray surface as calm; one rider describes it as hard to distinguish. S1: a meeting roster lists operations and one designer but no riders or disability advocate. These records are synthetic and contain no real people.

Map one evidence-shaped question for every lens

For the supplied waiting area, write one concrete question from each of the seven lenses and pair it with the most fitting W0/H1/D1/E1/U1/A1/S1 record or mark “not supplied.” Include at least one measurement question, one change-over-time question, one mechanism question, one resource tradeoff, one justified-value question, one form/experience question and one participation/decision question. Identify which proposed answer would exceed its record. Preserve the map before opening the key.

Check your attempt: Check the seven questions against evidence type and scope

A defensible map includes: science—how do sound readings vary across comparable times? W0 is only one three-reading snapshot; history—why was the stop moved in 2024 and whose accounts differ? H1 supplies the stated heat reason but not consultation; technology—how do character size and refresh work? D1; economics—which sign/seat mix fits $2,400 plus maintenance? E1 lacks maintenance costs; humanities—which values justify comfort, dignity and fairness? no record decides them; arts—how do contrast and surface form shape legibility and atmosphere? A1 supplies two perspectives, not a rule; society—who participated and who was absent? S1. Treating 71 dB as acceptable, two readers as all users or the calm description as consensus would exceed the supplied evidence.

- Every lens has a concrete question rather than a label
- Evidence type fits the question or is marked missing
- Measurement and justified-value questions remain distinct
- At least one overclaim is identified
