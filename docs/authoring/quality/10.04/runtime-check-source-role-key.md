Learning guide — unscored

Research plan and stopping rule

Decision: __. Subquestions: eligibility | limit | duration | effective date | exceptions. For each write: evidence needed | likely issuing body or expert | search terms or offline card | alternate perspective | consequence if wrong. Stop when every material claim has a fitting inspectable source or is explicitly unknown, the decision can be made within its stated scope, and one additional search is unlikely to change it. Stop earlier for unauthorized access, private data, paid access outside the plan or stakes requiring qualified advice.

Fictional lending-service source cards

P1 — Northstar Tool Library, “Standard Borrowing Policy,” issued 10 January 2026, effective 10 January 2026, locator northstar.example/policy. Text: members may borrow one item at a time for seven calendar days. The card does not define member eligibility or exceptions.

P2 — Northstar Tool Library, “April Limit Update,” issued 15 March 2026, effective 1 April 2026, locator northstar.example/april-update. P3 — River District Neighbors forum, post by “HelpfulNeighbor,” issued 20 February 2026, no official effective date, locator forum.example/thread-44. Text: “I think returns are monthly now.” The writer provides no policy link or role. These are fictional records; the locators must not be opened or treated as real rules.

Assign source roles and identify missing evidence

Classify P1, P2 and P3 by source role for the borrowing-rule question. Which card can change the item limit? Which can support the loan period after 1 April? What can P3 legitimately contribute? List the provenance fields present and the two required subquestions for which no card supplies an answer. Preserve the response before opening the key.

Check your attempt: Check source roles and gaps

P1 and P2 are primary official sources for the fictional service’s own rules. P2 is the only card that changes the item limit; P1 supplies the seven-day period and P2 explicitly retains other terms. P3 is a community claim that may reveal a question worth checking but has no authority to change policy. All cards supply a title or identifier, issuer/author, publication date and locator; P1 and P2 also supply effective dates. No card defines eligibility or exceptions.

- Source role is assigned in relation to the exact borrowing-rule claim.
- P2 changes only the item limit from its effective date.
- P3 is retained as a perspective or lead but not treated as policy authority.
- Eligibility and exceptions remain explicit evidence gaps.
