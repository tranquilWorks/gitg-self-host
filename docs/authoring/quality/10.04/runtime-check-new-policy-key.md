Audit an unseen effective-date conflict

Fictional source set: R1, official Lakeside Equipment Desk rule, published 2 May 2026, effective 5 May, locator desk/rules/r1: reservations last 48 hours; members may reserve one tool. R2, official amendment, published 12 May, effective 1 June, locator desk/rules/r2: reservations last 24 hours; all other R1 terms remain. R3, anonymous community post dated 15 May, locator forum/15: anyone can reserve three tools for a week. Write supported answers for 20 May and 3 June, citing each rule. Identify unknown eligibility and one verification route. Keep the attempt before checking.

Check your attempt: Check: Audit an unseen effective-date conflict

On 20 May R1 gives 48 hours and one tool; published R2 is not yet effective. On 3 June R2 gives 24 hours while the one-tool limit remains from R1. R3 has no policy authority; neither official card defines membership eligibility. Ask the issuing desk about eligibility or exceptions. Do not import the earlier library limit or duration.
