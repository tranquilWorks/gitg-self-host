Source and claim matrix

One row per claim: C-ID | exact claim | source code and locator | issuer/author | source role [primary / expert / community] | publication date | effective date | relevant passage in your own words | authority for this claim | conflict or limitation | disposition [supported / qualified / rejected / unknown]. Keep rejected and unknown rows. An answer is traceable only when another reader can locate the same source and see why it does or does not support the exact claim.

Bounded research answer and audit log

Question and decision context: __. Checked on: __. Confirmed: [claim plus source code]. Interpretation: [reasoning clearly labeled]. Unknown: [missing fact and consequence]. Before acting: [specific clarification route]. Scope limit: __. Audit one row per material claim: wording matches source [yes/no] | number/date matches [yes/no] | source has authority for this claim [yes/no] | current/effective for context [yes/no] | uncertainty retained [yes/no] | correction. A clean citation format cannot rescue a source that does not support the claim.

Resolve the dates and conflicting claims

A draft says: “The March document immediately allows two items for one month, and everyone may borrow.” Audit each clause against P1-P3. Write the corrected bounded answer for 20 March and for 2 April. State what remains unknown and why the community post does not settle it. Preserve the audit before opening the key.

Check your attempt: Check the date-sensitive answer

Every clause in the draft is defective. On 20 March, P2 has been published but is not effective, so P1 permits one item for seven days. On 2 April, P2 permits two simultaneous items and retains the seven-day period from P1. No card supports a one-month period or universal eligibility. P3’s unsupported belief cannot override the official sources. A correct answer labels eligibility and exceptions unknown and gives a specific official clarification route.

- Publication and effective dates are applied separately.
- The two-item limit begins on 1 April and the seven-day duration remains.
- Unsupported monthly duration and universal eligibility claims are rejected.
- The correction retains unknowns, source codes, check date and scope.
