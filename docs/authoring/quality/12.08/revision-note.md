# 12.08 prepared runtime repair

The former source-only draft used a simple coordination activity without separating
the materially different demands of power, speed and agility. The repair defines
coordination, reaction, speed, power and agility separately, then provides a complete
tabletop coordination route plus bounded existing-plan and instruction-first routes
for the higher-demand components. Each route declares prerequisites, materials,
technique or accuracy criteria, recovery, stop conditions and capacities not tested.

The author review disposition is **ready for independent cold-start and qualified
review**: the learner materials and answer reveals are complete and reject the
known false-equivalence failure. It is not formal quality acceptance. A tabletop
sequence cannot establish force, running speed, deceleration or athletic agility;
the package supplies no sprint, jump, loaded-power or rapid-direction-change
program. Actual learner, independent, qualified and owner review remain pending.
