Audit an adapted attempt

Check your attempt: Check performance and usability separately

R1 substitutes B1 for A2 and therefore fails the ordered-route criterion despite ending at B2. R2 follows C1, C2, B2 and explains up then left, satisfying this supported fictional task. The learner's easier-to-follow report concerns usability; the audio request identifies a remaining fatigue-related access need. Keep the agreed helpful package and explore audio with consent on a comparable fresh trial. These are simulated observations, not actual learner improvement.
