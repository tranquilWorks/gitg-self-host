Observe, imitate and check one tacit adjustment

Learning guide — unscored

Complete loose-cord bow reference

Use about 80 cm of soft cord loose on a table. Cross the ends, pass one end under the crossing and pull gently into a flat first knot. Fold one end into a loop. Wrap the other end around the loop's base, fold a bight of that end and push the folded bight through the opening behind the first loop. Hold both loops and pull them gently apart; leave free tails rather than pulling a tail through. Release by pulling one free tail. A willing demonstrator may show their equivalent method. Keep the cord away from bodies, animals, machinery and loads. This is not restraint, climbing, rescue or a safety knot. Stop for strain or confusion and ask the teacher to demonstrate again.

Audit a corrected attempt

Analyze this fictional record before revealing the check. First attempt has loops 9 cm and 3 cm and a right tail nearly swallowed. Teacher correction is to form the bight farther from the tip and equalize loops before adding tension. Learner restates leave enough tail and equalize first; teacher confirms. Retry has loops 7 cm and 6 cm, both tails longer than 8 cm, and releases when a free tail is pulled. Identify the preserved error, correction, change in disparity, functional check, and what this does not demonstrate. If practicing live, record actual observations instead of copying these measurements.

Check your attempt: Check the correction without overclaiming

The disparity decreases from 6 cm to 1 cm in the fictional record. The first near-swallowed tail and unequal loops justify the specific bight-position and equalizing cue. Restatement plus teacher confirmation checks understanding; the retry and successful release check this harmless result. It remains a simulated supported improvement, not actual learner evidence, independent retention, community membership or a safety-knot qualification. Failure to release would make the result partial despite better-looking loops; pain or humiliation calls for stopping.

- Restatement and retry distinguished
- Release checked separately from appearance
- Simulation and supported-result limits explicit
