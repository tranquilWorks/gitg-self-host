Create a note from a different source

Teaching source S2, “Comparing unit costs,” authored fictional card, revision 1: unit cost is total price divided by quantity in the same unit. A smaller unit cost does not establish affordability, suitability or less waste. Two pencil packs cost $6 for 4 pencils and $8 for 8 pencils. Close S2 and create a short own-words note with source, worked comparison, limitation, meaningful purchasing connection and a retrieval cue. Reopen S2 only to verify your note, preserving corrections. Add it as N4 under Shopping quantities; close it and test finding it from that cue.

Check your attempt: Check: Create a note from a different source

A sound note retains S2 revision 1, computes $1.50 and $1 per pencil, distinguishes lower unit cost from lower upfront cost, and names quantity needed or waste as an unresolved purchasing consideration. Many titles and phrasings work. A pasted card without a useful cue is incomplete. Report the actual retrieval result; the supplied calculation is not proof you found your note.

- A worked comparison and substantive limitation.
