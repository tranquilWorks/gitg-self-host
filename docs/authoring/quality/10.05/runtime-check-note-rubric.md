Supplied source card — comparing averages

Source S1, “Small-set summary definitions,” Grounded Growth fictional teaching card, revision 1, 8 September 2026. Mean equals the sum of all values divided by their count. Median is the middle value after sorting an odd-sized set; for an even-sized set it is the mean of the two middle values. For 1, 2 and 9 minutes, mean = (1+2+9)/3 = 4 minutes and median = 2 minutes. The high value pulls the mean above the median. Neither summary alone describes the full spread; range or the original values may still matter. This card is supplied instructional material, not an external research source.
