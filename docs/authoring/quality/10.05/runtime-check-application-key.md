Atomic note and revision template

Note ID and findable title: __. Source ID, title and revision: __. In my own words: __. Worked example with units: __. Meaningful connection—this helps when __ because __. Future-use question: __. Retrieval cue or index path: __. Known limit: __. Revision log: date | observed problem | exact change | reason. Keep one idea cluster that can stand alone; split the note only when two parts need different sources, cues or revision histories.

Three-entry retrieval index and test log

The fictional index begins with: Planning estimates → Comparing averages → Note N1; Shopping quantities → Unit conversion → Note N2; Reading queue → Source triage → Note N3. Replace N1’s title/path with your final choice but leave N2 and N3 as distractors. Retrieval test: cue used | expected note | found / alternate route / not found / support needed | elapsed band [under 1 minute / 1-3 / over 3] | barrier | repair | retest result. Time is diagnostic only, not a score, and accessibility support remains available.

Apply the retrieved note to a new dataset

After recording the actual delay and retrieval route, use the note to analyze 2, 3, 4, 5 and 16 minutes. Calculate mean and median with units. Explain which is more affected by the high value and what information is still missing if these summaries are used for planning. State whether you found the note, recalled the content without it, used a content hint or looked up the source. Propose one exact revision, then preserve the attempt before opening the key.

Check your attempt: Check the new application and revision

The sum is 30 across five values, so the mean is 6 minutes. The sorted middle is 4, so the median is 4 minutes. The value 16 pulls the mean upward more than it changes the median’s position. Neither summary states the range, individual values or shape of the set; here the range is 14 minutes. A useful revision could add “inspect spread or original values before treating either summary as a planning guarantee.” The evidence record must preserve the actual delay and distinguish note retrieval, recall, cues and source lookup.

- Mean is 6 minutes and median is 4 minutes with the calculation preserved.
- The explanation relates the extreme value to mean and median differently.
- Missing spread or distribution information is named.
- The revision responds to the observed use problem and the support/delay record is honest.
