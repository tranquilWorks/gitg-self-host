Observe, form and refine questions without inventing answers

Learning guide — unscored

Synthetic notice-board evidence packet

The fictional board is divided into four equal posting zones, Z1 through Z4. Twelve same-size notices are present. N1-N4 are in Z1, N5-N7 in Z2, N8-N10 in Z3 and N11-N12 in Z4. Overlap pairs are N1/N2, N2/N3, N5/N6, N8/N9 and N9/N10: five overlap pairs. Seven notices do not overlap another notice. Ten notices are fully inside a zone and two cross a zone boundary. A weekly log says expired notices were removed on schedule for four weeks. It contains no measure of readability, posting motives, board traffic or results from a board with clearer zones. This packet permits counting and question design; it does not establish why overlap occurs or what readers prefer.

Private question backlog

Use one row per question: Q-ID | exact wording | descriptive / causal / evaluative | priority reason | status [unexamined / investigated / answered within scope / partly answered / unresolved / deferred] | evidence and date | strongest limitation | next safe check | revisit trigger. Keep unresolved entries rather than converting uncertainty into a confident story. Store no names, message content or other private detail.

Record an honest resolution and next question

Using only the supplied packet, answer “How many overlap pairs are present?” Then record whether the question is answered within scope, partly answered or unresolved. Explain whether the result establishes why overlap occurs. Refine one causal question into a comparison with an independent variable, outcome and comparable condition. Add the original and refined questions to the backlog with an evidence limitation and revisit trigger. Preserve this attempt before opening the key.

Check your attempt: Check the resolution and refinement

The packet contains five listed overlap pairs, so the descriptive count is five and is answered within the packet’s scope. That result does not establish why posters chose locations or whether zones caused the overlaps. A defensible causal refinement names a condition and outcome, for example comparing overlap-pair counts under similar posting traffic before and after visible zone markings. The backlog should retain the descriptive answer, the causal unknown, the needed comparison and a trigger such as access to a comparable second record.

- The answer is five overlap pairs and cites the supplied list rather than guessing from the total notices.
- The resolution is limited to the synthetic packet.
- The causal refinement states condition, outcome and comparable context.
- The backlog retains uncertainty, next evidence and a revisit trigger.
