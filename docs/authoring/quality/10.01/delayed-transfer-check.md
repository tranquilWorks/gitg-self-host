Learn, retrieve, correct and check a new application

Learning guide — unscored

Delayed recall and new application

1. Explain mean.
2. Explain median, including what happens with an even count.
3. Explain range.
4. Identify and explain a numerator.
5. Identify and explain a denominator, including its nonzero condition.
6. Explain why a unit belongs with a measured number.

New application: three waiting times are 2, 5 and 11 minutes. Find the mean, median and range. Now change only the largest time from 11 to 17 minutes. Recalculate all three summaries, state each change in minutes, and explain which summary changes most and why. Record the actual delay and any help. Keep the attempt before opening its check.

Check your attempt: Check the delayed application

Mean: add the values and divide by their count. Median: sort the values; select the middle value for an odd count, or average the two middle values for an even count. Range: subtract the smallest value from the largest. Numerator: the number above the fraction bar; in a parts-of-a-whole example it counts the chosen equal parts. Denominator: the number below the bar; in that example it states how many equal parts form the whole, and it cannot be zero. Unit: the named quantity scale, such as minutes or meters; it gives a measured number meaning.

For 2, 5 and 11 minutes: sum 18 and count 3 give mean 6 minutes; median 5 minutes; range 9 minutes. For 2, 5 and 17: sum 24 gives mean 8 minutes; median 5 minutes; range 15 minutes. Changes are mean +2, median 0, range +6 minutes. The range changes most: its upper endpoint increased by six while its lower endpoint stayed fixed. The mean spreads that six-minute increase across three observations. The sorted middle stays 5. A response giving median 6 confuses the mean with the middle; a response saying all summaries increase by six does not follow the definitions.

- Check the original response before correcting it; do not replace the first attempt with the key.
- Each definition must explain its operation or meaning, not merely name the word.
- Calculations must match the supplied values and use the given units.
- Separate an incorrect response from an unattempted one; record support and actual delay without treating either as a personal score.
