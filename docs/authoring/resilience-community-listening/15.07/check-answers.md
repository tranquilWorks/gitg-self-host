# 15.07 — Corrective key — compare reasoning, not exact wording

1. It provides setting-specific experience, not an assessment of the lake, its cold-water risks or exit. Do not accept entry on that evidence. Obtain appropriate local conditions and qualified guidance; deferring the outing is valid.
2. Preserve assisted floating and assisted step exit as observed. Independent performance, other skills and other environments remain unassessed. Replacing “independent” with the actual supports is more accurate than labeling the entire effort a failure.
3. Seek trained/emergency help promptly from a safe position and provide location and observed difficulty. Do not enter, lean from unstable ground or improvise a human pulling drill. “Reach or throw” is bounded by safe position, equipment and competence; it is not an obligation to attempt an unsafe maneuver.
