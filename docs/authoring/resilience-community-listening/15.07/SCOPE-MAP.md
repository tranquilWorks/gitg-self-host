# 15.07 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Water competence and rescue awareness

**Scope (unchanged):** Swim, float, tread water, manage cold or moving water, and avoid unsafe rescue attempts.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Demonstrates basic aquatic competence and recognizes environmental hazards.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Swim, float and tread | Five-part sequence; assistance, omitted tasks and measured scope | Five-row observed/assisted/unassessed record | Treading and unsupported swimming remain unassessed in supplied case |
| Cold or moving water and environmental hazards | Cold-water shock awareness, current, visibility, entry, exit and flotation | A/B/C participation decisions and missing facts | No pool-to-river transfer or cold-water experiment |
| Rescue awareness | Recognize lack of progress; safe-position report; qualified help | Help-point communication and reach/throw limits | No in-water, unstable-edge, throwing or pulling drill |
| Demonstrated aquatic competence | Qualified adaptive lesson route with all five components recorded | Next instructor questions and honest water boundary | Dryland judgment and booking are not physical assessment |
