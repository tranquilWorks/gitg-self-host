# Individual author quality review — twelve sources

Reviewed on 2026-09-23 against the exact canonical entries, inherited current/recovery drafts, previous source companions and inspected primary sources. This is the author's editorial review. No independent reviewer, actual learner, qualified assessor or owner content acceptance is claimed. Formal M6K A/B/C stages remain pending.

## The standard used

Each guide was inspected for substantive concept teaching, a worked reasoning correction, complete supplied materials, an executable benign default, observable work, changed facts requiring revision, three fresh checks with explanatory answers, access adaptations and a specific real-transfer route. Every canonical facet is traced in its individual scope map. A facet's appearance as a word is not sufficient: the learner must use it in a decision, disposition, observation or qualified learning plan.

Physical competence remains a deliberately open requirement where the default cannot establish it. The alternative must still teach useful judgment; “ask an expert” alone is not a complete lesson. The guides therefore provide full paper cases and precise, appropriately supervised transfer tasks. Tests, counts and word length cannot close those physical or human-review gaps.

The broader reporting batch has not reduced the depth of individual guides: final counts are recorded in verification. More importantly, the twelve interventions use different objects of work and different failure conditions. Common navigation and evidence boundaries remain consistent, while the teaching and decisions are individually authored.

## Concrete changes made during this review

1. **15.09 incident communication:** the initial recovery teaching covered unauthorized-message checks but did not explicitly say what to do if messages had been sent. Added a minimal warning through an appropriate trusted route, without redistributing malicious links or private details.
2. **15.11 welfare-check trigger:** “after the notice” was insufficiently bounded. The plan now names 14:30 on Day 1 for agreed A/B check-ins and returns nonresponses to the organizer at that point.
3. **15.12 changing hazards:** respiratory cleaner-air guidance alone did not substantiate the smoke contrast. Added the successfully inspected EPA source and limited its application to reassessing the relevant action, not prescribing club ventilation.
4. **15.10 usable fallback copy:** the original copying instruction named agenda and notice route but then tested time and authority fields. Revised the instruction and supplied the complete harmless reference card so retrieval does not depend on guessing what to copy.
5. **15.08 retrieval usability:** added a minimal fictional initial station card with checking time, resource limits and unresolved health question. The learner must still calculate feasibility and update it from later facts.

## Cross-guide and answer-key review

Compared the new guides with each other and the preceding twelve. A mechanical scan found no identical sentence of at least eighteen whitespace-delimited words across distinct new guides or between new and preceding guides. This only detects literal repetition; it does not prove conceptual originality. The table above records the actual conceptual comparison. Shared structure is intentional, while each exercise changes a different decision-relevant fact.

All 36 fresh questions were read against their separate corrective answers. The keys explain the missing condition or mistaken inference, permit appropriately scoped uncertainty, and avoid rewarding a dangerous demonstration, invented consent or false certainty. Arithmetic examples use different values from the main cases. Not every question has a single exact sentence as its answer; no automated learner grading or scoring was introduced.

The 12 scope maps retain the canonical names, scopes, progress statements, classifications, evidence preferences and applicable professional boundaries. A short packet does not exhaust an entire competency. Later observations include supported, mixed, contradictory and unobserved elements, without counting author-supplied scenarios as real evidence. Actual human usability, specialist review and longitudinal transfer are not established by this editorial pass.
