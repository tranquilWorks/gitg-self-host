# 15.13 — Later observations — original fictional case

The fictional steward receives the report and says they will check discreetly. Record delegation acknowledged, not “harassment resolved.” No outcome from the steward's check is supplied.

The targeted person declines company: “No, thank you; I’m meeting a friend.” Accept the answer and allow space. Following to prove concern would contradict the offer's voluntary nature. Do not infer that the person was never affected or that another offer can never be helpful.

A different rehearsal partner proposes posting footage to “raise awareness.” No permission to record or share was given. That creates exposure unrelated to the person's requested support. Remove the posting step; a desire to help does not authorize publication.

In the staff-unavailable variant, the person leaves before you can safely offer support. Record no completed offer and outcome unknown. A thoughtful fallback decision is still planning work, but cannot be labeled a successful intervention.

No role-card response is an actual victim's consent. Your real evidence is the decision and benign rehearsal you performed, with the partner's voluntary feedback if actually obtained.
