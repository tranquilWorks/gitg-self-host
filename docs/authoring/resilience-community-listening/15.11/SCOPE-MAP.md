# 15.11 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Community disaster readiness and mutual aid

**Scope (unchanged):** Coordinate neighbors, skills, communication, supplies, transportation, welfare checks, and recovery support.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Participates in a realistic community plan or exercise rather than preparing only as an isolated household.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Neighbors, skills and communication | Consenting role assignments, source/time-preserving notice, teach-back | Bounded offer, accurate relay and correction | Solo work is preparation, no fabricated neighbor consent |
| Supplies and transportation | Four containers versus three requested; step-free transport unconfirmed | One stock remainder and explicit unmet transport need | Ordinary passenger places do not establish accessible capacity |
| Welfare checks | Agreed method/deadline, no response as uncertainty, organizer route | A/B check-in disposition, C excluded | No home entry, vulnerability list or diagnostic inference |
| Recovery support and realistic participation | Task states, handover, end of volunteering and contact use; real organization route | Closeout and actual accepted task if later performed | Acknowledged referral is not fulfilled need; qualified roles stay separate |
