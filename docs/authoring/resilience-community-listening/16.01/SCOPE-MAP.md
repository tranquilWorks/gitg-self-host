# 16.01 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Attentive listening

**Scope (unchanged):** Give full attention, reflect meaning, ask clarifying questions, and resist preparing a rebuttal.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** The speaker agrees that their position or feeling was understood before response.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full attention | Access supports, reduced competing tasks, honest missed information | Three content/importance/constraint anchors | No eye-contact or posture norm |
| Reflect meaning | Tentative summary, correction and holding competing wishes | Initial and revised reflection | No inferred diagnosis or imposed motive |
| Clarifying questions | A real information gap about timing/choice | Neutral question and changed understanding | No disguised persuasion or invented speaker reply |
| Resist rebuttal and speaker-confirmed understanding | Park response, invite correction, confirm before requested reply | Feedback status and response permission | Simulation, silence and polite assent are not real confirmed understanding |
