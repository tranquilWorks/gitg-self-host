# 16.01 — Fresh checks — attempt before opening the key

1. A colleague says, “I want to join the project, but I cannot work evenings.” Your summary is “You aren't interested.” Correct it and give one useful clarifying question.
2. A speaker confirms your understanding and then disagrees with your proposal. Does that invalidate the listening result?
3. A junior colleague smiles and says “fine” after you summarize. You did not explicitly invite correction, and they change the subject. What may you record?
