# 15.12 — Corrective key — compare reasoning, not exact wording

1. Retrieve the current underlying authority, date, setting, affected group and actions; check the club's applicable guidance. Healthcare instructions may have a different scope. Do not invent missing dates or isolation criteria from memory.
2. Reassess outdoor-air measures using current smoke guidance and the responsible venue operator. Use a confirmed suitable alternative or postpone/change format if unresolved. Neither the respiratory concern nor the smoke concern disappears because the other exists.
3. Only three confirmations are established; the other nine remain unconfirmed. Offer the agreed accessible alternative without requiring private diagnoses. Delivery, understanding and actual adherence are distinct, and none alone proves health outcomes.
