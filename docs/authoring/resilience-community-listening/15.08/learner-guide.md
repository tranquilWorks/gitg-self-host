# 15.08 — Travel and unfamiliar-environment safety

## Recover a travel plan when the last connection disappears

Use the invented destination card below before researching a real trip. In twenty-five minutes you will choose a feasible fallback, identify unresolved entry and health questions, and retrieve a minimal offline version of your plan. Do not book, travel, disclose identity documents or strand yourself to perform the exercise. Its point is proportionate preparation that still works when one ordinary dependency fails.

## A destination card is a set of dated claims

Entry permission depends on nationality, document type, transit points, purpose and dates. Advice for one traveler may not apply to another. Local law also differs from cultural etiquette: a legal restriction requires current authoritative verification, while a courteous question about a local custom need not become a stereotype about everyone there. Check local authorities and your own relevant consular service, not just another tourist's experience.

Health preparation includes destination hazards, needed care access, medicines and applicable requirements. A medicine permitted at home may have different rules at the destination or in transit. Verify with appropriate official sources and a clinician or travel-health service where relevant; do not change treatment to fit a worksheet. Travel insurance can have exclusions and may not cover a chosen activity. Record what you verified and what is unresolved instead of writing “health covered.” The U.S. State Department checklist illustrates these categories for U.S. travelers; it does not establish another nationality's eligibility. See S08 in [SOURCES](../SOURCES.md).

A contingency needs an endpoint, a way to reach it, sufficient resources and a communication route. “Get a taxi” leaves open whether it is lawful, accessible, available and payable. “Call home” may fail with no data, voice service or usable public phone. Independent verification matters when a stranger offers urgent transport or asks to hold your passport. A genuine disruption does not authenticate a proposed helper.

Keep documents and help details available without scattering sensitive copies. A minimal practice card can hold fictional locations and opening times. For a real trip, choose secure storage for sensitive records and an accessible backup suited to your circumstances. Do not send passport images to a stranger or put them in a public shared folder. A document copy can help replacement processes; it does not itself become valid travel authorization.

## Worked example

Mina's fallback note says “hotel near station.” At the time of disruption the desk confirms the hotel is full. Mina's original plan was a candidate, not a reservation. They ask official station staff to confirm a current alternative, including accessible transport and total cost, before leaving the staffed waiting area. The useful learning is checking the conditions of a fallback, not memorizing a particular hotel name.

## Complete fictional materials

You arrive at Larch Station at 20:10 with no mobile data. The final connection has been canceled. This is an invented place; the following facts apply only to this exercise.

| Option or resource | Dated station card, checked today at 19:50 |
| --- | --- |
| Staff desk | Open until 21:00; level route from waiting area; staff can confirm services and place an assistance call |
| Waiting area | Staffed while desk is open; no permission for overnight stay established |
| Lodge A | Room available until 20:40 if staff confirms hold; total room price 70 tokens; accessible room and entrance confirmed for the fictional traveler |
| Licensed desk-booked transport | Available now; total fare 18 tokens; suitable access confirmed; accepts the traveler's payment method |
| Station bus | Last departure 20:00; next departure tomorrow 07:00 |
| Traveler resources | 95 tokens available; 10 tokens reserved for an essential morning expense; actual documents retained privately |
| Communication | Mobile data unavailable; voice service untested; staff assistance call available; fictional trusted-contact label “Home contact” |
| Stranger's offer | “I can do it cheaply; give me your passport and follow me”; identity and service unverified |

Your pre-trip record has confirmed entry/document requirements for this fictional traveler but leaves medicine import permission unresolved. Local law research has a dated official-source placeholder; respectful culture preparation includes asking staff how the queue works and learning how to request help. These are different categories. The fictional record cannot authorize a real departure, and an unresolved medicine question needs resolution before any real trip.

## Resolve the disruption

1. Extract the live constraints: time, staff closing, data loss, access needs, money and reserved expense. Mark the bus unavailable tonight. Do not substitute yesterday's timetable.
2. Check Lodge A plus transport against available resources while preserving the reserve. Write the total and shortfall or remainder. Then decide whether to confirm that option, request a different supported alternative, or remain with staff while resolving the gap. No option becomes affordable because it is otherwise suitable.
3. Draft a factual request to station staff: connection canceled, access and communication needs, available spending limit, and request for verified accommodation/transport alternatives. Share only what they need; do not surrender documents to the unverified stranger.
4. Make a compact offline card using these fictional details: desk location/closing, current verified options and constraints, contact route, date/time checked and the unresolved question. The [supplied initial card](materials/station-card.txt) is a starting reference; make your own compact copy on paper or in a local note, close the original guide, and retrieve the card. Answer where help is, what fails tonight, and when the card needs updating. If retrieval fails, repair it and retry.
5. Write two pre-departure research tasks: resolve the medicine requirement through destination/transit authorities and appropriate clinical help; verify local laws and entry rules for the actual traveler and dates. Do not mark them complete from this fictional exercise.
6. Preserve your first decision and open the [later packet](later-packet.md). Revise only when a new fact supports it; keep the time of the new information.

Your artifact is a feasible-or-unresolved fallback decision, a private offline card, actual retrieval result and a short list of essential unanswered questions. A recovered plan may still require postponement; preparation does not guarantee transport or accommodation.

## Fit the exercise to your circumstances

For a lighter route, work only the timetable and cost check before adding documents and health. Use large print, screen-reader text or a trusted support person's reading, without removing your usual communication aids. Do not publish sensitive access or medical details in a group exercise. A harder version closes the staff desk early; identify the unverified replacement help route instead of inventing overnight permission.

For real transfer, select one actual planned trip and verify a small number of consequential details through current primary authorities: eligibility, medicine/health needs, local law, legitimate transport and emergency/consular contact routes. Check dates, opening times, payment and access. Ask respectful, setting-specific culture questions rather than inferring safety from nationality or appearance. Retrieve a privacy-conscious offline plan at home. No trip, purchase or public sharing is necessary to demonstrate the retrieval step. Finish the [fresh checks](check-prompts.md) and [key](check-answers.md).
