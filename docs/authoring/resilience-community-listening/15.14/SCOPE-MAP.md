# 15.14 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Workplace and activity-specific safety

**Scope (unchanged):** Learn hazard analysis, protective equipment, lockout or isolation, ergonomics, reporting, and stop-work responsibility.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Identifies hazards before work and can halt unsafe activity without normalization of deviance.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hazard analysis and ergonomics | Setup/activity/cleanup; reach, light, routes and others affected | W1–W5 analysis and actual harmless task if attempted | No contrived hazards or heavy movement |
| Protective equipment and control selection | Control hierarchy, hazard suitability, fit and substitution risks | Removal versus warning and new-obstruction check | No specialist PPE selection or safety certification |
| Lockout or isolation | Off versus stored energy; trained authorized site procedure | Paper-only W4 stop report | No real controls, unplugging, servicing, lock or guard changes |
| Reporting and stop-work responsibility | Unstable cabinet, repeated shortcut, authorized restart | Paused task and specific report; recheck after small change | No universal legal-right claim or normalization of uneventful shortcuts |

Use the [learner guide](learner-guide.md), preserve the first attempt before the [later packet](later-packet.md), and attempt [fresh checks](check-prompts.md) before the [corrective key](check-answers.md). See [inspected sources](../SOURCES.md) and the [individual author review](../QUALITY-REVIEW.md).

Canonical classification: applicability `context_contingent`; normative status `universally_relevant_as_literacy_but_conditionally_practiced`; formation modes: agency, interdependence, stewardship.

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: certification, performance_task, artifact, observer_feedback. This is preserved curriculum metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.

Canonical professional boundary (unchanged): Hands-on safety, medical, aquatic, survival, and defensive skills require qualified instruction and compliance with local law.
