# 15.04 — Individual canonical scope map

**Scope (unchanged):** Use boundaries, distance, voice, escape, bystander support, and conflict reduction before force.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can identify escalation and choose proportionate options that prioritize safety.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
