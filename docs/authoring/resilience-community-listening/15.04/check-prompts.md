# 15.04 — Fresh checks — attempt before opening the key

1. At a café, the nearest door requires squeezing past a person making threats. A second route is unknown. What should your plan say, and what should it avoid claiming?

3. You used a paper map successfully. A workplace asks whether you have demonstrated physical de-escalation competence. What evidence can you honestly offer, and what remains needed?
