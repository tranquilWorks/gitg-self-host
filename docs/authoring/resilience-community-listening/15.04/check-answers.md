# 15.04 — Corrective key — compare reasoning, not exact wording

1. The nearest door is not established as safely usable; the unknown route is not a verified alternative. Prioritize obtaining appropriate help and a safer option as circumstances allow; do not squeeze past to satisfy the exercise or invent an accessible exit. The important correction is acknowledging the changed danger and uncertainty, not choosing an unverified door confidently.
2. The condition keeps you engaged until the other person complies. An example revision is “I’m ending this conversation and going to the counter.” Use a verified endpoint, and omit speech if unsafe. Politeness alone does not make the original boundary executable.
3. Offer only the route decisions and paper rehearsal, with its limitations. Relevant employer procedures, qualified practical instruction and observed performance in the actual role remain separate. Course attendance by itself would not establish every practical skill either.
