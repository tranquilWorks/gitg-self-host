Read after preserving the first attempt. These outcomes are supplied possibilities, not reports of your actions.

A. The marker follows the level north route, and the learner asks the desk for help leaving. The phrase contains no demand to settle the queue dispute. This supports a usable first plan under the initial facts.

D. A partner declines rehearsal. A completed sketch remains a planning artifact; no interpersonal rehearsal occurred. Do not write “successfully de-escalated.”

Record which defect your own attempt exposed, or that none was observed in this limited exercise. Successful leaving does not require the fictional visitor to apologize, agree, or stop feeling annoyed. No outcome here establishes effectiveness in abuse, pursuit or physical assault.
