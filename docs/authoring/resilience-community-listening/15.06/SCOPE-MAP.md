# 15.06 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Outdoor survival fundamentals

**Scope (unchanged):** Secure shelter, water, warmth, navigation, signaling, fire, and food priorities.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can complete a safe supervised exercise and explain survival priorities.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shelter, water and warmth | Exposure-responsive priorities; accessible shelter; carried safe water and dry layers | Three specific dispositions and actual benign retrieval if attempted | No purification, cold exposure or shelter-construction qualification |
| Navigation and signaling | V/J/P route and return times; known location; contact and coverage dependencies | Route sketch, return decision, practice-only help message | No false distress or unsupported route assumption |
| Fire and food | Fire prohibition; warmth alternative; ordinary suitable carried food | Explicit not-authorized fire disposition and food provision | No fire, foraging or deprivation task |
| Supervised exercise and survival priorities | Instructor-led benign transfer with observed task and stop rule | Paper priority record now; actual supervised task later if undertaken | Indoor preparation does not satisfy outdoor performance requirement |
