# 15.06 — Fresh checks — attempt before opening the key

1. You have an emergency shelter item but have never used it. A trip partner says ownership proves preparedness. What is missing?
2. A fictional route takes 12 minutes to return from a checkpoint. At 14:18 the leader activates the return rule; the route remains suitable. What arrival time can you estimate, and what can that estimate not guarantee?
3. A fire ban removes your only planned warmth source. Should you practice secretly, omit warmth, or revise the trip? Explain a complete response.
