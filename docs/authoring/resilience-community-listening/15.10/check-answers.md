# 15.10 — Corrective key — compare reasoning, not exact wording

1. A 5-minute shortfall remains. Obtain explicitly agreed suitable capacity, change the task only with appropriate authorization, or use the cancellation route. Do not round availability up or treat willing effort as unlimited reserve.
2. Both copies share device access as a failure dependency. A genuinely available alternative needs a different accessible route and an actual retrieval check; two app names do not prove independence.
3. The cancellation and its communication remain consequential. Agree who owns the next action, when responsibility transfers, what was changed/notified and what remains unresolved. Return of a person is not proof that participants received a consistent update.
