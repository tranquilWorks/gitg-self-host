# 15.05 — Corrective key — compare reasoning, not exact wording

1. Sporting achievement and breaks do not validate the universal legal claim. Reject that claim and do not treat the offer as suitable until material safety and legal misrepresentation is resolved; seek a credible alternative. Legal authority does not come from a private training certificate.
2. Returning to punish differs from a current protective purpose, and changes in danger require reassessment. This guide cannot decide liability. Safer planning and current local professional advice are appropriate; do not invent an ongoing attack or infer lawful force from anger.
3. Record attendance and any actually observed exit-planning performance only. Physical defense remains unassessed. Verify relevant qualifications, consent, adaptation and injury arrangements before deciding on qualified practical instruction; deferral is valid.
