# 26.11 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Communal recreation and inclusive play

**Scope (unchanged):** Design leisure that accommodates different ages, abilities, finances, and comfort levels.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** More people can participate meaningfully because barriers and dominance were addressed.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ages, abilities, finances and comfort | Complete no-cost game, adult case and child-adaptation limits | Mode/pass rules and actual access requests | No diagnosis or age stereotype |
| Barriers and dominance | Fast open floor blocks typed answer; undescribed drawing | Specific first-round mechanism and agreed repair | Nominal turn is not usable opportunity |
| Meaningful participation | Different second prompt and received responses | Access facts separate from unknown enjoyment | No forced equal speech or universal accessibility |
