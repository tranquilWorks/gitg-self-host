# 26.11 — Fresh checks

Attempt these before opening [answers](check-answers.md).

1. A person passes in both rounds. Is making them speak in round three a valid test of inclusion?

2. A written answer arrives only after a fast open discussion ends. Is the primary repair to tell the writer to be quicker?

3. A new sequence admits written and drawn responses, but nobody answers the enjoyment question. What improved and what remains unknown?
