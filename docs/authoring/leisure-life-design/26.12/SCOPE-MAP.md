# 26.12 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Pilgrimage and meaningful journey

**Scope (unchanged):** Use travel or walking as disciplined encounter with place, history, devotion, memory, or personal transition.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** The journey includes preparation, restraint, reflection, and responsibility to the place and people encountered.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Place, history, devotion, memory or transition | Local library transition, archive perspective and carried question | Preparation that permits religious or nonreligious meaning | No fabricated shared historical meaning |
| Preparation, restraint and reflection | Suitable route, harmless no-post restraint and present users | Actual waiting/return and projection review | No deprivation, ritual copying or spiritual score |
| Responsibility to place and people | Authorized return of borrowed booklet | Receipt distinct from journey and unknown extra burden | No volunteer or community-effect claim |
