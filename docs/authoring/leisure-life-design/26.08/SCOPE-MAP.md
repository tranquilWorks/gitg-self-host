# 26.08 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Adventure and calculated challenge

**Scope (unchanged):** Choose experiences that expand courage and competence while managing real risk.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Completes a challenge that required preparation, uncertainty, and adaptive response.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Courage, capability and preparation | New library destination, known supports and complete map | Timed plan with chosen purpose | No clinical exposure or hazardous adventure |
| Real risk and adaptive response | Arrow ambiguity, staff-only boundary and turnaround | Actual supported route and shortened dwell | Help remains visible |
| Completed challenge and future limits | Same total time with different actions | Prediction comparison and bounded repeat decision | No general solo-navigation certification |

Read the [learner guide](learner-guide.md), preserve the initial attempt before the [later packet](later-packet.md), and answer [fresh checks](check-prompts.md) before [corrective answers](check-answers.md). See [sources](../SOURCES.md) and [individual review](../QUALITY-REVIEW.md).

Canonical classification: applicability cross_context_core; normative status cross_tradition_core_or_broadly_recurrent; formation modes: receptivity, interdependence, transcendence.

The canonical record has no professional_boundary field; its absence is preserved. Adaptations, fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Actual transfer requires suitable access, current conditions, willing participation and appropriate authority. No private participant records are required.

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: behavioral_adherence, artifact, community_feedback, longitudinal_review. This is preserved metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.

No runtime selection, action, evidence field, score, completion or participant record changes. Formal M6K A/B/C, independent cold-start, actual learner, qualified and owner content acceptance remain pending. Author review is not an acceptance receipt.
