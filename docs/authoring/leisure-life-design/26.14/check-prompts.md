# 26.14 — Fresh checks

Attempt these before opening [answers](check-answers.md).

1. A ten-minute session has seven minutes of clear drain view and three blocked. Can it be counted complete under R3 by averaging visibility?

2. A complete dry-weather observation reports no leaves visible. Does it establish a healthy ecosystem or successful drainage during heavy rain?

3. A project accepts one record and thanks the observer for an incomplete second record. How many usable comparison records exist, and may the observer remove plants to improve the next one?
