# 27.02 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Balancing competing goods

**Scope (unchanged):** Choose among work, family, health, service, rest, growth, pleasure, and security without pretending all can be maximized.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Makes difficult tradeoffs transparently and later reviews whether they were wise.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Work, family, health, service, rest, growth, pleasure and security | Eight dispositions and four feasible starting options | Protected obligations and live alternatives | No universal hierarchy or equal allocation |
| Cannot maximize every good | 180-minute budget, combinations and honest sacrifice | Two reasons, foregone good and reversal rule | No impossible make-up promise |
| Transparent choice and later wisdom review | Cancellation and mixed quiet-time outcome | Decision quality separate from pleasantness | No long-term balance or recovery claim |
