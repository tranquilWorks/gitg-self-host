# 26.07 — Fresh checks

Attempt these before opening [answers](check-answers.md).

1. A guest sees two participants leave before a closing ritual and writes “Their culture rejects this ritual.” What is established?

2. A public account describes a ceremony; a speaker agrees to one factual question. May a visitor record it and teach the ceremony?

3. Two participants give different accounts of a shared custom. Must one be discarded to produce a respectful summary?
