# 26.07 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Cross-cultural exploration

**Scope (unchanged):** Encounter unfamiliar ways of living without romanticizing, ranking, or demanding sameness.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can identify what was learned, what remains misunderstood, and how assumptions changed.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
