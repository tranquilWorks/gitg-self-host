# 26.10 — Communal festivity and celebration

## Mark a shared good without making a spectacle

Celebration gives a group a form for saying that something mattered. The form can be a feast, holiday, dance, game, music or ceremony; the essential choice is what this willing group wants to mark and who would bear the work. A small occasion is not a lesser substitute for an expensive one. Attendance, food and public happiness should not be entrance fees for belonging.

Use the fictional completion gathering below. Allow twenty minutes to plan, ten for the later account and ten for checks. No hosting, spending or personal disclosure is required. [W3C event accessibility guidance, S05](../SOURCES.md#S05) supports advance access planning and information available through suitable modes. The supplied event is not a compliance audit or a promise that every format fits everyone.

Worked example: after finishing a shared shelf, a group proposes a restaurant dinner. One member cannot afford it and another does not want public recognition. A brief private thank-you with no purchase and an optional written contribution can preserve the meaning. Calling the restaurant “optional” while giving all recognition there would not remove the barrier.

## The completed reading-corner project

Five fictional adults—Bea, Chen, Deni, Eli and Fran—have finished sorting a shared room's books. Bea and Chen sorted; Deni labeled; Eli arranged access; Fran checked the catalog from home. All agree the work may be marked privately. None consents to public names or photographs. Fran wants an asynchronous text contribution. Eli needs a clear route and a seat. Chen asks for no background music. Deni prefers to listen rather than speak. Bea can host but has only twenty minutes including setup and cleanup. All already have the needed room, chairs and a text channel; budget is zero.

The group agrees to a fifteen-minute occasion at 17:00. Setup takes three minutes and cleanup two. There is no food, gift, alcohol or costume requirement. The optional playful element is a one-sentence imaginary title for an ordinary object: “The chair that applied for annual leave.” People may offer another, read one, listen or pass. A holiday or religious form is not borrowed to make this invented occasion feel important.

Proposed sequence: two minutes to welcome people and name the shared work; four for optional appreciations including Fran's approved text; five for the playful element; two for a short shared memory; two to thank everyone and end. Keep the agreed access route clear and offer the text of spoken contributions when participants want that mode. Do not turn access requests into a request for diagnoses.

## Protect the meaning and the actual burden

1. Write an invitation giving purpose, time, cost, participation modes, no-photo boundary and a dignified decline. Check the fifteen-minute sequence against Bea's entire twenty-minute burden. Draft one appreciation for each contribution without ranking people by visibility.
2. Save the plan, then read the [later packet](later-packet.md). Identify what actually happened and whether the planned private memory was authorized. A photograph would not prove belonging even if one existed.
3. Review the optional feedback and actual preparation/cleanup time. Keep a mixed response. Decide one feature to repeat and one to simplify, without promising that every participant will feel joy next time.

## Reuse a form, not someone else's obligation

For support, use the supplied account only, shorten the live occasion with agreement or offer written participation. A feast can fit another group when dietary, financial, cultural, labor and access needs are genuinely addressed. Dance or music needs willing participation and suitable volume/movement; neither is required here. A private ceremony needs permission for the milestone or story being honored.

For a harder variation, Fran withdraws permission for the text before the occasion. Remove it even if the program is already printed; acknowledge remote work only within the remaining agreement. Do not ask why as a condition of respecting the withdrawal.

At a later actual small celebration, confirm the invitation and access first, then record only consented minimal facts about what was marked and the burden. Let someone decline praise or leave early without turning that into a character judgment. The event can create an opportunity for gratitude and memory; it cannot certify that every person felt included.

## Use the result at its proper scale

Capacity, access and context govern actual attempts. Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Human dignity is never scored.
