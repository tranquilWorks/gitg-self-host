# 26.10 — Fresh checks

Attempt these before opening [answers](check-answers.md).

1. A host has 18 minutes total; setup takes 4, the proposed occasion 13 and cleanup 3. Does it fit, and what can change?

2. One person attends silently and declines the optional feedback question. Can the host count their enjoyment as confirmed?

3. A remote contributor approved a private reading but not posting. Does unanimous enthusiasm from the people in the room authorize public sharing?
