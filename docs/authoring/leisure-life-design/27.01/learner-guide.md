# 27.01 — Life architecture

## Make a priority visible in an ordinary week

A value becomes easier to live when its ordinary arrangements support it. A calendar entry alone may fail if the materials are unreachable, a shared role is unagreed or the same money has been allocated twice. Life architecture connects those arrangements without treating a person as a project to optimize.

Use the complete fictional week below. Allow twenty-five minutes to design three changes, fifteen to compare the later week and ten for checks. No private calendar, financial account or health detail is required. [OpenLearn's wider work–life balance discussion, S09](../SOURCES.md#S09) includes personal, relationship, community and future commitments. The six-area map here is original synthesis, not a validated scale or a promise that planning removes structural barriers.

Worked example: a person values reading but stores their borrowed book beneath a heavy stack and schedules it during a care duty. Moving the book to a reachable place helps; relabeling the care duty as distraction does not. A suitable ten-minute opening after the duty may express the value better than a perfect hour that cannot occur.

## Morgan's three priorities and actual last week

Morgan chooses reliable existing care, welcome friendship and a usable household work surface. Work and essential care are already protected. The following windows are additional, genuinely discretionary capacity in this fictional week, not reclaimed sleep or someone else's labor.

| Window | Minutes available | Proposed action | Minutes planned |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Monday 18:00–18:15 | 15 | Place existing care supplies where used | 10 |
| Wednesday 19:00–19:20 | 20 | Send a specific welcome invitation | 5 |
| Saturday 10:00–10:30 | 30 | Clear Morgan's own work surface | 20 |

Last Monday supplies were difficult to reach and Morgan spent twelve minutes finding them; necessary care still occurred. On Wednesday Morgan thought about contacting a friend but sent nothing. On Saturday the surface remained covered because Morgan had assumed a housemate would remove shared items. The housemate had never agreed.

Morgan has two existing storage trays and 12 fictional resource tokens for discretionary use; essential costs are already protected. No purchase is necessary, and the proposed actions cost zero tokens. Care supplies may move only to an already suitable location within Morgan's existing care arrangement. Do not change treatment or discard materials. Morgan's own surface can be cleared without moving the housemate's possessions. The friend previously welcomed occasional invitations, but has not agreed to a meeting this week.

Map each priority across six areas: calendar, environment, resources, relationships, roles and recurring practice. For care: the Monday opening; suitable reachable supplies; existing tray with no purchase; an agreed help route if needed; Morgan's existing role; and a brief ordinary readiness check. For friendship, the environment may simply be a reachable contact method rather than furniture. Resources can mean time and access, not spending. Mark an irrelevant area honestly rather than inventing a duty.

## Design arrangements that other people have actually agreed to

1. Make one six-area card for each priority. Compare it with last week's facts and identify a mismatch. Keep the missing housemate agreement visible; do not translate it into Morgan's laziness or the housemate's failure.
2. Put one small action in each supplied window with a cue and fallback. Suggested cues are finishing the ordinary evening routine, opening the contact list and beginning the Saturday household period. Fallbacks may be arranging appropriate help, sending a shorter invitation or clearing only the accessible half of Morgan's surface. A fallback must still be useful and respect ownership.
3. Save the cards before the [later packet](later-packet.md). Classify each actual result as happened, adapted, deferred or blocked. Compare the lived week with the original priorities, not merely with how tidy the plan looks. Retain one useful arrangement, revise one mismatch and remove one unnecessary planning demand.

## Leave room for a life beyond the plan

Use spoken notes, a reader or three paper cards. No app entry, diagnosis, bank information or shared-role assignment is needed. For a harder variation, the Monday window disappears because care takes longer. Protect care, then reschedule or seek suitable support; do not mark the setup action completed because its purpose was good.

Later transfer is one ordinary week with three genuinely chosen priorities and a minimal actual record. A support person can clarify arrangements without choosing values on your behalf. A sent invitation is an action, not proof of friendship quality; an accessible surface is an environmental change, not a new identity. Calendar coherence cannot establish mastery or worth. The practical question is whether a particular arrangement helped a priority survive real constraints.

## Use the result at its proper scale

Capacity, access and context govern actual attempts. Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Human dignity is never scored.
