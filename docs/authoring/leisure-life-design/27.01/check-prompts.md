# 27.01 — Fresh checks

Attempt these before opening [answers](check-answers.md).

1. A weekly plan assigns a housemate to move shared belongings without asking. Does a neat calendar establish a workable arrangement?

2. A person spends 10, 5 and 12 minutes on the three actions instead of planned 10, 5 and 20. What is the difference, and does it show all three priorities are achieved?

3. An invitation is sent and receives no reply. May the review report a completed meeting or failed friendship?
