# 26.13 — Fresh checks

Attempt these before opening [answers](check-answers.md).

1. A person smiles politely after their supervisor jokes about their error. Does the smile establish free enjoyment or permission to repeat it?

2. A harmless object joke receives a neutral response and “please stop.” Is explaining the punch line a necessary repair?

3. A laugh occurs at 15:00, but a promised deadline correction remains unsent at 15:10. Can the pleasant exchange close the serious task?
