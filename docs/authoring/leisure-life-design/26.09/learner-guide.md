# 26.09 — Wonder and awe

## Give a question room to grow

Wonder begins with attending to something long enough to notice that your first description was incomplete. Awe may follow, but it cannot be required. A quiet encounter that corrects an assumption is worthwhile even without chills, gratitude or a dramatic change of life.

Allow five minutes of attention, ten of source study and ten of reflection, plus the fresh checks. The complete default is the scale description below, which can be read aloud. No telescope, travel, purchase, religious participation or looking at the Sun is involved. [NASA's Cosmic Distances, S04](../SOURCES.md#S04) explains astronomical distance units and light-travel delay. It supports the factual distinction here, not a promised emotional or therapeutic effect.

Worked example: a person examines a neatly repaired seam and first calls it effortless. Looking again reveals aligned edges, a change of stitch near a corner and a concealed join. The person revises “effortless” to “skill whose preparation I did not see,” credits the maker and still knows little about the process. Wonder need not turn the maker into a superhuman figure.

## A complete scale encounter: the signal card

Read this original description slowly: “A message travels from a lamp to a receiver. Nearby, arrival seems immediate. Move the receiver much farther away and the travel time matters. A distant light can tell you about an earlier event, not necessarily what its source is doing at the instant you receive it.” This is a conceptual description, not a home optics experiment.

The accompanying source facts are deliberately few: a light-year measures distance, and NASA describes sunlight taking about eight minutes to reach Earth. Read the source for context; no direct solar observation is requested. In a separate invented signal model, light from source A takes 2 years to reach a receiver and light from B takes 5 years. Both signals arrive at the same labeled receiver time, year 20. The model uses a fixed shared clock, no expansion or relative motion, and no brightness information. It is a teaching simplification, not a claim about named stars.

Before calculating, notice what is missing: source appearance, current state and any personal meaning. Write three details of the model, two questions and one initial association. An association such as “I am always seeing the past” is not itself a measured scientific or philosophical conclusion.

## Return to the object with one better distinction

1. Attend for five minutes, or a shorter suitable period, using text, speech or tactile counters if useful. Keep communication aids. Record a neutral response if that is what occurs. Different entry points—art, a natural pattern, an authorized sacred space or skilled work—remain legitimate alternatives, with their own permissions and sources.
2. Use the model to distinguish arrival time from emission time. Calculate both emission years, then separate facts, implications within the model and imaginative responses. Ask what observation would be needed to know either source's current state rather than filling the gap with a feeling.
3. Save the first note and read the [later packet](later-packet.md). Identify one changed question and one proportionate response actually made. An intention to become more grateful is different from crediting a source in a note or giving a familiar object another careful look.

## Perspective without a compulsory revelation

Awe can widen attention, but an immense distance does not make ordinary responsibilities meaningless. Do not use the encounter to dismiss another person's grief or demand that they feel small. Keep factual learning, personal meaning and action on separate lines. None is a dignity score.

For support, use only the written model with two labeled counters, or have someone read it. For a harder case, both sources emit in year 18: calculate their different arrival years rather than assuming simultaneous reception. No observational equipment is needed. On a later real encounter with art, craft, nature or science, seek one trustworthy explanation for a noticed feature, credit it and return to the object. In a sacred space, follow the keepers' actual invitation; access does not authorize imitation of ritual.

A useful result may show greater attention and a revised question with no emotional change. An intense feeling without checking a mistaken unit leaves the mistake intact. A grand pledge with no follow-through does not establish renewed responsibility. The scale model can teach a distinction; it cannot prove humility, health benefit or ultimate metaphysical truth.

## Use the result at its proper scale

Capacity, access and context govern actual attempts. Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Human dignity is never scored.
