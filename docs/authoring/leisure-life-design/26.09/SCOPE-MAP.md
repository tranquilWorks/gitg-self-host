# 26.09 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Wonder and awe

**Scope (unchanged):** Seek encounters with nature, science, art, sacred spaces, craftsmanship, or human excellence that expand perspective.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Awe produces attention, humility, gratitude, or renewed responsibility.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nature, science, art, sacred space, craft and excellence | Multiple routes; seam worked example and complete signal model | Specific noticing and source-informed question | One science route does not exhaust awe |
| Expanded perspective | Arrival/emission distinction and neutral later report | Revised unit and narrower knowledge claim | No forced emotion or metaphysical inference |
| Attention, humility, gratitude or responsibility | Actual source credit in a later note | Bounded response separated from grand pledge | No therapeutic outcome or virtue score |
