# 19.05 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Pregnancy, birth, and postpartum responsibility

**Scope (unchanged):** Understand bodily risk, consent, support, medical decisions, recovery, infant care, and postpartum mental health.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** A practical support and decision plan centers the pregnant or postpartum person's safety and agency.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bodily risk, consent and medical decisions | Ongoing agency and prompt qualified care for actual concerns | Role map and tabletop interruption response | No birth management, diagnosis, simulated symptom or false call |
| Pregnancy and birth support | Preferences guide discussion, not guaranteed course or helper authority | Accessible professional questions and support limits | Consent to a meal is not consent to clinical participation |
| Recovery and infant care | Meals, rest, visitors and separate competent infant caregiver | Dev’s accepted meal; Sal’s 18:00–19:00 handoff | No visitor/photo permission or infant authority inferred |
| Postpartum mental health and safety | Mental as well as physical concerns belong in care access | Useful quiet hour separated from health and next-day coverage | No clinical recovery, mental-health assessment or enduring coverage claim |
