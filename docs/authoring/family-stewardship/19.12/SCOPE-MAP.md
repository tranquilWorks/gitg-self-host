# 19.12 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Family labor, provision, and invisible work

**Scope (unchanged):** Recognize domestic, emotional, logistical, financial, and caregiving labor and distribute it fairly.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Responsibilities are visible, negotiated, and reviewed rather than silently assigned.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic, logistical and cognitive labor | Notice, plan, decide, remind, perform and follow up | Whole drinks-and-table unit traced and accepted | Task name or purchase alone is not end-to-end ownership |
| Emotional, financial and care work | Welcome, tension, resource limits and appropriate care authority | 18 available, 14 spent, 4 remaining; other work visible | Feelings not invoiced; money/gender/history cannot assign permanent roles |
| Fair negotiation and provision | Capacity, resources, decision room and help trigger | Owen’s completed unit with no supplied reminders or rescue | Equal hours not automatic fairness; essential care remains covered |
| Reviewed responsibilities | Hidden work and unnecessary standards both examined | Twelve-minute optional redo; next unit accepted, other redistribution unaccepted | One cycle does not settle total fairness or all family labor |

Preferred evidence types: qualified_assessment, observer_feedback, artifact, longitudinal_review. This is preserved metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.
