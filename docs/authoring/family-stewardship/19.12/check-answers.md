# Corrective answers

1. Visible preparation transferred; planning and monitoring did not. Make the remaining work visible, clarify scope and resources, and seek explicit acceptance of a whole unit rather than counting a name on a chart as full ownership.
2. Examine both actual completion and whether the extra standard was necessary and agreed. Optional rework should not automatically prove failure or return ownership. Negotiate the standard and decision room while preserving essential access and care needs.
3. No. Money, capacity, time and care obligations matter, but income and past patterns do not confer unilateral assignment rights. Negotiate a workable distribution with the people bearing the work and review actual cycles; keep essential care covered during changes.
