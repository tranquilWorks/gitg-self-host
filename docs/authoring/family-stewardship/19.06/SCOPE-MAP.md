# 19.06 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Miscarriage, infertility, and reproductive grief

**Scope (unchanged):** Recognize grief, ambiguity, medical burden, relationship strain, and differing meanings around reproductive loss.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can offer or receive support without minimizing, moralizing, or forcing disclosure.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grief and ambiguity | Miscarriage, infertility and treatment disappointment have different meanings | Known/assumed/unknown preparation | No cause, pregnancy status or future desire inferred |
| Medical burden and relationship strain | Practical burden can matter without disclosure; partners differ | Bounded meal offer, unavailable transport and Ash unknown | No minimizing, moralizing, synchronized grief or forced details |
| Offer or receive support | Acknowledgment, space and feasible help independent of gratitude | Declined call, accepted meal and actual delivery | Practical permission does not authorize further probing |
| Respectful follow-up | Person’s chosen pace governs contact | Two-week space honored; future draft unsent and unscheduled | No automatic later contact, resolved grief or support to both partners |
