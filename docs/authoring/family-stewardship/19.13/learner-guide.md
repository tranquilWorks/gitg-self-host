# 19.13 — Co-parenting, separation, and family restructuring

Prepare one authorized handoff that keeps adult conflict out of a dependent person's job. Allow twenty minutes to plan and ten to rehearse a fictional disruption. Use a real ordinary handoff only within existing care, custody and safety arrangements. Never create a late pickup, substitute a collector or involve a child in an experiment. If contact is unsafe or prohibited, use the appropriate authorized intermediary or professional route.

## Keep continuity separate from the adult relationship

Breakup, divorce, relocation, estrangement and a new partnership can change who is available, how information travels and what a dependent person expects. The legal, emotional and logistical questions are related but different. A clear breakup message does not settle custody or housing. A new partner does not automatically become an authorized collector. A move cannot simply be inserted into a schedule when it changes an existing arrangement or required permission.

Children and other dependents should not have to carry the adult dispute. Triangulation occurs when they are made a messenger, witness for one side or source of information about the other. Asking a child to relay a hostile message or choose whose account is true transfers adult work onto them. Listen to their needs without making them negotiate contact or prove loyalty.

Retaliation can appear as delayed information, withheld necessary items, financial pressure or disruption of a handoff. A task review should ask whether essential continuity is protected, not whether one adult won the argument. A dependent person's care, comfort and access are not bargaining chips. Their views matter within the proper process; they are not responsible for resolving an adult's hurt.

Predictable arrangements require more than a calendar entry. Clarify permitted time, place, authorized adults, necessary items, accessibility needs, communication channel and exceptions. Use existing professional care instructions where relevant; this exercise does not copy private medical details into an app. Define how the actual transfer is confirmed. A message saying someone intends to arrive is not the same as an accepted handoff.

Ordinary cooperation guidance has limits. Threats, coercion, prohibited contact or safety concerns may require protected communication or professional involvement. Do not insist on direct conversation, mediation or a joint meeting as proof of mature co-parenting. Existing orders and safety plans govern; changing them requires the appropriate qualified process.

## Worked example

A pickup is delayed. One adult considers sending the child a message blaming the other household. A better response uses the permitted adult channel, states the delay and follows the existing authorized fallback. If a proposed substitute is not approved, convenience does not create permission. The child can receive a simple age-appropriate explanation of what will happen without being asked to relay accusations or choose a collector.

## Supplied fictional case

Following a household change, an existing authorized arrangement provides for an ordinary pickup at 16:00 by Morgan. The permitted adult channel is an established scheduling service. An existing fallback says the child remains with the currently authorized caregiver until an approved handoff is confirmed. The case supplies no right to improvise a new collector or location. Necessary belongings are prepared, and a private care instruction remains in its proper channel.

At 15:40, Morgan reports a twenty-minute delay and proposes a new partner, Ellis, instead. Ellis is not on the currently approved collector arrangement. The exercise does not establish why approval is absent and does not require the child to decide. The safe fictional task is to preserve the authorized fallback and communicate facts through the permitted channel.

## Plan the handoff and the exception

1. Extract the existing authority, permitted time and place, adults, items, access needs and channel. Mark anything unknown rather than inventing a custody rule. Output: a compact handoff card whose sensitive details remain outside app evidence.
2. Draft a neutral practical update. Keep adult grievances and relationship history out of it. Output: time, relevant change, request and confirmation needed, without a child messenger or blame statement.
3. Apply the supplied delay and collector disruption in fiction. Check Ellis's status against the actual arrangement and use the existing fallback. Output: what remains authorized, what is not yet approved and which professional route would handle a requested change.
4. Distinguish proposed arrival, acknowledged plan and actual transfer. For a real routine event, use only the existing appropriate confirmation process. Output: the actual endpoint or a truthful statement that it has not occurred. Never stage a failed handoff for evidence.
5. Save the plan before opening the [later packet](later-packet.md). Review continuity, the dependent person's ordinary needs and any adult burden created by delay. Output: one logistical improvement that does not rewrite legal or safety arrangements.

## Support and transfer

For support, use only the fictional card and a two-sentence neutral update. For a harder version, direct contact becomes prohibited: preserve the authorized intermediary and seek qualified help rather than introducing an informal workaround. Real transfer may involve school transport, elder support across households or another routine change, but authority and the dependent person's interests must remain explicit.

One smoothly completed pickup cannot establish that separation is resolved, conflict is absent or the arrangement is legally adequate. A review can identify a specific continuity success and an unresolved scheduling burden. Grief, estrangement and future partnership decisions may require other support and time. The dependent person does not owe reassurance that the adults handled everything well.

## Scope and evidence boundary

Medical, reproductive, child-safety, guardianship, and elder-care decisions require qualified local guidance.

Applicability and normative status are role-conditional. No reproductive, parenting or care role is compulsory. Fiction does not establish actual participant evidence. Keep identifying, clinical and private family information outside app records. Formal M6K A/B/C, independent, actual learner, qualified and owner content acceptance remain pending.
