# 19.13 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Co-parenting, separation, and family restructuring

**Scope (unchanged):** Protect children and dependents during breakup, divorce, relocation, estrangement, or new partnership.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Reduces triangulation, retaliation, instability, and use of dependents as leverage.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Breakup, divorce, relocation, estrangement and new partnership | Emotional/legal/logistical changes and existing authority | Handoff card with approved adults and permitted channel | No unilateral custody, contact or relocation change |
| Protect children and dependents | Continuity, necessary items, accessible explanation and no leverage | Existing authorized caregiver continues until actual transfer | No child messenger, chooser, retaliatory withholding or live test |
| Reduce triangulation and instability | Adult facts stay in permitted adult channel | Delay handled; unapproved Ellis does not collect | Direct contact is not required when unsafe or prohibited |
| Review actual handoff and burden | Intention, confirmation and transfer differ | 16:25 actual, 25 minutes after original and 5 after revised | No standing change, resolved conflict or broad adjustment claim |
