# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. An adult calls alphabetic book order a safety rule, although returning the books to the basket resolves the shared-space need. What distinction matters?
2. A child returns one book with help. May the adult withhold dinner or affection until a whole shelf is sorted?
3. The child asks for more help on a tired day. Does that prove lost competence, and what should determine the next demand?
