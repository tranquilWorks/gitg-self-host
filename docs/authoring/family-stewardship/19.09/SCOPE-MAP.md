# 19.09 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Developmentally appropriate autonomy and discipline

**Scope (unchanged):** Adjust expectations, explanation, boundaries, consequences, and responsibility to age and ability.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Care increases safety and competence without humiliation, violence, or control beyond developmental need.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Age and ability appropriate expectations | Observed task, comprehension, access and fatigue; no fixed age cutoff | Two-book task versus unsupported shelf demand | No developmental diagnosis or peer comparison verdict |
| Explanation, boundaries and consequences | Shared-route purpose versus alphabetic preference; related repair | Brief reason, real choice and assisted return | No unrelated deprivation, humiliation, violence or affection withdrawal |
| Autonomy and responsibility | Help requests and understanding matter alongside action | Cue retained; adult work and shared carrying visible | Obedience does not establish independent skill |
| Adjust to developmental need | Second opportunity responds to fatigue and first observation | Task retained with support, not automatic increase | Changed conditions prevent a simple progress/regression claim |

Canonical classification: applicability role_conditional; normative status role_conditional; formation modes: interdependence, stewardship, receptivity.

No reproductive, parenting or caregiving role is compulsory. Fictional preparation and no attempt remain legitimate. A live route requires the appropriate actual role, permission and task authority; a worksheet creates none. An actual safeguarding or urgent care concern must use the proper prompt route rather than wait for an exercise.

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: qualified_assessment, observer_feedback, artifact, longitudinal_review. This is preserved metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.

Canonical professional boundary: Medical, reproductive, child-safety, guardianship, and elder-care decisions require qualified local guidance.

No runtime selection, action, evidence field, score, completion or participant record changes. Supplied fiction cannot count as actual observer evidence. Formal M6K A/B/C, independent cold-start, actual learner, qualified and owner content acceptance remain pending; author editorial review is not an acceptance receipt.
