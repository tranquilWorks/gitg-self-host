# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. An inherited joke humiliates some participants, but an adult says removing it destroys the tradition. What can be preserved and changed?
2. A relative agrees to tell a story at a gathering. Does that authorize recording, posting the story or sharing an unapproved photograph?
3. A solo learner writes a revised ritual and imagines warm feedback. What evidence of transmission and belonging has actually occurred?
