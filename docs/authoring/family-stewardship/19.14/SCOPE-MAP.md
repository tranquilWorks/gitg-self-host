# 19.14 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Family ritual, heritage, and intergenerational continuity

**Scope (unchanged):** Preserve stories, language, celebration, mourning, values, and practical knowledge while revising harmful inheritance.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** A family practice strengthens belonging and transmits meaning without coercive nostalgia.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stories, language and practical knowledge | Spoon story, translated word and safe empty-utensil gesture | Actual fictional telling, translation and demonstration | Not every heritage form taught by one object; no food task |
| Celebration, mourning and values | Different meanings, chosen family and optional grief participation | Patient attention preserved without forced belief | Attendance or fluency is not loyalty, authenticity or resolved grief |
| Revise harmful inheritance | Remove humiliating joke and exclusionary introduction | Feedback leads to changed wording actually retold | No coercive nostalgia or disclosure required to justify change |
| Belonging, permission and continuity | Accessible choices, other memories, opt-out and separate recording consent | Photograph withheld; no recording; newcomer voice retained | Solo draft cannot claim transmission; one event cannot establish whole-family belonging |
