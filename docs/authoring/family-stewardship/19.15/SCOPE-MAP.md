# 19.15 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Caregiver limits, respite, and shared responsibility

**Scope (unchanged):** Recognize burnout, resentment, role captivity, and the need for support, respite, and distributed care.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Care remains safer and more sustainable because limits and backup are explicit.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Burnout, resentment and role captivity | Concrete fatigue/errors/no-time pattern without diagnosis or recipient blame | Bounded relief need and essential/optional/movable tasks | One break not treatment or proof of moral worth |
| Support and distributed responsibility | Willing competent authorized backup, scope, resources and teach-back | Fin accepts interval; service remains uncontacted | General offer/kinship/booking not actual coverage or skill certification |
| Respite and explicit limits | Start/return acknowledgments and required versus optional contact | 14:15–16:00 actual coverage; care continues before start | No abandonment or fabricated 120-minute relief |
| Safer sustainable care | Review caregiver/backup experience and longer-term gap | 105 coverage minutes; 10 monitoring; 95 reported rest | Recurring commitment, funding and resolved burnout remain unproved |
