# Individual author editorial review

Reviewed 24 September 2026 for this source-only publication. This is author editorial review, not independent review. Each of the twelve guides, twelve separate later packets, 36 fresh prompts/corrective answers and exact canonical maps was examined for scope, depth, usability, distinctiveness, source limits and missing human evidence. Shared headings and professional/evidence boundary wording do not replace individual teaching or tasks.

Word counts describe saved text, not a quality quota or proof of learning. Source checks and literal repetition scans support integrity, not pedagogy. Formal M6K A/B/C, independent cold-start, actual learner, qualified and owner content acceptance remain pending. No participant response, safeguarding endorsement, professional acceptance or independent receipt is fabricated.
