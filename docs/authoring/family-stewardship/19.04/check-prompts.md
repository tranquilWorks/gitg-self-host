# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. A public-health definition uses a duration threshold. May a learner treat it as a personal instruction to delay asking about symptoms or reproductive concerns?
2. A pregnancy-prevention overview is medically reliable. Does it alone establish a suitable method and infection protection for a particular person?
3. A clinic offers a fertility consultation. What has that established about prognosis, and what decision still belongs to the person?
