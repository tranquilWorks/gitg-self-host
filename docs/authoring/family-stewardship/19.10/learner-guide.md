# 19.10 — Education, formation, and modeling in family life

Teach one safe practical action, show how its values work, and give the learner an independent retry. Allow fifteen minutes to prepare, fifteen to teach, and a later short retry. Use an existing appropriate family or care role with a willing learner, or use the supplied fiction. Do not recruit a dependent person to prove your effectiveness. Independence means appropriate agency with accessible support, not doing everything alone.

## Instruction carries more than a procedure

Family teaching transmits practical skill through demonstration, explanation and opportunities to try. It also models ethics: whether adults acknowledge mistakes, respect property and tell the truth when concealment would be convenient. Saying that honesty matters while hiding damage teaches a conflicting lesson. Make the connection visible without turning every ordinary error into a moral judgment about the person.

Emotional regulation can be modeled by pausing, naming a manageable frustration and choosing a repair. An adult need not appear flawless. A calm correction of their own mistake can show how to proceed when something goes wrong. The learner is not responsible for soothing the adult or demonstrating gratitude for the lesson.

Culture, faith and worldview may inform greetings, thanks, care for objects and why a family values a practice. Name a custom as a custom, explain its meaning and leave room for questions or a different interpretation. Teaching a family tradition does not require compulsory belief, public profession or punishment for doubt. A dependent person's voice includes questioning a reason, declining to disclose a belief and choosing their own appropriate words.

Media judgment enters ordinary decisions when an instruction arrives through a forwarded message, a video or a confident claim. Ask who has authority over this particular decision and how the instruction can be checked. A widely shared message about a library rule is not necessarily the owner's actual rule. Verification is a practical skill, not just skepticism toward unfamiliar people.

Instruction should reveal understanding beyond imitation. A learner may copy the adult's exact phrase without knowing its purpose. A fresh but comparable retry can show whether they can explain, choose a route and seek help when something is unknown. Necessary communication or physical supports can remain; removing them to manufacture independence would change the task unfairly.

## Worked example

An adult notices a bent page before returning a borrowed book. They say they feel embarrassed, stop themselves from hiding it and check how the owner wants damage reported. The learner sees property care, honesty, emotional regulation and verification within one ordinary action. The adult explains a household thank-you custom and lets the learner use their own words. The result is a teachable process, not a requirement to adopt the adult's personality or beliefs.

## Supplied fictional case

Authorized caregiver Mira helps Leo return a light borrowed book. The book and note are owned or fictional props; no real damage is created. A supplied note from the fictional owner says to return it to the designated shelf and report any noticed damage to the owner. A forwarded message claims all borrowed books should be left outside, but its author and applicability are unknown.

Mira demonstrates inspecting the book, recognizing the bent page, checking the owner's instruction and preparing an honest message. A family custom adds thanks, but Leo may choose their wording. A second fictional book later provides a retry with a different unknown. There is no need to post a video, create an account or record the learner's beliefs.

## Teach, retry and listen

1. Ask what the willing learner already knows and what help would make the task accessible. Identify the small practical goal and the relevant owner instruction. Output: a starting point that permits correction of your assumption about their knowledge.
2. Demonstrate inspection, source checking and an honest report. Model a brief regulated response to embarrassment. Explain which part is a property responsibility and which part is a family custom. Output: an observable demonstration with a reason for each essential action.
3. Invite the learner to explain the purpose and choose their own appropriate message. Ask what they would do about the unverified forwarded instruction. Output: their reasoning or established communication response, not a compulsory recitation. Accept a question as useful information about the lesson.
4. Offer the second fictional book for a retry with the same accessible supports. Observe whether the learner checks what is known, notices an unknown and seeks the appropriate clarification. Output: actual actions and help used, separate from your impression that the lesson went well.
5. Ask what was confusing or overexplained, and change one instruction accordingly. Save your account before the [later packet](later-packet.md). Output: a revision shaped by the learner's voice and a limited claim about the demonstrated skill.

## Support and transfer

For support, use pictures for inspect, check and return, and work through one step together. Accept pointing or an established communication aid. For a harder fictional case, supply two plausible messages with different origins: the learner must explain which question each can answer and seek clarification when authority is unclear. Do not reward confident guessing.

For real transfer, choose another harmless return or care-for-property task in your existing role. Keep actual owner communication and private learner details out of app records. A retry shows something about this small skill under these conditions; broader ethical formation, emotional regulation and independent judgment require repeated opportunities and appropriate outside perspectives. No compliance score can substitute for those observations.

## Source limits

[The AAP policy, S11](../SOURCES.md#S11), supports constructive teaching with appropriate expectations and without physical punishment or shaming. It does not validate this borrowed-book lesson, establish a media-literacy curriculum or determine a family's worldview. Those elements are original applications of the canonical scope. The task makes learner capability and voice inspectable while leaving comprehensive formation unclaimed.


## Scope and evidence boundary

Medical, reproductive, child-safety, guardianship, and elder-care decisions require qualified local guidance.

Applicability and normative status are role-conditional. No reproductive, parenting or care role is compulsory. Fiction does not establish actual participant evidence. Keep identifying, clinical and private family information outside app records. Formal M6K A/B/C, independent, actual learner, qualified and owner content acceptance remain pending.
