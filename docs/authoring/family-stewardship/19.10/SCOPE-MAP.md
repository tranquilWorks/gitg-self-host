# 19.10 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Education, formation, and modeling in family life

**Scope (unchanged):** Teach practical skill, ethics, emotional regulation, culture, faith or worldview, media judgment, and independence through example and instruction.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** A dependent person gains real capability and voice rather than merely compliance.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Practical skill and ethics | Inspect, verify instructions and honestly report damage | Demonstration and fresh missing-location retry | Copied thanks is not understanding; no actual return inferred |
| Emotional regulation and modeling | Adult acknowledges embarrassment and repairs without blame | Observable pause and honest response | Learner need not soothe adult; no durable regulation claim |
| Culture, faith/worldview and voice | Custom versus responsibility; questions and own wording allowed | Optional thanks and learner-requested instruction revision | No required belief, disclosure or personality imitation |
| Media judgment and independence | Owner authority versus unverified forwarded instruction | Checks source, notices unknown and asks with supports retained | No confident guessing, removed access aids or global independence claim |

Read the [learner guide](learner-guide.md), save an attempt before the [later packet](later-packet.md), and answer the [fresh checks](check-prompts.md) before [corrective answers](check-answers.md). See [sources](../SOURCES.md) and [individual review](../QUALITY-REVIEW.md).

Canonical classification: applicability role_conditional; normative status role_conditional; formation modes: interdependence, stewardship, receptivity.

No reproductive, parenting or caregiving role is compulsory. Fictional preparation and no attempt remain legitimate. A live route requires the appropriate actual role, permission and task authority; a worksheet creates none. An actual safeguarding or urgent care concern must use the proper prompt route rather than wait for an exercise.

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: qualified_assessment, observer_feedback, artifact, longitudinal_review. This is preserved metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.

Canonical professional boundary: Medical, reproductive, child-safety, guardianship, and elder-care decisions require qualified local guidance.

No runtime selection, action, evidence field, score, completion or participant record changes. Supplied fiction cannot count as actual observer evidence. Formal M6K A/B/C, independent cold-start, actual learner, qualified and owner content acceptance remain pending; author editorial review is not an acceptance receipt.
