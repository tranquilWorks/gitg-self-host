# 19.11 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Elder care, dementia, and later-life decision-making

**Scope (unchanged):** Understand changing capacity, advance planning, memory loss, safety, autonomy, respite, and family conflict.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** A care plan balances protection, dignity, legal authority, and caregiver sustainability.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Changing capacity, memory loss and dementia | Observed events, diagnosis, decision-specific capacity and authority separated | Ruth’s two reminders and explicit unknowns | Age/diagnosis/family vote is not automatic incapacity or power |
| Safety, autonomy and dignity | Accessible chosen help and proportionate risk comparison | Large print plus chosen call versus broad takeover | Urgent changes require prompt care; no worksheet diagnosis |
