# 19.07 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Adoption, fostering, stepfamilies, and chosen family

**Scope (unchanged):** Understand attachment, identity, legal responsibility, prior loss, loyalty conflicts, and non-biological kinship.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can support belonging without erasing origin, history, or the child's perspective.
