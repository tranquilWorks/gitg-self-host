# 19.08 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Child protection and safeguarding

**Scope (unchanged):** Recognize abuse, neglect, grooming, unsafe caregiving, and mandatory or appropriate reporting paths.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can act promptly to protect a child without conducting an amateur investigation or preserving family image.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Abuse, neglect, grooming and unsafe care | Recognize observed concern without assigning diagnosis or motives | Supplied disclosure separated from unknown facts | No proof threshold, leading interview or amateur investigation |
| Mandatory or appropriate reporting | Actual role/jurisdiction/circumstances, receiving versus information service | Dated bounded Arizona route card; statutory distinctions retained | Example cannot certify every route or transfer UK guidance numbers |
| Prompt protection | Immediate danger and applicable immediate duties override exercise sequence | No internal-delay substitute; calm non-blaming response | No false report/call, secrecy promise or confrontation |
| Family image and outcome limits | Protection rather than reputation or credibility testing | Unsubmitted outline and actual status distinctions | Rehearsal or acknowledgment does not establish safety |

Canonical classification: applicability role_conditional; normative status role_conditional; formation modes: interdependence, stewardship, receptivity.

No reproductive, parenting or caregiving role is compulsory. Fictional preparation and no attempt remain legitimate. A live route requires the appropriate actual role, permission and task authority; a worksheet creates none. An actual safeguarding or urgent care concern must use the proper prompt route rather than wait for an exercise.

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: qualified_assessment, observer_feedback, artifact, longitudinal_review. This is preserved metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.

Canonical professional boundary: Medical, reproductive, child-safety, guardianship, and elder-care decisions require qualified local guidance.

No runtime selection, action, evidence field, score, completion or participant record changes. Supplied fiction cannot count as actual observer evidence.
