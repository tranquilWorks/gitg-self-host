# Corrective answers

1. The current concern, immediate safety and applicable local reporting duty govern. Do not delay a required report for internal approval or conduct credibility interviews. Use the proper receiving or emergency route promptly; seek qualified clarification without making uncertainty an excuse for delay.
2. No. Reading information is not submitting to the receiving authority. Check jurisdiction, role, circumstances, channel and required timing. Support and emergency services have distinct functions; do not transfer a number or reporting rule indiscriminately.
3. Only fictional preparation can be claimed. No report, receipt, investigation or safety outcome occurred. Even an actual acknowledgment would not establish that all risk had ended. Keep real cases out of exercise artifacts and follow the proper protected process.
