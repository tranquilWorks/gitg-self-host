# Inspected sources and claim boundaries

Inspected on 24 September 2026. Primary papers, original institutional educational resources and first-party guidance support narrow teaching choices. Normative guidance, research findings and original task design are distinguished. All case dialogue, dates, decisions and exercises are original supplied fiction; no source validates these finished companions or creates participant evidence.

## S01

[Psychological Safety and Learning Behavior in Work Teams](https://dash.harvard.edu/entities/publication/13a7b031-0fdd-45ec-a7e0-2b80e2bc679f) — Amy Edmondson (1999).

Type: primary empirical paper, author-repository abstract inspected. Inspected: Citation and abstract. Used for 17.08, 17.14: Interpersonal risk and learning behavior in work teams.

Limit: Abstract-only use; association in 51 manufacturing teams, not a universal causal claim, productivity comparison or assurance that dissent is safe.

## S02

[How to implement CRediT](https://credit.niso.org/implementing-credit/) — NISO CRediT.

Type: first-party standards implementation guidance. Inspected: Researcher guidance and contributor review/confirmation recommendations. Used for 17.08: Clarify contributions, allow shared roles and confirm accurate credit.

Limit: Research-output setting; no personality assessment, universal team model or task validation.

## S03

[Our Epidemic of Loneliness and Isolation: The U.S. Surgeon General’s Advisory](https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/surgeon-general-social-connection-advisory.pdf) — US Department of Health and Human Services (2023).

Type: primary public-agency synthesis. Inspected: PDF pages 6, 9 and 17: solitude, structure/function/quality and community conditions. Used for 17.09: Differentiate forms and functions of connection and recognize structural barriers.

Limit: Selected sections only; no causal claim for this activity, social quota, health effect estimate or loneliness cure.

## S04

[Pursuing Meaningful Work: A Guide for PhDs and Postdocs](https://careered.stanford.edu/sites/g/files/sbiybj22801/files/media/file/stanfordphd_pmw_18-19.pdf) — Stanford Career Education (2018–2019).

Type: first-party career education guidance. Inspected: PDF page 11 / printed page 10, Build Your Network. Used for 17.10: Professional connection includes reciprocal support, ideas and information.

Limit: Only the stated network section is used. Original separate-consent design here is not a tested result from this guide; no career outcome promise.

## S05

[The Science of Effective Mentorship in STEMM — interactive guide](https://nap.nationalacademies.org/resource/25568/interactive/) — National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine (2019).

Type: primary consensus-report educational resource. Inspected: Defining mentorship and setting expectations; working alliance over time. Used for 17.11: Use explicit expectations and attend to growth and contributions across a mentoring relationship.

Limit: STEMM educational context; not evidence that age establishes expertise or that one exchange creates lasting friendship.

## S06

[UNESCO Universal Declaration on Cultural Diversity](https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/unesco-universal-declaration-cultural-diversity) — UNESCO (2001).

Type: primary normative declaration. Inspected: Articles 1–5, especially article 4. Used for 17.12, 17.14: Cultural belonging and participation remain bounded by human dignity and rights.

Limit: Normative declaration, not local legal advice, a complete account of communal duties or empirical exercise validation.

## S07

Type: primary theoretical/integrative paper, publisher abstract inspected. Inspected: Abstract and citation metadata. Used for 17.13: Receptiveness includes considering opposing views and can be shaped by interaction.

Limit: Abstract-only use; no persuasion guarantee, obligation to debate dignity, or methods-level validation of this exercise.

## S08

[Green flags in relationships](https://www.loveisrespect.org/resources/green-flags-in-relationships/) — love is respect / National Domestic Violence Hotline.

Type: first-party specialist educational guidance. Inspected: Communication, respect, trust, honesty, equality and boundaries sections. Used for 18.01, 18.03: Attend to respectful conduct and boundaries while considering a relationship.

Limit: Not a diagnostic checklist, compatibility score or universal philosophy of love. Source serves youth; these companions restrict live practice to suitable voluntary adults.

## S09

## S10

[Machine learning uncovers the most robust self-report predictors of relationship quality across 43 longitudinal couples studies](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32719123/) — Samantha Joel and colleagues (2020).

Type: primary empirical paper, abstract inspected. Inspected: Abstract and citation metadata. Used for 18.03: Consider relationship experience and the limits of predicting relationship-quality change.

Limit: Abstract-only use; existing-couple self-report datasets do not validate partner selection, the eight scope headings or an individual future forecast.

## S11

Limit: Abstract-only use; no individual diagnosis, clinical treatment claim or validation of the six behavioral teaching distinctions.

## S12

[Intimacy as an Interpersonal Process: The Importance of Self-Disclosure, Partner Disclosure, and Perceived Partner Responsiveness in Interpersonal Exchanges](https://www.affective-science.org/pubs/1998/LaurenFBPl1998.pdf) — Jean-Philippe Laurenceau, Lisa Feldman Barrett and Paula R. Limit: Opening sections only, not an independent full methods audit. Diary associations do not prescribe more disclosure, forced reciprocity or a universally safe intimacy exercise.

## S13

[Plan for Safety](https://www.thehotline.org/plan-for-safety/) — National Domestic Violence Hotline.

Type: first-party specialist guidance. Inspected: Personalized planning and responsibility for abusive behavior. Used for 18.01, 18.02, 18.03, 18.04, 18.05: Abuse concerns warrant individualized support rather than compulsory mutual communication.

Limit: US service context; verify suitable local resources and safe contact. No personalized plan or guaranteed safety; browser-history clearing is not presented as protection.
