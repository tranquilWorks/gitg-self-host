# 18.05 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Vulnerability and emotional intimacy

**Scope (unchanged):** Share relevant hopes, fears, needs, mistakes, affection, and uncertainty with a trustworthy partner.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Disclosure deepens mutual knowledge without coercion, oversharing, or strategic concealment.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hopes, fears, needs, mistakes, affection and uncertainty | Each disclosure form explained; course decision remains open | Purpose, depth and wanted response | No forced confession to fill every form |
| Trustworthy partner and readiness | Prior respectful conduct; request postponed to tomorrow | Actual willingness and completed later conversation | No safety certification or entitlement to attention |
| Mutual knowledge without coercion/oversharing | Correct application/enrollment misunderstanding | Accurate understanding and bounded schedule review | No equal disclosure quota or more-is-better rule |
| Privacy without strategic concealment | Friend confidence irrelevant; material course facts retained | Truthful minimum and qualified-duty boundary | Unsafe disclosure excluded; no impossible secrecy promise |
