# 17.14 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Collective belonging without conformity

**Scope (unchanged):** Participate in a group, tradition, or community while preserving conscience, dissent, and individual dignity.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can contribute loyally without hiding important disagreement or demanding sameness from others.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Participate in a group/tradition/community | Group purpose and willing equipment inventory | Completed useful contribution | Contribution neither buys acceptance nor purchases voice |
| Conscience and dissent | Written rule, informal spending test and authorized committee | Own concern and feasible alternative with unknown host/room | Secretary cannot approve policy |
| Individual dignity without sameness | Optional retreat and respect for others choosing it | Continue with limit, authorized review or safe exit | Genuine safety role requirements remain relevant |
| Interpret group response | Optionality statement, promised wording change, rejected alternative | Considered/accepted/rejected/implemented distinguished | Retaliation risk and later conduct not settled by a reply |

Canonical classification: applicability cross_context_core; normative status cross_tradition_core_or_broadly_recurrent; formation modes: interdependence, receptivity, stewardship.

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: behavioral_adherence, observer_feedback, longitudinal_review, artifact. This is preserved metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.

This companion changes no runtime selection, package, action, observation, completion, score or participant record. Supplied outcomes are fictional, never actual observer evidence.
