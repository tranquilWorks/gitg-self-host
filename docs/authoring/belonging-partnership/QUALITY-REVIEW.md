## Corrections and retained quality decisions

Two substantive revisions arose from the review: explicit adult ages in 17.11 establish the intergenerational scope, and 17.12 now separates the sixty minutes not performed from the forty minutes without even an offer. The latter also makes the remaining acceptance/performance requirement explicit. The first full source run found one literal boundary-check failure: 18.02 already prohibited required dating but lacked the explicit single-life statement. That statement was added, and the same boundary audit made optionality explicit in 18.03. No earlier test was changed or weakened; the full suite then passed.

The other case boundaries were retained deliberately: 17.08's entrance retest is absent; 17.09's return date is unconfirmed; 17.10 makes no introduction after refusal; 17.13 preserves disagreement; 17.14 distinguishes a promised message change from implementation; 18.01 keeps the counteroffer unaccepted; 18.02 ends pursuit; 18.03 preserves positive evidence alongside mismatch; 18.04 establishes only one check-in; and 18.05 leaves the schedule review unperformed.

All five partnership maps preserve role-conditional applicability and normative status and the exact canonical professional boundary. Fictional/no-attempt routes, single life, no compelled romance or disclosure, and suitable qualified resources are substantive learning boundaries. All earlier 93 additional source companions and their tests remain protected; this batch has no new legacy companion.

No material author-level scope or case defect remains after these revisions and checks. This is a limited source-publication judgment. Qualified safety review, real transfer and formal acceptance remain open; no full-domain pass is claimed.
