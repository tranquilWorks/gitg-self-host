# 18.01 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** The nature and practice of love

**Scope (unchanged):** Distinguish affection, desire, attachment, care, commitment, sacrifice, admiration, and possession.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Acts for another person's genuine good without erasing truth, self, or boundaries.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Affection, desire and attachment | Warmth, wish for attention and significance of bond | Motive account separated from known needs | Feeling strongly does not authorize a visit |
| Care and commitment | Quiet requested; shared library responsibility | Agreed action actually performed and prior obligation honored | An offer is not performed care |
| Sacrifice, admiration and possession | Proportionate chosen cost, valued qualities and unagreed debt | Sustainable boundary and hidden expectation review | No required self-erasure or flawless ideal |
| Genuine good without losing truth/self/boundaries | Separate care from own attention request | Usefulness report and pending counteroffer | No required partnership, reconciliation or unsafe stay |

Canonical classification: applicability role_conditional; normative status role_conditional; formation modes: interdependence, receptivity, agency.

Role-conditional adult practice is optional. Single life, a fictional route and a no-attempt choice are legitimate; none supplies actual relationship evidence.

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: self_report, observer_feedback, behavioral_adherence, longitudinal_review. This is preserved metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.

Canonical professional boundary: Consent, health, abuse, and legal concerns require appropriate medical, legal, or safeguarding resources.

This companion changes no runtime selection, package, action, observation, completion, score or participant record. Supplied outcomes are fictional, never actual observer evidence. Author editorial/source review is not independent cold-start, actual learner, qualified or owner acceptance; those remain pending. This map is not a formal M6K A/B/C receipt.
