# 17.10 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Networks and social capital

**Scope (unchanged):** Maintain ethical professional and community relationships through contribution, trust, and useful connection.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can mobilize or offer knowledge and help without reducing people to assets.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ethical professional/community relationships | Specific need, accurate expertise claim and public resource | Bounded resource or introduction decision | Status and visibility do not create availability |
| Contribution and trust | Separate permissions for exact description and contact route | Two permission statuses; declined introduction preserved | No group chat or private information without authorization |
| Mobilize knowledge/help without extraction | Public sheet answers present need; helper unavailable | Contribution made and limited usefulness feedback | No owed favor, influence dossier or claimed tutoring |
| Maintain relationships | Ordinary contribution, thanks and welcome follow-up | Commitments honored over time | One exchange cannot establish durable ethical maintenance |
