# 18.02 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Attraction and respectful courtship

**Scope (unchanged):** Understand mutual attraction, initiative, pacing, consent, rejection, safety, and honest intention.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can pursue or decline connection without manipulation, entitlement, or humiliation.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mutual attraction and initiative | Friendly adult peers; romantic interest unconfirmed | One honest modest invitation or clear decline | No dating quota or implied mutual attraction |
| Pacing, consent and safety | Specific meeting only, freedom to refuse and leave | Agreed versus unconfirmed next step | No power-dependency live route or visible-enthusiasm test |
| Rejection and honest intention | Definite no, no disguised professional pretext | Pursuit ends without recruiting persuaders | Group friendliness does not reopen romance |
| No manipulation, entitlement or humiliation | Gifts, spectacle, jealousy and persistence distinguished | Dignified response independent of date outcome | Harassment/stalking require appropriate support |
