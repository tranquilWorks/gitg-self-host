# Corrective answers

1. Product quality may be good, but the unagreed thirty minutes violates the role agreement and hides the true cost. Renegotiate scope or timing and recognize actual labor; an excellent result does not retroactively establish willing capacity.
2. Both can receive accurate research credit, with coordination recognized separately and naming permission checked. Roles may be shared. Credit is a description of contribution, not a contest to allocate one exclusive title.
3. Describe the usable product and identifiable improvements from particular contributions. Do not claim that collaboration was faster or better than isolated work without a credible comparison. A passed reader task is useful evidence with a narrower conclusion.
