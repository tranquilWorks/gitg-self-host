# 17.08 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Collaboration and complementary strengths

**Scope (unchanged):** Identify capabilities, coordinate roles, share credit, and use difference as an asset.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** A joint effort produces more value than isolated work while preserving dignity.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Identify capabilities | Skill, learning interest, access and offered time | Willing role agreement for facts, draft, review and coordination | No personality labels or imposed labor |
| Coordinate roles | Fact note, draft and return path for room conflict | Versioned artifact and actual handoff | Solo simulation is preparation only |
| Share credit | Verified facts, writing, checking and coordination named accurately | Contribution account with private naming preference | Shared role does not erase credit |
| Use difference as an asset | Reader questions and room-label correction | Specific improvement and visible five-minute rework | No superiority over isolated work without a comparator |
