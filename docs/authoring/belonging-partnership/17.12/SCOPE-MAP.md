# 17.12 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Kin, clan, neighborhood, and communal obligation

**Scope (unchanged):** Understand forms of belonging in which responsibility is inherited or communal rather than individually chosen.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can honor legitimate communal duties while identifying limits around harm, exploitation, and conscience.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inherited/communal belonging | Kin, clan, neighborhood and tradition; protected communal good | Particular duty basis and invisible labor account | No culture-wide stereotype or automatic entitlement |
| Honor legitimate duties | Care, role, agreement, history and qualified legal uncertainty | Accepted thirty-minute contribution and completion | Do not withdraw essential care as a test |
| Limits around harm, exploitation and conscience | Capacity, dignity and safe consultation | Controlled limit and unresolved burden | No forced confrontation under retaliation |
| Fair burden review | Ninety person-minutes, thirty done, twenty offered, forty unassigned | Offers versus actual work and future proposal | No default assignment to prior organizer or invented rotation |
