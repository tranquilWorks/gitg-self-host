# 17.11 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Intergenerational friendship and mentorship

**Scope (unchanged):** Build reciprocal relationships across ages and life stages, including both giving and receiving guidance.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Maintains a relationship in which perspective and care flow across generational differences.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Across ages and life stages | Two consenting adults with different generational experience | Mutual topic and voluntary questions | Age is not expertise or a generational representative role |
| Give and receive guidance | Past task request and current portal process | Contribution and received perspective on both sides | No equal repayment ledger |
| Contextual transfer | 2008 drop-in practice versus current authorized portal | Adapted principle and rejected obsolete mechanism | Institution, technology, care and opportunity differ |
| Maintain reciprocal relationship | Dated follow-up and permission for future exchange | Twelve-day report and declined further meeting | One exchange does not establish continuing mentorship |
