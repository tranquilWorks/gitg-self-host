# 17.13 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Friendship across deep difference

**Scope (unchanged):** Sustain trust and care where religion, politics, class, identity, or lifestyle diverge.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can name real disagreement without reducing the relationship to conversion, avoidance, or performative tolerance.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Religion, politics, class, identity and lifestyle | Distinct possible stakes and inquiry boundaries | Chosen welcome topic with actual positions | No category inference or forced debate about dignity |
| Name real disagreement | Ambition/leisure case and corrected caricatures | Fair summaries confirmed while priority difference remains | Understanding is not conversion |
| Sustain trust and care | Existing sketch walk independent of persuasion | Stop respected and ordinary commitment reaffirmed | Confirmation is not attendance or permanent harmony |
| Avoid conversion, avoidance and performative tolerance | Own view, uncertainty and genuine question | One understood point and one unresolved point | Harm may justify distance; friendship is not compulsory |
