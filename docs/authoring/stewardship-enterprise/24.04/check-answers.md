# Corrective answers

1. No. Polite endorsement after a pitch is weak evidence of behavior, cost, frequency or demand. Use neutral questions about actual recent instances and retain answers that weaken the idea.
2. User pain and purchasing authority. Record the problem evidence while leaving buyer willingness, process and budget unverified. The user cannot be treated as an authorized customer commitment.
3. Two completed interviews and one declined invitation. Preserve the refusal without pressure; use the actual sample’s limits. Do not invent the third response or relabel the supplied fiction as fieldwork.
