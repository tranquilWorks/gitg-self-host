# 24.04 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Customer discovery and problem validation

**Scope (unchanged):** Test whether a problem is real, costly, frequent, and underserved before building a solution.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can present evidence from actual users or buyers that changed the proposed offer.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Real, costly, frequent, underserved problems | Five neutral questions and predeclared disconfirmers; three full fictional records | A recurrent version issue; B adequate alternative; C approval delay | No actual sample or representative market demand |
| User and buyer evidence changes offer | Spending-approver perspective missing; convenience limits | New app narrowed to current-source change note | User pain and polite interest do not establish purchasing authority |
| Observable next test | Copied time becomes stale; narrower pointer proposed | Failed prototype preserved; revised task not yet tested | Later prototype is not a fourth discovery interview or actual validation |
