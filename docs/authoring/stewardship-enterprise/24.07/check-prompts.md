# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. A platform grows because users find many venues there, and venues join because many users search there. What mechanism is plausible?
2. An export would expose other customers’ names. Does that prove a permanent ban on all customer-owned data export is necessary?
3. A small rival claims predatory pricing because a larger service offers a lower price. Is the claim established?
