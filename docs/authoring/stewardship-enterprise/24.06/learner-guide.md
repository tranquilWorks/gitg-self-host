# 24.06 — Operations, accounting, and compliance

Create a small operating packet that someone authorized could inspect and continue without guessing the current order, approved facts or payment status. Allow thirty minutes for the packet and ten for a later handoff check. This is an operational tabletop with synthetic records, not an actual transaction or compliance certification.

## A reliable operation makes its state visible

Delivery depends on a chain of commitments: what was ordered, who can approve it, what version is current, when it is due, how quality is checked and whether payment has arrived. A folder full of documents is insufficient if the next person cannot distinguish an invoice from a receipt or an old draft from the approved version. Keep status and responsibility close to the record they describe.

Accounting records should make a transaction explainable rather than merely produce a plausible total. The IRS [recordkeeping overview, S11](../SOURCES.md#S11) connects business records with income, expenses and supporting documents. It does not provide this case with a universal retention period or establish tax compliance. The FTC's [personal-information guide, S13](../SOURCES.md#S13) supports limiting unnecessary information and controlling access. Reading it is not a security audit or a legal determination.

Controls should fit the operation's scale. A one-person enterprise may not have several staff, but it can still separate a draft from an approval, reconcile an invoice with payment evidence and arrange appropriate review for consequential matters. An informal conversation should not silently change a contract, payment deadline or permission to publish.

## Supplied order O9

The fictional customer orders one formatted activity notice for **50 units**. Owner Tessa accepts a scope of one page, customer-supplied facts, one revision and delivery by **Friday 4 p.m.** Payment is due **Monday at noon** after delivery. The customer approver is Ivo. Assistant Luz is authorized only to format and check against the source; bookkeeper Em may view the invoice and payment status. These appointments and terms exist only in the supplied case.

The first approved source says “Activity, Sunday at 13:00, Oak Room.” Ivo later corrects the time to **14:00** before release. Version 1 contains 13:00. Version 2 contains 14:00, and Ivo explicitly approves version 2 at Thursday 3 p.m. No real event, venue, person or message is represented.

The packet includes these complete minimal records:

| Record | Supplied entry | State and access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order | O9; one notice; 50; Friday 4 p.m.; one revision; facts require Ivo's approval | Accepted in fiction; owner and formatter need scope, approver sees relevant draft |
| Source history | v1 13:00; correction to 14:00 by Ivo; v2 approved Thursday 3 p.m. | Keep correction history; v2 is the approved content |
| Quality check | Page count one; time 14:00; room Oak; no extra contact details; matches approved source | Luz records check before simulated release |
| Delivery record | Version 2 delivered in the tabletop Friday 2 p.m. | Within the 4 p.m. deadline; no actual dispatch |
| Invoice | O9, 50 due Monday noon; synthetic invoice; unpaid | Owner and Em can inspect; invoice is not proof of receipt |
| Payment record | No receipt or payment confirmation supplied | Payment remains pending |

A correct invoice value does not establish cash. The operation has a 50-unit receivable under the supplied terms after delivery, with no payment evidence. Do not create a receipt to make the packet look finished. Likewise, a revised source does not establish approval unless the actual approver's disposition is present; this case supplies that disposition for v2 explicitly.

## Add the compliance questions before claiming readiness

Use a question register, with topic, unresolved fact, responsible owner and appropriate qualified route. For registration and permissions, Tessa needs the relevant official local business authority. Tax and record obligations need the applicable tax authority or qualified adviser; the inspected IRS page provides general U.S. recordkeeping context only. Employment or contractor arrangements need qualified local advice about actual working terms. Contract, consumer and refund obligations need review of the service and jurisdiction. Privacy requires a minimal-data purpose and the applicable rules. Accessibility and sector-specific requirements need the relevant authority or qualified specialist for the actual activity.

These seven headings identify work to resolve, not seven completed compliance checks. Only the specific IRS and FTC pages were inspected; no local regulator, contract, tax form or employment determination is represented as reviewed. If a material question remains unanswered, the actual operation may need to pause the affected commitment. A checklist cannot grant legal permission.

## Make the packet continuable

1. Assemble order, approved source, version history, quality check, delivery, invoice and payment status from the supplied entries. Label every record synthetic and keep unpaid status visible.
2. Assign responsibility for preparing, approving, checking, delivering and reconciling. Explain why Luz cannot approve a fact change and why Em's access to invoice data does not require unrelated customer information.
3. Create a single current-version pointer identifying v2 and its approval. Preserve v1 as superseded rather than deleting history or presenting both versions as current.
4. Write the seven-topic question register and distinguish inspected general guidance from unresolved local requirements. Assign Tessa to obtain appropriate answers before any actual operation relies on them.
5. Set a limited access and retention note. For this fictional tabletop only, synthetic working copies may be discarded 30 days after its review. That is an exercise housekeeping choice, not a legal business-record retention rule. Real records require applicable rules, holds and authorized disposal. Save the packet before the [later packet](later-packet.md).

For the handoff, ask a willing authorized reader to use only the packet, without your coaching, to identify current version, approver, next action, deadline and payment status. Record the answers before explaining or repairing anything, then repeat the affected task after correction. The later packet supplies an invented reader’s first attempt and retest for a fully fictional route. A self-walkthrough can help prepare, but it must remain labeled self-review and cannot replace an actual independent reader check.

## Adjust the task without hiding disorder

For support, use the table as the packet and answer the five handoff questions aloud. For a harder pass, make the source correction arrive after delivery and identify who can authorize a correction, which recipients need it and what records must persist. Do not pretend replacing a file recalls every prior copy.

Optional actual transfer should use a small process you own or have permission to review, with redacted or synthetic data. Do not access personnel files, customer identifiers or credentials to complete this lesson. A clear packet plus a later authorized continuation or reconciliation check could support progress. This supplied tabletop demonstrates how to inspect state while leaving actual reliable operation, lawful compliance and independent continuation unproven.

## Scope and evidence boundary

Canonical professional boundary: Business and economic education only; legal, tax, securities, employment, and regulatory questions require qualified advice. Applicability and normative status remain role_conditional; an enterprise role is optional. Use fiction or privately redacted records; keep credentials and sensitive identifiers outside app evidence.
