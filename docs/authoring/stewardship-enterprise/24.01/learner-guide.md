# 24.01 — Income, negotiation, and career economics

Connect an earnings request to useful capability and compare mobility with the time and costs it would require. Allow thirty minutes for the evidence and rehearsal, then a short later practice review. A larger headline salary is one input to a career decision, not proof that a move improves someone's life.

## Make the value claim inspectable

An earnings case is stronger when it identifies work delivered, who benefited, the limits of the evidence and a capability worth developing next. “I deserve more” may express a legitimate concern but does not explain the role, market or request. Conversely, useful work does not guarantee a particular employer's budget or compensation decision. Negotiation requires a request and response, not a promise that confidence will produce a raise.

Compare roles using duties, scope, location, qualifications, hours, travel, benefits and the costs of changing. Titles can hide different work. A national occupational profile can reveal relevant duties; it cannot establish a local vacancy, a personal offer or a guaranteed wage. Income growth can come from stronger capability, better market positioning or a better fit between work and compensation, with real tradeoffs in training and mobility.

Two inspected public role descriptions provide a bounded comparison: BLS [administrative assistants, S08](../SOURCES.md#S08) and [project management specialists, S09](../SOURCES.md#S09). Both include coordination, but the latter profile describes broader budget, staffing and project responsibilities. Their typical preparation and work patterns differ. This is a comparison of overlapping duties, not evidence that the roles are interchangeable or deserve a particular pay ratio. No national median is used as a personal offer.

## Supplied work record C5

Ren works in administrative scheduling. All figures and outcomes below are invented. An authorized, redacted work record would be needed for an actual attempt; do not upload employer or client information here.

| Delivered outcome | Supplied evidence | Benefit and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reduced missed handoffs in a weekly booking process | Before: 6 missed handoffs in 40 bookings; after: 2 in 40, from the fictional team log | Four fewer recorded misses; periods may differ and no causal trial was conducted |
| Made a handover checklist usable during absence | Substitute completed 8 of 10 stated tasks; two needed clarification | Partial continuity benefit, not a flawless handover or proof of independent role mastery |

Ren can discuss coordination and documentation with concrete examples. The capability gap is maintaining a small task budget while tracking changes; Ren has not independently managed project finances. A request should not claim that the two outcomes establish project-management qualification.

The supplied present role pays **3000 gross units for 160 work hours per month**. A hypothetical alternative pays **3300 gross for 180 hours** and adds **200 monthly transport cost**. Other costs, taxes, benefits, commute hours and contract terms are unknown; neither card is an actual vacancy. Gross pay per work hour is **18.75** currently and approximately **18.33** in the alternative. Subtracting only the additional transport cost leaves 3100 versus 3000, a **100-unit difference before taxes and all other differences**. It is not net take-home pay or an all-in hourly comparison.

## Prepare a specific, proportionate request

Ren's draft is: “Could we review my compensation and role scope at the next scheduled review? The supplied examples show fewer recorded handoff misses and a handover that worked for eight tasks. I would also like a bounded practice assignment in tracking a small task budget. What criteria and evidence would support a decision, and when can we review them?” This makes useful work visible while naming a gap. It does not invent an outside offer, threaten resignation or guarantee a raise.

A yes response still needs amount, effective date, role expectations and written confirmation through the real process. A no response can be followed by a request for reasons and realistic alternatives without accepting indefinite unpaid extra duties. “Not yet” needs a date and measurable criteria, not endless proof of loyalty. Employer constraints may be real; so may reasons to seek another role. A learner can decide not to negotiate now if the situation is unsuitable.

A short rehearsal makes each branch concrete. For yes: “Thank you; what amount, effective date and scope will be confirmed through the usual process?” For no: “What constraint or criterion prevents a change, and is a supported training option feasible?” For not yet: “What specific evidence should I bring, within my agreed workload, and on what date will we revisit it?” These are practice responses; none establishes an employer commitment.

For the supplied practice assignment, Ren tracks a fictional **100-unit task budget**. Approved planned costs are 40 materials, 30 delivery and 20 support, leaving **10 uncommitted**. A proposed extra 15 has no approval. Ren's first worksheet records 90 planned, 10 available and the 15 as pending; accepting it would exceed the ceiling by 5. Recognizing that limit is a useful skill demonstration. It is not authority to spend an employer's money.

## Produce the career decision brief

1. Write the two delivered outcomes with denominators and limitations. Distinguish an observed change from a proven causal contribution, and partial handover success from complete readiness.
2. Compare the two public profiles across duties, preparation and work pattern. Note the shared coordination skills and the broader financial responsibility Ren has not demonstrated. Record the retrieval date and keep live-job availability unknown.
3. Recompute the hypothetical pay and hours. List unresolved taxes, benefits, travel time, caring responsibilities, training costs and contract terms. Explain why the 300-unit gross increase cannot alone settle the decision.
4. Write the request and rehearse yes, no and not-yet responses. Each branch should specify what information or next step would make it actionable. Do not send the request on anyone else's behalf.
5. Complete the bounded 100-unit practice ledger, then save the initial brief before opening the [later packet](later-packet.md). Set a review date for capability evidence and for the career choice separately.

## Keep growth feasible and voluntary

For support, use one outcome and calculate gross pay per work hour before adding the missing-cost list. Record any help interpreting a role profile. For a harder pass, add a known care cost or unpaid travel hours and recompute the comparison; do not silently assume every extra hour is feasible. A role with lower immediate cash may offer learning, flexibility or security, but those benefits need evidence and the person's own priorities.

An optional actual attempt might be a private rehearsal, a permitted portfolio summary or a small approved practice task. Job applications, employer contact and negotiations are not required to use this source companion. Real tax, employment or contract questions need qualified advice. A practice record plus later supervisor or artifact evidence could support capability growth; the supplied budget and imaginary negotiation do not establish a raise, an offer, employability or actual workplace acceptance.

## Scope and evidence boundary

Canonical professional boundary: Business and economic education only; legal, tax, securities, employment, and regulatory questions require qualified advice. Applicability and normative status remain role_conditional; an enterprise role is optional. Use fiction or privately redacted records; keep credentials and sensitive identifiers outside app evidence. Human dignity is never scored.
