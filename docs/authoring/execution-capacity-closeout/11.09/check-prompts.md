# Fresh review checks

## Check 1 — A cost range crosses the rule

A three-attempt trial predicts at least two successful outputs, total active cost at most 24 minutes and no displaced duty. Outcomes are yes, no, yes. Recorded costs are 7, 8–11 and 7 minutes. Conditions are otherwise comparable, with no displaced duty. Calculate the possible total and choose a truthful disposition. May the reviewer record 22 minutes and retain the method without acknowledging the uncertainty?

## Check 2 — Several things changed

The old method produced one accepted output from three attempts. The new record has three from three, but a helper completed two outputs and a missing material also became available. The prior question was whether changing the learner's reminder alone improved their own follow-through. What is supported, what causal claim is not, and what concrete next comparison would respect necessary support? Should the original target or denominator be rewritten?
