# Workflow corrective guidance

## Complete paper output and test

An acceptable sheet is: “Face the cupboard; left and right are from your viewpoint. Find the hole punch in Upper Left, labeled PUNCH. Return the hole punch to Upper Left, PUNCH. Find blue tape in Lower Left, labeled BLUE TAPE. Return blue tape to Lower Left, BLUE TAPE.”

Its four location outputs are Upper Left, Upper Left, Lower Left, Lower Left. Each matches the supplied inventory. Compare your actual wording, not this model, when recording self-review. A clear equivalent is acceptable. Missing viewpoint or visible labels needs revision even if the four locations happen to be right.

Version 0's four written locations are Upper Left, Upper Left, Lower Right, Lower Left. Paper inspection detects the third error. The supplied simulated reader report has two passes, one failure and one not-tested step; the fourth instruction's paper correctness cannot convert that reader's unattempted return into an observed pass. Keep those evidence routes separate.

The paper closeout can say “Version 1 saved; four paper instructions checked; no physical resources borrowed; shared release pending actual keeper approval and willing-reader use.” It cannot say “Mira accepted delivery.” Preserve Version 0, your decision, first result and any retest. At checkpoint 1 I becomes Ready after S; after I, D becomes Ready. Only one card is Doing at any time.

## Check 1

A Done, B Doing, C Blocked, D Blocked. C needs the completed draft and confirmed access; D needs an actual print. The worker can own the action of asking about access; that does not make them the provider of the printer or guarantee a reply. Lee cannot acquire ownership without agreement. Printing cannot honestly start without its inputs, and a second Doing card would violate capacity. If B finishes while access remains absent, preserve B's output and keep C blocked with a useful next check.

## Check 2

Version 1 retains its dated two-check result. Version 2 is unaccepted with one known defect. Reopen affected drafting/verification work, correct the location in a new version and rerun the two-location acceptance test before closing the deliverable. The requester explicitly retained the requirement; reducing it after failure would falsify success. Record whether the previously shared copy needs a correction notice. A well-populated board without a corrected map is still unfinished.
