# 11.06 — individual canonical scope map

Canonical name: **Project and workflow management**.

Canonical scope: Track scope, ownership, dependencies, risk, decisions, status, and definition of done.

Canonical progress signal: A multi-step project reaches completion with fewer hidden surprises and abandoned threads.

The source addresses the facets below. All are teaching responsibilities; actual situations and live routes remain conditional on role, capacity, access, consent and safety. The default is a bounded practice, not proof of the whole competency.

| Facet | Teaching anchor in learner-guide.md | Work/material | Evidence and failure distinction | Further route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scope and ownership | Brief; Action 1 | Consenting solo ownership and authority record | Description allowed; changing cupboard and shared release not approved | A real keeper and willing collaborators must supply their own agreement |
| Dependencies and status | Five-card board; checkpoints 1–3 | Scope and inventory outputs, bounded WIP, blocked-input record | No card starts without inputs or closes without output | Track an external layout change with missing-input owner |
| Risk and decisions | Actions 1–2 | Ambiguous-location and absent-approval risks; preserved version decision | Decision relates a change to observed inventory | Review one real risk at the next useful checkpoint |
| Definition of done and responsible finish | Action 3 | Actual paper sheet, four tests, rework and closeout | Failed retrieval and not-tested return stay distinct; paper vs shared release | Real willing-reader test and keeper approval needed for shared release |

Prerequisites, burden and supported route are stated in the guide. The default requires no external account, purchase, real participant or private disclosure. Its separately revealed later packet and two fresh checks are instructional materials, not new runtime evidence or action IDs.

Canonical measurement asks for more than self-report, including independent evidence types; this simulated source exercise does not satisfy a score-advancement or mastery claim. Actual use, independent observation, longitudinal transfer and formal qualified/owner acceptance remain pending. The source is not compiler-selected.
