# Fresh pacing checks

## Check 1 — A bounded sprint that can fit

A fictional worker has a safe 40-minute task window, followed by a protected 15-minute recovery/transition, then an essential duty. Four verified labels are needed today to prevent an otherwise agreed low-stakes handoff being missed. Each label takes at most 6 minutes, and final checking takes 10. Existing restrictions permit this work. State whether a bounded sprint can fit, its stopping boundary, quality test, unused task-window minutes and remaining duty/recovery obligations. Does calling it urgent allow using the recovery if the estimate proves wrong?

## Check 2 — Quantity and rework

Record A: 12 entries attempted in 30 minutes, 4 wrong; correcting those 4 takes 12 additional minutes and all 12 are then verified. Record B: 10 entries attempted and verified in 30 minutes, no rework. Both records use the same acceptance rule. Calculate each accepted output before and after correction and total active minutes. Which has the higher final accepted-entries-per-active-minute rate? Can these two records alone establish a health effect or justify choosing A for every task?
