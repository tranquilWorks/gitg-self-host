# Fresh refusal and closure checks

## Check 1 — An unpleasant duty is still a duty

A fictional volunteer promised to return a borrowed display stand Saturday by noon through an agreed staffed desk. On Friday they decide not to participate in future optional displays and draft a refusal of next month's invitation. They also propose leaving the stand outside overnight; the owner has not replied. Classify the future invitation, current return and proposed new method. Draft a responsible ending without forcing approval or neglecting the stand.

## Check 2 — No opportunity is not follow-through

A person defines a safe low-stakes boundary and privately rehearses it. During the ten-day practice no relevant situation occurs. They answer both reading checks correctly and want to mark “direct statement” and “follow-through within seven days” observed. Are those claims supported? What honest next state is available, and must they create a disagreement or extend dates retrospectively to complete the practice?
