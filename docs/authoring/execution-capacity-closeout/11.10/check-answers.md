# Refusal and closure corrective guidance

## Default and mini-cases

A supports direct statement, proportionate departure, completion of the six-pack promise and key receipt. The organizer's disappointment does not require further agreement to the legitimate limit. B supports the statement and departure with a returned key, but only five of six promised packs. The missing supply is an external change, not evidence of a completed result. Retain the incomplete promise and proposed repair; another person's acceptance and actual repair remain unestablished. The extra display remains optional.

C is not a successful boundary: withholding the key to force cancellation is coercive. New retaliation risk stops the practice; appropriate trusted or qualified support must address safe resolution. Do not prescribe another confrontation or fabricate key receipt. D is preparation only, with no direct statement or follow-through. Unknown key return remains unknown.

For the obsolete project, cancel the ten-ticket goal because the game was cancelled; preserve three drafts as drafts and return the borrowed template under the existing Saturday-noon arrangement. The paper route can only record a planned return unless an event is supplied. Do not award completion of ten tickets.

For the exchange, end future optional meetings clearly while retaining the Saturday 11:00 puzzle return. Approval of the relationship ending is not required by these low-stakes facts. A new return method would need agreement; the old return remains the duty until addressed. No relationship-health or live closure result is established by the draft.

## Check 1

The future invitation is optional and can be declined without approval. The current stand return remains an existing promise. The outdoor drop-off is a proposed revision, unaccepted and not the agreed staffed route. A suitable message preserves Saturday-noon return and says future displays are declined; if that return becomes infeasible, communicate risk early and seek an agreed safe alternative. Leaving the stand outside or ignoring its return would not be responsible closure. The refusal should not demand that the owner become pleased or cancel the display.

## Check 2

The evidence supports defining and rehearsing, plus reading comprehension. It supports neither a direct communicated statement nor actual proportionate follow-through. Preserve no-opportunity/inconclusive status under the existing practice and use the appropriate defer/stop/review route without inventing a completed action. Do not manufacture a conflict, backdate an event or revise the original ten-day/seven-day history. Reading checks are not replacement application evidence, action units or new completion rules.
