# Scheduling update — reveal after saving the baseline and rule

These are alternative fictional branches, not events the learner experienced.

**A: access defeats a simple subtraction.** Everything through recovery finishes exactly as planned at 10:55. An unavoidable 20-minute interruption occupies 10:55–11:15 before the errand transition begins. The errand needs its full 30 minutes; the desk still closes at 11:45. No extension has been agreed. Produce the revised remaining schedule and identify what stays pending. Do not remove recovery retroactively.

**B: early deadline exposure.** The form needs its full 60 active minutes. An interruption occupies 09:25–09:45; the 20 minutes of form work already completed are retained. Forty active minutes remain at 09:45. The appointment still starts at 10:10, requires five minutes of transition, and cannot move by unilateral decision. At 09:25 the interruption's 20-minute duration is known. It is a call from the appointment desk, and reporting the form risk is possible in the first minute of that same call; this notification is included in the 20 minutes, not additional simultaneous work. Decide when to communicate, what the original plan can no longer accomplish, and which status to preserve if no reply arrives. No alternative is accepted in this packet.
