# Fresh accountability checks

## Check 1 — Acknowledgment is not approval

A file was promised by Tuesday 12:00 through an agreed local folder. Monday 10:00 its author proposes Wednesday 09:00 by email instead. The recipient replies at 10:05, “I saw this.” No other reply exists. At Tuesday 12:30 the file has not been delivered. Preserve the promise, classify the reply, give the current status and write a repair communication without inventing acceptance.

## Check 2 — Correct item, unconfirmed receipt

A borrower was authorized to leave one puzzle at a named staffed desk by 15:00. The agreement says receipt is the desk attendant's signed slip. At 14:50 the borrower leaves it at the desk while no attendant is present and takes a photo. At 15:10 there is still no signed slip or recipient confirmation. State what the photo supports, what remains unknown and the next appropriate action. Would a slip dated 15:20 later prove the original 15:00 receipt deadline was met?
