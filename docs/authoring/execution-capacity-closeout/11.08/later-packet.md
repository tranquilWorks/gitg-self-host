# Promise outcomes — alternative fictional branches

The Thursday noon risk message is stipulated sent within each branch. These are not messages the learner sent.

**A — explicit revision and receipt:** Thursday 12:30 Ren: “Yes, I accept Jo collecting Thursday at 18:00 and handing Blue Atlas to me Friday at 09:00 at the hobby room.” Thursday 18:00 Jo receives the book. Friday 09:00 Jo says it was handed over. Friday 09:05 Ren: “Blue Atlas received in good condition at 09:00. Thank you.”

**B — ambiguous then absent reply:** Thursday 12:30 Ren: “Thanks for letting me know.” A single clarification at 13:00 asks whether the exact Jo arrangement is accepted. No reply arrives before 16:00. The borrower retains the book. Friday 17:00 the book has not been returned. Saturday 10:00 Ren says: “I left without it and could not use it on the trip. Please return it to me Monday at 18:00 here.” The borrower has a confirmed feasible no-cost route for that Monday handoff and can accept it. No Monday delivery event is supplied.

**C — accepted revision, failed receipt:** Ren gives the same explicit Thursday 12:30 acceptance as A. Jo collects Thursday at 18:00. Friday 09:00 Jo reports the hobby room was shut and retains the book safely. No handoff occurred. At 09:10 Ren is told of the miss and asked about a feasible later handoff. No reply or delivery is supplied. The accepted Friday 09:00 revision was missed; the later proposal is pending. The original Friday 17:00 record still exists as history, not a silently restored agreement.
