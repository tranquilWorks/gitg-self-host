# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. Four people attend a planning meeting and agree the board needs changes. Does this establish all users' agreement or permission to remove notices?
2. Twelve notices include three confirmed expired, eight current and one undated. Only the expired removals are authorized. What remains, and what must stay uncertain?
3. A user succeeds after the heading is enlarged, and an owner agrees to next week's check. What result and maintenance claims are justified?
