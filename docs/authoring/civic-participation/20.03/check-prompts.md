# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. Group labels are swapped while every relevant fact stays the same, and only the disliked group now receives a lower capacity. What does that reveal?
2. A specific credible safety concern arises. Does equal treatment require ignoring it, or automatically justify permanent exclusion?
3. A declaration describes freedom of association. Does that alone establish an enforceable right to use any room under this exercise's proposed procedure?
