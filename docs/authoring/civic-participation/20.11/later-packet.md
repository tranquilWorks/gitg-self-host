# Later packet — a usable procedure preserves dissent

Supplied fiction, not an adopted policy or actual community outcome.

The revised draft removes ideological endorsement and replaces the vague offense condition with relevant conduct limits, reasons and review by the hall's designated owner through an accessible written channel. Targeted harassment still goes to the responsible safety route; no learner investigates or decides its final disposition.

Requests can arrive at the desk or through an equivalent written channel before the same decision deadline. For the fictional scheduling conflict, the applicants are first offered another available time. If neither accepts, the draft proposes a disclosed random draw among equally eligible requests, with owner review of procedure. In this case one applicant accepts the alternative, so no draw is performed. An access request is accommodated through the written channel without a demand for private history.

The disliked-view applicant completes an ordinary paper booking under the same quiet-use and capacity conditions. The exercise contains no actual room use. One participant continues to disagree with the organizer's views and the tie-break proposal; that dissent is recorded without blocking the uncontested booking. No consensus, legal compliance, resolved harassment or long-term coexistence is established. The draft remains subject to actual owner and affected-user review.
