# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. Someone has trouble with an online form. What can you infer about their ability to decide, and what should you ask before acting?
2. A person accepts an explanation, says thank you and keeps a draft. Which of submission, a confirmed place and useful help has actually been established?
3. The same person requests reading support again. Must you withdraw it to prevent dependence, or take over the whole task to save time?
