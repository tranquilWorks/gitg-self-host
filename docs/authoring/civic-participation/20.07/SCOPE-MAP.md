# 20.07 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Helping without paternalism

**Scope (unchanged):** Ask what is needed, preserve agency, understand context, and avoid making help about superiority or identity.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Support increases another person's safety or capacity rather than dependence or humiliation.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ask what is needed and understand context | Goal, mode and unknown cause of difficulty | Lee chooses large print and term explanation | No literacy, disability or capacity assumption |
| Preserve agency | Task consent, credentials and final choice | Device retained; waiting-list request remains draft | No seat, submission or expanded permission |
| Avoid superiority and humiliation | Useful task evidence versus gratitude | Accessible contact retained and located | No disclosure, praise or ideology condition |
| Increase safety or capacity appropriately | Chosen ongoing help can be legitimate | Future brief support offered but unscheduled | No forced independence or broad safety claim |
