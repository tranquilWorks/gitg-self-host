# Corrective answers

1. Difficulty does not determine decision-making ability or its cause. Ask the person's goal, preferred help and permission; distinguish an access or design problem from an unsupported personal judgment. Keep choices and credentials with the person.
2. Neither submission nor a confirmed place follows. Gratitude alone also does not prove usefulness. Check the selected task and the person's account or concrete task evidence. In this case the explanation, accessible copy and located contact support a narrow benefit; the request remains a draft.
3. Neither response is automatic. Continuing chosen assistance can preserve agency. Agree the next task and your limits without forcing independence, withholding useful information or expanding authority. A future offer is not a scheduled session.
