# 20.09 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Public discourse and information responsibility

**Scope (unchanged):** Consume and share news with verification, context, proportionality, and awareness of manipulation.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can remain informed without becoming addicted to outrage or spreading weak claims.
