# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. Someone wants an offline option. Is “you reject technology” a fair summary, and how can you check?
2. Two people deliver a shared notice while retaining different priorities. Must one have surrendered a conviction for the cooperation to count?
3. A person declines the conversation or task. Can their refusal be recorded as bad faith or evidence about their whole group?
