# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. A survey invites 200 users, receives 50 responses and records 40 positive satisfaction answers. Which percentages are known, and whose views remain unknown?
2. A university produced the report, an agency funded it, a newspaper expanded its population and a party asserted effectiveness. Does prestige prove truth, funding prove fraud or satisfaction prove causation?
3. Where should a denominator error in a headline be addressed, and what does a drafted correction request establish about the institution's response?
