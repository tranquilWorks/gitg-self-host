# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. Twenty of forty interested people do not attend. Can you infer low motivation, a demographic identity or discrimination from that number alone?
2. Attendance rises from 20/40 to 28/40 after a venue change and an extra reminder. What changed numerically, and what causal claim remains unsupported?
3. A step-free route helps one user, but overall attendance does not rise in another case. Does that prove the access change was useless?
