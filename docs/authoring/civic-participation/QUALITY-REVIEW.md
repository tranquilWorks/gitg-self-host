# Individual author editorial review

Reviewed 24 September 2026 for this source-only publication. This is author editorial review, not independent review. All twelve guides, separate later packets, 36 fresh prompts/corrective answers and exact canonical maps were examined for breadth, depth, usability, distinctiveness, source limits and missing human evidence. Common headings and required boundary wording do not replace individual teaching or task design.

Word counts describe saved text, not a quality quota. Automated source tests and literal repetition scans support consistency, not pedagogical efficacy. Formal M6K A/B/C, independent cold-start, actual learner, qualified and owner content acceptance remain pending. No participant response, professional endorsement or independent receipt is fabricated.

## 20.01 — Social interdependence

- **Facet/depth and usability:** Infrastructure, labor, law, knowledge, environment and inherited institutions each receive a branch with a sourced fact or unknown. The library encounter connects benefits not personally produced to a wanted, feasible reciprocal action. Alex returns before the due date and reports damage; staff accept and log it. Repair, labor pay and ecological effects stay unknown. Institutional access and maintenance duties remain visible. [Canonical map](20.01/SCOPE-MAP.md). Saved guide: 1086 words.
- **Distinctiveness:** A dependency-to-responsibility trace, distinct from civic authority navigation and from the commons usability repair. Gratitude is not an unlimited debt or a defense of unfair institutions.
- **Fresh-answer discrimination:** Fee payment does not prove self-sufficiency; an unwanted donation can impose burden; accepted return and damage report do not prove repair.
- **Source inspection and limits:** S01 is the IFLA overview only. No linked manifesto, budget, employment or footprint evidence is claimed. See [source register](SOURCES.md).
- **Missing human evidence:** Actual responsible use, stewards’ perspectives, repeated contributions and institutional effects remain unobserved. Independent and owner content acceptance remain pending.
