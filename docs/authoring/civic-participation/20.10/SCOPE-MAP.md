# 20.10 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Stewardship of commons and environment

**Scope (unchanged):** Care for public spaces, ecosystems, shared knowledge, institutional trust, and resources used by others.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Leaves a shared system more usable, resilient, or understood.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public spaces and shared resources | Permission, users, rules and material condition | Borrowing shelf and reversible card | Shared does not mean unilateral control |
| Ecosystems, knowledge and institutional trust | Different resource dynamics and competence | Physical depletion versus copying; reliability and maintenance | No habitat intervention or measured trust outcome |
| Leave the system more usable | Whole instruction area and willing user trial | Covered instruction repaired; three later returns correct | Baseline inventory not matched trial |
| Resilience and stewardship | Suitable reuse and accepted maintenance | Reused card; future weekly check accepted | No quantified ecological saving or durable outcome |
