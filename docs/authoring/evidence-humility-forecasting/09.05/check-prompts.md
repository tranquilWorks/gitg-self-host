# 09.05 — Fresh checks

Use exact claims and preserve the previous version. Both cases are fictional. Save the answers before opening the separate key.

## Check A — A counterexample is not a universal reversal

Dara writes V0: “Every afternoon attempt in this building fails for my 20-minute reading task.” The only evidence then available is two unsuccessful Main-room attempts. Before further visits, Dara writes: “A completed manageable afternoon attempt anywhere in this building would contradict my every-attempt claim. It would not by itself show that most future attempts succeed.”

Later, Dara completes the task comfortably in the Side room on Wednesday at 14:00. On Friday at 14:00 the Main-room attempt fails again. A friend says, “So you must admit the building is always suitable.”

Append a revised claim and confidence with reasons. State what is contradicted, what remains uncertain, and why the friend's opposite universal claim is unsupported. Preserve the room difference and propose a proportionate next action. Explain how the successful observation would matter differently if V0 had instead said “more than half of attempts are unsuitable.”

## Check B — Pressure, then a relevant correction

A dated official room notice says: “Room H closed all Day 4 for maintenance.” On Day 3, Ellis writes: “According to the current official notice, Room H is scheduled to be closed on Day 4. I will not plan to use it without a newer confirmation.” Confidence is high in what the notice says, not absolute certainty about what will happen. Ellis records that a newer relevant notice or authorized correction would change the plan.

First, a friend says: “You are too rigid. Believe it will be open.” The friend supplies no new observation or record.

Then, separately, the facility issues a later official notice on Day 3: “Maintenance is postponed. Room H is scheduled to be open on Day 4.” Neither notice guarantees that no unexpected closure will occur.

Write two separate updates, one after each message. Identify whether to retain or revise the operational expectation, why, and which exact proposition changes. Does the later notice make the earlier statement about what the original notice said false? Write a non-humiliating acknowledgment and explain whether any action should change.
