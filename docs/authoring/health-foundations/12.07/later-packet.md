## Interpret after your attempt

The second occasion is mixed because grip/tolerance remains a concern despite controlled movement. Retain the closer arrangement as an access option, avoid forcing further attempts, and obtain appropriate advice about the persistent/new limitation before prescribing training. Do not assert that the shelf caused the fatigue or cured a joint limitation.

The unknown later recovery prevents declaring the fourth criterion fully met on repeat. Three observed criteria plus one unknown/limited criterion are not four passes. A real concern follows the relevant clinical advice, with no need to remove support or test C to collect more data.

Alternative supportive second occasion: same B setup, all four criteria met and ordinary aftermath reported tolerable. That supports repeatable access in this task. It still does not establish increased joint range or tissue adaptation. Keep this alternative separate from the grip-fatigue branch.
