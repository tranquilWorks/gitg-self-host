# 12.07 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Mobility, flexibility, and joint capacity

**Scope (unchanged):** Maintain usable range of motion, tissue tolerance, balance, and stability.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Moves through required daily and recreational ranges with control and minimal limitation.

Health and exercise content must be adapted to the person and does not replace licensed medical care.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Usable range of motion | Definitions, Noor A/B/C and distance calculation | Relate range to a daily task without forcing an unfamiliar reach | Shelf distance is not joint range or a clinical measure |
| Tissue tolerance | Fourth criterion and repeat grip/aftermath record | Retain fatigue and missing later recovery before training decisions | No cause, tissue adaptation or prescription follows from two tasks |
| Balance | Stable seated setup, familiar supports and stop rule | Keep support and report dizziness/instability separately | No unsupported balance test or fall provocation |
| Stability | Predeclared criteria and harder support/load change | Control and support remain conditions of a usable task | Successful placement alone does not certify stability |
| Daily and recreational controlled movement | Worked drawer example, cup access and transfer | Retain useful access and identify qualified training needs | One daily task does not establish general mobility or recreational range |
