**Canonical competency:** Nutrition fundamentals

**Scope (unchanged):** Understand energy balance, protein, fiber, micronutrients, hydration, food quality, allergies, and individual constraints.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Maintains a practical eating pattern without fad dependence or obsessive control.

Health and exercise content must be adapted to the person and does not replace licensed medical care.
