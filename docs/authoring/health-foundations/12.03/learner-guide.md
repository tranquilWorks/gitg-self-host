# 12.03 — Nutrition fundamentals

## Make nourishment practical without turning it into a score

A workable eating pattern supplies energy and nutrients, fits the person's needs and can be repeated with available money, time and support. One impressive label or one meal cannot establish that a whole diet is adequate. This guide teaches energy balance, protein, fiber, micronutrient variety, hydration, food quality, allergies and individual constraints through a complete meal-planning case.

Allow 15 minutes for the meal and budget, 10 for the later repeat and 10 for checks. Use paper, a calculator or dictation. All foods, prices and outcomes below are invented. No purchase, tasting, private dietary disclosure or calorie/weight tracking is required. The default produces a practical meal plan and a repeat decision; it does not show that you prepared or ate a meal. Health and exercise content must be adapted to the person and does not replace licensed medical care.

## Understand the functions and the label's limits

Food provides energy for bodily functions and activity; energy balance concerns intake and expenditure over time, not the moral quality of one plate. [Protein supplies building material](https://www.medlineplus.gov/ency/article/002467.htm), [fiber supports digestive function](https://www.medlineplus.gov/dietaryfiber.html), and varied foods can contribute different vitamins and minerals. Fluid is another need, with individual restrictions or swallowing plans taking priority. Needs vary with the person, circumstances and care plan; no intake target is supplied here. [NIDDK's nutrition overview](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diet-nutrition) supports the broad energy/nutrient distinction.

Food quality includes useful nourishment, safety, enjoyment and practical fit. Convenient canned, frozen or prepared foods can be suitable; “fresh” and “expensive” do not establish adequacy. A familiar cultural meal need not be replaced by a standardized plate. Variety is an option to make a pattern workable, not a demand to ignore sensory limits or eliminate tolerated foods.

A label's serving size describes the unit used for the listed information; it is not a personal portion prescription. Multiply per-serving values by the number of servings actually considered, and keep per-container and per-serving units separate. [FDA's serving-size explanation](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-facts-label/serving-size-nutrition-facts-label) supports this calculation. The numbers below teach label arithmetic, not a nutrient quota.

Allergy information is a safety constraint. Read the applicable label, including ingredients and relevant cross-contact information, and follow the person's existing allergy plan. An unreadable label or missing voluntary warning does not prove safety. Do not taste-test a suspected allergen. [FDA's food-allergy guidance](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-food-labeling-and-critical-foods/food-allergies) concerns U.S. labeling; other jurisdictions differ. A fictional “verified for this case” status is not assurance about any real product.

## Worked example: a container has two servings

A fictional pot lists protein 6 g and fiber 3 g per serving, two servings per pot. The entire pot supplies 12 g protein and 6 g fiber by label arithmetic. That says nothing about whether the person should eat the whole pot or whether the day is nutritionally adequate. If the pot costs 2.40 units, purchasing it costs 2.40 even when half is used now; allocating 1.20 to this meal does not reduce the cash needed at checkout.

## Complete materials: Ari's two lunches

Ari has a familiar eating plan and a sesame allergy. For this synthetic case only, R, B, V, F, P and O have been checked against Ari's existing allergy plan, including relevant handling, and are tolerated. S contains sesame. U has unreadable ingredients and unknown cross-contact status. There are no other stipulated dietary restrictions. Never infer these statuses for products outside this case.

Available money for the two lunches is 8.00 units. Each lunch has at most 8 minutes of preparation. A refrigerator, microwave, safe drinking water and the listed packaging instructions are available. Ari's existing plan uses one listed portion each of R, B, V and O for this familiar lunch; this is a fictional personal plan, not a serving recommendation. All packs contain exactly two such portions except where shown. Prices are whole-package cash costs, not per-portion costs.

### Action 1 — Build a meal that fits

Make the R/B/V/O lunch and decide whether to include F. Identify energy, protein, fiber, micronutrient variety and fluid sources, and at least two unknowns about the whole day. Calculate whole-package cash, one-lunch preparation time and the provided protein/fiber subtotal. Do not invent values for F or P. State the safety disposition of S and U separately: known incompatible and unverified are different reasons not to select them.

Use `foods | five functions | known safety basis | unknowns | whole-package cash | preparation | opening/use plan | alternative`. Save this before [later-packet.md](later-packet.md). Your decision concerns the supplied plan's fit; no one needs to eat the fictional quantities.

### Action 2 — Use the later meal and repeat record

The later packet supplies actual simulated preparation and repeat times. Check the label's storage boundary before claiming the second meal is usable. Compare cash already spent with extra cash required for a replacement. Time and storage can defeat a meal that looks nutritionally attractive. Do not consume questionable food to satisfy a repeat requirement; if the case no longer supplies a feasible safe option, record the access gap explicitly.

### Action 3 — Preserve nourishment and flexibility

Choose retain, adapt or seek support based on the repeat. Name a realistic alternative protein or preparation within the same constraints, preserving allergy verification and the existing plan. Distinguish an alternative calculated on paper from an actual second meal. If a real pattern raises adequacy concerns, restrictive eating, unwanted weight change or medical-diet uncertainty, bring a specific question to a qualified clinician or dietitian rather than escalating self-tracking.

Attempt [fresh checks](check-prompts.md) before [corrective guidance](check-answers.md). A complete source artifact contains the feasible first plan, honest nutrient unknowns, storage/cash accounting and repeat decision. It does not add an application completion condition or imply whole-diet adequacy.

## Access, harder work and transfer

Use a calculator, read-aloud labels or a consented preparation helper. Prepared, blended or culturally familiar equivalents may be used only when compatible with actual allergy, swallowing and other care needs. Food assistance and preparation support are valid practical routes; a budget shortfall is not a character judgment. Keep personal food/health records private.

Harder case: before purchasing anything, replace B with P and retain R/V/O/F. Compute 7.40 units cash and 7 minutes preparation; both fit. Protein and fiber totals are now unknown because P and F lack figures. Do not keep B's nutrient numbers after replacing it. This is a feasible alternative for the fictional Ari only because P's tolerability is explicitly supplied.

At a later ordinary real opportunity, use familiar safe foods within your established needs, make or obtain the meal, and compare a second occasion for satisfaction, access, cost and preparation burden. Keep other meals and fluid needs in view. Do not impose a deficit, supplement regimen or elimination diet. Actual food preparation is behavioral evidence only if performed; simulated records and author review are different evidence types, and two meals do not prove broad nutrition competence.

**Supportive:** A tolerated, affordable pattern is actually repeated with appropriate storage and acceptable burden.

**Mixed:** The first meal works, but timing or access makes its repeat require another arrangement.

**Contradictory:** An allergen is tested, unsafe leftovers are used, or nourishment is withheld to make the numbers look favorable.

**Inconclusive:** A menu is calculated without an actual meal on the live route, or missing label facts are silently filled in.
