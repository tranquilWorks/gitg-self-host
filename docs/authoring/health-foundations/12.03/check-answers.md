# 12.03 — Corrective guidance

1. Two servings give 8 g protein and 4 g fiber. Buying the package requires 3.60 units even though two-thirds of its cost is 2.40. Serving size is a label unit, not a portion recommendation. If you used the allocated cost as cash, redo the budget with whole packages.
2. One topping is unverified; the other is known incompatible. Neither is established suitable. “Plant based” does not resolve an allergy, and absence of information cannot become reassurance. Use a known suitable alternative or obtain reliable information consistent with the existing allergy plan; do not taste-test uncertainty.
3. Keep the known subtotal and mark the total unknown. A relevant verified label or appropriate qualified advice could resolve a genuine question. A copied number does not establish adequacy, and a target invented for this exercise is not an individual nutrition need.

This is source reasoning about a synthetic meal, not a dietetic assessment.
