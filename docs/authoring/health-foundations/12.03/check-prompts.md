# 12.03 — Fresh checks

1. A fictional container holds three servings, each with 4 g protein and 2 g fiber. The whole container costs 3.60 units. Calculate the label quantities for two servings and the cash needed to buy the container. Does the label tell the person to eat two servings?
2. A topping says “plant based”; its ingredients are unreadable and cross-contact information is absent. The person has a relevant allergy. Another topping explicitly contains that allergen. Distinguish the statuses and explain whether either is established suitable by those facts.
3. A planned lunch includes a food with no protein value supplied. A reviewer substitutes a different food's number to reach a target. Correct the calculation and explain what additional information, if actually needed, could resolve the gap without prescribing a diet.
