# 12.01 — Corrective guidance

1. Vaccination status is uncertain; the request was acknowledged, but the record has not arrived. Ask the appropriate records/clinical service what history is available and who can interpret individual needs. Withhold both “never vaccinated” and “protected,” and do not choose doses from absence. If you treated a blank as zero, separate the observation (blank portal) from the historical event it cannot establish.
2. The slot is offered, not confirmed, and care is not completed. Ask about transport/access and confirm an achievable appointment through the service. Later attendance and the actual care provided need their own evidence. If you marked the offer booked, restore the conditional wording instead of inventing a confirmation date.
3. Draft the bounded dental question only. Additional disclosure and public sending exceed permission and are unnecessary. Keep private details in authorized care channels. Faster response does not supply consent, qualified advice or confidentiality.

All three require a supporting fact and an explicit unknown; polished prose without those distinctions is incomplete. These are author-provided educational keys, not clinical assessment or independent review.
