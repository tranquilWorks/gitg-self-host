# 12.01 — Replies to reveal after preserving the draft

These branches are separate simulated futures; neither is an actual service contact.

## Branch A: partial information

Day 1, 11:00: Morgan authorizes and sends the records request privately. Day 1, 14:00: the service acknowledges it. Day 3, 10:00: Morgan receives a correctly identified record. It lists one historic entry and says earlier records were not transferred. It does not state present vaccination needs. Day 3, 15:00: the clinic confirms a routine review on day 8; the appointment has not happened. The pharmacist, dentist and private sexual-health discussion have no new event in this branch.

Update each status. Draft a teach-back that distinguishes the received partial record from a completed review and asks who will resolve the remaining history. Preserve the medicine and dental next actions. Do not use the single entry to construct a vaccine schedule.

## Branch B: no record

Day 1, 11:00: Morgan authorizes and sends the request. The service acknowledges it at 14:00. No further reply arrives by the start of day 4. Morgan has not contacted the records desk yet. There is no new symptom information and no other care event.

Use the supplied day-4 follow-up rule. Write “follow-up due, not yet made.” Acknowledgement proves receipt of the request only. Do not invent a missing vaccination, repeat dose or completed clinical review.

## Review after your attempt

Branch A supports received partial record and booked review, while interpretation and care remain pending. Branch B supports sent/acknowledged request and an open follow-up. In both, the information gap is narrower than “all preventive care complete.” If a new symptom concern arises in life, use timely qualified advice appropriate to it rather than treating day 4 as a symptom waiting rule.
