# 12.01 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Health literacy and preventive care

**Scope (unchanged):** Understand screening, symptoms, medications, vaccination, dental care, sexual health, and how to work with clinicians.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Maintains appropriate preventive care and seeks qualified evaluation when warranted.

Health and exercise content must be adapted to the person and does not replace licensed medical care.
