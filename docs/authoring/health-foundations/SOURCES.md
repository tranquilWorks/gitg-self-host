The canonical curriculum and the exact six existing `docs/authoring/exercises/12.yaml` entries were read in full. Their IDs, scope, action/evidence semantics and clinical boundaries remain unchanged. The current `contracts/tailored-practice-authoring.yaml` and PR72 physical-capacity scope/quality records establish why 12.05 and 12.08 are skipped. Morgan's records exchange, Lee's seven-night dataset, Ari's package/budget/storage case, Rowan's care card and garment alternative, Dev's loop/return/recovery record, Noor's shelf comparison, all fresh checks and corrective prose were newly authored for this batch. They are not copied clinical interventions, validated questionnaires or recovered participant stories. Numeric times, doses, distances and thresholds are explicitly fictional case constraints or original educational rules; they do not come from clinical standards. Do not apply them as personal health instructions.

The teaching distinctions and scope maps are the author's synthesis from the canonical scope and inspected sources. Synthetic scenarios teach reasoning and bounded preparation; actual health behaviors, qualified observations and longitudinal outcomes require separate events. The default never pretends desk work trains aerobic endurance or changes joint range. Later live routes preserve established plans and can end in adaptation, support, defer or appropriate care.

## Unresolved reviews and integration

Author editorial review and automated fixture/source checks can identify arithmetic, chronology, missing-data, access and claim defects. They are not independent cold-start review, actual learner acceptance, clinical/dietetic/exercise specialist approval, cultural universality, accessibility validation, therapeutic benefit or psychometric validity. Those reviews remain pending individually for all six, including evaluation of triggering language, care access, nutrition/allergy communication and exercise boundaries. A citation is not a review receipt.

No runtime package, compiler selection, action ID, observation allowlist, score, completion rule, active/paused instruction, formal review ledger or participant record changes. Future integration must use the existing authoring/compiler and current/recovery projections with separately applicable verification. Source publication is neither clinical release nor production deployment.
