# 12.02 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Sleep and circadian health

**Scope (unchanged):** Manage schedule, light, caffeine, environment, stress, and recovery to support sufficient sleep.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Sleep is regular enough to support cognition, mood, and physical function.

Health and exercise content must be adapted to the person and does not replace licensed medical care.
