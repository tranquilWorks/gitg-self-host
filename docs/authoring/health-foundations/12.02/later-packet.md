## Comparison and next step

Baseline opportunities are 480, 450 and 480 minutes: 1,410 total, 470 mean. Trial opportunities are 480, 480, 480 and 420: 1,860 total, 465 mean. This is five minutes less mean opportunity, not five minutes less measured sleep.

The change was used on 3/4 trial nights. Manageable functioning was reported on 2/3 baseline nights and 2/3 observed trial nights, with 1/4 trial outcomes missing. Calling trial functioning 3/4 manageable fabricates T3; calling it 2/4 manageable silently treats missing as non-manageable unless explicitly labeled a conservative sensitivity calculation. With T3 unfavorable the trial proportion would be 2/4; with it favorable, 3/4. The missing report can change the decision.

The observed proportion meets Lee's toy “no worse” rule, but missingness and a care-related interruption make benefit inconclusive. A defensible decision is retain the acceptable audio provisionally for convenience, make no sleep-benefit claim, and revisit on comparable ordinary nights without increasing tracking or reducing care. Rejection is also reasonable if Lee finds the routine unwanted; completion does not require a favorable result.
