# 12.02 — Fresh checks

1. An opportunity runs 22:50–06:20. The person reports an awakening of unknown length. Calculate opportunity and state what can be said about actual sleep. Would moving bedtime to 23:20 to finish the log preserve that opportunity?
2. Two of three baseline mornings were manageable. In a four-night trial, two mornings were manageable, one difficult and one missing. A reviewer writes “75% improved, so the routine cured insomnia.” Correct the numerator, denominator, missingness and claim.
3. A trial appears favorable but removes a needed safety light and coincides with a different work shift. Identify the support correction and the comparison limit. Do not design a treatment schedule.
