# 12.06 — Session and aftermath records

Ordinary breathing is reported back after 3 minutes; later that day and the next morning ordinary function is reported usual. No warning sign is reported. Before session 2 on a later suitable day, the existing plan and usual recovery are rechecked and remain appropriate.

Session 2: same loop, aid and planned effort. After 4 minutes Dev notices unusual breathlessness, stops at the accessible exit and uses the appropriate urgent medical advice route. The record does not include a diagnosis, a measured recovery time or clearance to resume. A friend suggests completing the remaining 2 minutes later to reach the target.

Preserve actual work as 6 + 4 = 10 minutes across two attempts, not 12 completed minutes. The missing recovery record is unknown, not a quick recovery. Stopping is consistent with the plan; resuming to earn the remaining minutes is not supported. The next step follows the medical advice obtained, with no exercise repeat or escalation assumed in this record.

The first session supports tolerability under its conditions. The second supplies an adverse observation requiring the stated help route; this is mixed evidence about the two-session participation plan, not a claim that stopping was poor behavior. Two sessions cannot establish fitness gain or diagnose the cause.

Alternative supportive branch, replacing session 2 entirely: 6 easy minutes, same conditions and usual aid, ordinary breathing after 3 minutes, later function usual, no concern reported. This supports repeating the unchanged plan, not automatic progression. Keep the branches separate; do not borrow the alternative's recovery for the main session.
