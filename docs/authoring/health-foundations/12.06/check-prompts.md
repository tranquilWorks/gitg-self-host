# 12.06 — Fresh checks

1. A fictional existing plan allows 10 minutes total movement. The chosen route requires 6 minutes outward and 6 to return, with no early exit. Does it fit? Can a promised future faster return be used to make it fit?
2. Two sessions take the same time, but the second removes usual assistance and has no recovery record. The learner concludes “same dose, no burden, ready for intervals.” Identify the changed condition, unknown and unsupported transition.
3. A person stops a familiar effort at a warning sign. A friend proposes finishing the missed minutes that evening. State what is recorded and which authority, rather than the target, controls any resumption.
