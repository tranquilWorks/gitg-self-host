# 12.06 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Cardiovascular endurance

**Scope (unchanged):** Develop aerobic base, higher-intensity capacity, and recovery appropriate to health and goals.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can sustain and recover from meaningful cardiovascular effort.

Health and exercise content must be adapted to the person and does not replace licensed medical care.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aerobic base | Concepts and Dev route/dose plan | Include return, easy effort, support and repeated opportunity | Six minutes is a fictional individual plan, not a prescription |
| Higher-intensity capacity | Intensity distinction and harder pace case | Recognize changed demand and identify need for individualized preparation | No intervals, stress test or capacity measurement is prescribed |
| Recovery | Immediate and delayed observations; main/alternative later branch | Unknown recovery stays unknown; warning sign stops the attempt | No automatic progression, diagnosis or medical clearance |
| Health and goals | Existing-plan authority, accessible routes and PEM boundary | Select an appropriate familiar route or preparation/defer | Qualified advice and actual conditions govern a live attempt |
| Sustain and recover from meaningful effort | Route purpose, two-session comparison and real transfer | Compare participation, effort and aftermath under comparable conditions | Two attempts cannot demonstrate measured cardiovascular adaptation |
