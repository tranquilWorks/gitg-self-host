# 12.04 — Corrective guidance

1. The clothing action occurred; appearance feelings remain. Care does not require a favorable feeling. Review whether the garment supports comfort and access, and leave the feeling honestly recorded. Do not invent a positive emotion to make the action count.
2. Appearance does not establish health or rule out an eating disorder. Repeated interference deserves appropriate support without a diagnosis from this exercise. The message is drafted, not sent, and no response or care is established. Using the chosen support route and qualified service is an available next action; it has not yet happened.
3. No. A style preference can coexist with nourishment, comfort and dignity. The boundary is making care contingent on achieving an appearance, using deprivation or compensation, or dismissing real concerns. Do not replace one compulsory appearance rule with compulsory body positivity.

These checks assess the supplied reasoning. They are not an eating-disorder screen or treatment evaluation.
