# 12.04 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Food relationship and body image

**Scope (unchanged):** Separate nourishment and health from shame, comparison, compulsive control, or status.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can pursue health and appearance goals without degrading dignity or creating harmful behavior.

Health and exercise content must be adapted to the person and does not replace licensed medical care.
