# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. An instruction looks polished but says “put everything back” without defining the normal state. Which quality work matters next?
2. The author explains a missing step during a successful first use. Can that be counted as independent usability?
3. A revised paper-room instruction works once without hints. What does that establish about a real room's safety and the author's reputation?
