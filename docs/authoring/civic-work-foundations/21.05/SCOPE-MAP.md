# 21.05 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Quality, craftsmanship, and reputation

**Scope (unchanged):** Match care, robustness, elegance, documentation, and finish to consequence and purpose.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Work becomes associated with trust because quality is consistent, not performative.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Care and consequence | Correctness/access and authorized specification | Complete paper-room setup/restore/contact | Toy not real safety or accessibility certification |
| Robustness and documentation | Final state, exceptions and version history | NORMAL STORAGE includes six chairs and the table; column 4 clear | No ambiguous restore instruction accepted as complete |
| Elegance and finish | Useful readability versus decoration | Essential restore defect repaired before border | Appearance not conformance |
| Consistent quality and reputation | Two comparable uses without hidden rescue | First assisted; second uncoached with new reader | Not two uncoached successes or durable reputation |
