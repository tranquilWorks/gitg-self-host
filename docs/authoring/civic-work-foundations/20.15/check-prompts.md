# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

2. A favored group asserts that alternatives are exhausted, but a relevant review route has not been tried. What changes and what does not?
3. Does accountability require waiving legal rights, and can later moral language prove that an earlier convenience-based act was principled?
