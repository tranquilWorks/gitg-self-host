# 21.04 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Deliberate mastery and expertise

**Scope (unchanged):** Target weaknesses, practice at the edge of ability, seek expert critique, and integrate components.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Performance improves on a meaningful external standard.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Target weakness and edge of ability | Fixed external procedure and baseline component | Damaged-item route against three criteria | Effort/praise not performance |
| Expert critique | Relevant skilled advice versus prestige/preference | Supplied steward correction verified against rule | No actual expert receipt |
| Focused practice | Two discriminating safe examples | P1 normal BLUE; P2 damaged desk | Two component checks not whole-task mastery |
| Integration and meaningful standard | Fresh whole-task cases and regressions | F1 loses log: 2/3; F2 corrected: 3/3 | No actual learner, retention or broad transfer |

Canonical classification: applicability context_sensitive; normative status cross_tradition_core_or_broadly_recurrent; formation modes: agency, stewardship, interdependence.

Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Civic learning does not compel belief, affiliation, voting, public disclosure or participation. Work learning does not compel paid employment, a role change or unlimited availability. Actual work requires appropriate authority, consent, context and qualified guidance where needed; keep private and protected records outside app evidence.

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: performance_task, artifact, observer_feedback, objective_indicator. This is preserved metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.

The canonical record has no professional_boundary field. Practical exercise safeguards do not add a canonical boundary.

No runtime selection, action, evidence field, score, completion or participant record changes. Supplied fiction is not actual observer evidence. Formal M6K A/B/C, independent cold-start, actual learner, qualified and owner content acceptance remain pending; author editorial review is not an acceptance receipt.
