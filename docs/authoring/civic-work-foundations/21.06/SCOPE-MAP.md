# 21.06 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Customer, stakeholder, and user understanding

**Scope (unchanged):** Understand who is affected, what problem matters, and how success is experienced downstream.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Improves work because real needs—not only internal assumptions—shape decisions.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who is affected | Primary user, helper and excluded nonuser | Invitation task and approved contact/format | Senior preference not all-user knowledge |
