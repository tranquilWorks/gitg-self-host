# 21.07 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Systems and organizational awareness

**Scope (unchanged):** See interfaces, incentives, dependencies, constraints, failure modes, and institutional context.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Prevents or solves a problem beyond the immediate assigned task.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Interfaces and dependencies | Inputs, outputs, owners and readiness | Q1→writer→approver→publisher→correction | Latest timestamp is not approval |
| Incentives, constraints and context | Metric hypothesis and approval window | Draft-count hypothesis; 10:00–10:30 approval | No motive inference or authority bypass |
| Failure modes and agreement | Receiver confirms version-binding gap | Three owners accept change; ambiguous card held | No unilateral redesign or invented publication |
| System effect beyond assigned step | Corrected test, burden and maintenance | N2 approved; N3 draft; 2+1 known added minutes | No net saving, throughput or durable reliability claim |

Canonical classification: applicability context_sensitive; normative status cross_tradition_core_or_broadly_recurrent; formation modes: agency, stewardship, interdependence.

Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Civic learning does not compel belief, affiliation, voting, public disclosure or participation. Work learning does not compel paid employment, a role change or unlimited availability. Actual work requires appropriate authority, consent, context and qualified guidance where needed; keep private and protected records outside app evidence.

Canonical measurement boundary: Use at least two independent evidence types, including one behavioral, observer, artifact, objective, or longitudinal indicator; self-report alone is insufficient for score advancement. Preferred evidence types: performance_task, artifact, observer_feedback, objective_indicator. This is preserved metadata, not a new exercise scoring rule. Human dignity is never scored.

The canonical record has no professional_boundary field. Practical exercise safeguards do not add a canonical boundary.

No runtime selection, action, evidence field, score, completion or participant record changes. Supplied fiction is not actual observer evidence.
