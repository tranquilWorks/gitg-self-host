# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. An angry post identifies a real scheduling error but blames every member of a group. Must you accept or reject its factual concern and tactic together?
2. Someone says every contradictory record is proof of a cover-up. What is wrong with that evidence rule, even when you like their preferred outcome?
3. A dated maintenance record explains a booking change. Does this prove fair handling, no possible misconduct, or a successful correction to an audience?
