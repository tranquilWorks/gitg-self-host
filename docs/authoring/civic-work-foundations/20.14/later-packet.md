# Later packet — a partial offer leaves a requirement unmet

Supplied fiction, not actual participant evidence.

In one separate refusal rehearsal the owner says no change can be promised until staffing is checked. The learner asks which constraint is unresolved and when the owner can give a definite answer, without claiming that a review date has been agreed. This is a hypothetical branch, not an additional event in the accepted-offer sequence.

In the second tabletop branch the owner supplies a matching text notice and offers a telephone line from 12:00 to 13:00. The two users acknowledge the text improvement but say the telephone-only route does not meet the requested written access channel. The learner credits the text notice, retains the written-channel requirement and drafts a follow-up to the owner about monitoring and a feasible deadline.

The follow-up remains unsent, and no written access route is accepted or implemented. No public campaign, mediation session, staffing change or actual meeting outcome occurs. The record distinguishes a useful partial concession from a complete solution and a reviewed next step from automatic escalation.
