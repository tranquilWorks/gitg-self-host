All Cedar facts, dates, quantities, materials and responses are original supplied fiction, not actual participant or specialist acceptance evidence.

## S01

[Identifying conspiracy theories](https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/coronavirus-response/fighting-disinformation/identifying-conspiracy-theories_en) — European Commission / UNESCO educational collaboration; pandemic-era resource.

Type: first-party public education. Inspected: General mechanism, own-belief and response sections: scapegoating, closed evidence loops, checking and avoiding ridicule. Used for 20.13: Separate a checkable claim from manipulative rhetoric; allow correction even for a preferred conclusion.

Limit: Pandemic-specific claims and examples are not adopted as current facts. Credential or tone heuristics are not truth tests. No linked handbook, campaign diagnosis or efficacy validation.

## S02

[What mediation is and how it can help — Mediation at work](https://www.acas.org.uk/mediation) — Acas; updated October 18, 2022.

Type: primary public-service guidance. Inspected: Opening explanation of an impartial mediator, voluntary participation and control over agreement. Used for 20.14: Distinguish assisted agreement from an imposed decision.

Limit: Workplace context. Confidentiality, legal effect and suitability statements are not universal civic or jurisdictional rules. Not a protest, strike or restorative-process operating manual. UN Peacemaker and DPPA pages could not be retrieved and are not claimed inspected.
