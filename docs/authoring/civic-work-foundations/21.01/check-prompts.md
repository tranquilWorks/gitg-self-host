# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. A prestigious option has a high overall score but may exceed a non-negotiable hours limit. Can its other benefits cancel that uncertainty?
2. A role-holder describes 36 hours while an advertisement says 30. Which facts have you learned and which authority question remains?
3. A manager agrees to discuss redesigning your current role. Does that establish approval, mentoring capacity or a completed career decision?
