# Later packet — the stress case is not funded by an invented reserve

Supplied fiction, not actual participant evidence or a policy forecast.

In the alternative stress scenario twenty of the same 100 members each need 80 units. Contributions remain 1,000 and needs total 1,600: a 600-unit shortfall before administration. The ordinary scenario's 200 was a pre-cost arithmetic remainder, not an established reserve that may be spent twice.

Compare conditional options. An extra verified reserve of 300 would still leave 300 unpaid before costs; no such reserve is actually supplied. Increasing everyone's contribution from 10 to 16 would produce 1,600 if all 100 could pay, but leaves no administrative allowance and does not establish affordability. Limiting support to 50 for each of 20 recipients would use 1,000 and leave 30 of each stated 80-unit need unmet, again before administration. Broader funding would require an identified source and would transfer some burden elsewhere.
