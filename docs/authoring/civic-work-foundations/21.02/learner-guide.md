# 21.02 — Professional reliability

Make one small commitment that another person can plan around, give an early evidence-based update and close the handoff honestly. Allow ten minutes to agree scope, a twenty-to-forty-minute work period and a brief review. Use a harmless agenda or the supplied fiction. Reliability does not mean limitless availability or preserving a dependable image by hiding uncertainty.

## A commitment is shared information

The receiver needs to know what will arrive, when, in which usable form and what they can do with it. Confirm the recipient, purpose, version, acceptance criteria and deadline, including time zone where relevant. Inspect your capacity and required inputs before saying yes. An enthusiastic promise is not more reliable than a smaller specific commitment that can be kept.

Preparedness means arranging the permitted materials and a place to track the next step. Organization should reduce uncertainty rather than create a elaborate system no one uses. Put the checkpoint early enough to act on a missing input. Identify who can supply it, what you can finish without it and when you will raise the risk. Do not make the recipient discover an obvious gap only at the deadline.

Status labels should describe evidence. “Drafted” means text exists, not that facts or usability are confirmed. “Blocked” should identify the missing dependency and a next owner or question. “Untested” protects the distinction between an artifact and demonstrated use. “Done” requires the agreed endpoint, not merely effort or a sent file. A changed forecast should arrive while the receiver still has choices.

An early update can preserve the other person's ability to plan even when the original result will be late. State the gap, forecast and alternatives without blame. A smaller provisional deliverable may be useful if the recipient explicitly accepts that scope. You cannot silently redefine completion after discovering that the full version is difficult. Keep the original commitment visible alongside the revision.

Punctuality is one dimension of reliability; usefulness is another. A file delivered early but missing a critical location is not the full ready-to-send agenda. Version labels and restrictions prevent a provisional artifact from being treated as final. Close the loop on acceptance, correction and what the recipient must still do. An acknowledgment of receipt may not establish usability unless the actual acceptance question was addressed.

Learn from the estimate without turning every difference into a moral judgment. Missing inputs, avoidable rework and interruptions affect effort and elapsed time differently. Record what was actually spent if known, how the forecast changed and one preparation improvement. A single handoff supports confidence in that instance; sustained professional reliability requires repeated evidence and respects rest, access and contractual boundaries.

## Worked example

An agenda writer has the discussion order but lacks a confirmed room. At the checkpoint they distinguish complete text from the blocked location. The receiver agrees to use a clearly marked planning draft privately. Later the full agenda arrives after the original deadline. Both facts matter: early communication was useful, and the original complete deliverable was not on time.

## Complete fictional commitment

On 10 October, Noor agrees to send Maya a plain-text agenda by 16:00 UTC, with a checkpoint at 12:00 UTC. The meeting is 12 October, 10:00–10:30 UTC. The complete agenda inputs are **Welcome and purpose, 5 minutes; review the draft notice, 15 minutes; decide owners and next dates, 10 minutes**. The recipient needs the time, location, ordered topics and durations, version and any required action. Use **Room N2** only after confirmation by the hall owner; that input is initially missing.

Noor estimates 25 minutes of drafting work and has a 35-minute available work period. Maya's agreed channel is a fictional private planning inbox, with no real address or actual transmission. Only the hall owner can confirm the room. No protected workplace or client data are involved.

## Prepare, update and deliver

1. Restate the original acceptance criteria, deadline and purpose. Gather the topic inputs, flag the room dependency and record the checkpoint. Output: a commitment the receiver can inspect, not an unqualified “I'll handle it.”
2. At the checkpoint distinguish completed content, missing location and remaining checks. Draft a specific owner query and a forecast. Output: an early factual update with an alternative provisional scope for Maya to accept or reject.
3. Create the agenda from the supplied topics and verify that durations total 30 minutes. If provisional scope is accepted, mark the room unconfirmed and prohibit attendee circulation until final. Output: a usable version for its agreed purpose, not the original complete result.
4. Save your forecast before the [later packet](later-packet.md). Track actual delivery, acceptance, later confirmation and final version separately. Output: an honest timing and scope account.
5. Compare estimated and actual effort and identify one preparation improvement. Close the receiver's remaining action, or state it clearly if unknown. Output: a specific lesson without claiming lasting trust or unlimited availability.

## Support and transfer

Use an oral or accessible agenda and a simple reminder if writing or a complex tracker adds friction. A harder case has a missed checkpoint: acknowledge it, give the current evidence and renegotiate rather than backdating a fictional update. The goal is truthful coordination, not a flawless story.

Real transfer can use a volunteer or household collaboration where employment information is sensitive. Confirm actual recipients and permission before sending anything. Keep private details in approved systems and broad status outside them. A rehearsal can improve wording but cannot establish an actual delivery, a person's trust or a long-term professional reputation.

## Source use

[NACE's professionalism description, S08](../SOURCES.md#S08), includes preparation, dependability, task completion, detail and accountability. Only that section is used; personal branding is not an outcome measure here. The specific deadline, acceptance states and agenda are original teaching design, not a validated reliability score or employment standard.


## Scope and evidence boundary

The canonical record has no professional_boundary field. The practical limits in this guide are exercise safeguards, not added canonical metadata.

Canonical applicability is context_sensitive. Paid employment, a career change or a particular work role is not compulsory. Normative status remains cross_tradition_core_or_broadly_recurrent. Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Keep private or protected records outside app evidence. Formal M6K A/B/C, independent cold-start, actual learner, qualified and owner content acceptance remain pending.
