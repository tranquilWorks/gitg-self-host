# 12.12 — Changed answers and accountable repair

Reveal after the three starting-card rehearsals. Each branch is separate and fictional.

## Branch A: withdraw before action

In a different mutually willing rehearsal, Sasha and Toni both agree at 10:00 to one handshake. At 10:01, before any touch, Toni says “Actually, no touch.” Sasha stops the approach and asks nothing further. No handshake occurs. At 10:02 Sasha asks whether a photo would be welcome; Toni gives no clear response. No photo is taken. The earlier handshake yes supports neither touch after withdrawal nor a photo.

## Branch B: acknowledge an overstep

Start again from no touch permission: Toni clearly asks for a wave. Sasha mistakenly starts unwanted contact, immediately stops and says, “I touched you after you asked for a wave. I'm sorry. I'll give you space.” Toni asks for space and no further discussion. Sasha steps back; no photo or private explanation is retained. The overstep occurred and remains in the record. Stopping and respecting space are repair actions; forgiveness and renewed touch permission are not supplied.

## Branch C: assistance and privacy

Toni uses a written “wave only” card. A helper accurately reads it aloud with Toni's permission. The helper has no permission to share why Toni prefers it. Sasha waves if Toni still wants that greeting and does not request a reason. Communication assistance is compatible with Toni's own choice; it does not turn the helper into the owner of that choice.

Your review should distinguish no touch after withdrawal, an actual overstep followed by bounded repair, and supported communication. None establishes real participant consent or a universal medical/legal rule.
