# 12.12 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Bodily autonomy and personal boundaries

**Scope (unchanged):** Protect consent, privacy, touch, medical choice, and freedom from coercion or exploitation.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can state and enforce bodily boundaries while respecting the same rights in others.

Health and exercise content must be adapted to the person and does not replace licensed medical care.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Consent and touch | Three starting cards and withdrawal branch | Let a current specific answer control action; no-touch option | No historical yes or silence promoted to permission |
| Privacy | Worked access example, separate photo permission and supported card | Retain minimal categories; do not share private reasons | Touch/private photo permission does not transfer to publication |
| Medical choice | Medical-preparation card and four questions | Ask about proposal, options, privacy/support and pause process | No universal legal rule, capacity assessment or clinical decision |
| Coercion/exploitation | Applicability boundary and harder hug-required case | Prefer safe support/fictional route when power makes live action unsafe | No required confrontation or disclosure |
| Reciprocal boundaries and repair | T card, later overstep and key | Respect either person's decline; stop/repair without demanding forgiveness | Repair does not create retroactive or renewed consent |
