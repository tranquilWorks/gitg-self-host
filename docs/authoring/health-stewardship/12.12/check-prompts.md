# 12.12 — Fresh checks

1. Both people agree to a handshake, but one withdraws before contact. The other says the earlier yes must be honored. What current action is permitted, and must the withdrawal be justified?
2. Someone agrees to a private greeting photo but says nothing about posting it. A helper also knows a private health explanation. Identify what permission exists, what remains absent and what the helper should retain or disclose.
3. An overstep occurs; the person asks for space and no more discussion. The actor wants to keep apologizing until forgiven. State the immediate action, honest record and limit on repair. Does the greeting example decide medical-capacity law?
