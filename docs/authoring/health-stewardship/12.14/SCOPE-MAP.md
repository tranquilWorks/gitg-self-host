# 12.14 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Functional aging and lifelong movement

**Scope (unchanged):** Train for independence, bone health, balance, carrying, getting up, mobility, and continued participation over decades.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Current habits explicitly protect future function, not only short-term performance.

Health and exercise content must be adapted to the person and does not replace licensed medical care.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Independence and continued participation | Valued garden goal and function/support table | Define participation with assistance as legitimate | No unaided living test or aging prediction |
| Bone health | NIAMS reference and explicit qualified-question row | Include missing loading advice without prescribing impact | A visit does not measure bone change |
| Balance and mobility | Familiar path, aid and unassessed outer path | Choose only the suitable familiar route or defer | No unsupported balance challenge |
| Carrying and getting up | Agreed supplies support and familiar assisted transfer | Keep aids/help and unresolved transfer questions visible | No unfamiliar loaded carry or floor-rise test |
| Strength/endurance habit over time | Eight-minute existing route, two opportunities and recurrence | Count actual movement separately from seated/waiting time | Two outings do not prove broad capacity or lifelong benefit |
| Adaptation across changing capacity | Cancelled return, unavailable bench variation and monthly triggers | Protect round-trip access and agree alternatives before claiming participation | A proposed home visit is not a completed visit |
