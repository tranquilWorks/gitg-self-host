# 12.14 — Functional aging and lifelong movement

## Connect today's habit to a future activity worth keeping

Lifelong movement is easier to plan around a valued activity than an abstract demand to stay young or independent. Visiting friends, tending a garden or joining a familiar group can require strength, endurance, balance, carrying, transfers and mobility—but also transport, accessible environments and willing support. Assistance can preserve participation; it is not the opposite of a worthwhile outcome.

This guide supplies a complete fictional garden-participation map and two-opportunity review. Allow 15 minutes for the map and schedule, 10 for later outcomes and 10 for checks. No walk, floor rise, balance challenge, loaded carry, purchase or health disclosure is required for the default. Its output is a maintenance/adaptation plan based on supplied facts, not a claim of improved bone health or decades of preserved function. Health and exercise content must be adapted to the person and does not replace licensed medical care.

## A whole function map without a universal exercise program

Strength supports producing force; endurance supports sustained activity; balance and stability matter when managing position; mobility provides usable movement. Carrying and getting up or transferring combine several capacities and environmental conditions. They are not tests someone must perform unaided to deserve participation.

Bone health belongs in the map even when the chosen activity cannot establish a bone outcome. NIAMS describes [exercise and bone health](https://www.niams.nih.gov/health-topics/exercise-your-bone-health), including the need for appropriate individual advice with low bone density or physical limitations. This guide does not prescribe loading, impact, a fall-risk test or a bone-density measurement. A qualified plan may address a training need while access supports remain in place.

Current habits can support a future participation goal without guaranteeing future ability. Record a recurrence that fits the person's existing plan, a review date and earlier review triggers such as changed health, support or access. Calendar recurrence is not evidence of actual habit. Two suitable opportunities can test practical fit, not lifelong adaptation.

## Worked example: the limiting factor is access

Taylor comfortably uses a familiar short indoor route with their usual aid, but a venue moves upstairs with no usable lift. Repeating the route faster does not solve the venue barrier. Asking for a suitable room or another participation format can protect the goal while movement continues under the existing plan. Declining an inaccessible visit is not evidence that Taylor's physical capacity declined.

## Complete materials: Lian's community garden plan

Lian wants to keep joining a friend for conversation at the community garden over coming years. Lian already has an individualized plan for a familiar supported route: up to 8 minutes total for the familiar garden-path segments and bench-transfer transitions on a suitable outing, including return, with a seated break and usual aid. Transport boarding is separately covered by the existing plan; it is not silently assigned zero physical effort. It is already appropriate and familiar in the case; no increase is authorized. Ordinary function and recovery must be suitable before each opportunity. No new symptoms are reported at the starting checkpoint. These fictional limits are not instructions for the reader.

Initial visit plan: transport arrives at 10:00, route to bench 10:00–10:04, seated conversation 10:04–10:24, return route 10:24–10:28, confirmed pickup at 10:30. Each four-minute segment includes its familiar supported bench-transfer transition: settling at the bench on arrival and leaving it on return. The two minutes before pickup are supported waiting at the accessible door, not extra movement or a spare exercise. The 30-minute on-site schedule ends when pickup begins; boarding follows its separate existing support arrangement. The seated conversation fits Lian's established needs in this case. The friend has explicitly agreed to both the assistance and the stated timing. Do not allocate their help beyond that agreement.

### Action 1 — Build the present-to-future map

For every row write `valued purpose | current function | support | unresolved question | suitable next action`. Identify strength, endurance, balance, mobility and bone-health questions separately. Select the familiar route as the bounded current habit; it does not replace the other rows. Verify both return and transport timings before calling the whole visit feasible.

Write the existing 8-minute limit and the pre-opportunity check: usual recovery/function, no concerning change, suitable conditions, aid and agreed assistance/transport. If a relevant condition fails or is unknown, adapt or defer rather than attempt a new test. Serious symptoms require appropriate medical help.

### Action 2 — Preserve the first plan and analyze two opportunities

Save the map and timing before [later-packet.md](later-packet.md). The packet contains one completed opportunity and a later planned opportunity whose conditions change. Use `opportunity | movement actually performed | support/access confirmed | participation | recovery | next decision`. A missed trip is not zero ability; an unknown recovery is not ordinary recovery. No one should do extra movement to make up an inaccessible outing.

### Action 3 — Make maintenance and adaptation concrete

Set a provisional recurrence at the next ordinary weekly opportunity only while the existing plan remains suitable. Review in one month or sooner if health, ordinary function, recovery, transport, environment or consented support changes. Preserve a qualified question about any unaddressed function, including appropriate bone-health considerations. Name an access improvement available now and a fallback for changed capacity, such as a confirmed accessible meeting place or a conversation at home with consent. A proposed alternative is not already agreed.

Attempt [fresh checks](check-prompts.md) before [corrective guidance](check-answers.md). A complete source artifact is a whole function/support map, round-trip plan, honest two-opportunity review and a recurring plan with triggers. These outputs do not add application actions, typed evidence or completion requirements.

## Access, harder practice and real transfer

Use text, a reader or a helper to analyze the case. Seated participation, wheeling, assistive transport and supported transfers remain legitimate options. No movement is needed for the default. A live route requires a familiar suitable action or an appropriate qualified plan; preparation alone is not movement evidence.

Harder variation: the usual bench becomes unavailable, and the only offered seat requires an unfamiliar transfer. Identify the missing suitability and assistance information. Find an already suitable alternative or use an appropriate advice/access route; do not practice an unsupported transfer to keep the booking. Participation may need a different setting while the physical question is addressed.

At later real opportunities, use one familiar appropriate activity twice under existing limits and supports, with ordinary recovery checked before repeating. Record absent opportunities honestly. Keep the meaningful activity visible when capacity changes; more support may sustain the goal. Over longer periods, actual comparable functional and qualified observations could inform a maintenance plan, but this simulation and two outings cannot prove future independence, bone change or injury prevention. Self-review, independent observation and clinical assessment remain distinct.

**Supportive:** A familiar habit and confirmed supports enable actual valued participation with tolerable aftermath.

**Mixed:** Movement is manageable but transport, seating, assistance or recovery prevents the next planned visit.

**Contradictory:** Aids are removed or an unfamiliar balance/transfer/load challenge is used to prove independence.

**Inconclusive:** A future goal or calendar exists without linking functions, support and actual opportunities.
