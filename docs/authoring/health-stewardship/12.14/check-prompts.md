# 12.14 — Fresh checks

1. A current familiar plan allows 10 minutes total movement. A route needs 6 minutes to the bench and 6 to return. Does a 20-minute seated conversation make it fit? Identify the practical correction without prescribing training.
2. One outing occurs; the next is cancelled because return transport is unavailable. A proposed home visit has no agreed time. State completed movement opportunities and participation status without treating the cancellation as lost ability.
3. A future goal needs a transfer and bone-health discussion not covered by the current plan. A friend suggests an unfamiliar floor-rise test without support to prove independence. What can be done now, and what remains for an appropriate qualified route?
