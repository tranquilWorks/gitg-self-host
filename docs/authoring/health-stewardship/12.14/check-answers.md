# 12.14 — Corrective guidance

1. The stated route movement requires 12 minutes, exceeding the limit by 2. Seated conversation is not negative movement time and cannot cancel the return demand. Use an already suitable shorter/access-supported arrangement or defer until a feasible option is confirmed; do not add training to rescue the arithmetic.
2. One movement opportunity is completed out of two planned. The cancelled outing is an access barrier, not evidence of lost physical capacity. The home visit is proposed, not agreed or completed. Preserve both the prior success and the unresolved return/alternative arrangement.
3. Keep accessible participation and current supports while preparing the specific transfer and bone-health questions for an appropriately qualified person. Do not perform an unfamiliar unsupported floor rise. Planning or advice is not a measured functional or bone outcome, and assistance is compatible with a valued lifelong goal.

These keys assess planning and evidence distinctions, not independence or physical capacity.
