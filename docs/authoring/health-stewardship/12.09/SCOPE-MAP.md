# 12.09 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Body composition and metabolic health

**Scope (unchanged):** Understand weight change, muscle, fat, waist, blood markers, and sustainable methods.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Uses health and performance evidence rather than appearance alone to guide change.

Health and exercise content must be adapted to the person and does not replace licensed medical care.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weight change | Measure reference and Ellis scale row | Keep unchanged total distinct from unmeasured components | No body category or personal monitoring requirement |
| Muscle and fat | Separate tissue row; fresh check 1 | Withhold tissue claims without a suitable measure | No composition test or physique inference |
| Waist | Measurement reference and missing waist row | Preserve missingness and method limits | No compulsory waist target |
| Blood markers | Clinical context reference, review request and later branch | Distinguish flag, booking, attended review and agreed follow-up | No diagnosis, unit conversion guess or metabolic verdict |
| Sustainable methods | Existing meal/activity plan, action card and transfer | Preserve nourishment, support, burden and review trigger | No restriction, supplement regimen or outcome guarantee |
| Health/performance rather than appearance | Worked bag and three task records | Separate comparable task-time change from changed load | Task observation is not measured tissue or broad health change |
