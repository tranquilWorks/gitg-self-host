# 12.11 — Substances and altered states

## Resolve the actual risk without experimenting on yourself

A useful substance decision identifies the product, possible interactions, current impairment, activity demands and whether outside help is needed. “Legal,” “natural,” “prescribed” and “everyone uses it” are not interchangeable safety conclusions. Neither fear nor reassurance substitutes for the specific facts.

This source companion teaches risk literacy across alcohol, nicotine, recreational substances, performance drugs and medications. Allow 15 minutes for the five-part case review, 10 for the transport/update decision and 10 for checks. Use the complete fictional packet, paper or dictation. No substance purchase, use, withdrawal experiment, live contact or private substance history is required. Health and exercise content must be adapted to the person and does not replace licensed medical care. This is not dosing, detoxification, treatment or performance-enhancement instruction.

## Five questions, different risks

Ask: what is the actual substance and label; what other medicines or substances matter; what activity requires alertness; is control, harm or withdrawal a concern; and which qualified or emergency route is appropriate? Apply the questions to each category without assuming all effects are identical.

| Category | Useful distinction | What this exercise cannot conclude |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alcohol | Impairment, interactions and a pattern of dependence are different questions | No amount or waiting time is declared safe for driving |
| Nicotine | Dependence and product-related harms matter even without obvious intoxication | No product is called harmless or prescribed as a personal cessation treatment |
| Recreational substances | Ingredient, strength, impairment and interaction information may be uncertain | A familiar seller, name or peer report does not establish contents or safety |
| Performance drugs | An appearance/performance goal does not establish medical indication or safe use | No cycle, dose, combination or sourcing advice |
| Prescription/nonprescription medicines | Appropriate treatment can coexist with impairment, interactions or dependence risk | A label warning is not permission to stop treatment abruptly or share a prescription |

NIAAA explains [alcohol–medicine interaction risks](https://www.niaaa.nih.gov/publications/brochures-and-fact-sheets/harmful-interactions-mixing-alcohol-with-medicines); check the actual label and pharmacist/prescriber rather than a guessed safe interval. FDA explains [nicotine dependence and differing product risks](https://www.fda.gov/tobacco-products/health-effects-tobacco-use/nicotine-why-tobacco-products-are-addictive). NIDA describes [risks of anabolic steroid misuse](https://nida.nih.gov/research-topics/anabolic-steroids). These references support category distinctions, not an individual regimen or moral ranking.

Possible alcohol or sedative dependence needs medical support before a sudden reduction or stop; severe withdrawal can be dangerous. See [NIAAA's withdrawal boundary](https://www.niaaa.nih.gov/health-professionals-communities/core-resource-on-alcohol/alcohol-use-disorder-risk-diagnosis-recovery) and [FDA's benzodiazepine warning](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-and-availability/fda-requiring-boxed-warning-updated-improve-safe-use-benzodiazepine-drug-class). This does not mean continue unsafe use without help: obtain timely appropriate medical advice, and use emergency services for suspected overdose, severe withdrawal or immediate danger. Do not wait for a worksheet, routine appointment or transport plan in an emergency.

## Worked example: the ingredient is missing

Quinn is offered a product called “natural focus.” No ingredient list is available. They also take a medicine whose interaction information they do not know. The useful record is “contents and interaction unknown,” not “safe because natural” or “all products are equally dangerous.” Quinn does not try it for evidence; they can decline and ask a pharmacist a specific question if there is a real treatment/product concern. A refusal rehearsal is not actual use or treatment.

## Complete materials: Remy's journey home

At 20:30, Remy is at a low-stakes social meeting. Remy's fictional prescribed product M has an existing label warning about drowsiness, alcohol and driving; the exercise supplies no dose or medicine identity. Remy is currently drowsy after taking it as prescribed and has not consumed alcohol. No overdose or acute emergency sign is supplied. A friend offers alcohol and says coffee will make driving fine. A pharmacist question about the actual product is unresolved. No medication change is authorized.

Remy can decline the offered alcohol and avoid driving while arranging suitable help. The host has already agreed Remy can wait safely inside with support until 21:00. Remy has zero transport money available tonight. The following are the only currently supplied options:

| Option | Driver / mode | Confirmation and timing | Cost and access |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Friend driving | Friend reports drinking; offers 20:40 pickup | No fare; unsuitable impairment concern |
| B | Paid ride | No booking or pickup confirmation | 12 units; funds unavailable |
| C | Trusted sober, alert friend | Explicitly confirmed 20:50 pickup at accessible door; 10-minute journey | No fare; usual aid accommodated; no solo walk required |

These facts make C feasible as a plan, not a completed journey. Safe waiting beyond 21:00 has not been agreed. A support person can help contact the service or arrange alternatives with Remy's consent; no public disclosure of medicine or use history is needed.

### Action 1 — Separate the immediate decision from the unresolved question

Complete the five-question record. Distinguish Remy's current drowsiness, the offered alcohol, a possible interaction and the driving decision. Do not use “no alcohol consumed” as proof that Remy can drive. Record dependence history as not supplied, not automatically absent; this fictional case does not require a detoxification decision.

### Action 2 — Make the safe immediate plan concrete

Draft a short decline and transport message: “I am not driving tonight. I will use the confirmed pickup and wait inside.” You need not share a medicine name. Calculate C's arrival home and the waiting margin before closing. Explain separately why A and B fail the current constraints. State the fallback trigger: if pickup becomes uncertain or later than safe waiting, contact the host/trusted support immediately to arrange an explicitly agreed safe option; do not infer an extension or substitute impaired driving.

Draft the pharmacist/prescriber question privately: what does the actual product's warning mean for current activities and relevant interactions, and what instructions should be followed? Do not stop or change prescribed treatment from a worksheet. Save the plan before [later-packet.md](later-packet.md).

### Action 3 — Use the update and preserve help status

Apply both later branches separately. Track `decline/rehearsal | transport planned | pickup occurred | arrived | qualified question sent | reply received | treatment completed`. Only supplied events can advance a status. A sober ride does not resolve dependence or medication questions, and a booked consultation is not treatment completed. If actual use is becoming unsafe, compulsive or difficult to control, use an appropriate confidential qualified service; support is not contingent on a moral judgment.

Attempt [fresh checks](check-prompts.md) before [the key](check-answers.md). The default completes a risk record, refusal/transport rehearsal, update and help plan. It does not establish actual contact, altered-state experience or a changed real-world choice, and adds no application evidence rule.

## Access, challenge and transfer

Abstainers can complete the packet without exposure. Use text, an interpreter or a consented support person; keep sensitive details private. A help route that is inaccessible remains unresolved until an alternative is actually identified, not until a website is bookmarked.

Harder variation: a different person reports possible dependence and asks for a weekend self-detox schedule. Identify timely medical support as the next step; supply no taper or abrupt-stop plan. Severe symptoms or suspected overdose use emergency help. The five categories do not require identical management.

Later real transfer may be one appropriate pharmacist question, support contact, transport arrangement or low-pressure refusal. Record what actually occurs and what remains pending; never initiate use or provoke withdrawal to generate evidence. Qualified assessment, actual behavior, self-review and this author-provided simulation remain distinct.

**Supportive:** Specific uncertainty reaches the appropriate professional and a feasible safe immediate choice actually occurs.

**Mixed:** The boundary holds but a cancelled ride or inaccessible service leaves further support work.

**Contradictory:** Impairment is ignored, unknown products are tested or possible dependence is met with unsupervised abrupt cessation.

**Inconclusive:** General facts or a draft are recorded without resolving the actual decision or help access.
