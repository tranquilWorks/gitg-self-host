# 12.11 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Substances and altered states

**Scope (unchanged):** Understand alcohol, nicotine, recreational drugs, performance drugs, and medication risks without moral panic or naïveté.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Makes informed choices and seeks help if use becomes unsafe or compulsive.

Health and exercise content must be adapted to the person and does not replace licensed medical care.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alcohol | Category table, Remy drowsiness/offer and interaction sources | Separate current impairment, offered alcohol and driving decision | No safe driving amount or countdown |
| Nicotine | Category table and FDA source | Distinguish dependence and product harms from obvious intoxication | No personal cessation treatment or harmless-product claim |
| Recreational drugs | Ingredient uncertainty and worked natural-focus case | Retain unknown contents and avoid use as evidence collection | No sourcing, preparation or dosing instruction |
| Performance drugs | Category table and NIDA source | Goal/marketing does not establish safe medical indication | No cycles, combinations or enhancement regimen |
| Medication risks | Product M label and pharmacist question | Preserve treatment while resolving specific warnings/interactions | No abrupt prescribed-treatment changes or sharing medicines |
| Unsafe/compulsive use and help | Dependence/emergency boundary; harder self-detox case | Use appropriate medical help; distinguish contact from treatment | No withdrawal test, schedule or moral label |
| Informed actual choice | Three transport options and two later branches | Calculate waiting/arrival; preserve confirmation and unresolved help | Rehearsal does not establish live behavior |
