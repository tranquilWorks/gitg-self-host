# 12.11 — Corrective guidance

1. Do not drive while drowsy; arrange appropriate safe support/transport. Absence of alcohol does not erase medicine-related impairment, and coffee does not establish driving fitness. Ask the pharmacist/prescriber about the actual product and activity/interaction instructions; do not independently change treatment.
2. Pickup is 10 minutes after safe waiting ends; predicted home arrival is 20:25. Seek an explicitly agreed suitable waiting extension or alternative journey. Neither a timestamp nor a request creates that agreement, pickup or arrival.
3. Possible dependence needs timely medical support before a sudden reduction or stop; severe withdrawal or overdose concerns use emergency care. Unknown ingredients remain unknown: do not test the product, assume “natural” means safe or invent interaction clearance. These are different risks requiring specific information, not a shared do-it-yourself regimen.

These checks assess risk reasoning and do not establish clinical competence or actual substance behavior.
