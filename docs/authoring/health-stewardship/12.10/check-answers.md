# 12.10 — Corrective guidance

1. 50 minutes becomes 90, an increase of 40 minutes or 80%. Both frequency and duration changed. This does not quantify physiological stress, prove productive adaptation or diagnose overtraining; retain intensity and recovery unknowns.
2. The request is acknowledged, return advice is not supplied and no return is completed. Function, symptoms and relevant qualified advice control the decision; elapsed days do not supply clearance. Keep follow-up open and use appropriate urgent care for serious concerns.
3. Do not provoke or mask the warning merely to finish. Stop the provoking action and seek appropriate advice. Existing prescribed treatment is not changed by this exercise, and pain does not identify a specific injury from the vignette.

These keys assess decision quality in supplied cases, not actual recovery or medical competence.
