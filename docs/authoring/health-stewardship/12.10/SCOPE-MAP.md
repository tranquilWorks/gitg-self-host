# 12.10 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Recovery, fatigue, and injury prevention

**Scope (unchanged):** Distinguish productive stress from overtraining, pain, illness, and structural injury.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Adjusts load, sleep, nutrition, and care before temporary strain becomes lasting damage.

Health and exercise content must be adapted to the person and does not replace licensed medical care.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Productive stress versus overtraining | Load/resource reference and 60-to-120-minute case | Count duration/frequency without equating time to physiology | No diagnosis or universal progression threshold |
| Pain and structural injury | NIAMS boundary, unknown pain and stop/care plan | Do not provoke/mask pain; seek appropriate evaluation for serious signs | No injury treatment or diagnostic test |
| Illness and fatigue | Five-part record and later reduced-fatigue branch | Retain incomplete illness information and persistent function change | Feeling better alone is not recovery |
| Load, sleep, nutrition and care | Actions 1–3; resources and deferred session | Protect support and recovery resources; obtain applicable advice | No deprivation, stimulant compensation or prescribed-treatment change |
| Return and prevention | Main/favorable branch and harder equipment change | Review function/advice before a return; avoid simultaneous unagreed increases | Planned return is not performed return or proof of prevention |
