# 12.13 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Grooming, dress, and presentation

**Scope (unchanged):** Maintain hygiene, clothing, posture, and appearance appropriate to health, context, identity, and respect.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Presentation supports dignity and purpose without becoming the basis of worth.

Health and exercise content must be adapted to the person and does not replace licensed medical care.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hygiene and health | CDC handwashing explanation and dirty-hands case | Separate actual care task from scent/beauty expectations | No new irritant, procedure or clinical skin-care plan |
| Clothing | Six item cards and provisional B/D/L/I | Check condition and actual movements; use existing resources | Brand/standing appearance is not functional fit |
| Posture | Usual positioning/aid boundary and movement check | Preserve comfortable participation without forced posture | No universal body alignment standard |
| Context and presentation | Verified organizer rules, timetable and later occasion | Distinguish real rules, preferences and unsupported assumptions | Compliments cannot validate access or comfort |
| Identity, respect and worth | Accessory I, sensory route and fresh checks | Retain compatible identity choices and appropriate support | No dignity score, identity concealment or dress-code legal ruling |
