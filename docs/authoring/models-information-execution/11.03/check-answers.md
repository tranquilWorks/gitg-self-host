# 11.03 — Corrective guidance

## Main practice

P1 records three correct finds and **two correct returns out of three**. The eraser is returned to A instead of its labeled B home. The 12-minute effort, zero purchases, own-paper permission, clear D strip, and no answer-selection help satisfy those stated prototype constraints. The missed return means the full operational target is not met; the result is partial for the tested self-review scope.

One repair is to make the return labels more distinct or change their placement, keep P1's failed result, and test a versioned P2. The packet does not supply a P2 result. A repeated self-test would still be influenced by knowledge of the mapping; it would not establish independent user usability or lasting household benefit.

The goal card should connect finding and returning to the chosen reason, not reward buying containers or making a neat picture. It should distinguish prototype acceptance from the larger benefit. The learner may choose a different valid mapping if the one-to-one homes and all stated constraints remain explicit.

Repair a weak answer by adding the predeclared criteria, actual result for each one, the route and assistance, and a specific next decision. A useful defect is information, not an invitation to alter the recorded past.

## Check A — Activity is not the intended benefit

An acceptable version might be: “By the end of this weekend, try one no-purchase arrangement of my own reading corner that permits a chosen 15-minute reading attempt with the book accessible and no need to move another person's belongings; record the actual duration and whether the arrangement meets my stated comfort needs, then keep, revise, or stop it.” Comfort is user-reported, not a diagnosis, and a trial need not force a duration that becomes uncomfortable.

Two checks could inspect access to the reading material without disturbing protected belongings and record an actual chosen reading attempt with its comfort report and stopping reason. Preserve no spending, permission, and existing access requirements. A missing or stopped attempt is not automatically 15 successful minutes.

Boxes are an unproven method, not the benefit. Learning that the person prefers another location can justify stopping this goal and recording why. The goal-definition skill may be exercised well even when the proposed reading-corner project is abandoned. Do not relabel an abandoned project as achieved.

## Check B — Missing evidence is not success

The first layout fails a protected constraint even though its item tests pass. Remove or relocate the decoration within authority, preserve the original failure, and retest the clear-strip condition and any affected functions. A changed requirement must be justified and versioned; it cannot retroactively make version 1 pass.

The second layout has incomplete evidence for returning. Three finds and an absence of recorded errors do not establish three correct returns. Run the specified return checks or mark that acceptance dimension unknown. That is different from a demonstrated incorrect return.

The exact next check should target the missing or failed requirement rather than repeat an unrelated successful metric. Neither purchasing more materials nor improving the photograph resolves these evidential gaps.
