## Main practice

Active preparation is `S5 + I5 + D10 + A10 + C5 = 35` minutes, within the 40-minute allowance. Adding E20 and R5 gives **60 active minutes** across preparation, session, and closeout. The response wait and fixed Day 6 session date are elapsed-time constraints, not active work that can be added as two minutes or ignored when promising a date.

After S, both I and D can proceed logically. A needs the relevant replies; C needs A and D; E needs C and the agreed time; R needs E. A useful first artifact is the written scope card or invitation draft with its consent/access question. A draft can be completed without claiming it was sent.

In READY, the fictional replies support the participation/time/access basis for readiness, and the questions are drafted. Final confirmation remains to be issued and the session has not occurred. Do not mark M5 complete. The text materials must actually be prepared/provided in the live arrangement before claiming the corresponding support is delivered.

A milestone should name its evidence, not merely a number of tasks checked off. The learner's real scope and draft artifacts support planning practice; fictional reply analysis supports simulation only.

## Check A — A circular plan and a missing agreement

The two stated dependencies form a cycle: final confirmation requires accepted arrangement, while choosing an arrangement requires final confirmation. Marking tasks complete would conceal the defect rather than resolve it.

Separate a **provisional arrangement proposal** from its **acceptance** and the **final confirmation**. Draft a no-pressure proposal containing the relevant time and access choices, then seek the necessary reply on an authorized live route. In a simulation, produce the draft and retain the reply as missing.

Final confirmation remains blocked until the actual required agreement exists. Removing the logical cycle does not create the missing participant's consent. That is a distinct external dependency with its own checkpoint and fallback.

## Check B — Parallel logic is not extra workers

The only single-worker orders are **A-B-C-D-E** and **A-C-B-D-E**. All tasks require **30 active minutes**. With enough independent workers and zero transitions, the network lower bound is `5 + max(10,5) + 5 + 5 = 25` minutes.

When B becomes eight minutes, total single-worker active work is **28** and the parallel network lower bound is `5 + max(8,5) + 5 + 5 = 23`. The graph has not supplied a second worker, confirmed their availability, or measured the task durations. Those assumptions must be checked before using the figures as real commitments.

Repair an overclaim by naming the resource assumption and separating calculated lower bound, active effort, observed duration, and actual milestone completion.
