# 11.02 — Corrective guidance

## Main practice

Full tasks total `60 + 30 + 60 + 75 + 45 + 60 = 330` minutes; with the 20-minute buffer the plan requires **350**, exceeding 210 by **140**. Every smallest nonzero version totals `60 + 30 + 30 + 30 + 15 + 30 = 195`; with the buffer this is **215**, still five minutes too much. At least one optional demand must be deferred, unless an actually agreed constraint changes.

One feasible example is O=60, M=30, R=30, P=0, G=15, L=30, buffer=20: **185 minutes**, with 25 unallocated. It protects obligations and connection, retains some growth and leisure, and defers the hobby index. The 25 spare minutes do not complete P's 30-minute minimum version. A different defensible example retains P=30, defers G, keeps L=30, and uses the same O/M/R/buffer: **200 minutes**. The difference requires an explicit preference reason, not a claim that one universally maximizes flourishing.

A 60-minute visit is also feasible in some plans if other options are removed. For example O=60, M=30, R=60, G=15, buffer=20 totals 185 while P and L wait. The smaller visit is not mandatory; its availability is one choice authorized by the fictional agreement.

With total capacity reduced to 160, O=60 + M=30 + R=30 + buffer=20 uses **140**, leaving 20. G's 15-minute reduced practice fits, leaving five. P and L each require at least 30, so neither fits that remaining 20. This conclusion follows from the specified versions, not a moral rule against projects or leisure. Preserve the earlier board and communicate any changed intention.

Repair an infeasible plan by showing the full accounting, not silently deleting the buffer or inventing shorter versions. A draft communication and a ranked board do not establish actual delivery or agreement in the real world.

## Check A — Loudness is not a deadline

The promised review and buffer use 70 minutes, leaving 30. The optional poster or leisure block could fill that remainder, but not both. The poster's actual required timing remains unknown; its urgency wording is neither verified necessity nor proof of manipulation.

Choosing leisure can be justified while clarifying the poster's timing and declining to promise delivery now. Choosing the poster can also be justified by a stated current preference, provided the review is protected and the leisure tradeoff is acknowledged. New information about a real deadline or consequence could change the order, but should not be invented.

All three activities require 120 minutes even before adding the buffer. Removing the buffer does not make them fit 100 and would violate the supplied constraint anyway. Neither silence nor a question establishes the requester's acceptance of delay.

## Check B — A reduced version needs a real agreement

The draft's total was `60 + 30 + 30 + 15 + 20 = 155`, leaving five. Replacing the visit with the actually agreed 60-minute version raises it to **185**, exceeding 160 by **25**. Even dropping the 15-minute practice leaves 170, still too much.

One route is to seek a different visit time or agreed format while keeping the visit unresolved; another is to explicitly defer it with appropriate communication where no binding visit promise prevents that. Do not record a proposed change as accepted. If an actual commitment makes unilateral deferral inappropriate, the plan is blocked pending renegotiation rather than magically feasible.

The point is to make the real constraint visible. The friend does not become unreasonable simply because the smaller plan would be easier to schedule.
