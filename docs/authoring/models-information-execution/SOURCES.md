## Model terminology — NASA Glenn

https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/wind/valid/tutorial/glossary.html

The inspected glossary distinguishes calibration, verification, validation for intended use, and prediction outside the validation database. Its specific context is computational fluid dynamics and it attributes several definitions to AIAA guidance. The six-card task borrows only those conceptual distinctions; it does not apply a certification standard or claim that software arithmetic tests establish physical model validity. No glossary passage or example is reproduced.

## Time-management evidence and limits

Brad Aeon, Aïda Faber, and Alexandra Panaccio (2021), *Does time management work? A meta-analysis*, PLOS ONE 16, e0245066:
https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0245066

The inspected results and limitations discuss associations with performance and well-being, heterogeneous interventions, substantial cross-sectional evidence, and the importance of resources and context. This is background for avoiding a productivity-only interpretation and unwarranted causal promises. The original three-evening record, energy labels, capacity constraints, and trial are not taken from the paper. No effect-size or therapeutic claim is made for our audit or priority board.
