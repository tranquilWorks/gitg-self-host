# 08.05 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Tolerance of discomfort

**Scope (unchanged):** Remain present with boredom, uncertainty, frustration, embarrassment, fatigue, craving, and temporary pain.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Completes worthwhile action while discomfort is present and reviews what was learned.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Remain with bounded ordinary discomfort | Five-paper task and safe stop distinction | Purpose, two attempts and observed response | No induced pain, deprivation or unsafe fatigue |
| Worthwhile action despite boredom | Complete sorting rules and destinations | First two-minute pass completed | Correct output is not tolerance growth |
| Review what discomfort teaches | Pain stops second attempt at forty seconds | 160 seconds with uncertainty retained | No four-minute completion or clinical inference |
