# 08.10 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Completion and perseverance

**Scope (unchanged):** Limit simultaneous commitments, define done, work through plateaus, and close or consciously terminate open loops.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Finishes a meaningful commitment despite boredom or difficulty, or ends it for a reasoned cause.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Limit simultaneous commitments and define done | Three loops with active/parked/ended status | Three-paragraph note and reopen criteria | No arbitrary abandonment of shared duties |
| Continue through plateaus | Perfect-introduction obstacle and supplied missing sentence | Fourteen minutes over two bounded windows | No endless polishing or unsafe persistence |
| Finish or consciously terminate | Actual reopen check and retired decoration | Completed artifact and reasoned private closure | Sunday review and external notice not invented |
