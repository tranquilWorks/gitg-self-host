# 08.03 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Impulse control

**Scope (unchanged):** Delay speech, spending, eating, sex, anger, quitting, and novelty seeking long enough to choose deliberately.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can resist or redirect a strong impulse for a reason consciously endorsed.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Delay an urge long enough to choose | Novelty cue and complete event notice | Cue/urge/reason/choice record | Other impulse domains need context-specific safeguards |
| Act from endorsed reasons | Information purpose before chosen break | One twenty-second pre-action interval | No required denial of every urge |
| Resist or redirect honestly | Second tab opened before noticing | Later redirection distinguished from prevention | No general impulse-control or treatment claim |
