# 07.14 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Chronic dysregulation and skill generalization

**Scope (unchanged):** Practice regulation skills across different triggers, relationships, stress levels, and environments rather than only under ideal conditions.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can use more than one strategy in real situations and identify when a strategy stops working.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Across triggers, relationships, stress and environments | Two ordinary contexts and conditional two-strategy menu | Availability, attempts, behavior and effects separated | No induced distress or high-stress generalization |
| More than one strategy in real situations | Four supplied uses with distinct outcomes | Two actual fictional strategies; missing access retained | Fiction is not participant evidence |
| Recognize when a strategy stops working | Worsening reread and bounded revision | Stop and revised menu with pending return | Revision not yet tested; qualified care when needed |
