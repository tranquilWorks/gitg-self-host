# 08.08 — Consistency and minimum standards

## Define a minimum that is honest about what it preserves

A minimum standard can keep a chosen practice connected across difficult days while a fuller standard remains meaningful on ordinary days. It needs an explicit content boundary, not just a feeling of effort. A care pause is also a legitimate status. Neither the minimum nor a pause should be relabeled as the full session.

Plan an ordinary and a naturally difficult opportunity within seven days. Do not create overload to test discipline. The NHS discussion of manageable activity and balance [S06](../SOURCES.md#S06) supports a feasible scope. These local session lengths are original teaching choices, not a universal rule for recovery or consistency.

## Three phrases for a fictional index

Fictional Blair is learning a small indexing vocabulary. The complete material is: “LUMA means light; TERA means ground; NERI means water.” These are invented labels for this exercise, not words attributed to a real language. The normal standard is ten minutes studying, retrieving and checking all three mappings. The difficult-day minimum is two minutes retrieving and checking one mapping. A care pause means no learning attempt because health, urgent care, rest or access needs take priority.

Retrieval means first trying the meaning from memory, then checking the printed mapping and correcting it if necessary. Rereading alone is a different activity. The worked minimum is to cover the answer to LUMA, offer a meaning, uncover “light” and correct the response if needed. Do not add speed, perfect recall or a required emotional state.

1. Write the normal, minimum and pause standards before the week. Name an actual capacity condition for using each. Normal capacity permits the ordinary session; a busy but safe day may allow the minimum; an illness or urgent duty can require pause. Preserve nonnegotiable care, treatment and safety obligations rather than making them smaller.
2. At each natural opportunity choose the fitting standard, perform what is feasible and label the actual work. Save the plan before the [week record](later-packet.md). If no difficult day occurs, the minimum remains untested. If only one minute occurs, record that partial attempt instead of rounding up to two.
3. Review continuity and the difference in content. One phrase checked is not three. If reduced capacity persists, revise the actual ongoing standard openly rather than calling every day an exception forever. A new standard is a future agreement, not a rewrite of past sessions.

For support, use spoken retrieval, large type or a single accessible card. For a harder case, an error appears in the minimum: checking and correcting it still performs the intended learning process; no extra punishment session is owed. A missed day can be acknowledged and resumed without a streak score.

This exercise concerns optional personal learning. It does not authorize reducing a required safety procedure, prescribed treatment or duty owed to another person. A later review can ask whether the chosen minimum is still useful and feasible. Two sessions and one pause do not establish long-term consistency or mastery of the mappings.

## Use the result at its proper scale

Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Subjective ratings, source exercises and completion do not diagnose, establish mastery or score human dignity. Actual situations require appropriate access, consent, authority and support.
