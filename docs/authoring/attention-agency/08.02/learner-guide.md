# 08.02 — Mindfulness and present attention

## Return to one present activity under workable conditions

Present attention means noticing when attention has moved away and returning to the current task, body, person or surroundings. It does not demand an empty mind, perfect stillness or uninterrupted concentration. Catching drift earlier may be a private observation; counting distractions can itself become another task. NCCIH [S04](../SOURCES.md#S04) notes uncertainty and safety limits in mindfulness research. This ordinary attention experiment is not meditation treatment or a substitute for care.

This companion follows the existing presence practice: one usual fifteen-minute window, one fifteen-minute window with exactly one changed condition, and a fifteen-minute repeat of the more workable condition within seven days of the first attempt. Allow five to fifteen minutes of setup and a brief final review within the ten-day protocol. The first action is due by day three; plan the repeat early enough to meet the seven-day interval. Keep the standard windows distinct from an accessible duration adaptation; never treat an imagined repeat as performed.

## A complete, ordinary activity

Fictional Dana chooses a quiet task of arranging a small paper route card. The whole material is: “The entrance is south of the courtyard. The reading room is east of the courtyard. The water point is west of the courtyard. The north door is staff-only.” Draw or describe these four relations, check them against the text and reread at an ordinary pace for the remaining window. Accuracy and output quantity are not the experiment's score. No real navigation depends on this card.

Dana's usual setup places an optional puzzle magazine beside the card. The single changed condition will put that magazine on a shelf. Lighting, chair, task and access aids stay the same as far as practical. Essential alerts, medication routines, movement and fidgets remain available. A necessary interruption is recorded honestly, never suppressed to earn completion. Do not use driving, machinery or another hazardous activity as the attention task.

Other appropriate anchors can be an ordinary external sensation, a consenting person's words or a comfortable body sensation. These are choices, not four required tests. If inward attention is distressing, choose an external activity or stop; eye closure and breath control are unnecessary. A person is never an object to monitor, and conversation requires their ordinary consent.

1. Choose the safe activity and usual arrangement. Begin deliberately and try the fifteen-minute window. When you notice drift, return gently to the chosen activity without tallying it. Afterwards record whether the window was actually attempted and completed, and a brief qualitative note if useful.
2. Change only the optional magazine placement for a second fifteen-minute attempt. Compare which condition was more workable and what else differed. Save the comparison before the [repeat record](later-packet.md). “No clear difference” is an honest result; choose a safe feasible condition for repetition without inventing a winner.
3. Repeat that condition within seven days of the first attempt, then review all three. A repeat scheduled or occurring later cannot be called an on-time repeat. The existing practice requires all three actions attempted, at least two completed, a comparison, the timely repeat and final review; this source does not loosen those requirements.

For support, use accessible text, dictation, movement or a different suitable activity. The existing protocol also permits consistently shortened windows when fifteen minutes is not workable: choose a comparable shorter duration for all windows and record that limitation, without relabeling them full fifteen-minute windows. Deferral or another practice remains available. For a harder case, a necessary call interrupts the changed window: preserve the interruption and compare only what was observed. No productivity ranking, surveillance or clinical claim follows from the result. Broader presence with people or under stress remains future work, not established by one paper task.
