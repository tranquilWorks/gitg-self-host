# 07.13 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Mental-health literacy and help-seeking

**Scope (unchanged):** Recognize the limits of self-help and signs requiring medical, psychological, spiritual, legal, or emergency support.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Maintains a practical support plan and uses qualified help when functioning or safety is impaired.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Limits of self-help and signs for different support | Four-level card with observed duration and functioning | Appropriate routing and opening request | No diagnostic or treatment decision |
| Medical, psychological, spiritual, legal and emergency routes | Role distinctions, location verification and access barrier | Dated practical contacts for a real plan; fiction permitted | US routes are not universal |
| Use qualified help when needed | Booked assessment and separate willing friend | Contact status and transport still unresolved | Booking is not attended care or improvement |
