# 09.11 — Fresh checks

## Check A — The late shared artifact

You promised a draft poster to two collaborators by 17:00. At 16:30 you know only the text will be ready. One collaborator needs the text for an accessible audio version; the other needs the complete image tomorrow. Both accept a private shared update. You have no permission to promise either person's extra work. A friend says to wait until tomorrow because announcing incomplete work is embarrassing.

State the protected good, the relevant timing and role differences, and a proportionate update. Explain what can be handed over now, what remains uncertain, and who may decide whether a revised arrangement works. Repeat the analysis after changing only one fact: the audio collaborator cannot use the shared text channel but has an agreed voice route. What should change, and what should remain the same?

## Check B — An exception or an excuse?

A library craft group asks members to notify an agreed coordinator when they cannot attend. Case 1: a participant has a working safe channel but prefers not to send an update because another member is unpopular. Case 2: the ordinary channel is unavailable; a safe, already agreed relay exists. Case 3: the ordinary channel is unsafe and no safe substitute is currently established. The activity is optional and no emergency is present in these fictional cases.

For each, identify the relevant facts, a feasible next step, and any remaining duty or unresolved limit. Do not invent a new contact or require unsafe contact. Explain why the same principle need not require the same immediate action. State what would justify reviewing your decision later.
