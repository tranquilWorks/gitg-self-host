## Check A

Record X's success and Y's failure honestly, then inspect whether either reveals a useful contingency for future choices. A contingent review need not change the original process rating.

The added pre-choice warning breaks the identical-information premise. Append the actual outcome and a correction record explaining that the later forecast was not prospective. If the original cannot be recovered, mark that limit; do not manufacture a replacement with an earlier date.

Remove identifying material from any proposed submission and use minimal neutral codes in the practice copy. Avoid making further copies of private quotations. Existing application evidence must remain within its own allowlist; the source template does not expand it. The real reminder was only planned, so acknowledge it was not scheduled at the claimed time. A future review can be scheduled prospectively without inventing past execution.
