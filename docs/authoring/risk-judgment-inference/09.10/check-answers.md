# 09.10 — Corrective guidance

## Main practice

A defensible plan uses an authorized, freshly checked alternative with a preserved view, or defers. It does not assume permission to move the assistive device. E1 allows only the bounded simulated move under the checked conditions. E2 requires a hold at S while the route is occupied. E3 remains on hold or requires a genuinely different authorized plan; the desire to finish supplies neither permission nor capacity.

An occupancy check increases knowledge; a lower stack changes visibility; choosing C changes exposure to A and B; waiting changes timing. Name these distinct mechanisms. Another person's access remains a constraint rather than a cost you may silently impose. If your record treats a hold as successful transfer, separate the decision result from the task result.

## Check A

X's known configuration obstructs shared access. Y's unknown stability is a different unresolved condition, not evidence that Y is equally bad or safe. A proportionate next step is to leave the display unopened while an authorized person checks Y and its surrounding access. If Y is verified suitable, that addresses the specified concerns; if not, find another permitted arrangement or defer.

For the six dimensions, reasonable entries include: possible collision, loss of access, or falling display as harms; severity described in relation to the actual setup rather than assigned a fabricated number; likelihood not quantified from the packet; exposure whenever users need the aisle or approach the table; detection before opening through inspection rather than after an incident; control limited to display placement and checks; mitigation by a verified suitable non-obstructing arrangement. Exact consequence judgments remain conditional because table dimensions and users' circumstances are not supplied.

Specify who can inspect, what evidence is needed, and a threshold such as “do not open while the aisle is blocked or stability remains unverified.” A no-incident month has an unknown denominator and may concern different conditions. It does not establish zero probability. The repair is not to demand absolute proof of no imaginable harm: it is to resolve the decision-relevant condition and use a proportionate reviewed method.

## Check B

Three passes: 1 − (9/10)^3 = 271/1000 = 27.1%. Six: 1 − (9/10)^6 = 468559/1000000 = 46.8559%. Adding the per-pass probability counts overlapping multiple-mismatch possibilities more than once. The six-pass event is more likely under these stated assumptions, but it is not certain.

An end-of-process detector can identify a mismatch for correction; it does not prevent its first occurrence. Whether correction succeeds needs a separate observation. With one common fault, the independence product is unjustified; dependence might change the combined probability and the packet does not specify enough to replace it with a new number.

These are invented probabilities about token mismatches. They supply no measured injury rate, safe load, or professional clearance. If you used the arithmetic to select a real carrying method, retract that conclusion and identify the missing exposure, capability, and consequence evidence.
