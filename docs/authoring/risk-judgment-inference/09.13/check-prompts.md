# 09.13 — Fresh checks

## Check A — Averages, extreme observations, and sampling

Five complete waits in group P are **4, 4, 4, 4, 24** minutes. Group Q has **5, 5, 5, 5, 5**. These are separate convenience samples, not paired or randomized groups. Calculate both means, medians, and ranges. Does Q look better by mean, median, both, or neither? Explain why the 24-minute wait cannot be removed merely because it is inconvenient; name a legitimate data-quality investigation without inventing its result.

For an independent sampling toy, a population contains cards 1, 3, and 8. All three unordered two-card samples without replacement are equally likely. Enumerate their means and compare their average with the population mean. Explain why this demonstration does not produce a confidence interval for the wait-time populations.

A person is selected because of one unusually high noisy measurement and later has a lower result without an intervention. Give a non-treatment explanation and state an assumption needed for it.

## Check B — Prediction, causation, and flags

For a fresh observed dataset, x = **0, 1, 2, 3** and y = **1, 2, 2, 5**. Fit the line using the guide's formulas, predict y at x = 2, and identify whether predicting x = 6 is interpolation or extrapolation. Does its slope establish what would happen if you intervened to increase x?

Separately, 1,000 fictional items include 20 true review cases. A tool detects 16 of them and falsely flags 98 of the 980 noncases. Complete the two-by-two table. Calculate the true-case base rate, sensitivity, false-positive rate, and true-case fraction among flags. Compare that last fraction with the guide's 100-item example, despite both tools having 80% sensitivity and 10/90 versus 98/980 false-positive fractions. Do not assume those two false-positive rates are identical.

Finally hold the **10% false-positive rate** fixed in a second comparison: a 100-item batch with 10 true cases has 8 detected and 9 false flags. Compare 8/17 with 16/114 and explain the base-rate effect under these matched rates.
