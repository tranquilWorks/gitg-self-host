**Scope (unchanged):** Recognize guilt, intimidation, gaslighting, love-bombing, false urgency, triangulation, and exploitative reciprocity.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Responds through clarification, evidence, boundaries, support, or distance.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
