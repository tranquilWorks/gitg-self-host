# 16.15 — Fresh checks

Use only these fictional facts; [answers](check-answers.md) follow separately.

1. A discount really ends at 17:00, but a seller says, “Don't compare anything; decent people trust me.” Which part is verified, and what remains pressure?
2. Two friends remember the agreed meeting place differently; neither has a record. Can you conclude gaslighting? What is a useful next step?
3. After repeated monitoring, someone threatens your housing if you ask for help. Is a forceful boundary rehearsal the appropriate next attempt?
