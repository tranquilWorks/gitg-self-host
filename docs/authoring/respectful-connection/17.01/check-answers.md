# 17.01 — Corrective answers

1. No. The role and queue constrain the person's ability to decline. Complete the transaction courteously and leave personal contact unpursued. Politeness at work is not sufficient evidence of a welcome social opening.
2. Offer a brief genuine exchange and let them leave without extending it. A true “See you at the usual session” may fit if appropriate; no private contact request is needed. Warmth and unavailability can coexist.
3. Preparation completed; real initiation not attempted. Choose one suitable optional opportunity at the next session, respecting context and refusal. Do not invent an interaction to avoid an incomplete record.
