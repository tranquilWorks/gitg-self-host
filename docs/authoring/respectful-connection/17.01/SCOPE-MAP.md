# 17.01 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Initiating connection

**Scope (unchanged):** Approach strangers, acquaintances, coworkers, neighbors, and peers with curiosity and appropriate openness.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can begin contact without treating every interaction as transaction or performance.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strangers and acquaintances | Shared puzzle context and previous ordinary familiarity | Optional opening plus small truthful self-detail | No immediate sensitive disclosure or contact collection |
| Coworkers, neighbors and peers | Work/queue, doorway/phone and shared activity conditions | Role-sensitive moment selection | Five contexts taught; no approach quota |
| Curiosity and appropriate openness | Listen to answer, reciprocate lightly and accept quiet | Actual continuation or courteous end | No favor, sales or approval conversion target |
| Nontransactional beginning | Sam permits shared table but requests quiet | Limit honored without withdrawal as punishment | Role-play and rehearsal are not real initiation |
