# 17.04 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Reliable support

**Scope (unchanged):** Identify whether someone needs listening, practical help, encouragement, information, advocacy, or space.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Provides useful support without assuming, rescuing, controlling, or disappearing.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Listening, practical help and encouragement | Ask mode; twenty-minute listening or one errand | Chosen bounded support with truthful capacity | Distress does not authorize advice or outcome promises |
| Information, advocacy and space | Checked answer, exact disclosure permission, welcome pause | Mode-specific action and limits | No unauthorized contact, rescue or repeated checking |
| Reliability and usefulness | Thirty-five-minute errand; window shrinks to twenty | Prompt renegotiation and actual recipient feedback if available | Notification is not completed errand |
| No assuming, rescuing, controlling or disappearing | Do agreed part; changing modes requires permission | Performed help versus broader unresolved need | Crisis/regulated needs outside ordinary friend task |
