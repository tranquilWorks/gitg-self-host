# 17.04 — Corrective answers

1. Acknowledge the mode switch, stop the unsolicited advice and ask whether they want to return to listening or hear an option. Relevant knowledge does not override the chosen form of support.
2. The task needs twenty-eight minutes, three more than available. Renegotiate before commitment, choose a feasible alternative or decline. Reaching the destination is not the same as completing the whole promised task.
3. Drafting permission is not sending or disclosure permission. Agree the exact recipient, content and action before acting, and stay within competence and applicable processes. Keep the person in control of their communication.
