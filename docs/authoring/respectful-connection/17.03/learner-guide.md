# 17.03 — Keep an existing friendship present in ordinary life

A friendship can receive attention through remembering, contacting, inviting, celebrating, assisting and staying present during unremarkable weeks. This companion supports the existing **Deepen One Existing Friendship** practice. Its fourteen-day duration and three actions remain unchanged. Choose one existing friend you value, where contact is welcome; defer without penalty if none is suitable. This is not a way to revive unwanted contact or handle abuse, stalking, crisis or unsafe dependence.

## Maintain continuity without making a loyalty test

Remembering means carrying forward a non-sensitive detail the person freely shared. Contact makes that attention visible. An invitation offers a specific opportunity to spend time together. Celebration responds to actual good news, assistance responds to an actual need with permission, and ordinary presence keeps care from appearing only when there is a crisis. These forms overlap, but none entitles you to a response.

Feeney and Collins's theoretical model includes relational support during adversity and opportunities for growth [S13](../SOURCES.md#s13). The inspected abstract motivates attention beyond crises; it does not validate this practice's duration or establish that one cycle deepens a friendship. The legacy timing comes from the existing protocol, not from a research-derived dose.

Depth does not require a traumatic disclosure. A hobby goal, a busy season, good news or a meaningful everyday change can support a substantive conversation. Ask what the friend wants to discuss and follow their actual interest. If they prefer lighter contact, honor it. A lower-intensity interaction can be relationally worthwhile without satisfying a more demanding action description.

## The three existing actions

**A1, Listen to what matters now:** initiate a substantive conversation about something currently meaningful in the friend's life and spend at least ten minutes primarily listening. Let the friend set disclosure depth. A genuine follow-up question can show attention, but do not keep questioning to force “meaningful information.” Use a mutually workable medium, including accessible or asynchronous communication; do not invent elapsed listening time from an unopened message.

**A2, Make a specific invitation:** propose an activity and date. Include practical access, distance, cost and energy constraints. A proposal and a mutually scheduled interaction are different facts. “Maybe someday” is vague; “Would a free twenty-minute call on Sunday at 16:00 work?” is specific, yet remains unconfirmed until the other person agrees.

**A3, Follow up:** within seven days, refer to something the person actually shared and ask how it developed. The existing seven-day follow-up remains intact. A follow-up can welcome ordinary news without demanding progress or explanation. Set yourself a private reminder that contains no sensitive detail.

Use the app's original fields honestly; this companion adds no evidence field, completion rule or score. In particular, user initiation does not establish reciprocity, and a specific proposal does not establish the original “future interaction scheduled” marker. Internal resistance, expected reciprocity and observed reciprocity remain separate. Completing the practice never establishes mastery or the quality of the friendship.

## Worked example: interest without an invoice

Mara's friend is learning a short piece of music and is pleased with a small improvement. Mara listens, asks what part was satisfying and celebrates that reported progress. Mara offers a free shared listening session on a particular date, with no assumption that performance is owed. If the friend declines because of fatigue, Mara can accept that and follow up about the original topic within seven days if contact remains welcome.

Mara need not send advice, purchase a gift or turn the conversation into coaching. Assistance might fit if the friend asks for help finding a practice space; it does not fit merely because Mara wants to be useful. Staying present can mean remembering the topic in a later ordinary exchange rather than generating a new emotional event.

## Your case: a friend's small project

All facts are fictional. You and Eri already enjoy occasional contact. Eri has said that completing a small balcony drawing matters to them and welcomes a call on Monday 2 November at 18:00. During the call, Eri talks about choosing a view and enjoying the process. You primarily listen for twelve minutes, then exchange practical news for three minutes. Eri says no help is needed and mentions hoping to try another sketch by the weekend.

1. **Prepare the real-world equivalent carefully.** Choose a welcome friend and medium, allow enough time for the existing listening action and identify a non-sensitive starting topic. Do not tell the friend they must help you complete a metric. Output: a contact plan and permission-sensitive opening.
2. **Separate what happened from what you hoped.** In the supplied case, twelve minutes primarily listening meets the stated ten-minute duration; the extra three practical minutes are not needed to inflate it. Note the actual meaningful topic without copying private details. Output: listening duration and observed action facts, with reciprocity still not owed.
3. **Offer one specific shared activity and date.** For Eri, propose a free twenty-minute remote sketch session on Saturday 7 November at 15:00, allowing another option or decline. Celebrate the reported enjoyment; do not offer unsolicited instruction after Eri declined help. Output: invitation status, not an assumed booking.
4. **Save your account, then read the [later packet](later-packet.md).** Check the follow-up interval and record the invitation's actual disposition. Output: attempted/completed/pending facts tied to the existing actions, with no rewritten rules.

## Sustain a form both people can bear

For support, choose a free remote activity, written contact, AAC or a lower-sensory setting that fits both people. If the substantive listening requirement is not met, report that honestly rather than rounding up. The existing completion process remains responsible for deciding completion from the original actions and fields; the companion does not award it.

For a harder attempt, you feel disappointed after a declined invitation. Notice the expectation privately, accept the answer and avoid turning follow-up into a request for reassurance. For transfer, revisit the friendship after ordinary time has passed: what form of presence was actually welcome, and what cadence fits care, work and distance? Do not infer consistent reciprocal investment from one fictional or real cycle. Answer the [fresh checks](check-prompts.md) before reading their key.
