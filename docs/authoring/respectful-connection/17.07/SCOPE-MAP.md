# 17.07 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Hospitality and inclusion

**Scope (unchanged):** Welcome people, notice outsiders, create comfort, share resources, and facilitate participation.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** A gathering or group becomes more accessible because of deliberate action.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome and notice outsiders | Invitation/arrival/participation journey | Complete truthful invitation and private question route | No public vulnerability labels or compulsory sociability |
| Create comfort and share resources | Music change, chairs, materials, optional food and quiet space | Authorized implemented change with truthful limits | No allergen or universal-access guarantee |
| Facilitate participation | Join/watch/break choices and agreed introduction | Actual option used or declined and basic rule access | Watching can precede joining; no insider knowledge test |
| Deliberate increase in accessibility | Music helps but rule jargon remains | Specific effect, next repair and pending retest | Intent, occupied seats or one improvement do not prove inclusion |
