# 16.18 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Language access and communication accommodation

**Scope (unchanged):** Use interpreters, plain language, captions, augmentative communication, written follow-up, and accessible formats.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Another person can participate meaningfully because a communication barrier was reduced.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Interpreters and plain language | Role/competence boundary; complete selectable-text invitation | Chosen support and preserved facts | No untrained/minor default for high-stakes interpretation |
| Captions and AAC | Relevant option selection; time for direct response | Arranged support and actual device task | Captioning alone need not solve language or response barriers |
| Written follow-up and accessible formats | Named choices, entrance information and usable record | Revised artifact and agreed reply route | No image/color-only meaning or unrequested sensitive record |
| Meaningful participation | Understand AND choose/question/disagree; thirty-second form failure | Comprehension, failed submission and pending retest separated | Helper cannot substitute a choice; self-audit is preparation |
