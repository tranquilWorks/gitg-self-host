# 16.18 — Corrective answers

1. Only one barrier may have been reduced. Add a usable refusal/alternative response route and check the person's actual attempt. An accessible incoming message does not establish two-way participation.
2. Verify the person's preference for assistance and allow their own response through the chosen method. Do not equate speed with competence or assume the companion has authority to choose. A helper's contribution can support communication when agreed without substituting meaning.
3. No. Use the responsible healthcare organization's qualified language-assistance process and applicable requirements. HHS guidance discourages untrained individuals and minors as interpreters. This source companion cannot certify competence, give jurisdiction-specific legal advice or authorize a shortcut.
