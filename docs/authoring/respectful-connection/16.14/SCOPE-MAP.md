# 16.14 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Etiquette, courtesy, and first impressions

**Scope (unchanged):** Use greetings, punctuality, dress, manners, attention, and context to communicate respect.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Others experience the interaction as considerate and appropriately composed.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Greetings and manners | Written/spoken greeting, materials/food/photo permissions | Context-fit greeting and actual permission status | Touch, accent and eye contact are not requirements |
| Punctuality | Doors versus start; six-minute walk and delayed bus | Arrival time, notification and revised entry plan | Transport delay does not establish character |
| Dress and attention | Practical invitation requirements; communication aid preserved | Existing suitable clothing and attention arrangement | No purchase, class or attractiveness test |
| Context and considerate first impression | Stated facts versus assumptions; specific thanks and repair | Actual feedback if volunteered, otherwise uncertainty | Intent and a fictional host reply do not establish others' experience |
