# 16.14 — Fresh checks

These cases are fictional. Answer before opening [the key](check-answers.md).

1. Doors open at 09:50, the activity starts at 10:00, and your transport arrives at 09:47 with a seven-minute walk. When would you reach the door? Is arriving before the doors open necessary?
2. A host calls phone use rude, but a guest uses a phone to communicate. What should change, and what would be an inappropriate inference about the guest?
3. You dressed carefully and sent a thank-you, but the host says you photographed their work without permission. What does a useful review and repair include?
