# 17.02 — Corrective answers

1. Kept plans support reliability within those agreements; slow replies may limit rapid coordination. Known care demands are relevant. Neither global indifference nor guaranteed trust follows from these facts.

## Changed-case answer

Acknowledge the explanation and respond to the proposed catch-up according to your availability. The delay alone does not establish betrayal; the proposal is an intention, not completed connection. Observe whether the agreed next contact happens and discuss a repeated pattern if one emerges. Respect privacy and avoid surveillance.
