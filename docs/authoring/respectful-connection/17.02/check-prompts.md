# 17.02 — Fresh checks

Try these fictional cases before reading [the key](check-answers.md).

1. A friend replies slowly during a care-heavy month but keeps agreed plans. What evidence supports or limits a judgment about reliability?
2. You want to know whether an acquaintance respects privacy. Should you plant a false secret and watch for gossip?
3. Both people enjoy meeting, but one wants weekly late evenings and the other can offer monthly daytime contact. Name a reasonable direction without a character verdict.
