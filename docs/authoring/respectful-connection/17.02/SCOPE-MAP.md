# 17.02 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Friendship formation and selection

**Scope (unchanged):** Assess reciprocity, trust, character, compatibility, influence, and shared activity over time.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can explain why a friendship should deepen, remain limited, be repaired, or end.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reciprocity, trust and character | Three encounters; kept or renegotiated commitments and interruptions | Observation/interpretation/unknown review | No response-speed or charm verdict |
| Compatibility, influence and shared activity | Different free times and genuine sketch offer | Feasibility and effect of shared conduct | No secret loyalty tests or manufactured vulnerability |
| Over-time selection | One correction does not settle all future conduct | Provisional direction and later update | Three observations are a sample, not certification |
| Deepen, limit, repair or end | All four forms explained without a worth ranking | Direction supported by specific evidence and wishes | Ordinary mismatch distinct from unsafe harm |
