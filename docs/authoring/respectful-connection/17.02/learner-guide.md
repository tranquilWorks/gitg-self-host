# 17.02 — Choose how a friendship should develop

Friendship selection is a judgment about the form of relationship you can freely sustain, not a ranking of people's worth. This practice compares several ordinary interactions, identifies a question that remains open, and uses a genuine shared activity to learn more without staging a test. Allow twenty minutes for review, a welcome activity if available and ten minutes afterward.

## Look for patterns while keeping explanations open

Six facets matter in different ways. **Reciprocity** concerns mutual contribution over time; it is not identical message counts. **Trust** concerns what someone does with commitments, privacy and vulnerability. **Character** appears in particular conduct, such as treating an absent person fairly; it is not a score you can infer from charm. **Compatibility** concerns whether your rhythms, interests and limits can coexist. **Influence** concerns what you encourage in each other. **Shared activity** provides a setting in which these things can be observed without an interview about being a good friend.

Hall's friendship research examines associations among time together, activities, talk and closeness [S12](../SOURCES.md#s12). The inspected abstract and Study 1 limitations do not justify an hour quota or a causal promise. Our three-interaction review is a practical sampling aid, not a validated instrument. Three observations may expose uncertainty; they cannot certify a person's character.

Response speed is particularly easy to overinterpret. A delayed reply can coexist with reliability, care, disability, work or caregiving demands. Conversely, constant messaging can coexist with broken promises. Compare conduct against actual agreements and known capacity. Do not turn “I prefer more frequent contact” into “they are incapable of friendship.”

There are at least four legitimate directions. **Deepen** when mutual interest and reliable conduct support a little more investment. **Remain limited** when the current form fits but greater closeness does not. **Repair** when a specific, addressable rupture and willingness make repair plausible. **End** when continuing is unwanted or repeated harm makes reduced access appropriate. None requires dehumanizing the other person or making a public case against them.

## Worked example: slow replies, reliable plans

Tess feels unsure because Rowan replies after several days. Across three actual plans, Rowan has either arrived as agreed or warned Tess early and offered a workable alternative. Rowan says evenings are difficult because of care responsibilities. Tess can still decide that frequent spontaneous contact will not fit, while recognizing evidence of reliability.

A sensible next step is an occasional planned activity both enjoy, not sending a fake urgent message to measure loyalty. If Tess needs to discuss one missed commitment, the topic is that commitment and its effect. A concealed test creates misleading evidence because the situation was designed to provoke a response.

## Your case: three supplied encounters

This fictional record concerns an ordinary safe acquaintance, Dara. At a first walk, you initiated; Dara arrived on time, asked about your interest in drawing and respected your wish not to discuss work. Before a second, Dara cancelled a day ahead for a family task and proposed a free shorter walk the following week; you accepted, and that walk happened. At a third group activity, Dara disagreed with your game choice without mocking you, but twice interrupted another participant. You do not know whether Dara noticed. You both enjoy drawing outdoors; your free time is usually mornings, Dara's usually afternoons.

You are considering a twenty-minute sketch session on Sunday at 11:00 in the public garden, with an indoor library alternative only if its room is confirmed. You genuinely want the activity and can accept a decline. No threat, manipulation or abuse is supplied.

1. **Review the pattern under six headings.** Use the three encounters for reciprocity, trust, character, compatibility, influence and shared activity. Separate observations from interpretations: interruptions are observed; contempt or distraction is unconfirmed. Output: evidence, contrary evidence and unknowns for each heading.
2. **Choose a provisional direction.** Explain why modest deepening, remaining limited or a specific repair might fit. Ending is also an available choice if you do not want continued contact, but do not invent harm to justify it. Output: a direction and the concrete information it depends on.
3. **Offer a genuine activity.** Include time, duration, cost and an easy decline or alternative. Do not promise the indoor room. Do not plant a secret, provoke jealousy or withhold attention to see whether Dara chases you. Output: a usable invitation and a question it could naturally clarify, such as practical compatibility.
4. **Compare the [later packet](later-packet.md).** Update the timing judgment and review the interruption without turning one correction into universal proof. Output: a revised relationship direction and what further ordinary experience would matter.

## An honest limit can be a good outcome

If the best fit is an occasional group activity, that can be a complete friendship form rather than a failed attempt at intimacy. Different friendships need not provide every kind of support. Long-distance or low-frequency relationships can contain meaningful reciprocity, while frequent proximity alone does not establish trust.

For a real review, use interactions you can actually recall. If there are fewer than three, say so; do not fill gaps with imagined evidence. Keep detailed private notes outside the application and minimize identifying information. A record of your chosen behavior and uncertainty is enough for this companion. Do not create a spreadsheet that ranks acquaintances as assets.

For support, work through the supplied record first, then choose one naturally occurring shared activity. If no welcome opportunity exists, defer the real attempt. For a harder case, a friend acknowledges a problem but the same behavior recurs: distinguish a repair intention from changed conduct over time. If abuse, threats or unsafe dependence appear, stop the ordinary selection experiment and use the safety route in 17.06.

For transfer, revisit your decision after another real interaction, naming what changed your view and what did not. A declined invitation may clarify availability without resolving trust, compatibility or affection. The [fresh checks](check-prompts.md) require reasons for a direction, not a verdict that someone is a good or bad person.
