# 17.05 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Reciprocity and healthy dependence

**Scope (unchanged):** Give and receive help, ask directly, share burdens, and avoid both exploitation and prideful self-sufficiency.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Relationships contain mutual contribution without exact scorekeeping.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Give and receive help | Five-minute invitation review; use actual location correction | Direct ask, received contribution and specific thanks | No unnecessary immediate repayment |
| Ask directly and share burdens | Clear scope, deadline, fallback and genuine no | Bounded request and review of usefulness AND cost | Declined request is not help received |
| Avoid exploitation and prideful self-sufficiency | Receive without erasing need; repeated-limit patterns | Own reaction and concrete boundary if necessary | Unequal capacity does not erase dignity |
| Mutual contribution without exact scorekeeping | Forty-minute extra offer remains unaccepted; later contribution optional | Agreed versus offered work and longer-term uncertainty | One exchange cannot establish durable mutuality |
