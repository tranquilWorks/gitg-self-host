# 17.05 — Fresh checks

These fictional cases have a separate [answer key](check-answers.md).

1. You say “no pressure,” then tell a friend you will stop inviting them if they decline a favor. Is refusal genuine?
2. A friend with less capacity receives more practical help over several months and offers welcome attention when able. Does numerical inequality establish exploitation?
3. A helper offers an extra hour of work beyond an agreed ten-minute task. You neither accept nor reject. Can you treat the hour as promised work or as a debt you owe?
