# Inspected sources and claim boundaries

Inspected on 24 September 2026. These are primary papers, their author abstracts, original educational resources or first-party professional/provider guidance. Research, normative advice and practice design are not interchangeable. All case people, dialogue, dates and task designs are original supplied fiction. No source validates the finished companions or closes a human review.

## S01

[The Principles of Etiquette](https://emilypost.com/advice/the-principles-of-etiquette) — Emily Post Institute.

Type: first-party etiquette guidance. Inspected: Principles and respect/consideration/honesty sections. Used for 16.14: Distinguish a purpose of courtesy from a particular manner.

Limit: Normative advice, not universal cultural evidence or a first-impression outcome guarantee.

## S12
