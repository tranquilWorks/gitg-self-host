# 16.16 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** High-context, indirect, and face-sensitive communication

**Scope (unchanged):** Recognize that respect, disagreement, refusal, and emotion may be communicated through implication, timing, silence, hierarchy, or relational context.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can interpret indirect signals cautiously and adapt without treating one communication style as universally superior.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Respect, disagreement, refusal and emotion | Relational meaning without an emotional confession | Several interpretations and minimal practical question | No universal decoder or privileged style |
| Implication, timing and silence | Ari's words, agenda change, absent answer branch | Observation separated from hypotheses and consent | Silence remains unconfirmed |
| Hierarchy and relational context | Senior member is not authorized for others; privacy offer | Appropriate channel and decision authority | No forced public dissent or isolation |
| Cautious adaptation | Volunteered refusal replaces obstacle hypothesis | Revised disposition and limited public account | Formal/consent-sensitive decisions retain explicit processes |
