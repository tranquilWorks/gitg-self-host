# 16.16 — Corrective answers

1. You know the colleague has not committed. State the real cutoff through a welcome channel and obtain whatever explicit authorization the shared-funds process requires. Without it, do not order on their behalf. Indirectness cannot substitute for authority.
2. Retain individual preferences as unknown and offer an appropriate voluntary way to respond or decline, where the process permits it. Do not label silence agreement or infer fear as a fact. Clarify who actually has decision authority.
3. Share only the attendance disposition if authorized or required by the agreed process; do not reveal the private reason or invent a different one. A neutral “They won't be joining” can preserve coordination without turning confidentiality into deception.
