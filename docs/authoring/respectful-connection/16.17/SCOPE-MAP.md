# 16.17 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Intercultural communication and code-switching

**Scope (unchanged):** Adapt to different norms of directness, hierarchy, turn-taking, emotion, time, privacy, and formality while preserving integrity.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can communicate effectively across cultures and explain what was adapted and what boundary remained.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Directness, formality and turn-taking | Chat request versus titled committee agenda note | Two real messages and seven-dimension record | Observed local preferences, not national stereotypes |
| Hierarchy and time | Secretary circulation role; Tuesday cutoff versus Wednesday discussion | Authority and scheduling conflict kept explicit | No invented approval or moved deadline |
| Emotion and privacy | Tone unknown; private contacts cannot be forwarded by default | Unknowns and preserved consent boundary | No forced identity performance or concealed disagreement |
| Integrity across adaptation | Free, optional, one hour, twelve places, second session unconfirmed | Fact comparison and comprehension/format checks | Understanding is not assent or proof of intercultural transfer |
| Code-switching costs and effective communication | Mutual process alternatives and burden review | What changed, what stayed true and actual response if available | Fictional readers cannot establish real effectiveness |
