## Changed-case answer

Make the privacy and publication conditions explicit, allow a name-only or task-focused introduction and a pass, and obtain permission for what enters public notes. Voluntary speaking does not authorize publication of every detail. Ask about needed process changes without attributing the request to a nationality or universal cultural rule.
