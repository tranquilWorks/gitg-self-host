# 17.06 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Boundaries and harmful relationships

**Scope (unchanged):** Recognize abuse, chronic contempt, manipulation, incompatibility, and repeated non-repair.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Reduces access or exits when necessary without dehumanization or endless tolerance.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Abuse, contempt, manipulation and incompatibility | Different definitions and low-risk repeated nickname case | Behavior/impact/repair/recurrence/safety/response account | No diagnosis or demand to prove abuse before limiting contact |
| Repeated non-repair | Apology followed by recurrence; host promise not changed behavior | Separate action, promise and later evidence | No further harm required for a perfect dataset |
| Reduce access or exit when necessary | Feasible departure, different group, limited contact or support route | Controlled action and actual exposure review | Unsafe dependence/threat branch needs individualized planning |
