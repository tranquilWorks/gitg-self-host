# 17.06 — Fresh checks

Attempt these fictional cases before [the answers](check-answers.md).

1. Two friends want different contact frequencies and disagree without contempt or threats. Must one prove the other abusive to limit contact?
2. Someone apologizes after each public humiliation, but the behavior continues. What evidence would distinguish a promise from repair?
3. A person who monitors devices threatens retaliation if you seek help. Should you publish a detailed account or announce a new boundary to test their response?
