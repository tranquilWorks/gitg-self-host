# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. A backward draft confuses a reader, so you replace it with a polished chronological draft before saving anything. What evidence was lost?
2. A reviewer declines your short piece. Must you obtain praise elsewhere to finish the feedback part?
3. Naming the finder fixes a pronoun problem, but the story still feels flat. Did the retry prove the whole experiment successful?
