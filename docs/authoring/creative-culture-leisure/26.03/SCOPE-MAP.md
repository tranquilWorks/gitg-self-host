# 26.03 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Hobbies and lifelong interests

**Scope (unchanged):** Develop activities pursued for enjoyment, identity, community, and skill outside primary work.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Maintains at least one durable interest without needing monetization or prestige.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Enjoyment, identity, learning and company outside work | Four low-cost puzzle sessions over two weeks | Original five-word puzzle, corrected category, variation, optional exchange | No equipment/status purchase or lifelong-identity claim |
| Develop a durable interest | Small feature learned; solver needs hint and leaf explanation | All four occasions inform a weekly twenty-minute plan | One person and four sessions do not establish independent broad appeal |
| Without monetization or prestige | Zero-purchase materials kept in a folder; pressured public format declined | Modest private return and future review chosen | Future month, income and lifelong commitment unproven |
