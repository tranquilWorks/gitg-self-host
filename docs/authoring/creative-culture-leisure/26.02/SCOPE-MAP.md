# 26.02 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Rest and leisure

**Scope (unchanged):** Use unstructured time, recreation, Sabbath, vacation, and recovery deliberately rather than defaulting to numbing consumption.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Leisure reliably restores attention, relationship, or vitality.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unstructured rest and chosen recreation | Quiet and familiar music across three twenty-minute occasions | Agitating, apparently restorative and neutral outcomes all retained | No medical treatment or causal restoration claim |
| Deliberate recovery and fit | Optional notifications limited while urgent contact remains available | Context differences inform a modest repeat opportunity | No required mood, finished entertainment or productivity return |
| Sabbath, vacation and sustainable leisure | Worldview and capacity options with shorter supported routes | One practical enabling condition and a plan-change reason | Rest need not earn its place; broader fatigue causes unresolved |
