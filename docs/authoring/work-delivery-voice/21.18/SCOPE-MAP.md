# 21.18 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Inclusive and accessible work

**Scope (unchanged):** Design expectations, tools, communication, hiring, and advancement to reduce irrelevant barriers.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** A work process becomes more accessible while preserving legitimate job requirements.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
