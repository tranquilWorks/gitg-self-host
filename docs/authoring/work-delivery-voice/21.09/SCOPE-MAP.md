# 21.09 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Collaboration and team contribution

**Scope (unchanged):** Coordinate, communicate, document, share credit, challenge constructively, and support collective outcomes.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** The team becomes more capable and reliable because of participation.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coordinate and communicate | Agreed inputs, output, capacity and receiver | Kai wording; Sora facts, agenda, approval and sending | No unilateral authority over peers |
| Challenge constructively | Observation, consequence, question and correction | Arrival 17:50 and start 18:00 both valid | Difference is not automatically error |
| Document and share credit | Version, decision, open question, next owner | K2 receiver readiness and specific contributions | Praise is not team-performance evidence |
| Collective outcome | Dependable handoff and retrospective | Omitted contact supplied; readiness confirmed | Rework and durable team capability unmeasured |
