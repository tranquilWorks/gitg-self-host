# 21.14 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Efficiency, thoroughness, and quality tradeoffs

**Scope (unchanged):** Choose when work should be fast, sufficient, robust, elegant, reversible, or exceptional.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Quality level is consciously matched to consequence rather than driven by haste or perfectionism.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
