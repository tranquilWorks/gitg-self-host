# 21.14 — Efficiency, thoroughness, and quality tradeoffs

Choose a quality level that fits the consequence of the work, then hold the essential standard when time becomes tight. Allow twenty minutes to compare three tasks, one bounded work session and fifteen minutes to review. Perform only the harmless paper task. A timer helps expose tradeoffs; it does not waive a requirement or qualify anyone to release consequential instructions.

## Match care to what failure would mean

Fast, sufficient, robust, elegant, reversible and exceptional describe different qualities, not a single ladder of personal virtue. A brainstorming note can be rough if its purpose is to make options visible for immediate discussion. A reusable guide needs accurate facts and enough context for an absent reader. A safety-critical instruction needs its own qualified process and evidence. Applying the same effort level to all three can waste attention on the first while underchecking the last.

Consequence includes who relies on the result, how an error is discovered and how readily it can be corrected. A wrong draft title seen by its author is different from a wrong entrance on a notice copied to many readers. A file can be editable while its downstream effects are difficult to reverse. Consider distribution and reliance, not only whether an undo button exists. Required organizational standards remain requirements even when a particular outcome happened to be harmless.

Define sufficient in terms of purpose. “Good enough” without criteria often means “I feel done” or “the deadline arrived.” A disposable note may need three intelligible options and one open question. A reusable guide may need verified time, access and ownership. State what may remain rough and what cannot. This makes stopping a reasoned decision rather than an excuse for haste or endless polishing.

Robustness adds useful resilience to foreseeable variation: clear units, an update owner or a missing-input rule. Elegance can reduce cognitive effort through a simple structure. Neither should be dismissed as decoration when it makes the result usable. Optional ornament, by contrast, may add no meaningful value under the task's constraints. Exceptional effort is justified by a particular need, not by the fear that ordinary competent work is embarrassing.

Budget effort in stages: establish essentials, verify them, then consider refinement. If an essential fact is still unknown at the boundary, classify the work as unready. Ask whether the accepting owner can approve a genuine narrower purpose or a new date. Do not move an essential into the optional column after discovering it takes time. A smaller internal draft can be useful, but it must be labeled for that limited use.

Inspect your impulse to stop or continue without diagnosing yourself. Are you avoiding an uncomfortable verification question by polishing typography? Are you calling the task finished to relieve deadline anxiety? The answer is not always “work harder.” It may be ask for the missing input, stop unnecessary decoration, renegotiate a commitment or refer a consequential task to the proper owner. Actual outcomes and user needs should govern the decision.

Review both effort and readiness. An overrun can expose an unrealistic estimate or an unresolved dependency. A successful result does not prove that skipped assurance was justified. A draft left unready at the time limit can be the responsible outcome. Describe what additional care bought and where extra work would have little value, while keeping the original estimate visible.

## Worked example

A reusable guide reaches its twelve-minute budget with a missing evening entrance. Its author could use the main entrance from memory, but that would convert uncertainty into a false fact. The guide stays DRAFT. After the authorized answer arrives, seven more active minutes complete and check it. Nineteen minutes is an overrun against the original budget, even though the final guide is useful. The lesson is to confirm the dependency earlier, not to pretend the timer made the guess acceptable.

## Three complete task cards

**A — Disposable discussion note.** A private paper discussion starts at 16:30. Produce three possible names for a fictional map club and one question about how the group will choose. Names may be rough and no name will be adopted from this note alone. Keep it legible and label it BRAINSTORM. Suggested budget: eight minutes. There is no need for a logo, exhaustive market research or publication.

**B — Reusable newcomer guide.** Supply purpose, time, access/location, materials and contact for **Photo Review, 6 November 2026, 20:00–20:30 UTC, Cedar Center, Elm Room**. Purpose: compare paper photographs; bring **one print**; questions: **Photo Desk**, a fictional role. The evening entrance is **unconfirmed** and must be supplied by **Venue Owner**. The readable plain-text guide must include an accepted update owner and be rechecked when event details change. The current proposed writer has a twelve-minute first work period; decorative styling is estimated at five optional minutes. None of this authorizes release without the required facts.

**C — Consequential operating instruction.** A proposed instruction would govern hazardous equipment. The equipment, hazards, standards, reviewer competence and operating authority are not supplied. Classify the need for the governing qualified review and evidence; **do not write, test or execute an operating procedure**. A toy checklist or arbitrary time budget cannot supply missing assurance.

For B, the paper source contains exactly the facts above. Drafting can proceed with a visible unresolved entrance; no source establishes that the daytime entrance is open. The proposed update owner is Photo Desk, but acceptance must still be confirmed. These missing inputs are part of the quality decision, not a request for you to invent a real venue.

## Make and inspect the quality decision

1. Give each card a consequence, reversibility judgment, minimum result, verification method and stop condition. Output: three different quality decisions, with C referred to its proper process.
2. Choose A or B; the [later packet](later-packet.md) illustrates B. Produce essentials before optional finish and record the time boundary. Output: a draft with honest readiness and unresolved facts.
3. Compare the supplied result with the declared criteria. Preserve any authorized extension and actual active effort separately from waiting. Output: readiness and effort judgments that can disagree.
4. Identify one valuable extra check and one low-value refinement. Output: a next-time rule tied to consequence, not a universal command to go faster or polish more.

## Support and transfer

Use spoken text, larger print or a shorter first session. A harder branch offers an attractive template that omits the update owner; do not let finish hide maintainability. For real work, follow actual quality standards, acceptance rules and professional duties. Stop when a task exceeds your authority or the harmless practice scope.
