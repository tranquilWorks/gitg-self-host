# 21.11 — Career adaptability and marketable capability

Turn a changing work demand into a small demonstration of transferable skill. Allow two twenty-five-minute reviews and thirty minutes for a sample. Use the fictional notices and inventory below. You do not need to buy training, change jobs, predict an industry or use confidential employer work. The question is what you can now demonstrate under a new requirement, with your existing strengths and constraints kept visible.

## Distinguish a signal from a forecast

A vacancy notice describes one organization's stated demand at one time. It may contain a firm requirement, a preference, an aspirational list or an advertisement. A practitioner's account describes experience from a particular position. Neither is the whole labor market. Record source, date, claim type and scope before translating a signal into a learning priority. Contradictory sources are useful because they show where a conclusion is fragile.

Technology can change a tool or workflow while leaving the underlying reasoning valuable. A reporting system may automate a total but still require someone to check units, define what the number includes and explain it to another person. Industry practices can change what must be documented; social changes can make remote participation and accessible communication more important. These influences interact, so do not explain every new demand with a single fashionable technology.

Separate the durable fundamental from the implementation. Numerical checking can transfer from paper to a spreadsheet. Clear writing can transfer from a local meeting to an asynchronous report. A reliable handoff can transfer across tools while retaining source, assumptions, owner and next action. Transfer is not automatic: an expert in one tool may still need practice in a new format. Existing competence is a resource, not a reason to skip a meaningful demonstration.

Choose a gap from an artifact rather than anxiety. “I need to learn everything about automation” cannot guide a bounded task. “My report gives a number but not the inputs or calculation a remote reader needs” can. Set the acceptance criterion before revising, use a free or already available resource, and make the sample small enough to finish within your actual time. Family responsibilities, disability, income and access are legitimate constraints, not lack of ambition.

Marketable capability means useful work someone can inspect, not exaggerated credentials. A checked sample supports a specific claim about that task. It does not make you an accountant, data scientist or expert in every reporting tool. Describe what you did, under which supplied conditions and with what remaining help. Do not publish protected work or make a portfolio artifact look like paid client experience when it is a simulation.

Update the plan from both success and uncertainty. A correct calculation with an unreadable summary still misses the accessible-reporting demand. A polished summary with an unreproducible number misses verification. One finished sample can justify a next practice step while leaving employment outcomes unknown. If the two job notices disagree about a tool, practicing the shared fundamental may be a better immediate choice than buying a tool-specific credential.

## Worked example

A report says “27 units available.” Its author knows the calculation but a remote reader cannot reproduce it. The missing capability is transparent explanation, not necessarily a new software package. Adding starting quantity, movements, reserved stock, formulas and an accurate text summary makes the reasoning inspectable. A fresh dataset then checks whether the skill transfers beyond copying one answer.

## Complete fictional evidence and sample

These are invented market signals, not real job advertisements or current labor-market evidence. **Notice A, 1 September 2026:** a records role requires a readable summary, reproducible quantity check and remote handoff; experience with fictional **Quartz** is preferred. **Notice B, 12 September 2026:** another records role requires the same three outputs, accepts any approved tool and does not require Quartz. **Practitioner note, 18 September 2026:** one willing fictional records worker says automated exports still need checks of definitions and reserved quantities; this is one account, not proof of a sector trend.

Technology signal: automated exports and a named tool preference. Industry/workflow signal: records must support remote checking. Social/access signal: recipients need readable asynchronous information. Their relevance in this case is stipulated. No source gives hiring probabilities, wages, training returns or a forecast that the old role will disappear.

The starter inventory is labeled **PAPER-STOCK-A, 20 September**, with units of paper packs. Beginning stock **20**, received **30**, issued **18**, and **5 packs reserved** within the remaining stock. There are no other movements. The old report states only “27 available.” A reserved pack remains physically on hand but cannot be counted as available for the new request. The intended reader must reproduce on-hand and available quantities without asking the author.

Acceptance requires four things: identify the dated input card and units; show both calculations; distinguish on-hand from available; and provide a plain-text handoff with the remaining limitation. The authorized input card is the source for this toy, not evidence of a real count. For a fresh transfer, **PAPER-STOCK-B, 21 September** has beginning **15**, received **25**, issued **17**, reserved **4**, no other movements and the same units. All materials are supplied; a new tool is optional.

## Build a demonstrated adaptation

1. Compare the three dated signals and classify each statement. Map the shared demands to numerical checking, communication and dependable handoff. Output: a short evidence map with the Quartz disagreement and sample limits.
2. Assess the old report against the four criteria and choose the missing reproducibility as a gap. Use these instructions and the supplied cards as the free learning resource; set a thirty-minute limit. Output: a specific capability target without a purchase or credential claim.
3. Write the A report with formulas, definitions and a readable summary. Save it before the [later packet](later-packet.md). Output: a non-sensitive version a remote reader could check.
4. Produce the B report from its own inputs, then compare with the supplied reader check. Output: a fresh transfer result, not a copied number.
5. Update the plan: name the narrow demonstrated capability, a remaining practice need and the unresolved market conclusion. Output: an honest sample description and next step.

## Support and transfer

Use a calculator, spoken arithmetic or a simple table; the criterion is reproducibility, not mental speed. A harder case omits reserved stock: report on-hand and mark availability unknown rather than assuming zero reservations. In actual career work, inspect current relevant sources, obtain permission for accounts and preserve protected materials. One sample cannot resolve licensing, contractual or high-consequence career decisions.

## Scope and evidence boundary

The canonical record has no professional_boundary field. These practical safeguards do not add canonical metadata. Applicability remains context_sensitive; normative status remains cross_tradition_core_or_broadly_recurrent. Paid employment, a role change or public disclosure is not compulsory. Fiction and no attempt are legitimate. Keep private or protected records outside app evidence. Human dignity is never scored.
