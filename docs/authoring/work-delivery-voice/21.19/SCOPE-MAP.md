# 21.19 — Individual canonical scope map

**Canonical competency:** Work boundaries, rest, and freedom from total identity

**Scope (unchanged):** Protect health, family, civic life, worship, leisure, and dignity from indefinite work expansion.

**Evidence of progress (unchanged):** Can set or negotiate a boundary and maintain a sense of worth beyond occupational performance.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and supplied work | Inspectable output | Remaining breadth / limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Protect chosen nonwork roles | Duty, urgency, priorities and valued activity | Optional twenty-minute task has ten minutes before close | No compulsory leisure or family role |
| Set or negotiate a boundary | Status, next start and accepted urgent route | Jo accepts next-day slot; Tess already covers Duty Desk | No imposed coworker burden |
| Rest and logistics | Actual activity and interruptions | Thirty planned, seven checking, twenty-three drawing | Not uninterrupted rest or a tested new habit |
| Worth beyond performance | Urge versus duty and responsible repair | Next-day 09:20 finish, 09:25 acceptance | No health outcome or worth score |
