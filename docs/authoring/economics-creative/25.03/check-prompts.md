# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. You prefer a richly ambiguous sentence. The new task is a brief factual notice. Must the same sentence remain the best choice?
2. A reader interprets your restrained departure as routine tidying. Is that proof of poor taste or proof that the work has no value?
3. You reread your own revision after a break and notice a stronger pause. Can you label that independent audience feedback?
