# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. A 12% rate applies to a base that falls from 500 to 450. What happens to revenue?
2. A seller remits an eight-unit charge and the supplied price response shifts five to buyers. Who bears the modeled burden?
3. A project has a six-unit annual surplus but future upkeep and existing debt are unspecified. Is long-term sustainability proved?
