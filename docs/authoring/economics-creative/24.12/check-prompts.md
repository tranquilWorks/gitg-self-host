# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. Workers hold shares through an employee-ownership arrangement. Can you infer that every worker has one vote over all enterprise decisions?
2. A service has 240 revenue and 210 operating costs. A fee reduction removes 50 revenue. Is the original 30 reserve contribution enough to make the new operation balanced?
3. A nonprofit holds a public hearing. Does that prove affected users can control the board decision?
