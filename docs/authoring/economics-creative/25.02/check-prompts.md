# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. A description gives a red box and a torn label but no sound. May you log a creak as an observed detail to reach eight entries?
2. You rename a cup a fortress but its shape, light and position change nothing in the scene. What would make this a stronger transformation?
3. A later description moves the paper beyond the shadow. Can you keep your earlier observed alignment as a fact about the new scene?
