# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. You change punctuation, replace half the words and add a new setting, then attribute the effect entirely to a pause. What is missing?
2. A non-speaking maker marks three passes and produces a performance script. Must they claim an audible performance to count their work?
3. A reader understands the revised line but finds it less mysterious. Did the revision simply fail?
