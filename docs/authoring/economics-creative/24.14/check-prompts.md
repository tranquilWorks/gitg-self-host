# Fresh checks

Attempt before [reading answers](check-answers.md).

1. Producer P makes eight grain or four cloth per hour; Q makes six grain or six cloth. Who has comparative advantage in cloth, and would 1.5 grain per cloth lie between their opportunity costs?
2. A 24-unit buffer faces three weeks at 12 units per week. How much demand remains uncovered without replenishment?
3. A 200 foreign-unit invoice changes from 0.8 to 0.9 domestic units per foreign unit. What changes, and does a cheap quote establish lawful supply?
