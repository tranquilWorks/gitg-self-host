These three competencies form one guided-learning cohort: make tacit judgment
visible, transmit it with holder authority, and make participation accessible.
This relationship justifies the three-item batch; materials and evidence remain
individual, with no shared generic completion claim.

Community immersion and professional qualification remain untested. No safety-knot use. |

Each guide has nine sections and two separately revealed checks, a different
worked example, a usable no-partner analysis route, accessible/advanced pathways
and supportive, partial, adverse and inconclusive outcome boundaries. The source
actions remain three stable runtime actions, not nine new scored actions.

10.12 uses original low-stakes instructional material without an efficacy claim.
[UNESCO Articles 2, 13–15](https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/convention-safeguarding-intangible-cultural-heritage)
support transmission, community participation and respect for access restrictions;
the exact consent form is product-authored, not a treaty procedure.
[CAST's FAQ](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/more/frequently-asked-questions/)
supports barrier-aware design that retains meaningful learning goals; it does not
validate the fictional route trial. Prior source applicability is unchanged.

Byte-exact pre-projection packages are pinned in each quality folder. Prospective
recovery checks allow only declared compiler output while preserving IDs, parent
mappings, completion rules, scoring, activation and event replay. Formal scope,
instructional, cold-start, integration, learner, qualified and owner reviews stay
open. Author readiness is not independent acceptance.
