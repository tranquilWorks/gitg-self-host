# Learning to dependable performance: three runtime rewrites

The owner's 8 September continuation explicitly prioritizes more substantive
competency content after PR #70. This related three-ID cohort connects retaining
and applying an idea (10.01), correcting one weak operation (10.02), and producing
consistently acceptable work (21.03). Each has its own materials, evidence and
claim limits. Their tasks are not interchangeable: remembering a definition does
not demonstrate a live formula correction, and one correct formula does not
establish dependable outputs across three briefs.

These are original educational exercises. Selection means authored runtime content,
not formal A/B/C, independent learner, qualified or owner acceptance. The formal
ledger remains unchanged. Readers and separately labeled checking files live in
each competency's quality folder; runtime uses the canonical package YAML.

## 10.01 — The science of learning

Starting ability: read or listen to short explanations and attempt small arithmetic
with ordinary access supports. The complete supplied route is a bounded trial of
learning methods. Someone already fluent in these ideas needs genuinely unfamiliar
material and a checking source before claiming a fitting challenge; the guide
states that prerequisite rather than pretending easy recall demonstrates growth.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and practice | Observable evidence and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retrieval | `learning-methods`; first, second and delayed prompts require original responses before keys | Six initial explanations per session; label content cues/lookups separately from access aids |
| Spacing | `how-to-use`, action 2 and action 3 | Private record of actual intervals; day-one work cannot stand in for week-delayed retention |
| Interleaving | `second-mixed` alternates range calculation, fractions, mean, units and median explanation | Responses require selecting among methods; simply rereading six definitions is insufficient |
| Elaboration | `six-ideas`, contrasting worked examples and action 1 | Explain total/count versus middle position and why equal answers do not make methods equivalent |
| Examples | Even set and skewed set before different learner inputs | Compare methods, then apply to a fresh changed-extreme-value question |
| Sleep | `learning-methods`, action 2, adaptation | Protect ordinary sleep/rest and supports; no sleep measure or claimed sleep effect |
| Feedback | Three separate keys and `session-record` | Original error, correction and reason remain distinguishable; copying a key is not initial recall |
| Retention and transfer after delay | `delayed-transfer`: original and changed largest value, with explanation | Arithmetic desk checks verify the key. Only an actual delayed learner attempt could establish retention or transfer for that learner |

All facets are jointly part of this learning-method trial; format and supported
response modes are alternative access routes. The six ideas are not an intelligence
test. A response can be numerically correct yet fail explanation or unit checks.
The next review targets a particular unresolved method or application error.

## 10.02 — Deliberate practice

Starting ability: recognize four small whole numbers; use familiar spreadsheet
input or the complete paper route. The spreadsheet component is choosing live input
references, not recalling a known total. Paper preserves inclusion, exclusion and
recalculation while leaving automatic updating untested. A coach is optional and
must freely agree; changed content help limits the comparison.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and practice | Observable evidence and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Isolate a weak component | `method`, action 1, baseline with four designated inputs and one distractor | Four criteria fixed before attempting; baseline identifies a missed or unstable criterion. All-correct work is maintenance, not invented weakness |
| Edge of competence | Bounded time and harmless synthetic inputs; `next-cycle` provides nonadjacent inputs if the default is routine | A fitting challenge permits inspectable errors; no speed pressure or unsafe escalation |
| Feedback | Separate baseline key and `diagnosis` | Exact endpoint, inclusion, copied-total or input-entry defect maps to one correction |
| Repeat | Fresh comparable retry dataset with unchanged criterion meanings and supports | Retain original formula/addends, both results and observed fraction out of four; extra help or time is a comparison limit |
| Increase difficulty | `next-cycle` changes only adjacent to nonadjacent input selection after control | A concrete later cycle with checking procedure; difficulty does not increase inside the retained scored retry |
| Measurable improvement beyond time | Per-criterion baseline/retry comparison | Omitted-input example is 1/4; corrected retry is 4/4. 4/4 to 4/4 is maintenance. No claim of causality, lasting retention or whole-skill mastery |

The existing four action identities, bounded-frequency measures, durations,
comparability rules and completion rule are retained exactly. Teaching C1–C4 does
not replace those app evidence fields. The progression is an unscored future route,
not a fifth action or extra completion credit.

The executed `10.02/component-desk-run.json` checks competent, partial, plausible
incorrect and unrelated-input cases using synthetic arithmetic and a deliberately
limited formula parser. It does not claim a spreadsheet application was operated.
A typed correct total fails to establish live input references and updating.

## 21.03 — Foundational craft competence

Starting ability: extract supplied facts and enter or speak them using a familiar
safe tool. The complete default samples communication-design craft through three
fictional notices. No real event, publication or licensed activity is implied.

| Canonical facet | Teaching and practice | Observable evidence and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core knowledge | `preparation`, action 1 | Identify authoritative brief, required facts and accuracy versus decoration |
| Skills | Separate seed-swap, repair-café and sketch-walk attempts | Extract and arrange three distinct fact sets; keep first outputs and copied-date errors visible |
| Tools | Familiar paper/text/speech route and supplied layout | Name actual tool and support conditions; unrelated professional tools remain untested |
| Standards | Supplied F1–F5 accuracy and U1–U2 usability criteria | No averaged score lets polished layout cancel an incorrect date or missing contact |
| Safety requirements | Fictional briefs, no publication, bounded component and capability-fit route | No invented authority, real contact data or hazardous/regulated work |
| Repeated competent work | Same standard across three outputs, correction and contact-change retest | Record all three rows independently; a single impressive notice does not establish repeatability |

Accuracy is mechanically desk-checkable for the supplied labeled-text fixtures.
Reading order, zoom and paper/speech access require the actual chosen inspection;
uninspected access remains unknown. The retained QA-01 five-case run and specific
wrong-date exercise distinguish tidy but inaccurate output. Three fictional
notices do not establish general occupational competence or live audience usability.

## Source and review boundaries

Existing canonical source references and their limitations remain unchanged. This
cohort adds original instructions, arithmetic, fictional briefs and product checking
criteria, not new empirical, clinical or intervention-effectiveness claims. The
curriculum's broader evidence preference for independent types is not satisfied by
an author's synthetic desk check or by the application's existing closeout rule.
Independent cold-start, real learner follow-through, qualified and owner reviews
remain pending and are not counted by the tailored coverage total.
