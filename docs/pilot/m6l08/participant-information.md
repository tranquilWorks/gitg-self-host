We want to learn whether you can understand and use the product over several
weeks. We are evaluating the product, not you. Its assessment is provisional;
practice completion does not establish mastery or measure human worth.
Participation is voluntary. You can decline any task, take breaks, keep your
screen private, use your access aids, or stop without losing ordinary product
access. Tell the observer if anything feels unsuitable or unsafe.

With your permission, an observer records which product task you tried, the
study week, whether you completed, could not complete, stopped or skipped it,
whether help was offered, a broad time band you choose, optional recommendation
fit, and a broad barrier category. We use a random study reference to connect
these observations across weeks. This is pseudonymous, not anonymous. This
observation study adds no audio/video recording, automatic activity tracking or
remote telemetry. Normal assessment timing remains part of the product's private
assessment record; its optional calibration reuse is described below.
We do not copy your assessment answers, private notes, contacts, share codes,
practice evidence, developmental scores or details of real-world interactions.

Your normal account records remain on the self-hosted instance and in its
backups under the operator's account policy. Study observation records are
separate from those records. A study summary stays private even when it omits
individual rows. Any later sharing of findings must be explained and separately
reviewed; this agreement does not permit publishing your private records.

You can ask the named contact to withdraw your study observations. They will
stop collection, exclude them from new analyses, remove the applicable working
copies and regenerate retained summaries under the stated policy. Backups and
already distributed files cannot always be recalled immediately; the dates and
recipients above explain those limits. Stopping study observation does not
silently delete your account or withdraw assessment-calibration consent.

Assessment calibration is a **separate optional choice** on each completed
assessment's in-app consent page. That page lets you inspect the contribution
and withdraw it from future exports. It includes item responses and timing,
uses a separate linkable token, and explains that earlier downloaded files cannot
be recalled automatically. Declining calibration does not prevent this usability
study. We do not link the two datasets under this protocol.
