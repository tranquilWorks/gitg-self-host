# Begin the actual study

These are ready-to-complete drafts for the coordinator. No invitation has been
sent, session booked, consent obtained or reviewer assigned by creating this
file. Use the [launch record](review-and-corrections.md) to record the responsible
people and candidate approval, then use the drafts below. Keep completed contact,
consent and scheduling records in the restricted study workspace, outside Git.

## Invitation draft

Fill every bracketed field and confirm the contact route before sending.

> We are inviting adults who may find a guided planning and practice tool useful
> to help us evaluate Grounded Growth. We want to learn where the product is
> clear, confusing, burdensome or inaccessible. This evaluates the product, not
> your ability or worth, and is not a treatment or a test of personal improvement.
>
> The proposed commitment is one 30–45 minute entry session, ordinary voluntary
> use, and three optional weekly contacts of about 10–15 minutes each. You may
> skip tasks, take breaks, use your access aids or stop at any time. There is no
> requirement to complete a practice or disclose private life details.
>
> With your separate agreement, the observer records a small set of categorical
> usability observations. The study adds no recording or automatic tracking.
> Assessment-data reuse is another optional choice; you can decline it and still
> take part in the usability study. We will explain records, recipients,
> retention and withdrawal before asking for consent.
>
> Sessions: [location or approved remote arrangement and proposed dates].
> Compensation, if any: [state the actual arrangement, including withdrawal].
> Contact: [coordinator and approved contact method].
> To express interest, contact the coordinator. Please do not send assessment
> answers, health information, private notes or account credentials.

An expression of interest is not observation or calibration consent. Do not
place people into an observation dataset merely because they responded.

## Short screening conversation

Use the same neutral prompts with each interested person; let them decline:

1. Are you an adult, and is the product's supported language suitable for you?
2. Would a tool connecting personal direction, one practical action and weekly
   review be relevant to something you want to try? No private explanation needed.
3. Would an entry session and optional contacts over three weeks be feasible?
4. What device/input method would you prefer? What session accommodations would
   make participation workable? No diagnosis or sensitive demographic is required.
5. Have you used similar planning tools before? A broad yes/no answer is enough.
6. May the coordinator contact you through the method and frequency you choose?

Record only what is needed to arrange a suitable session and describe recruitment
coverage. Keep contact details separate from the random study reference. Do not
recruit by score, apparent motivation, personality category or expected success.
Do not pressure an employee, dependent or service recipient to participate.
Record selection constraints and coverage gaps honestly; this is a convenience
cohort and cannot establish population prevalence or fairness.

## Reviewer invitation brief

> Please review the attached M6L-08 protocol and your proposed area of
> responsibility: [UX/accessibility, measurement/statistics, or privacy/safety].
> We need a named assessment of the study design and, later, its actual evidence.
> An automated test pass is not the requested judgment.
>
> Before collection, identify necessary changes to recruitment, task/practice
> scope, consent, data fields, access arrangements, missingness handling and
> analysis. State which questions the proposed data cannot answer. Record your
> relevant competence, scope, conflicts, limitations and whether the study is
> suitable to proceed within that scope. There is no expectation of endorsement.
>
> After collection, review the agreed private evidence packet, including
> unsuccessful and inconclusive observations. Record supported findings, limits,
> unresolved questions and proposed versioned corrections. Do not sign claims
> beyond your competence or the evidence. [Coordinator] will agree access,
> deliverables, dates and any fee before data is shared.

One person may cover multiple roles only where their competence and conflicts
support it. Do not invent names, signatures, acceptance or credentials. Share no
participant file simply to solicit reviewer interest; send the source protocol
first, and arrange private evidence access under the disclosed agreement later.

## Fieldwork sequence

Day numbers are relative to an actually agreed start, not booked dates. A missed
contact remains unobserved unless the participant volunteers more information.

| When | Coordinator action | Retained output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before invitations | Assign coordinator/custodian and reviewers; complete the launch record, consent details, candidate/version and applicable review conditions | Private responsibilities and launch decision; approved invitation route |
| Recruitment | Send the completed invitation through the agreed route; screen without sensitive data; agree access arrangements and contact frequency | Private recruitment/contact flow and coverage gaps |
| Day 0 | Explain disclosure; record voluntary observation consent; assign independent random reference; perform cycle 0 tasks from the protocol | Actual consent receipt and observed outcomes; no guessed rows |
| Day 7 | Reconfirm willingness; offer cycle 1 check-in, weekly review and next-week planning tasks | Cycle 1 observations or explicit unknown/non-return status |
| Day 14 | Reconfirm willingness; offer cycle 2, including recovery only where relevant | Cycle 2 observations and conflicting findings preserved |
| Day 21 | Offer cycle 3, history and exit tasks; confirm withdrawal/retention choices | Cycle 3 observations, known/unknown study flow and lifecycle record |
| After collection | Exclude withdrawals, generate a new private summary, assemble consent/provenance and limitations for reviewers | Restricted source/summary digests and qualified review packet |
| After review | Record actual findings and warranted corrections; schedule bounded follow-up implementation/retest | Signed conclusions, correction register and unresolved axes |

This three-week product study does not itself supply all eight assessment evidence
axes. Use the [assessment evidence plan](assessment-evidence-plan.md) to arrange
separately reviewed measures and any longer follow-up; do not infer them from
product completion or silently join the two datasets.

The immediate execution input is the coordinator's identity/contact, proposed
participants or recruitment route, and reviewer assignments. If records already
exist, provide their authorized private paths and consent/provenance information
instead of starting a duplicate collection. Never put private records into a PR.
